## Geographic availability: what the new comparator filter proves

- **Company registration country is not the trader's eligibility.** Keep the old legal-entity filter distinct from the new country-of-residence filter.
- Three states: **available under a cited official policy**, **explicitly restricted**, **unverified**. Missing data is never a positive availability assertion.
- Product scopes differ. For FTMO, CFD availability for a Chinese resident **does not** mean FTMO Futures is accessible in mainland China.
- Citizen status, permanent residency, KYC, sanctions, trading-platform access and future rule changes can override a headline country status. Do not geolocate a visitor and present that as a residence guarantee.
- The initial filter uses official published territorial policies for **FTMO CFD, Topstep, My Funded Futures and Bullwaves Prime**, and offers 40 country choices. It will expand **firm by firm, after source verification**. The per-firm source and date are visible in the filter's evidence section.

## Affiliate controls and GOAT Funded Trader (GFT)

Do **not** remove affiliates or move PropRadar ranks because of a payment, negotiated referral rate, or unsourced social posts. Equally, no one earns placement by paying. The program must pass the same legal-entity, payment-eligibility, fraud/Trustpilot, product-rule and country checks as every other firm.

The official GFT help center documents several potentially material **contractual payout conditions**:

- First reward request is subject to identity verification and signing the trader agreement; a risk-team review may hold a reward and the general two-business-day processing period has exceptions.
- Payout methods have limits; bank transfer is currently offered only in specified African jurisdictions. Other residents need alternate processors.
- The published VPN/VPS policy expressly invalidates profits from trades executed through a VPN/hosting service and can trigger a straight breach (with a narrow first-login exception).
- Shared account, wallet and location policies can trigger separate account and payment reviews.

These are **not**, without more, findings that GFT routinely refuses valid payouts. Preserve the existing score and commercial relationship until a documented, material finding passes the project's sensitive-change approval gate. Request details/documentation of concrete cases before a public warning or downgrade.
