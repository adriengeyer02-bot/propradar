# Contractual rules recalibration — batch 2 (19 November 2026)

**Decision principle:** published restrictions are not automatically hidden; sanction severity must follow the explicit individual consequence, and one generic discretionary clause is counted once. Do not treat mere complaints or contract possibilities as independently proven refused payouts. `SCORING.md` clarification is staged in open PR #186. This batch is **not a certificate of complete Terms/FAQ coverage for all firms**.

- [Current Base Camp news conditions](https://alpinefunded.com/faq/base-camp-news/): **Standard** may have an account hard breach for opening **or closing**, including stops, within ±5 minutes; **Swing** permits news. Do not silently apply the restriction to all variants or dismiss this tension as resolved.
- [Current Base Camp payout conditions](https://alpinefunded.com/faq/base-camp-payouts/): 5% profit, three trading days, early payouts held until the third without the paid add-on, fixed level-based rewards.
- [Homepage](https://alpinefunded.com/): sells **three** main programmes including Alpine Pass, and broadly advertises rewards within 24 hours or a $500 extra credit. This headline needs written clarification against the Pass weekly processing timetable; do not characterize the difference as a proved payout denial.

### Verified corrections made to its structured rules

1. The 'gambling' threshold is **more than half of trades held under two minutes**, not the previous one-minute figure. It is a **trading-style proportion**, not a universal minimum hold on every individual trade.
2. The programme-specific, well-linked trading-rules FAQ is `clear`, not automatically `buried`. It specifically says the two currently structured programme families can lose the account for violating its prohibited strategies, so `blocking` is **retained**; the recalibration is **not** a blanket reduction.
3. A single `discretionary-clause` now captures the legally distinct power to decide what conduct is 'unprofessional' / reputationally harmful and what sanction applies (GTC §§7.3, 12.3), plus the ongoing-contract term-change power (§15.1). It is a **contractual risk**, not proof that the firm actually confiscated earned payouts.
4. The Base Camp payout restriction is readily available in its dedicated FAQ, so its clarity changes to `clear` while severity remains `major` (first two payouts held without the add-on). The old Peak news FAQ URL currently redirects to the new site; that specific programme/counterpart rule needs re-confirmation before recategorisation.

Calculation with the **unchanged** V1 formula: raw load **28.5 → 30.25**, restriction deduction **−7 → −8**. Editorial base **58**, proposed buyer-facing score **51 → 50**. The increase is due to the newly verified discretionary contract risk, not to the already advertised trade restrictions. It is **not** a manual editorial downgrade and is pending review. Affiliation is irrelevant.

**Still missing before completion:** the actual terms governing **Alpine Pass** (the September PDF does not name that programme), formal allocation of Standard versus Swing news restrictions in the per-programme keys, current 2-Step news exemptions, and the identity/current terms of the third-party funded-programme counterparty. The latter is a future-contract possibility under GTC §9, not proof of missed earned rewards. Verify payout claims independently; company-issued reward certificates are not independent bank proof. A separate complete Alpine Pass audit could change the programme average and must not be invented in advance.
