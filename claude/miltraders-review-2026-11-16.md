# MilTraders — initial review, 16 November 2026

## Editorial assessment

58/100, watchlist, partially verified. This is a new editorial score, not a measured probability or a replacement for an older rating. Positive weight comes from identifiable programme rules, one-time USD fees, detailed withdrawal conditions and a named contracting operator. Confidence is limited by a small public review sample, company-only payout aggregates, unverified registry details and contractual discretion. No affiliation, no authenticated payout trail and no adjudicated payment-failure finding are recorded.

A $500 minimum and a 90% simulated-funded share apply; withdrawal caps do not equal profit share.

## Legal and payment limits

The [terms v3.0](https://miltraders.com/terms/) name MILTRADERS L.L.C-FZ, Meydan Free Zone, Dubai; formation 2643914, commercial licence 2643914.01; Meydan Grandstand, 6th floor, Meydan Road, Nad Al Sheba, Dubai. These are company disclosures, not independent registry authentication or financial authorization. Article 5.3 expressly disclaims broker/financial-intermediary status.

- Articles 2 and 19 apply rule amendments to existing accounts.
- The [website FAQ](https://miltraders.com/faqs/) promises the pending payout after a gambling-behaviour finding. Terms 12.5 and the [current help article](https://intercom.help/miltraders/en/articles/14664370-what-is-considered-gambling-behavior) instead allow approval or refusal at the firm's discretion. The profile preserves this discrepancy; it does not infer a particular unpaid claim is valid.
- Article 10 makes selection for a live account compulsory, blocks simulated accounts and preserves eligible payouts already requested, with specified refunds for certain other accounts. The first three withdrawals on a live account have an 80% trader share, then 90%.
- Article 9.6 suspends payouts during insolvency. Additional verification extends the stated 48-hour processing time.
- Article 21 describes an internal complaints process, England and Wales law and LCIA arbitration seated in Dubai, with an exception for small claims.
- The [refund policy](https://miltraders.com/refund-policy/) has a final-sale rule after service commencement, with listed exceptions and stated EU/UK withdrawal conditions. The profile does not give a legal opinion on enforceability.
- [News rules](https://intercom.help/miltraders/en/articles/14664351-can-i-trade-during-high-impact-news-events): Zero funded accounts must be flat around restricted high-impact USD announcements ±60 seconds; other listed programmes permit news within a coherent strategy.

## Public feedback and payout provenance

[Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/miltraders.com) displayed 3.9/5 across 13 reviews. The visible language-filtered material includes positive platform feedback, a withdrawal-refusal allegation and a disputed KYC complaint with a company reply attributing the check to Rise. Individual claims were not authenticated, the complete review population was not manually coded, and no social sentiment counts are invented. A general investment-risk category notice is not treated as rating withholding or review-manipulation evidence.

The [company homepage](https://miltraders.com/) displayed $266,990 paid since launch and $52,670 over 40 payouts for November 2026. This is an official company aggregate, not independent reconciliation. The [company's proof article](https://intercom.help/miltraders/en/articles/16007282-how-do-i-know-miltraders-actually-pays) points to anonymised social/Discord certificates and private Rise records; no authenticated customer receipt or transaction trail was established here.

Founding year, independent company registry validation and comprehensive court/regulator searches remain gaps. Failure to find independent press or registry corroboration in this scoped pass is not a finding that no such records or proceedings exist.
