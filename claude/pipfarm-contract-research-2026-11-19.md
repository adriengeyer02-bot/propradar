## What the current primary sources say

1. **Programmes and scope:** The [official challenge comparison](https://help.pipfarm.com/en/articles/0873123-challenge-mode-comparison) distinguishes Classic, Endurance and Consistency, each sold as one-stage or two-stage. The [payout guide](https://help.pipfarm.com/en/articles/9367703-how-payouts-work) also distinguishes three Instant variants (Equity Trailing, EOD Trailing, Payout Trailing), Pay with Profits and One Step Light. Terms differ by programme and date of purchase.
2. **Withdrawal timing:** The [payout guide](https://help.pipfarm.com/en/articles/9367703-how-payouts-work) gives **30 days by default** for funded payouts, optionally 14, 7 or 0 days at extra cost; there is generally a **seven-day personal interval across accounts**, except the first on-demand reward. It separates manual review (2–3 business days) from subsequent transfer (another 2–3). The old generic claim that every account can request payouts weekly is not current across all programmes.
3. **Payout caps:** The [current policy](https://help.pipfarm.com/en/articles/1900230-payout-caps) caps the **first payout at 3%**, raising the cap by one percentage point with each payout **without a maximum**, for accounts purchased on or after **1 November 2026**. Older accounts retain their purchase-date caps. The [August change notice](https://updates.pipfarm.com/en/changelog/challenge-builder-updates) describes a former 6% maximum; do not mix cohorts.
4. **Consistency:** The [Consistency Mode rules](https://help.pipfarm.com/help/articles/5684942-consistency-mode-introduction) require best day / cumulative rewards **≤40%** (35% on some pre-27 August two-stage accounts); the [Payout Trailing Instant variant](https://updates.pipfarm.com/help/articles/3262423-payout-trailing-instant-account) requires **≤20%**, five winning days and a **+6%** reward-eligibility threshold. The [Pay with Profits guide](https://help.pipfarm.com/articles/8246911-pay-with-profits-payouts) specifies different ratios and Classic minimum funded days (four after one-stage, three after two-stage), resetting per payout.
5. **Risk strikes:** The [Pip Protector policy](https://help.pipfarm.com/en/articles/5854241-pip-protector) applies to most funded formats: first hit halves the permitted risk window, second halves it again **and permanently halves the profit share**, third terminates the account. **Pay with Profits and One Step Light have no strike protection:** reaching Max Risk breaches the account directly. Do not generalise these exceptions away.
6. **Profit from gaps:** The [gap policy](https://updates.pipfarm.com/en/help/articles/0106326-gap-trading) reduces the gain on a profitable price gap **≥0.2%** by the greater of **half the whole trade's profit or the gap profit**, whether the gap was intentional or not. Affected payout eligibility may be postponed. The source explicitly says this is a soft breach **without account disqualification**.
7. **Instant account expiry and payout-dependent drawdown:** The [Payout Trailing Instant documentation](https://updates.pipfarm.com/help/articles/3262423-payout-trailing-instant-account) describes a 90-day default payout deadline (180/360 paid extension; 360-day total life ceiling). Withdrawals permanently consume the 4% (5% at Rank 2+) max-loss buffer, until the floor locks at starting balance.
8. **Copy trading:** The [guidelines](https://updates.pipfarm.com/help/articles/2914190-copy-trading-guidelines) allow a trader to mirror **their own** accounts even across firms, but say copying **another person's** signals/trades leads to immediate termination.
9. **Discretionary reviews:** The [toxic-behaviour policy](https://help.pipfarm.com/en/help/articles/9077334-toxic-trading-behaviour) describes open-ended high-risk patterns and possible warnings, probation and payout-stage reviews. It does **not** prove that all flagged accounts lose previously earned rewards. The [news policy](https://help.pipfarm.com/en/articles/9200020-news-trading-policy) explicitly permits news trading but says repeated all-in news positions may prompt these reviews.
10. **Pay with Profits:** The [first-payout terms](https://help.pipfarm.com/articles/8246911-pay-with-profits-payouts) deduct a one-time fee of **0.5%–2.5% of the initial balance** depending on Risk Tier, **before profit share**; the first payout must at least cover the greater of that fee or 1%.
