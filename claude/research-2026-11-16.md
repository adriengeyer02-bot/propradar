- [Terms](https://x-funded.com/terms-and-conditions/), labelled 21 April 2026: OTHERSIDE LLC, UAE, is the counterparty. Funded accounts are simulated. Standard funded accounts impose 45% consistency per trade idea, 1% risk per trade idea, mandatory stop-losses, news restrictions and no weekend holding. Withdrawal thresholds say more than five trading days and more than fourteen active days; tables abbreviate these as five and fourteen. The dispute provisions mention arbitration and UAE courts. No finding of illegality is made.
- [Affiliate-referred policy](https://help.x-funded.com/payout-policy-dashboard/payout-split-policy/), 3 August 2026: after the first approved payout, the base share is capped at 50% across current and future accounts, subject to possible further adjustments. The 100% add-on does not override that cap after the first payout unless expressly stated; retroactive application is reserved. PropRadar has no X Funded affiliation.
- [Instant Funding](https://x-funded.com/instant-funding/): $2K–$200K, displayed €249–€2,899; 6% trailing overall limit, 4% daily limit, 20% consistency, mandatory stop-losses. Offer says one anniversary month without a dated expiry. Availability must be rechecked.
- [Payment FAQ](https://help.x-funded.com/payout-policy-dashboard/when-will-i-receive-my-payout/): review may take seven days, approved requests usually process in three to five working days plus one to two for receipt. Remaining balance resets after an approved payout; unwithdrawn amounts are described as unrecoverable.
- [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/x-funded.com): 583 reviews; TrustScore unavailable for a guideline breach; review-removal notice. Neither a historic score nor a removal count is inferred. This is not payout-failure evidence.
