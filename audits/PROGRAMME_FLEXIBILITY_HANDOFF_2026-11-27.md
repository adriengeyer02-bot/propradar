# Programme flexibility and coverage (2026-11-27)
Owner direction: company score /100 and programme flexibility /5 are separate. Do not feed the latter into Scale C. Do not publish historical score charts until the firm scale is stable.

## Evidence contract
- A programme means a distinct currently offered product, not an aggregated firm. Separate variants when restrictions differ (e.g. Standard/Swing, new/legacy cohorts). Record account size, evaluation/funded phase, currency, contract version, date, and exact official URLs; do not infer missing terms.
- Record pricing and all sizes, upfront/completion fees, subscription, refunds, resets, target(s), daily/max loss, basis and lock/reset of drawdown, payout eligibility/timing/caps/splits/buffer, consistency formula and denominator, news/weekend/EA/copy rules, KYC and restrictions; compare official product catalog against the actual profile, including new/discontinued offers.
- The five rating axes: drawdown, payouts, consistency, freedom of trading, extra funded conditions. Each 0 / 0.5 / 1 point. No missing axis defaults to 1 or 0. Programme ratings require all 5 evidence-backed axes, a clearly scoped variant and a reviewed rubric. A partial reading is not a rating.
- Proposed rubric (review before publication): drawdown static 1, EOD 0.5, intraday 0; consistency no rule 1, threshold >=30% with delayed rather than lost withdrawal 0.5, lower or forfeiture 0. For payout: unrestricted access with ordinary timing <=14 days 1, moderate delay/cap 0.5, severe caps/forfeiture 0. For trading: broad ordinary permissions 1, material restrictions 0.5, severe restrictions 0. For funded conditions: ordinary objectives/KYC only 1, material extra eligibility conditions 0.5, permanent payout loss or hard termination 0. Thresholds for payout/trading/funded need owner approval and calibration; no complete star rating published yet.
- Do not use global score, affiliate relationship, or missing product data as rating evidence. Do not compare EUR fees with USD loss budgets without explicit conversion.
- First pilot evidence: FTMO current CFD 1-Step, 2-Step and Instant comparison and objectives. Two verified axes each; 2-Step has variant ambiguity, legacy 1-Step has different consistency denominator, Instant uses trailing on balance and no reset. All three remain unrated.
- New simulations are independent educational scenarios. Prefill only exact verified parameters; leave unknowns blank. Price tables preserve source currency and exact product ID. User must confirm omitted fees and convert loss budget to same currency manually. No eligibility guarantee.
