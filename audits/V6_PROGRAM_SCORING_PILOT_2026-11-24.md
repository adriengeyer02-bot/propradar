# V6 pilot — 24 November 2026 (research only)

Owner direction: objective evidence and per-program risk scores. **No public scores or production routes changed.**
Data: [20 firms](v6-program-scoring-pilot-20-2026-11-24.json). Engine: `app/lib/scoringV6Preview.ts`.
 
## Formula
85 + age (0–10) + verified payout bonus (0–5) – corroborated incidents (0–35) = editorial score.
Program score = editorial – documented applicable restriction penalty (0–40) – confirmed Trustpilot withheld penalty (8 once), bounded 0–100. The strongest instance of the same risk family is applied once. No final score ceiling 64/69/79.
 
For the controlled pilot, payouts and incidents are fixed at zero, meaning not reassessed, not a declaration of absence. Founding year is a conservative proxy, not verified continuity of the contracting entity. Trustpilot notices checked 24 November for Audacity, For Traders, Funding Traders, Finotive and Crypto Fund Trader. Other domains not freshly checked in this pilot. All values are research-only and ineligible for public ranking.
 
## Fixed scoring anchors
0 ordinary objective rule; 1 limited extra restriction; 2 material transparent delay/eligibility condition; 3–4 strong transparent restriction, short appeal, or ability to change existing contracts; 5 gain/loss asymmetry or narrow subjective reward risk/chargeback forfeiture; 6 material subjective trading eligibility affecting rewards; 8 severe discretionary reward sanction, approved but unpaid reward suspension/forfeiture, or distinct personal liability; 10 sweeping discretionary reward forfeiture without cause; 12 arbitrary cancellation of already approved rewards, only if explicitly supported. Never count the same consequence twice. A disclosed 50% consistency condition which merely delays payout is not a forfeiture.
 
## Publication requirements
- Firm identity, current activity, versions and applicable scope, objective adverse incidents, exact-domain Trustpilot. Unknown payout evidence is never a publication blocker by itself.
- Per-program catalog of current family and variants, exact phase and cohort, terms and FAQ, applicable reward agreement or equivalent authoritative documentation. Missing critical terms => mark **not rated**, no automatic ranking, no score penalty for the missing document.
- General score median over distinct current product families, not account sizes, coupons, redundant add-ons or isolated stages. Multiple variants within a family aggregated before firm median. Critical clause warnings visible regardless of median. For this pilot, sample median is descriptive only; XFA or LIVE ELITE alone is not a complete firm score.
- Explicitly preserve CK Capital editorial veto and existing production until approved full backtest. No sponsor input.
