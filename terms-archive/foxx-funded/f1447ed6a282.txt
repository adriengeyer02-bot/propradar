Trading Rules:
Gambling Policy:

At Foxx Funded, we maintain a strict policy against "all or nothing" trading strategies. These approaches, which rely on taking excessively high-risk positions with the hope of a large payoff, are not acceptable. We focus on partnering with traders who demonstrate the ability to effectively manage risk, generate consistent profits, and maintain a disciplined approach to trading. Trades that resemble gambling or rely on an all-or-nothing mentality are not tolerated, as they do not reflect a trader’s ability to assess and manage risk, make sound decisions, and achieve consistent success in the financial markets.




Additionally, order layering—the practice of splitting positions into smaller trades to avoid slippage and artificially distort the intended replication of real market conditions—is deemed abusive. This practice attempts to bypass risk management protocols and is considered a violation of our trading policies. Specifically, placing more than 4 layers of orders based on the same trade idea, resulting in a total risk exposure exceeding 2%, will be classified as a prohibited activity.




Identifying Gambling Behavior in Trading:




Loss Recovery Tactics & Opposite Direction Strategies - Engaging in loss recovery strategies—such as placing large, impulsive trades in an effort to quickly recover from prior losses—reflects a high-risk, emotionally driven approach that undermines consistent, strategic trading. This includes tactics like Martingale systems, where position size is increased after a loss, or opposite direction recovery, where a trader immediately enters a trade in the reverse direction following a losing one, hoping for a quick bounce-back.These methods bypass the core principles we assess: risk management, discipline, and strategic decision-making. They compromise the integrity of your evaluation by distorting trading data and reveal a mindset of “recover or lose it all,” which is not aligned with sustainable performance.




One-Sided Bets - Placing trades in one direction without proper analysis or risk management. This high-risk approach relies on luck over strategy and undermines consistent, disciplined trading.




System Exploitation - Any attempt to manipulate the platform, deceive the firm, or engage in actions that create unnecessary risk to the system is strictly prohibited.




Single-Trade Challenge Passes - Relying on one large trade or idea to meet the profit target instead of showing consistent, controlled trading throughout the challenge.

Undisciplined Trading - Trading without a clear system, proper risk management, or unrealistic profit expectations. Frequent strategy changes, impulsive decisions, and chasing quick profits reflect a lack of long-term planning and growth.





In cases where trades are deemed to violate these standards or fail to align with realistic market conditions, we reserve the right to take the following actions: Restarting from Phase 1 , a hard breach of your account or denial of payout.
