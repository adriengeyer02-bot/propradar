Nova Challenge

Rules, profit target, drawdown, payouts

Updated over 2 weeks ago

🚀 Nova is a simplified evaluation model that allows traders to start their journey for only $5, making it one of the most accessible challenges available.
​

⚡ Overview


The Nova Challenge requires traders to reach a 5% profit target while staying within an 8% maximum overall loss, with a Trailing Drawdown
​

There are no minimum trading days, allowing traders to complete the challenge at their own pace. Once the target is achieved without violating the rules, the trader can activate their funded account by paying the Funded fee for the selected account size.

💡 Why Nova?

With a $5 starting fee, traders can prove their strategy and trading discipline before committing.
​

This structure allows traders to:

Test their skills with a minimal fee

Access funded trading opportunities more easily

Only pay the full Funded once they successfully pass the evaluation
​
​


📅 Payouts

Supported payout methods include:

Cryptocurrency

Bank wire.

Payouts are available on demand once the trader receives their funded account.

Withdrawal requests can be submitted directly from the trading dashboard.

Important note: once a payout is requested, the drawdown is locked in at the initial account balance. When you request a payout, your max drawdown is fixed at the initial balance. If you request a full payout, it means that you will instantly breach the total drawdown. You will need to keep a buffer in the account to continue trading

For example, in a $10,000 account, the drawdown limit will be fixed at $10,000 after a payout. To continue trading, you must leave a buffer in the account.

Minimum payout amount: $100.

Payouts are processed within 2–5 business days.

The 7 profitable days do not need to be consecutive and reset after each payout.

Eligibility begins on the 8th day after meeting the daily profit criteria.

Example: For a $25,000 account, 0.5% equals $125 per day.

The payout button becomes green/enabled once eligibility criteria are met.


✅ Allowed Trading Strategies

Manual trading (scalping, swing, intraday)

EAs are permitted

❌ No high-impact news trading ±2 minutes

 

🔄 Account Reset

Breached your Nova Funded account? No problem. You can reset it and get back to trading without starting a new challenge from scratch.
​

After a reset, previous profits do not transfer, and you must restart the 7 profitable days requirement to request a payout.

How It Works:

If a Nova Funded account breaches, you can reset it directly from your dashboard:

Log into your dashboard

Go to the Accounts tab

Find your breached Nova Funded account

Click Reset Account

Complete payment on the checkout page (a 10% discount is automatically applied, no code needed)

A new funded account of the same size is issued instantly, and you're ready to trade again

💵 Reset Fees

Resetting comes with an exclusive 10% discount off the original Funded fee, applied automatically every time.

 

📌 Good to Know

You can reset the same account as many times as you like

The reset fee stays the same every time, it does not stack additional discounts on repeat resets
