Compare Challenges: Which one is right for me?
October 13, 2026

Atmos offers multiple funded trading programs to suit different trader styles. Whether you prefer fast access, structured phases, or instant funding, here’s a side-by-side comparison to help you choose.

⚡ Key Highlights

2-Step Standard → Classic model, 10% → 5% targets, static drawdown.

 

Instant Funding → Skip evaluation, start trading day one, payouts based on a 14-day cycle, drawdown locks at initial account size
​

Nova → 1-Step challenge, start for $5, with a 5% profit target, 4% daily drawdown, payouts on demand. Skill-first entry for new traders.

🧾 Quick Recommendations

 

Choose 2-Step Standard if you prefer the classic prop firm model with static drawdown.

 

Choose Instant Funding if you want to skip evaluations and start trading funded capital from day one.
​

Choose Nova if you want to start for just $5, pass a simple 1-Step challenge, and unlock a funded account with a 5% profit target and 4% daily drawdown.

💡 Example Scenarios

Disciplined swing trader? → 2-Step Standard (structured evaluation).

Need fast funding? → Instant Funding.

Want compounding potential? → Instant Funding.

Looking to start small and prove your skills? → Nova ($5 entry, 5% profit target, 4% daily drawdown)
