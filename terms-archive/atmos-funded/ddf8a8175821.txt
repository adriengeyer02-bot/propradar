How Payouts Work
Updated over 2 weeks ago

Payouts allow you to withdraw profits from your funded account. Atmos offers weekly, bi-weekly, or on-demand payouts depending on your plan.

⚡ Overview

 

2-Step Standard→ Payouts every 14 days

Instant Funding → 14-day payout cycle 

NOVA → On Demand (only after completing 7 profitable trading days in the funded stage; profits from the challenge phase are not eligible)
