What rules must I follow during a challenge?

The trader must adhere to the established limits for daily and maximum drawdown, as well as the target profit figures for each stage. At Stages 1 and 2, opening opposite positions on the same instrument in different challenges (BUY and SELL) is prohibited, as this is considered a conflict of interest and may lead to account cancellation. The use of arbitrage, manipulation, and other forms of unfair trading is prohibited. On the Funded Account, any trading strategies are allowed, including hedging.

What happens if I exceed the loss limit?

If any of the loss limits is exceeded, the challenge will be permanently locked.

How is the Daily Loss limit calculated?

The daily loss limit is the maximum allowable drawdown within a single trading day.

Daily Loss = Current Equity − Balance at the beginning of the day

Balance — your account balance, taking into account all closed positions and deducted fees.
Equity — the current value of the account, taking into account all open and closed positions.

Important: The daily loss limit resets at 00:13 UTC+4. You can review the calculations in more detail here

Can I participate in multiple challenges simultaneously?

While you are taking a challenge, the number of simultaneous challenges is not limited at stages 1 and 2. You can also hold several funded accounts, but no more than one of each size per market. For example, you can trade a $100,000 funded account on crypto and a $100,000 funded account on US Stocks at the same time, but not two $100,000 accounts on crypto.

I've completed the challenge. What's next?

After successfully completing the challenge, you will gain access to a funded account, where you can trade with the company's capital and receive 80% of the profit earned. The transition to a funded account happens automatically, in about 10 minutes after the stage conditions are met: the profit target is reached and the minimum number of trading days is completed.

What is Settlement Time and when does it occur?

Settlement Time is the daily calculation of your account results. It occurs at the end of the trading day at 00:13 UTC+4.

Which crypto assets can I trade?

What is the maximum leverage I can trade with?

Leverage of up to 1:5 is available for trading.

Is using a Stop Loss mandatory?

No, placing a Stop Loss is not mandatory. However, it is crucial not to exceed the established loss limits. If any limit is reached, positions will be forcibly closed, and the challenge will be terminated. The rules for placing Stop Loss and Take Profit can be found here

Can I trade using different margin modes simultaneously?

No. You can only open positions in one mode at a time — either Cross or Isolated Margin. To switch modes, you must first close all positions and limit orders in the current trading mode.

Can I hedge positions?

Yes, you can open positions in opposite directions within a single challenge (for example, long and short on the same asset). Opening opposite positions at the same time across several funded accounts in order to neutralise exposure or secure a guaranteed outcome is prohibited.

Can I hold open positions for several days?

Yes, this is allowed. However, carefully monitor the loss limits — open positions are also considered in their calculation.

Do open positions affect loss limits?

Yes, open positions (unrealized profit/loss) directly affect the Equity calculation and, consequently, the loss limits.

Can I trade during weekends or important news events?

Yes, you can trade at any time. We do not restrict trading during news releases or on weekends.

Are there any withdrawal restrictions?

Yes, the following conditions apply:

You can withdraw 80% of the profit earned every 14 days.
The maximum withdrawal amount per account is $10,000 every 14 days.
The minimum payout amount is $1.

You can learn more about the withdrawal process here

What is a trading day?

A trading day is a day on which at least one position was opened. For example, if you opened a position one day and closed it another, it will count as one trading day.

Is spot trading available?

No, only futures trading is available on our platform.
