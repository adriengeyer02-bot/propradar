Are there any prohibited trading rules?

Yes. Hedging is not permitted, including same-instrument hedging across accounts, correlated-instrument hedging, and coordinating offsetting positions with other traders. An index future cannot be held or traded while it is within 2% of its CME price limit. Reasonable scaling and averaging are allowed, but repeatedly adding size to losing positions as a routine part of most trades is considered excessive martingale-style trading and may result in an account review. Scalping trades held for 5–30 seconds are allowed. HFT-style trading is not permitted; if more than 10% of your trades are opened and fully closed within two seconds, your account may be flagged and paused for review.
