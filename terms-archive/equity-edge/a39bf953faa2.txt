TRADING RULES AND GUIDELINES
1 - Trading Conduct and Regulations

We provide 6 Equity Edge Evaluation challenges:

1-Step Legacy Evaluation

1-Step Swift Evaluation

1-Step Flagship Evaluation

2-Step Legacy Evaluation

2-Step Swift Evaluation

2-Step Flagship Evaluation

Successful completion of either is required to qualify as a funded trader. Possessing a dynamic trading system and a robust risk management profile is essential for passing the evaluation challenges. Once you achieve your profit target, our Risk Team will conduct a manual review of your entire evaluation period, concluding within 3 working days.

We also provide an Instant account where you can start generating profits towards your payout straight away without the need to pass a challenge first.

In the event of any violation of the rules outlined in this section, immediate closure of all positions on the broker's platform will occur, the Customer’s Account will be terminated, and the right to payout will be forfeited.

1-Step Legacy Evaluation

Within the 1-Step Legacy evaluation challenge, the profit target is set at 10% of the starting demo account simulated balance.

Daily loss limit: 4%

The daily simulated drawdown is set at 4%.

Maximum Loss Limit: 6% (Trailing)

The max simulated drawdown is still set at 6% trailing from your highest simulated balance or equity achieved at all times.

Example

Consider starting with a $100,000 demo account, a daily simulated drawdown level of $96,000, and a max trailing simulated drawdown of $94,000. If you realize a simulated profit of $5,000, elevating your demo account balance to $105,000, the new daily simulated drawdown level for the next day becomes $100,800 (computed as $105,000 - 4%). The max simulated trailing drawdown will be $98,700 (computed as $105,000 - 6%) as it trails from the highest recorded simulated balance of $105,000. Similarly, if you attain a simulated profit of $9,000 at any point in time, the max simulated trailing drawdown will be $102,460 (computed as $109,000 - 6%). Notably, simulated drawdown encompasses profits, losses from closed positions, swap fees, and commissions associated with simulated trading.

Resetting Simulated Daily Drawdown and Max Simulated Trailing Drawdown:

The daily simulated drawdown resets every day at 22:00 London time. The max simulated trailing drawdown is always based on the highest recorded simulated balance or equity.

2-Step Legacy Evaluation
Phase 1

In the initial phase, we evaluate your trading skills and encourage you to establish your identity as a trader. Your goal in Phase 1 is to reach a profit target of 10% without violating any other rules. Upon successful completion, you can expect to receive your Phase 2 account within 2 hours.

Phase 2

Phase 2 is designed to validate the results and achievements of Phase 1. In this stage, your objective is to attain a profit target of 5% without violating any other rules.

Daily Loss Limit: 5%

Traders have a safety net called the Maximum Daily Loss, capping the amount they can lose each day. This rule uses the higher value between equity and balance and sets the limit at 5% of the starting equity or balance (whichever is the highest). Put simply, the trader's total losses for the day, including both current and closed positions, shouldn't go beyond this 5% limit. The reset happens every day at 22:00 UK time.

Maximum Loss Limit: 10%

The Maximum Loss Limit ensures that the equity or balance stays above a specific level. This regulation is fixed at 10% of the initial account size. For instance, if a trader has a $100,000 account with a 10% Maximum Loss Limit, the equity or balance must not dip below $90,000 under any circumstances.

Instant Account
Daily Loss Limit: 3%

The maximum daily loss limit is calculated as 3% of the higher of the starting balance or equity for each trading day. This is based on the account balance or equity at rollover which occurs at 10pm UK time.

Maximum Loss Limit: 5%

The maximum total loss trails at 5% from your highest balance or equity achieved on your account. After requesting a payout, your maximum total loss resets back to your account initial balance, and will start trailing again once your balance or equity reaches 5% profit from your initial balance.

2 - Permitted Trade Actions During News and Weekends
Legacy / Swift Challenges
Evaluation Challenges

Traders are permitted to hold trades over the weekend. Holding trades through high-impact news is allowed however we have a restricted 4 minute window in which no trades can be opened or closed.

Funded Trader (1-Step and 2-Step Challenges)

Traders are permitted to hold trades over the weekend. Holding trades through high-impact news is allowed however we have a restricted 4 minute window in which no trades can be opened or closed.

If you open or close a trade during the restricted time window of 2 minutes before/or until 2 minutes after, profit made on these trades will be deducted upon the payout request on the funded account.




Flagship Challenges
Evaluation Challenges

Traders are permitted to hold trades over the weekend and there are no restrictions on high-impact news during the Evaluation phase.

Funded Trader (1-Step and 2-Step Flagship Challenges)

Traders are permitted to hold trades over the weekend. Holding trades through high-impact news is allowed however we have a restricted 10 minute window in which no trades can be opened or closed.

If you open or close a trade during the restricted time window of 5 minutes before/or until 5 minutes after, profit made on these trades will be deducted upon the payout request on the funded account.




Instant Account

Traders are not permitted to hold trades over the weekend. Holding trades through high-impact news is allowed however we have a restricted 16 minute window in which no trades can be opened or closed.

If you open or close a trade during the restricted time window of 8 minutes before/or until 8 minutes after, profit made on these trades will be deducted upon the payout request on the Instant account.

Note

USD high impact news restricts trading on all trading pairs

Non-USD high impact news only restricts trading for trading pairs linked to that specific currency

Non-USD high impact news restricts trading for both forex pairs and indices

Examples

CHF high impact news or speeches will only apply to CHF trading pairs. Non-CHF trading pairs can still be traded during this time.

EUR high impact news or speeches will only apply to EUR trading pairs. Non-EUR trading pairs can still be traded during this time.

USD high impact news or speeches will apply to all trading pairs. No trades should be executed as per the news rule restrictions for your account.

EUR high impact news also applies to GER30 / FRA40 / ITA40

GBP high impact news also applies to UK100

Speeches

During speeches, the same time restrictions apply as for high-impact news events, except that the restricted time window starts before the speech, and ends after the speech concludes. 
e.g. For an Instant Account, you are not allowed to open or close a trade 8 minutes before the speech begins and 8 minutes after the speech ends.

TAKE NOTE: If the profits generated from news trades exceeds 25% of the trader's profit share, the payout will be rejected, your profit share will be forfeited and the account will be reset back to its initial balance.
​
Repeat offences may also lead to the termination of your account.




3 - Trading Strategy

Traders have the freedom to employ their preferred trading methods. Holding positions during news events, keeping trades over the weekend (only for 1-Step and 2-Step accounts), and utilizing lot sizes within the leverage's capacity are all allowed. However, engaging in trading practices intended to exploit the platform's inefficiencies (such as gap trading, high-frequency trading, server spamming, latency arbitrage, toxic trading flow, one sided betting, hedging, group hedging, long-short arbitrage, reverse arbitrage, tick scalping, martingale, server execution, opposite account trading) is strictly prohibited. Copy trading or account management by third-party vendors, along with any such activities involving Equity Edge, will result in account termination. Copy trading between two Equity Edge Challenge accounts is allowed but no more than two accounts. The use of Expert Advisors (EAs) and trade copiers is not permitted and will lead to evaluation denial, payout denial, and account closure. Manual trading does not exempt accounts from copy trading; any overlapping trades on the same asset will be considered copy trading, even if they are not executed at the same time.

Note that no copy trading is allowed on Instant accounts.

8 - Merging Accounts




Legacy / Swift / Flagship Accounts

You may hold multiple accounts under a single email address. However, you cannot use multiple emails to purchase additional accounts.

The maximum capital allocation at Equity Edge for Funded Accounts is $200,000, subject to the following limits:

Flagship accounts: up to $200,000.

Combined Legacy and Swift accounts: up to $100,000.

Combined Flagship, Legacy, and Swift accounts: up to $200,000.

This limit includes the value of passed Challenge Accounts where contracts have not yet been issued. However, there is no cap on the value of active Challenge Accounts.

Examples

If you have $100,000 in Legacy accounts, you can also hold $100,000 in Flagship accounts, totalling $200,000.

If you have $200,000 in Flagship accounts, you have reached the maximum allocation and cannot hold additional accounts.

If you have $50,000 in Flagship accounts, you can hold up to $100,000 in Legacy or Swift accounts and an additional $50,000 in Flagship accounts, totalling $200,000.

Account merging is not allowed.




Instant Accounts

There is no limit to the number of Instant accounts that can be purchased at any one time, however you may not trade more than $300,000 at any one time. This can be across one or multiple accounts. Violating this rule will result in your accounts being terminated and any pending payouts forfeited.

Example

You buy 4 x $100k accounts which comes to a total of $400k. You may trade 3 of these accounts which is within the max $300k allocation allowance. If you breach one of those 3 accounts you were trading, then only may you begin trading another $100k account which will bring you back up to the $300k allocation limit.
