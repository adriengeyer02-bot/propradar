 [Instant Funding] What happens if I violate the rules?

 [Instant Funding] What happens if I violate the rules?

 The Rules are simple:

Daily Drawdown not to be exceeded
MAX trailing Drawdown not to be exceeded. Locks at initial balance at withdrawal or if 8% profit is achieved.
Do not use EAs/Bots
No copy trading
Do not trade/make use any of the prohibited strategies
News Trading not allowed. Do not trade 5 minutes before, during or after High Impact news
Max allocation on Instant Funding: Do not use more than 1 Instant Funding account at a time
Proper Risk Management
Don’t become inactive for more than 60 days. Place at least 1 trade every now and then, before 60 continuous days.

For more information on each of the above, please, click on the link for each section.
If any of the above is observed or happens, the account breaches and will not be able to use it anymore.
As the add-on “Performance Protect” isn’t supported for the instant funding, if you have profits but the account breached already, the profit is forfeited and aren’t eligible to request payout.
You can always purchase a new one.
