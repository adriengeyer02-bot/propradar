How do I withdraw from my Axi Select account?
Find out how to withdraw funds from your Axi Select account, what happens to your Allocation Account, and what to be aware of before proceeding.
Updated 2 months ago

The funds in your Axi Select trading account belong to you and can be withdrawn at any time. However, withdrawals directly affect your Allocation Account and may impact your profits and program participation. 

What you need to know: 

Withdrawals can only be made from your Axi Select trading account. The Allocation Account is Axi-funded and cannot be withdrawn from under any circumstances. 

Your Axi Select trading account and your Allocation Account are two separate accounts. Your Axi Select trading account holds your own funds. Your Allocation Account is funded by Axi and mirrors your trades using a multiplier — you cannot withdraw from it directly. 

When you withdraw from your Axi Select trading account, the Allocation Account is immediately reduced by the withdrawal amount multiplied by your current multiplier. For example, withdrawing $1,000 with a x10 multiplier reduces your Allocation Account by $10,000 instantly. 

Withdrawals are initiated directly from the Client Portal, where you select the available payment method based on your region and RTS requirements. 

Open positions in your Allocation Account will not be automatically closed after a withdrawal. It is your responsibility to monitor and manage open trades after withdrawing. 

When you initiate a withdrawal, a pop-up message will appear outlining the specific impact on your payouts, program status, and Quarantine risk. You must acknowledge this message before the withdrawal can proceed. 

If you withdraw all funds and fall below the minimum fund requirement for your current stage, you will be dropped from that stage and will need to restart from the Seed stage. 

Any withdrawal amount in Seed stage, will lead the allocation account to reset and profit lost, as we do not have performance fee payouts in the Seed stage. 

Withdrawing during certain periods will affect your profit share for that month. See: How does withdrawing from my Axi Select account affect my profits? 

Before making a withdrawal, ensure you have read the Withdrawal Cases and Program Rules documents. 

Important: Your Axi Select trading account and Allocation Account are not the same. You can only withdraw from your Axi Select trading account. Any withdrawal immediately impacts your Allocation Account balance and multiplier.
