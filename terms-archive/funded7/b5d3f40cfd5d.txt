Payouts at Funded7: Fast, Flexible, and Secure

At Funded7, we ensure fast and seamless payouts, with multiple withdrawal options to suit your needs.

✔️ Minimum Payout Requirement

The minimum amount a trader can receive after the profit split is $100.
Example:
50/50 split → You need to withdraw at least $200 (50% = $100).
80/20 split → You need to withdraw at least $125 (80% = $100).
If your share is less than $100, the payout will not be processed. You can request a withdrawal once you reach the minimum.

✔️ Payout Processing Rules

Withdrawals automatically close all open trades.
Your account enters read-only mode until the payout is processed.

✔️ Payout Frequency

Traders: Eligible for payouts every 7 days from the last withdrawal or account creation date.
Affiliates: Can request payouts every 7 days from their last withdrawal.

✔️ Payout Methods: Choose What Works Best for You

We offer two secure withdrawal options:

1️⃣ Revolut Instant

Need your payout instantly? Revolut Instant allows you to receive funds directly to your Revolut account using your Revolut tag.

🔹 How to withdraw via Revolut Instant:
1. Submit your payout request from your dashboard and select Revolut Instant.
2. Enter the payout amount and your Revolut @username (tag).
3. Ensure you have submitted your KYC documents (only required for your first payout).
4. Once approved, your funds will arrive in your Revolut account within minutes.

2️⃣ Crypto via ETH/USDC

Prefer to receive your payout in crypto? ETH/USDC allows for fast, low-fee transfers.

🔹 How to withdraw via ETH/USDC:
1. Submit your withdrawal request from your dashboard.
2. Enter the amount and your crypto wallet address.
3. Ensure you have submitted your KYC documents (only required for your first payout).
4. Receive your payout directly to your wallet in ETH/USDC.

3️⃣ Bank Transfer

Prefer a direct bank transfer? Choosing bank Transfer, you can receive payouts straight to your bank account.

🔹 How to withdraw via Bank Transfer:
1. Submit your payout request from your dashboard and select Bank Transfer.
2. Enter the payout amount and your bank details.
3. Ensure you have submitted your KYC documents (only required for your first payout).
4. Once processed, the payout will be sent to your bank account. Timing may vary depending on your bank’s processing.

📌 Important Notes:

All open trades will automatically close when a payout is requested.
Your account will remain in read-only mode until the payout is processed.
The maximum withdrawal amount per month is $10,000 per client.
