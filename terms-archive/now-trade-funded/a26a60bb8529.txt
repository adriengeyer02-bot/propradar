How often can I withdraw?

Once you transition to a live funded trader, you must engage in trading with us for a minimum of 30 days to qualify for your initial payout. After that, you can request bi-weekly payouts.

Time limited

For the Standard, Progressive and Small Accounts challenges the assessment time is unlimited. The Quickpass challenge has 30 days.

Are there any profit targets on a live account?

There are no profit targets required to be reached on the live funded account. However, the account must end in a positive balance at the end of the 30-day trading period to make a withdrawal.

Use EAs

The following strategies are not allowed:

High-frequency trading (manually or using a bot)
Hedge arbitrage trading
Tick scalping
Grid trading
One-sided betting
Latency arbitrage trading
Reverse arbitrage trading
Copying trades from other traders

If any of these strategies are detected in your live account, it will be considered a violation of the rules and NowTrade will terminate your account.

Withdraw evaluation profits

Evaluation accounts use simulated funds; we only pay out on performance once you are funded. We are purely educational.

Is there an age requirement to get funded?

The minimum age to participate in all of our programs is 18 years of age or older.

How many accounts can I trade at any given time?

NowTrade was founded by experienced traders and liquidity risk managers. We see $200,000 as an optimal maximum amount of capital before excess levels of slippage and poor trade conditions not suited to retail traders will set in.

Do you offer instant funding?

No, NowTrade is looking for profitable and consistent traders to partner with. To be awarded capital, traders must prove they can profit consistently whilst working within the rules.

Is hedging allowed?

No hedging between two accounts: you cannot open trades that are exactly opposite one another on different accounts.

How can I withdraw my live profits?

The withdrawal option typically appears on your account the day after your scheduled withdrawal date.

For example, if your withdrawal date is the 15th of the month, the option to withdraw will appear on the 16th. Kindly remember that in order to initiate a withdrawal, it is essential to close all your trades, as only then will the withdrawal button become active.

Where do I find my login details?

You can expect to receive your account login information within 5 minutes of your payment. Upon the successful creation of your account, you will be notified via email. If you do not locate our email in your inbox, kindly check your spam/junk folder.

Account inactivity?

At NowTrade our inactivity period is 30 days.

Is HFT allowed?

Currently we are not accepting HFT accounts. We are working on this and expect to have them in the future.

Does my account have to be in my name?

The Account Owner is the name of a person who is authorised to have the trading account, verifiable using specific personal information. As such, the account must be created in your own name.

How do your consistency rules work?

Consistency rules apply to Quick Pass accounts. Standard accounts only hold true to the “profit” consistency rule.

Do you allow trading over the weekend?

We do not allow holding positions over the weekend on any accounts, but we allow weekend trading on crypto pairs.

Can I merge my accounts with you?

No, please be aware that we currently do not permit the merging of accounts.

How long do withdrawals take?

After you request a withdrawal, your request will be approved within 1-3 business days.

What is your leverage?

At NowTrade our leverage is 1:50 for evaluation accounts and 1:30 for funded accounts. Please keep in mind that it can not be modified.

Do you have a minimum withdrawal amount?

Our minimum withdrawal amount is $100.
