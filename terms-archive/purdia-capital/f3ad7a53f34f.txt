Trading Policy

Identity limits, prohibited conduct, and enforcement for Purdia Capital accounts. Read this together with the Terms and Trading Rules.

Purpose

This Trading Policy sets out identity limits, prohibited trading conduct, and enforcement actions for Purdia Capital accounts, evaluations, funded accounts, and related services.

It is part of your agreement with Purdia Capital LLC, together with the Terms & Conditions. Program operating rules, such as profit targets, minimum trading days, and drawdown, are published on the Rules page.

Rule Violations, Cheating, and Exploits

Traders must follow all published trading rules, risk parameters, and this Policy. Cheating, exploiting, manipulating, or attempting to game the platform, accounts, payouts, or risk systems is prohibited.

Prohibited conduct includes, without limitation, exploiting platform, broker, or data-feed behavior; manipulating fills, latency, or execution; using prohibited strategies to extract payouts without genuine trading risk; misrepresenting identity or location; and any other activity Purdia Capital determines is abusive or unfair.

If Purdia Capital determines that a trader has violated trading rules, cheated, or exploited the system, Purdia Capital reserves the right to permanently ban that trader, cancel all unpaid and pending payouts, and close all of that trader's accounts.

When Purdia Capital exercises that right, the trader will receive a refund of fees paid for any account that is still active and has not failed. No refund is provided for accounts that have already failed, been breached, been lost, or been closed because of trading activity, missed targets, or risk-parameter violations.

Coordinated and Cross-Group Trading

Organized or coordinated trading between multiple people is prohibited. This includes group copy trading, signal sharing used to trade in concert, and any arrangement where traders work together to pass accounts, manage risk across identities, or extract payouts as a group.

Cross-trading between different traders, households, or identities is not allowed. Copying trades across a single trader's own accounts is permitted only within published product limits and remains subject to all other rules in this Policy.

Purdia Capital may treat matching trade patterns, timing, devices, IPs, or other signals as evidence of coordinated or cross-group trading.

One Trader Per Household, Address, and IP

Unless Purdia Capital grants an exception in writing, only one trader may operate accounts from a given household, residential address, or IP address.

Additional people in the same household, at the same address, or sharing the same IP may not each hold separate accounts. This identity limit is separate from any product cap on how many accounts a single trader may hold.

Purdia Capital also enforces a one-account-per-device policy. Using VPNs, proxies, shared connections, or other methods to circumvent household, address, IP, or device limits is prohibited.

Purdia Capital may determine, at its sole discretion, whether accounts are connected by household, address, IP, device, payment method, or other evidence, and may refuse, merge, suspend, or close accounts that violate this limit.

Inactivity

If a trader has no genuine trading activity for more than 30 days, the account will be marked inactive and the trader will receive a notice.

Genuine trading activity means placing real, active orders in the ordinary course of trading. Logging in, holding idle positions, or placing token trades—such as flipping micro contracts solely to keep an account open—does not count. Purdia Capital determines, at its sole discretion, whether activity is genuine.

After the notice, the trader has 30 days to request reactivation. If no reactivation request is received within that period, the account will be permanently disabled and cannot be used.

Enforcement

Purdia Capital may investigate suspected violations, request information, pause trading, withhold payouts during review, and take any of the actions described in this Policy.

Enforcement decisions are made at Purdia Capital's sole discretion. A refund for an active, non-failed account under the cheating and exploits section does not restore access, reverse a ban, or create a right to new accounts.

Changes, Acceptance, and Contact

Purdia Capital may modify this Policy at any time. Updated versions are effective upon posting unless otherwise specified. Continued use of the website or services means you accept the revised Policy.

By accessing and using the website or services, you acknowledge that you have read and agree to this Trading Policy.

Questions about this Policy may be sent to support@purdia.com.
