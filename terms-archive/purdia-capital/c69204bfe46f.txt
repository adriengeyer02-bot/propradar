Traders are responsible for understanding how order execution works and for managing risk appropriately during volatile market conditions.Read full article?Contract Expiration & RolloverAll futures contracts have expiration dates, and traders are responsible for trading the current active contract with the most volume and liquidity. As contracts approach expiration, trading volume and open interest gradually shift into the next contract on the calendar. This process is known as the contract rollover, or “roll.” Purdia Capital requires traders to always trade the current front-month contract.
