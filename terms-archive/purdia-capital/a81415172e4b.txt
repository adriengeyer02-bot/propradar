Hitting the DLL is a soft breach. It does not fail the account. Instead, trading is halted for the remainder of the current trading session, and the DLL resets at the market close. Some account types include a DLL by default, while others do not. Traders may also request a smaller DLL for additional risk control.Read full article?The Max DrawdownThe max drawdown determines the minimum balance your account is allowed to reach. If your account balance drops below that threshold at any point — even during an open trade — the account fails immediately. Different account types calculate and move that threshold differently, but the enforcement itself is always immediate.Read full article?Max Drawdown TypesDrawdown is one of the most important risk parameters in futures trading and plays a major role in how Purdia Capital accounts operate. Different account types may utilize different drawdown models, and understanding how each one behaves is critical before trading.Read full article?What & When Can I Trade?Purdia Capital traders are permitted to trade a wide range of futures products during normal market hours. Traders may hold positions throughout the trading session, but all positions and working orders are automatically closed prior to the daily market close.Read full article?Position Size LimitsAll accounts include position size limits that determine the maximum number of contracts a trader may hold at one time. These limits are enforced directly by the platform and cannot be exceeded. In Live Funded Accounts, position limits are determined in coordination with the trader’s assigned risk manager and may change over time based on account performance and risk profile.Read full article?News TradingPurdia Capital does not restrict traders from trading during scheduled economic news events in simulated accounts. However, news trading can involve extreme volatility, slippage, liquidity issues, and poor fills, and traders are fully responsible for managing those risks. Live Funded Accounts may also experience temporarily increased margin requirements from the broker during major news events.Read full article?Automated & Copy TradingSemi-automated trading and trade copiers are permitted at Purdia Capital, provided the trader remains actively involved in monitoring and managing the account. Fully automated “set-and-forget” trading systems that operate without active trader oversight are not permitted. Traders remain fully responsible for all activity generated by their software, strategies, and connected systems.Read full article?Live Funded Account Scaling PlanPurdia Capital allows Live Funded Accounts (LFAs) to scale over time based on account growth, trading history, and risk management performance. Scaling is designed to gradually increase buying power and position size limits for traders who demonstrate responsible account management and long-term consistency.Read full article?Permitted and Prohibited Trading PracticesPurdia Capital allows a wide range of trading styles, including scalping, micro scalping, discretionary trading, DCA strategies, and semi-automated trading. The primary prohibited practice is hedging between highly correlated positions or accounts. Traders are also expected to trade responsibly and may not attempt to exploit technical issues, platform behavior, or operational loopholes.Read full article?Trading Days & Profitable DaysTrading Days and Profitable Days are tracked based on calendar days using the US Eastern Time (ET) timezone. Purdia Capital counts these from midnight to midnight ET, even though futures trading sessions themselves operate on a different schedule. This can sometimes create confusion because a single futures trading session in Tradovate may span two separate Trading Days under Purdia Capital’s rules.Read full article?Contract Expiration & RolloverAll futures contracts have expiration dates, and traders are responsible for trading the current active contract with the most volume and liquidity. As contracts approach expiration, trading volume and open interest gradually shift into the next contract on the calendar. This process is known as the contract rollover, or “roll.” Purdia Capital requires traders to always trade the current front-month contract. Continuing to trade expired or low-volume contracts can result in poor fills, excessive slippage, and execution issues.Read full article?Account Inactivity and Closure PolicyAll Evaluation, Sim Funded, and Live Funded Accounts purchased on or after October 1, 2026, will be permanently closed if no trade is executed for 14 consecutive calendar days. Traders expecting an extended absence may request an extension in advance, but approval is discretionary and accounts closed for inactivity cannot be reopened.Read full article?Percentage-Based Consistency RuleSome Purdia account types use a Percentage-Based Consistency Rule to determine eligibility for account progression and payouts. The rule measures whether net profit is distributed across multiple trading days rather than concentrated in one unusually profitable day.Read full article?Protected BufferThe Protected Buffer is a portion of your account balance that must remain in the account while you continue progressing toward a Live Funded Account. It is not a fee, penalty, or amount that is deducted from your account.
