Profit Sharing Condition:

For the Customization packages – to qualify for profit sharing, you must meet the following requirements:

1. Profit Target: Achieve the specified profit target for your trading account.

2. Profit Consistency: Maintain consistent profitability over the evaluation period.

Before submitting the withdrawal request, you must continue trading until your best trading day percentage falls below the Profit Consistency Ratio, if it is higher than the Profit Consistency Ratio of your total profits made. Our Risk Management team will reject your request if you don’t follow these instructions.

For the 51010 packages, the profit sharing is up to 90%

If the performance level is less than 6%, the performance-based reward of the 510Zero package will be reduced to a fixed 10%, except for clients from Vietnam, Indonesia and Thailand, for whom this adjustment will apply if the performance level is below 8%.

For the Free packages the profit sharing is up to 90%.

Reactivate Profit Sharing
An action to reactivate the trading account after hitting the Risk Consistency Rule. The profit sharing for the next payout will be reduced to 20%, then gradually increased back to 50%–90% in subsequent payouts.

Reset Profit Sharing
An action to reactivate the trading account after hitting the Risk Consistency Rule, with the profit sharing maintained at 50%–90% for future payouts.
