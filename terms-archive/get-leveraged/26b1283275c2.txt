FAQ / Trading Rules
Can I trade news events?

Last updated: August 25, 2026

High-impact news trading is restricted on all Leveraged programs except Sprint.

Restricted Window Rules

You may not open, increase, fully close, or partially close a position within five minutes before or five minutes after a high-impact news event.

This applies to market orders and any pending order that executes during the restricted window, including stop, limit, take-profit, and stop-loss orders.

Positions opened before the restricted window may remain open through the event. However, they may not be fully or partially closed during the restricted window.

Breach Consequences

A breach may result in removal of affected profits, an account repeat, restriction or termination of the account, or other action under our Terms and Conditions. The outcome depends on the severity and frequency of the breach.

📅 Official News Source: The official source for high-impact events and their timing is the calendar in your Leveraged dashboard.
