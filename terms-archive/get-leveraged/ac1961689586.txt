FAQ / Leveraged Programs
What is the Leveraged Instant Program?

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Leveraged Instant gives you a simulated trading account straight away, with no evaluation. Your goal is to meet the program’s trading and reward requirements so you can request a reward.

Program Requirements
Rule / Metric	Specification
Evaluation Phase	None (Direct Funding)
Daily Loss Limit	3%
Maximum Loss (Equity Trailing)	6%
Floating Loss Limit	2%
Consistency Rule	20%
Minimum Profitable Days	5 days (≥ 0.5% profit per day)
Profit Buffer	3% of initial account balance
Reward Requests	Every 14 days
Reward Cap	First two rewards capped at 10% of account size each
Reward Share	Up to 95% with add-on
What is the floating loss?

Floating loss is the loss on trades that are still open. On Leveraged Instant, your combined open loss across all positions is limited to 2% of your current account balance. If it reaches -2% at any moment, the account is closed permanently.

This rule is separate from the 3% daily loss limit and the 6% maximum loss. All three apply at all times.

EXAMPLE

With a $100,000 balance, your open trades together cannot be down more than $2,000 at any given moment.

What is the profit buffer?

The profit buffer is the profit you must leave in your account when you request a reward. On Leveraged Instant, it is 3% of your initial account balance.

EXAMPLE

On a $100,000 account with a balance of $108,000, you have $8,000 of profit. You leave $3,000 in the account as your buffer, allowing you to request a reward on the remaining $5,000.

How does the reward cap work?

Your first two rewards are each capped at 10% of your account size. On a $100,000 account, each of your first two rewards can be based on up to $10,000 of profit.

From your third reward onward, there is no cap.

How much does Leveraged Instant cost?
Account Size	Standard Price
$10,000	$89
$25,000	$179
$50,000	$299
$100,000	$499
⚠️ Important Notice: You must follow all trading objectives to stay eligible for rewards. Reaching the 2% floating loss limit at any moment results in immediate and permanent account closure.
