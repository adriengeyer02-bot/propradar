QT ONE
The QT ONE Plan is designed for traders who want a fast and straightforward route to
becoming funded.

Complete a single evaluation phase by meeting the trading objectives and following the
trading rules. Once your account has successfully passed our Risk Review, you'll receive
your funded account and can begin trading.

With a single evaluation phase, no recurring subscriptions, and a streamlined funding
process, the QT ONE Plan offers a simple pathway for traders looking to prove their
consistency and discipline.

Daily Drawdown Limit
The Daily Drawdown is 3% of your starting account balance and is recalculated at the start
of each trading day based on the higher of your previous day's closing balance or closing
equity.

This means the maximum daily loss amount remains fixed at 3% of the starting account size,
but the daily loss threshold moves up whenever your previous day's closing balance or
equity increases.

For a $10,000 account, the Daily Drawdown amount is always $300.

Example – $10,000 Account
 Previous Day's Higher of Closing Balance           Daily Loss          Daily Drawdown
                 / Equity                             Limit                Threshold

 $10,000                                          $300              $9,700

 $11,000                                          $300              $10,700

 $11,500                                          $300              $11,200

In this example, the Daily Drawdown amount remains fixed at $300 because it is based on
the original account size. At the start of each trading day, the platform compares the
previous day's closing balance and closing equity, uses whichever is higher, and subtracts
$300 to determine the Daily Drawdown threshold for that day. This threshold is then fixed for
the remainder of the trading day and is recalculated again at the start of the next trading day.

CORE RULE – Floating Loss Rule
The total floating loss on your account must not exceed 1% at any time.

The floating loss is calculated using the combined unrealized loss across all open positions.
Exceeding this limit will result in a hard breach of your account.

Maximum Floating Loss: 1%

This rule applies to funded accounts only.

INACTIVITY RULE
All QT ONE accounts are subject to a 7-Day Inactivity Rule.

   ●​ For accounts where no trades have been placed, the inactivity period begins from the
      date the account is created.
   ●​ Once trading has commenced, the inactivity period is calculated from the date of the
      most recent closed trade.

If no trading activity occurs within the applicable 7-day period, the account will be considered
inactive and will result in a hard breach.

QT TWO
The QT TWO Plan is designed for traders who prefer a structured evaluation process that
rewards consistency, discipline, and responsible risk management.

Complete both evaluation phases by meeting the trading objectives and following the trading
rules. Once you have successfully passed the evaluation and completed the Risk Review,
you'll receive your funded account and begin your journey as a QT Funded trader.




How It Works
Purchase Your Evaluation

Purchase your QT TWO evaluation account and begin trading immediately.

Complete Phase 1

Achieve the 8% profit target while complying with all trading objectives and trading rules.

Complete Phase 2

Achieve the 5% profit target while continuing to meet all trading rules.

Risk Review

Once both phases have been successfully completed, your account will be reviewed by our
Risk Team to ensure compliance with all trading rules.

Get Funded

Upon successful review, your funded account will be issued, allowing you to begin trading as
a funded trader.




Evaluation Objectives
   ●​   Phase 1 Profit Target - 8%
   ●​   Phase 2 Profit Target - 5%
   ●​   Minimum Trading Days - 4 Days (Each Phase)
   ●​   Daily Drawdown Limit - 4%
   ●​   Maximum Drawdown Limit - 8% (Static)
Daily Drawdown Limit
The Daily Drawdown is fixed at 4% of your starting account balance and does not
increase as your account grows.

This means the maximum amount you can lose in a single trading day remains the same
throughout the lifetime of the account.

For a $10,000 account, the Daily Drawdown is always $400.

Example – $10,000 Account
     Previous Day's Closing        Daily Loss Limit    Daily Drawdown Threshold
            Balance

 $10,000                           $400                $9,600

 $11,000                           $400                $10,600

 $11,500                           $400                $11,100

In this example, although the account balance increases, the Daily Drawdown remains fixed
at $400 because it is based on the original account size. The daily loss threshold is
calculated by subtracting the fixed daily drawdown amount from the previous day's closing
balance.




Evaluation Rules
In addition to meeting the evaluation objectives, all traders are expected to demonstrate
disciplined and responsible risk management throughout the evaluation. Excessive exposure
and high-risk trading behaviour are not permitted.

Exposure Rule

   ●​ Your total risk exposure must not exceed 75% of your Daily Drawdown Limit at any
      time during the evaluation.
   ●​ Engaging in high-risk or "all-or-nothing" trading behaviour is strictly prohibited.



Key Conditions
If your total risk exposure reaches or exceeds 75% of your Daily Drawdown Limit, the Risk
Team may:

    ●​ Deny funding.
    ●​ Reset your account back to Phase 1.

The purpose of the evaluation is to assess consistent and disciplined trading rather than the
ability to pass using a single high-risk trade.​
