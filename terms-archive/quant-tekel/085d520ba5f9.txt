Frequently Asked Questions
Last Updated June 17, 2025

Explore answers to your most pressing questions about our brokerage services, trading conditions, platform options, and account features.

What trading platforms does Quant Tekel support?

We offer three industry-leading trading platforms to suit different trading styles and preferences: TradeLocker for modern, web-based trading with advanced features, cTrader for professional traders seeking sophisticated tools and automated trading capabilities, and MetaTrader 5 (MT5) for those who prefer the world’s most popular multi-asset platform with extensive technical analysis tools.

What is the maximum leverage offered?

Quant Tekel offers competitive leverage up to 1:100, allowing you to control larger positions with a smaller initial investment. This leverage ratio provides excellent trading opportunities while maintaining responsible risk management standards. Please note that leverage can magnify both profits and losses.

What instruments can I trade?

Our diverse portfolio includes three major asset classes: Forex pairs covering all major, minor, and exotic currencies for 24/5 trading opportunities; Commodities including precious metals, energies, and agricultural products; and Cryptocurrencies featuring popular digital assets like Bitcoin, Ethereum, and other leading cryptocurrencies with weekend trading available.

Are your spreads really from 0.0 pips?

Yes, we offer institutional-grade spreads starting from 0.0 pips on major currency pairs during peak trading hours. Our direct market access and relationships with top-tier liquidity providers enable us to pass on these ultra-competitive spreads to our clients, ensuring you get the best possible trading conditions.

Is Quant Tekel regulated?

Yes, Quant Tekel (Pty) Limited is a fully regulated brokerage registered in South Africa. Our registered office is located at 60 Noll Avenue, Gatesville, Cape Town, Western Cape, 7764, South Africa. We operate under strict regulatory oversight to ensure the highest standards of client fund security and operational transparency.

Do you allow crypto trading on weekends?

Yes, unlike traditional markets, our cryptocurrency trading is available 24/7, including weekends and holidays. This means you can take advantage of crypto market movements whenever they occur, giving you uninterrupted access to digital asset trading opportunities throughout the week.

How do I open a live trading account?

Opening a live account is quick and straightforward. Simply visit our website, click on “Open Account,” and complete the online application form. You’ll need to provide basic personal information, verify your identity with the required documents, and once approved, you can fund your account and start trading immediately.

Are demo accounts available?

Yes, we offer free demo accounts with virtual funds so you can practice trading strategies, test our platforms, and familiarize yourself with market conditions without risking real money. Demo accounts come with the same features and real-time market data as live accounts, providing an authentic trading experience.

What deposit and withdrawal methods do you support?

We support two secure and efficient payment methods: Cryptocurrency deposits and withdrawals for fast, borderless transactions with popular digital currencies, and Wire transfers for traditional bank-to-bank transfers. Both methods are processed promptly to ensure you have quick access to your funds.

Is there customer support if I need help?

Yes, our dedicated customer support team is available 24/6 (closed Saturdays) to assist you with any questions or concerns. Whether you need help with platform navigation, account issues, or trading queries, our knowledgeable support staff is just a message or call away to ensure your trading experience is smooth and successful.

What is the minimum deposit to start trading with Quant Tekel?

You can start your trading journey with Quant Tekel from just $100. This low minimum deposit makes professional trading accessible to traders of all levels, allowing you to test our services and build your trading skills without a significant initial investment.

Can I open multiple trading accounts under Quant Tekel?

No, to maintain account security and comply with regulatory requirements, each client is limited to one trading account. This single account policy helps us provide better service, ensure proper risk management, and maintain the integrity of our trading environment. Your one account can access all available instruments and platforms.
