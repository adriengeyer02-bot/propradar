How
Profit Target
Works?

In the Prop Number One Instant Funding program, there is no profit target since your account is already funded from the start.

A profit target is typically the required net profit from closed positions that traders must achieve to advance to the next phase. However, since there are no evaluation phases in Instant Funding, this requirement does not apply.

That said, to be eligible for a withdrawal, traders must achieve a minimum profit of 1% of the account's initial balance.

For example: if you receive an Instant Funding account with a balance of $100,000, you will need to generate at least $1,000 in profit before you can request a payout.
