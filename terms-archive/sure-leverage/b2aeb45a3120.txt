Withdrawals & Payouts

What is the minimum withdrawal?

0.5% of your starting balance.

How are payouts sent?

Via Riseworks.io or USDT (TRC20).

Each payout has a fee of 2% or $10, whichever is higher.

After a payout, your account balance resets to the starting balance.

How long do payouts take?

Eligible payouts are processed within 24 business hours. If we miss this, you get an extra 10% on your profit split.

Exceptions: weekends and bank holidays, compliance or risk reviews, and delays in Rise onboarding or waiting on your reply.

What happens if my payout is denied?

If soft breach deductions take your profit to zero or below, the payout is denied. See your product's rules for the penalties.

Account Limits & Funding

How many accounts can I have?

Unlimited evaluation accounts.

What is the maximum funding?

$400,000 total across all your active accounts and products.

Only active accounts count. Breached, failed, closed or inactive accounts don't.

Example: an active $200,000 account plus two active $100,000 accounts = $400,000. That's allowed. A breached $100,000 account from before doesn't count.

If a new account would take you over $400,000, you can't open it until your active total is back under the limit.

Is there a scaling plan?

No. There is no scaling plan at the moment.

Rules & Breaches

What's the difference between a soft and hard breach?

Soft breach: the profit from that trade is deducted at payout. Your account stays open. Examples: tick scalping, news trading.

Hard breach: your account is closed. Examples: hitting max or daily drawdown, and the prohibited practices below.

Some rules, like tick scalping, aren't flagged automatically. They're checked at payout or during a risk review.

What trading practices are prohibited?

Tick scalping (trades held under 2 minutes): soft breach, profit deducted.

High-frequency trading (HFT): hard breach, account closed. Allowed only in the EA Challenge phase.

Latency arbitrage: hard breach, account closed.

Reverse arbitrage: hard breach, account closed.

Account management (someone else trading your account): hard breach, account closed.

Is martingale allowed?

No

Is there a breach for inactivity?

Yes. Place at least one trade or pending order every 30 days, or the account may be closed as inactive.

Eligibility

Who can buy an account?

You must be 18 or over.

Are there restricted countries?

We can't accept traders from: Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Myanmar, Russia, Crimea, Donetsk and Luhansk (Ukraine), Somalia, Syria, Pakistan and Algeria.

Maximum total funding is $50,000 for traders from: Malaysia, Vietnam and Taiwan.

We also can't serve anyone on a sanctions list, anyone with a financial crime or terrorism conviction, or anyone previously banned for breaching our terms.

Are evaluation fees refundable?

No. Evaluation fees are non-refundable.

Am I trading real money?

No. All accounts are demo accounts with virtual funds using live market prices.

Sure Leverage may copy trade our best traders on live accounts at our discretion.
