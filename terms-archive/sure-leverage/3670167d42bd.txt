Overview

What is One Step Pro (Futures Style)? A single-phase evaluation with an end-of-day (EOD) trailing drawdown. Hit the profit target to move to a funded account.

Maximum Drawdown: EOD trailing, by account size

Daily Drawdown: fixed, by account size (none on $25,000)

Profit Consistency Rule: 40% (funded only)

Profit Split: 90%

Payout Frequency: Every 3 days

Max Total Funding: $400,000 across all accounts

Trading Rules

Stop Losses

Not required.

News Trading

Not allowed. Trades must not be opened or closed 5 minutes before or after a red folder news event. Profit from these trades is deducted at payout.

Expert Advisors (EAs)

Allowed.

Copy Trading

You can copy your own trades.

Copying other traders' trades in the same timeframe is not allowed.

Averaging Into Trades

Allowed.

Tick Scalping

Keep trades open for over 2 minutes. Shorter trades are a soft breach.

Consistency Rules

Profit Consistency Rule (40%, Funded Only)

Your best single day can't be more than 40% of your total profit for the payout cycle.

Formula: best day profit divided by total profit.

Example: on a $50,000 account, best day $1,000 and total profit $4,000. $1,000 / $4,000 = 25%, so you're within the rule.

It resets after every payout. Not meeting it is not a breach. You just keep trading until you do.

Drawdown & Targets

Profit Target

By account size, see the table above.

Max Loss Limit (EOD Trailing)

Your limit sits your Max Loss Limit below the highest end-of-day balance or equity your account has reached.

It only moves at the 5pm EST snapshot. Intraday highs are ignored.

It only ever moves up, never down. Hit it and the account is closed.

Example 1: Intraday spike, flat close ($50,000, $2,000 limit)

Start: equity $50,000, limit $48,000.

Midday equity hits $50,900, then falls back to $50,000.

At 5pm EST equity is $50,000. The spike is ignored, so the limit stays at $48,000.

Example 2: Higher close ($50,000, $2,000 limit)

At 5pm EST equity is $50,600 (closed profit, open profit, or both).

New end-of-day high, so the limit trails to $48,600.

If you lose the next day, it stays at $48,600.

Daily Loss Limit (Fixed)

A fixed amount by account size that doesn't change over time (see table). Hit it and the account is closed.

Payouts

All of these must be met for each payout. They reset after every approved payout.

Payout Schedule

You can request a payout every 3 days.

24-Hour Payout Guarantee

Payouts are processed within 24 business hours of an eligible request.

If we miss this, you get an extra 10% on your profit split.

Exceptions: weekends and bank holidays, compliance or risk reviews, and delays in Rise onboarding or waiting on your reply.

Rule Breaches & Payout Denials

Soft Breaches (Profit Deductions)

If you break a funded account rule (for example tick scalping or news trading), the profit from that trade is deducted from your total profit.

If deductions are less than your profit, the reduced payout is still processed.

If deductions are more than your profit, the payout is denied.

Maximum of 20 soft breaches before the account is closed. These are checked at payout, not automatically.

If 50% or more of your trades are soft breaches, the payout is declined.

Payout Denial Penalties

1st denial: profit split is halved.

2nd denial: account closed.
