INSTANT FUNDING ZERO

How much can I withdraw?

Instant Funding Zero has no profit cap. Simply build your buffer and request rewards on profits above it. The buffer must be rebuilt after each reward.

Important: With trailing drawdown, withdrawals can bring you closer to your maximum loss limit.

When can I request rewards?

Trade for 14 days, clear your profit buffer, and meet the account’s eligibility requirements. Then request On-Demand Rewards.

Is martingale allowed?

No. Martingale strategies are not allowed on any SLF account.

Is there a consistency rule?

No, Instant Funding Zero has absolutely no consistency rules. Trade freely.

Can I trade news?

News trading is not allowed. Please avoid opening or closing trades within 5 minutes before or after a news event.

What is the floating loss rule on Zero Static?

Good news: with Static drawdown, your loss floor never moves. To keep trading responsible, Zero Static comes with one extra rule: your floating loss can’t go over 1% of your account. Please don’t over-risk, and size your trades so you stay inside it.

You get 2 chances. The 1st time you go over 1%, your reward split is halved. The 2nd time, the account is closed.

This rule only applies when you choose Static drawdown.

What is not allowed?

You can copy your own trades across accounts you own, but copying trades from other traders is not allowed.

Expert Advisors (EAs) are not permitted on this account type.

Reverse hedging across any accounts is strictly prohibited, with zero tolerance.

High-frequency trading (HFT) is not allowed. Trades must remain open for more than 2 minutes. Closing trades sooner may result in a soft breach.

Rules & breaches

How many soft breaches do I get?

You get 20 soft breaches, so there is room for mistakes. After 20, your account(s) are closed.

How much total funding can I have?

You can manage up to $200,000 in total funding across all your SLF accounts.

What’s the difference between a soft and hard breach?

Soft breach: The profit from the affected trade is deducted at reward, but your account stays open.

Hard breach: Results in account closure. Examples include exceeding drawdown limits or engaging in prohibited trading practices.

Some rules are reviewed at reward or during a risk review rather than automatically.

What trading practices are prohibited?

We want traders to trade fairly! The following practices are restricted:

Tick scalping: Trades held under 2 minutes are soft breaches, with profits deducted.
High-frequency trading (HFT): Hard breach resulting in account closure. Only permitted during the EA Challenge phase.
Latency arbitrage: Hard breach resulting in account closure.
Reverse arbitrage: Hard breach resulting in account closure.
Martingale: Martingale strategies are not allowed.
Account management: Having someone else trade your account is a hard breach resulting in account closure.

Is martingale allowed?

No, martingale trading strategies are not permitted.

Is there an inactivity rule?

Yes, simply place at least one trade or pending order every 30 days to keep your account active. Accounts inactive for longer may be closed.
