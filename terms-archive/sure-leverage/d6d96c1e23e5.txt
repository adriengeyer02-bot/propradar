📘 Instant Funding Static – FAQ & Rules
📘 Instant Funding Static – FAQ & Rules

Please read this to understand the rules of the challenge.

S
Written by Sure Leverage Funding
Updated over a week ago
Overview

What is Instant Funding Static? No challenge phase. Buy an account and start trading straight away, funded instantly.

Maximum Drawdown: 8% static

Daily Drawdown: 4%

Profit Consistency Rule: 25%

Profit Cap: 5% per payout cycle

Profit Split: 50% rising to 80%

Payouts: Instant (request as soon as you meet the requirements)

Max Total Funding: $400,000 across all accounts

Trading Rules

Stop Losses

Not required.

News Trading

Not allowed. Trades must not be opened or closed 5 minutes before or after a news event. 

Expert Advisors (EAs)

Not allowed on Instant Funding Static.

Copy Trading

You can copy your own trades.

Copying other traders' trades in the same timeframe is not allowed.

Martingale

Not Allowed to add into a losing position 

Tick Scalping

Keep trades open for over 2 minutes. Shorter trades are a soft breach.

Consistency Rules

Profit Consistency Rule (25%)

No single trading day can make up 25% or more of your total profit for the payout period.

Example: if your best day is $1,000, you need $4,000 total profit before you can request a payout.

Not meeting it is not a breach. You just keep trading until you do.

Drawdown & Targets

Maximum Drawdown (8% Static)

Your equity can never drop more than 8% below your starting balance.

It is fixed from day one. It doesn't trail and doesn't reset.

Example: on a $100,000 account, equity must stay above $92,000.

Daily Drawdown (4%)

At 5pm EST each day we take the higher of your balance or equity and subtract 4% of your starting balance. That's your floor for the next day.

Example 1: Floating Profit

$100,000 account, balance $100,000, equity $102,000 at 5pm EST.

Next day equity can't drop below $98,000 ($102,000 minus $4,000).

Example 2: Floating Loss

$100,000 account, balance $100,000, equity $98,000 at 5pm EST.

Balance is higher, so next day equity can't drop below $96,000 ($100,000 minus $4,000).

Floating Loss Rule

Your floating loss (open losses plus swaps and commissions) must never be more than 1% of your starting balance, measured from your current balance. This applies whether you're in profit or not.

1st breach: profit split is halved.

2nd breach: account closed.

This rule can be removed with the Floating Loss Removal add-on at checkout.

Example 1

$100,000 account at a $100,000 balance. Open trades push equity below $99,000. That's a breach.

Example 2

$100,000 account at a $102,000 balance. Open trades push equity to $100,900, a $1,100 floating loss. That's a breach, even though you're still above your starting balance.

Payouts

Profit Split

Your split rises with each payout:

 

1st payout: 50%

2nd payout: 55%

3rd payout: 60%

4th payout: 65%

5th payout: 70%

6th payout onwards: 80%

Payout Schedule

Instant payouts. Request as soon as you meet the payout requirements. There's no fixed cycle date.

Profit Cap & Max Withdrawal

Profit cap per cycle: 5% of starting balance.

Max withdrawal per cycle: 5% of starting balance.

Example: on a $200,000 account, the most you can withdraw per cycle is $10,000.

Minimum Profitable Days

4 profitable days before each payout.

A profitable day is a day with at least 0.5% profit.

24-Hour Payout Guarantee

Payouts are processed within 24 business hours of an eligible request.

If we miss this, you get an extra 10% on your profit split.

Exceptions: weekends and bank holidays, compliance or risk reviews, and delays in Rise onboarding or waiting on your reply.

Rule Breaches & Payout Denials

Soft Breaches (Profit Deductions)

If you break a funded account rule (for example tick scalping or news trading), the profit from that trade is deducted from your total profit.

If deductions are less than your profit, the reduced payout is still processed.

If deductions are more than your profit (for example $3,000 profit and $4,000 deducted), the payout is denied.

Maximum of 20 soft breaches before the account is closed. These are checked at payout, not automatically.

If 50% or more of your trades are soft breaches, the payout is declined. For example, 10 breached trades out of 15.

Payout Denial Penalties

1st denial: profit split is halved.

2nd denial: account closed.
