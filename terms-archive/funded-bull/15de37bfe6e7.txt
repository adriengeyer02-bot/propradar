5.2 Profit Sharing

FundedBull operates a profit-sharing model to reward traders who successfully generate profits using their funded trading accounts. Upon submission of a Payout Request from your Funded Account, trading on the account will be automatically locked until the payout is executed. This precautionary measure is implemented to prevent overutilization of your current margin, particularly when it is about to decrease. Trading activity on the Funded Account will resume promptly once the payout is fully processed. Upon processing a Payout Request from a Funded Account, the maximum drawdown limit will reset to the initial account balance. For this reason, it is recommended to retain a portion of your profits if you intend to continue trading on the Funded Account.

The details of the profit-sharing arrangement, including payout thresholds and schedules, are outlined below.

5.2.1
Profit Sharing Thresholds

Profit sharing is based on the performance of the trader and the size of the funded account. The following thresholds apply for payout:

For $5,000 and $10,000 accounts, traders must achieve a minimum profit share of $200 to qualify for payout.
For $25,000, $50,000, $100,000, and $250,000 accounts, traders must achieve a minimum profit share equivalent to 2% of the account size to qualify for payout.

Example Threshold Calculations:

For a $25,000 account, the profit share threshold is $500.
For a $50,000 account, the profit share threshold is $1,000.
For a $100,000 account, the profit share threshold is $2,000.
For a $250,000 account, the profit share threshold is $5,000.
5.2.2
Profit Sharing Percentage

The profit generated by the trader is shared between the trader and FundedBull according to the following structure: traders retain 90% of the profits, while FundedBull retains the remaining 10%.

5.2.3
Payout Schedule

Profit share is distributed according to a predefined payout schedule. Initial payouts are made after a 14-day period, with subsequent payouts processed within 7 days. However, it's important to note that only accounts that are not breached are eligible for payouts, even if there are trading gains available on the account following a breach. FundedBull reserves the right to adjust the payout schedule based on trading performance and other operational considerations.

5.2.4

Profits are calculated based on the net performance of the trader’s account after deducting any applicable fees, commissions, and other costs. FundedBull provides detailed account statements to traders, outlining the profit calculation and the resulting payout amount.
