Does ThinkCapital have a consistency rule?
DOES THINKCAPITAL HAVE A CONSISTENCY RULE?

ThinkCapital applies a consistency rule to Bolt and Bolt Flex accounts only. Your best day in a payout cycle can be no more than 20% of the total profit from that cycle's profitable days on Bolt, or 25% on Bolt Flex. It is a payout condition on simulated funded accounts, not a breach: if your best day is above the cap, keep trading until it falls within the limit. Lightning, Dual Step and Nexus accounts have no consistency rule.

Example: the profitable days in a payout cycle add up to $10,000 in profit, and the best day made $2,500. Losing days are not subtracted from this total.

Bolt: the cap is 20% × $10,000 = $2,000. The $2,500 day is over the cap, so a payout cannot be requested yet. If further profitable days lift the cycle total to $12,500, the $2,500 day is exactly 20% and the payout can be requested. Bolt Flex: the cap is 25% × $10,000 = $2,500, so the $2,500 day is exactly at the cap and the payout can be requested.

The consistency rule does not apply to Lightning, Nexus or Dual Step accounts.
