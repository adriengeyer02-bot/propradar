7.6.2. Best-Day Consistency Cap. At the time of each Pay-out Request in respect of an Instant Funded Account, the closed net profit of the single highest closed-net-profit calendar day within the relevant Reference Period shall not exceed twenty percent (20%) of the cumulative closed net profit for that Reference Period (the “Best-Day Consistency Cap”). Where the Best-Day Consistency Cap is not satisfied, the Pay-out Request may not be submitted until it is satisfied. For a Bolt Flex Account, the Best-Day Consistency Cap is twenty-five percent (25%) instead of twenty percent (20%).

7.6.3. Initial Balance Drawdown Lock. Each Instant Funded Account is subject to a maximum overall loss limit of six percent (6%) of the Initial Balance, which trails the high-water equity of the Account until the equity of the Account first reaches the Initial Balance plus six percent (6%), at which point the breach level of the maximum overall loss limit shall be permanently fixed at the Initial Balance of the Account, and shall not thereafter trail upward regardless of any subsequent equity highs (the “Initial Balance Drawdown Lock”). The daily loss limit of three percent (3%) of the Initial Balance, measured against equity and reset daily at 5 PM EST based on the end-of-day equity, shall continue to apply on its standard basis before and after the Initial Balance Drawdown Lock and is unaffected by the Initial Balance Drawdown Lock. Either limit being reached shall constitute a breach of the Account. For a Bolt Flex Account: (i) the maximum overall loss limit is seven percent (7%) of the Initial Balance and trails the highest closed balance of the Account (not its equity) until the balance first reaches the Initial Balance plus seven percent (7%), at which point the Initial Balance Drawdown Lock applies, and unrealised profit on open positions does not move the breach level; and (ii) the daily loss limit of three percent (3%) of the Initial Balance is measured against balance and reset daily at 5 PM EST based on the end-of-day balance.

7.6.4. Dynamic Leverage. Each Instant Funded Account is subject to dynamic leverage tiers, the precise levels of which are set out in Schedule 3 (Dynamic Leverage Schedule). Aggregate open exposure on each Instant Funded Account is evaluated per asset class in real time and the applicable leverage adjusts automatically in accordance with the tier table in Schedule 3.

7.6.5. Scaling. A Customer holding an Instant Funded Account becomes eligible for twenty percent (20%) increase in the Account's Capital where, on the same Account, all of the following are satisfied: (i) cumulative closed profit over a three (3) month review period is equal to or greater than ten percent (10%) of the Initial Balance (approximately 3.33% per month); (ii) reviews are conducted every three (3) months; and (iii) the Customer has received least four (4) successful Pay-out Requests in respect of that Account during that review period. Scaling may only be applied at a moment when the Account has zero open positions. Upon scaling, an amount equal to twenty percent (20%) of the original Initial Balance shall be added to the then-current closed balance of the Account, and the Account's new floor for purposes of the Initial Balance Drawdown Lock under Clause 7.6.3 shall be the original Initial Balance increased by twenty percent (20%) for each scaling applied; the Customer's pre-scaling profit cushion above the prior floor shall accordingly be preserved as the post-scaling cushion above the new floor. The aggregate Capital of all Instant Funded Accounts and Bolt Flex Accounts held by a Customer, including any scaling, shall not exceed USD 500,000. The mechanics for applying for, calculating, and effecting scaling are as published in our Frequently Asked Questions, which are incorporated herein. A Customer may hold not more than one (1) active account per Initial Balance size across Instant Funded Accounts (Bolt) and Bolt Flex Accounts combined (for example, an active USD 25,000 Bolt account prevents an active USD 25,000 Bolt Flex account, and vice versa), with a combined maximum Initial Balance of USD 192,500.
