What is the ThinkCapital Nexus Challenge?
WHAT IS THE THINKCAPITAL NEXUS CHALLENGE?

ThinkCapital's Nexus Challenge is a three-phase evaluation with profit targets of 7%, 6% and 5% for Phases 1, 2 and 3. The daily loss limit is 4% of the initial balance, measured from each day's closing balance. The maximum drawdown is 8%, fixed to the initial balance. You can trade and hold over the weekend, with dynamic leverage up to 1:100 in forex.

Format: Three-phase evaluation.

Daily drawdown: 4% of the initial balance, measured from the day's closing balance at the 5 PM ET reset.

Maximum drawdown: 8%, fixed to the initial account balance.

Profit targets: Phase 1 = 7%; Phase 2 = 6%; Phase 3 = 5%.

Trading conditions: Weekend trading and weekend holding allowed. Leverage up to 1:100 (dynamic).

Optional add-ons: News Trading, Weekly Payout and 90% Profit Split.

All evaluation phases (Phase 1, 2 and 3) and ThinkCapital funded accounts use a simulated data feed that replicates real market conditions.
