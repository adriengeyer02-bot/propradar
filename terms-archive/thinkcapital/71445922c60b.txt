How often can I get a payout from ThinkCapital?
HOW OFTEN CAN I GET A PAYOUT FROM THINKCAPITAL?

ThinkCapital simulated funded accounts offer a payout every 14 days as standard. The first cycle starts with your first trade on the funded account. Each later cycle starts with your first trade after the previous payout. The Weekly Payouts add-on shortens the cycle to 7 days on Lightning, Dual Step and Nexus. On Bolt and Bolt Flex, the On-Demand Payouts add-on lets you request a payout at any time.

Standard (all programs): 14 days, counted as 24-hour periods from the first trade of the cycle, not in calendar or trading days.

Weekly Payouts add-on (Lightning, Dual Step, Nexus): a 7-day cycle for the whole life of the funded account, if selected at purchase.

On-Demand Payouts add-on (Bolt, Bolt Flex): request a payout at any time, with no limit on the number of requests, if selected at purchase.

Other payout conditions

Lightning, Dual Step and Nexus: at least 3 profitable days in the cycle. Bolt and Bolt Flex: best day no more than 20% (Bolt) or 25% (Bolt Flex) of the total profit from the cycle's profitable days, and for the first payout only, profit of at least 6% (Bolt) or 7% (Bolt Flex). Every program: all positions closed and a minimum payout of $100.
