What is the ThinkCapital Bolt Flex program?
WHAT IS THE THINKCAPITAL BOLT FLEX PROGRAM?

Bolt Flex (Instant Funding) is ThinkCapital's second instant funding plan, so there is no evaluation phase. It has a 3% balance-based daily drawdown and a 7% trailing maximum drawdown that follows your highest balance and locks at the starting balance once the balance grows by 7%. A 7% profit target applies to the first payout only.

Format: Instant funding. You receive a simulated funded account without completing an evaluation. Account sizes: $2,500, $5,000, $10,000, $25,000 and $50,000.

Daily drawdown: 3% of the initial balance, measured from the day's closing balance at the 5 PM ET reset. Floating profit or loss at the reset does not move the floor.

Maximum drawdown: 7% of the initial balance, trailing your highest balance. It locks at your initial balance once the balance reaches 7% above it. Only closed profit moves the floor.

Profit target: 7% for your first payout only. No profit target applies to any payout after that.

Consistency rule: your best day in each payout cycle can be no more than 25% of the total profit from that cycle's profitable days before you can request a payout.

Payouts: every 14 days from your first funded trade, with an 80% profit split, a $100 minimum and no payout cap. Each later cycle starts with your first trade after the previous payout. There is no minimum number of profitable days.

Leverage: the same margin schedule as Bolt, from up to 1:100 on the smallest forex positions. Cryptocurrency is not available on Bolt Flex.

News trading: not allowed. No trading activity from 5 minutes before to 5 minutes after a high-impact news release (a 10-minute window), and no add-on is available. A violation is a hard breach.

Weekend holding: not allowed by default. Close all trades before Friday 21:00 UTC; an account with open positions at that time is breached.

Inactivity: open at least one new trade every 30 days.

Account limits: Bolt Flex shares one limit with Bolt: one active account per size across both plans, $192,500 combined. An active $25,000 Bolt account blocks a $25,000 Bolt Flex account, and the other way round.

Refund: the fee is refunded automatically with your 3rd payout, or in full within 14 days of purchase if the account has not been traded.

Expert Advisors: EAs are allowed in principle but cannot currently be deployed, because the ThinkMarkets app does not support automated trading. TradingView-based tools, such as indicators, can be used.

Add-on	Price	With the add-on	Without it

Add-on prices are added to the base price and the total is rounded to the whole dollar. For example, a $10,000 account with Weekend Holding costs $191 × 1.15 = $219.65, rounded to $220.

Bolt Flex accounts are simulated accounts that use a data feed replicating real market conditions.
