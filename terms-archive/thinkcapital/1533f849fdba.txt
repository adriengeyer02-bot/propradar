WHAT HAPPENS IF I BREAK A THINKCAPITAL TRADING RULE?

A ThinkCapital rule breach either ends the account or starts a warning, reset and ban process, depending on the rule. Drawdown breaches, restricted news trading, 30 days without a trade, fraud and chargebacks end the account. So does holding Bolt or Bolt Flex trades past Friday 21:00 UTC without the Weekend Holding add-on. Grid trading, latency arbitrage and other prohibited strategies follow the warning, reset and ban process, while copy trading normally results in a ban.

Rule breaches that end the account

1. Maximum drawdown breach: equity or balance reaching or falling below the maximum drawdown floor terminates the account automatically.

2. Daily drawdown breach: equity or balance reaching or falling below the daily floor terminates the account automatically.

3. News trading: No trade can be opened or closed, no pending order filled and no stop loss or take profit hit from 2 minutes before to 2 minutes after a high-impact (red-folder) release, or from 5 minutes before to 5 minutes after on Bolt and Bolt Flex. A violation terminates the account and forfeits profits.

4. Weekend holding on Bolt and Bolt Flex: open positions at Friday 21:00 UTC without the Weekend Holding add-on terminate the account.

5. Inactivity or failed verification: 30 days without opening a new trade, or failing verification, leads to termination.

6. Illicit trading activity (fraud): immediate termination.

7. Violating the Terms and Conditions: immediate termination.

8. Chargebacks: a chargeback made without first contacting ThinkCapital is a serious violation and leads to immediate cancellation.

Forbidden trading practices

Grid trading, latency arbitrage and the other practices on the prohibited strategies list are considered gambling practices and are subject to the following consequences. Each finding is reviewed by the Risk team before any action is taken. First confirmed violation: a formal warning. Second: an account reset or a final warning, decided case by case. After two resets: a ban from ThinkCapital programs. A confirmed violation can also lead ThinkCapital to cancel all of the trader's accounts, and the accounts of anyone colluding with them. Copy trading and hedging across accounts are the exception: a confirmed violation normally results in a ban.
