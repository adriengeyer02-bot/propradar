Can I trade during news events with ThinkCapital?
CAN I TRADE DURING NEWS EVENTS WITH THINKCAPITAL?

ThinkCapital does not allow news trading by default on Lightning, Nexus, Dual Step Intraday, Bolt or Bolt Flex accounts. You cannot trade from 2 minutes before to 2 minutes after a high-impact release, or 5 minutes either side on Bolt and Bolt Flex. Dual Step Swing allows news trading, and Lightning and Nexus offer a News Trading add-on. A violation is a hard breach.

Standard rule (Lightning, Dual Step Intraday, Nexus)

Applies in both the challenge and funded stages. From 2 minutes before to 2 minutes after a high-impact event (a 4-minute window), no trade can be opened or closed, no pending order filled and no stop loss or take profit hit. Positions opened before the window can be held through it.

Avoid all pairs during Red Folder events on ForexFactory. 'All Day' red-folder events are not counted.

Bolt and Bolt Flex (stricter)

The same restrictions apply from 5 minutes before to 5 minutes after a high-impact event (a 10-minute window). No News Trading add-on is available on either plan.

Exceptions

Dual Step Swing allows news trading by default. If you bought the News Trading add-on with Lightning or Nexus, news trading is allowed in all phases.

Violations: a hard breach. The account is terminated and profits are forfeited.
