Any payment FFN makes to a trader under its published payout rules is compensation earned under the

4. Risk of Loss in Futures Trading
Trading futures and other leveraged derivatives involves substantial risk of loss and is not suitable for
every person. Leverage can work against you as well as for you, and market conditions can change
rapidly and without warning. In live markets it is possible to lose more than an intended or anticipated
amount. Past performance, whether actual or simulated, is not indicative of future results. You should
never trade with money you cannot afford to lose, and participation in an FFN program does not
eliminate, reduce, or transfer the risks inherent in futures trading.

No trading strategy can eliminate risk. Strategies using combinations of positions, such as spreads, can
be as risky as outright long or short positions. Contingent orders, such as stop-loss or stop-limit orders,
will not necessarily limit losses to intended amounts, because market conditions — including gaps, halts,
fast markets, and limit moves — can make it impossible to execute such orders at, or near, the intended
price. Day-trading strategies carry additional risks, including exposure to leverage, competition with
professional traders, and the cumulative effect of commissions and fees on net results.

can all cause live results to differ materially from simulated results. Performance achieved in a simulated
account may not be replicable in a live account.

Capital in live-funded accounts is provided by FFN or its partners and at all times remains their property.
Traders do not deposit funds into, and do not hold any ownership interest in, live-funded accounts.
Continued access to a live-funded account, and eligibility for payouts from it, are governed by FFN’s
published Trading Rules and may be affected by trading losses. Any additional data or platform fees
applicable to funded accounts are described in FFN’s Help Center.
