Important: Although there is no Daily Loss Limit, the 6% trailing Maximum Loss Limit is strictly enforced. If your equity drops below the trailing limit at any point, your account will be considered breached.

How the 6% Trailing Maximum Loss Limit Works

The Maximum Loss Limit (MLL) is set at 6% of your initial balance. This limit trails your account's highest recorded balance but is capped at your initial starting balance.

How it trails: If you make a profit, the Maximum Loss Limit moves up. For example, on a $10,000 account, the initial MLL is $9,400. If your balance grows to $10,200, the MLL moves to $9,600.

 

The Cap: Once the Maximum Loss Limit reaches your initial starting balance ($10,000 in this example), it will never move higher, even if your profits continue to grow.

 

Losses: Trading losses do not move the Maximum Loss Limit downward.

Important Note on Performance Rewards and Maximum Loss Limit: Requesting a Performance Reward brings your account balance closer to your Maximum Loss Limit. Since the limit does not move down after a reward is issued, traders should manage their rewards carefully to maintain a sufficient buffer.For example, on a $10,000 account, the initial MLL is $9,400.

If your balance grows to $10,200, the MLL moves to $9,600. If you decide to take a $100 Performance Reward, your balance after the request will be $10,100, and the MLL will remain at $9,600, reducing your buffer from $600 down to $500. The MLL never moves down, so managing the size of your reward requests is important to maintain a safe buffer.

Breach Condition

If your account balance falls below the active MLL at any time, the account is considered breached.

MLL After Withdrawals

When a withdrawal is made, a new trading cycle begins. The Maximum Loss Limit does not reset or decrease; it is carried forward from the previous cycle at its last achieved level. The distance between your account balance and the MLL at the start of the new cycle becomes the fixed gap that the trailing mechanism preserves going forward.

From the moment the new cycle begins, every dollar of profit immediately moves the MLL upward by the same amount, maintaining that same gap. The MLL does not wait for your balance to reach any previous peak before it starts moving; it trails your balance continuously from the very first dollar of profit in the new cycle.

Example:

Cycle 1:

Account grows to a highest balance of $10,450

MLL reaches $9,850

Trader withdraws $100

Cycle 2 begins:

Starting Balance: $10,350

MLL carried forward: $9,850

Gap between balance and MLL: $500—this gap is preserved and trailed going forward immediately

Key things to remember:

The MLL is never reset after a withdrawal

The MLL is carried forward at its highest achieved level

Trailing resumes immediately from the first dollar of profit in the new cycle

Withdrawals reduce the gap between your balance and the MLL—manage this carefully

Trading Cycle and Performance Reward Eligibility

In the Stellar Instant Account, you can claim your Performance Reward either on-demand by achieving 5% account growth or on a bi-weekly (14-day) cycle if growth is less than 5%, provided you meet the minimum 1% growth requirement.

 

On-Demand Performance Reward (5% Growth)

You can request your Performance Reward once your account reaches 5% growth — but eligibility is confirmed at the End of Day (EOD).

The system checks your account eligibility at the End of Day (EOD).

If your account meets the 5% growth milestone at the time of the EOD check, the Transfer to Wallet option is enabled.

The option remains available until you place a new trade.

Once a trade is placed during the current trading day, the transfer option is disabled immediately.

It will remain disabled until the next EOD eligibility check.

At the next EOD, the system will re-evaluate eligibility and re-enable the option if 5% growth is still maintained.

Example: Starting balance: $10,000. Profit: $500 (5% growth). If your account meets 5% growth at Monday EOD, you can transfer your reward. If you place a trade on Tuesday before transferring, the option will be disabled. It will be checked again at Tuesday EOD and re-enabled only if 5% growth is still maintained.

Bi-Weekly (14-Day) Performance Reward

If your growth is less than 5% but at least 1%, you can withdraw Rewards 14 days after your cycle start date. This option is useful if you want regular payouts without hitting the 5% growth target.

Example: Starting balance = $10,000. Profit = $300 (3% growth). Eligible for withdrawal after 14 days.

Important: The minimum growth requirement for any Reward is 1%. If growth is below 1%, you cannot request a Reward and your cycle will be extended.

Scale-Up

FundedNext Stellar Instant Account includes a performance-based scale-up plan that can grow your account up to 10 times your initial balance as you consistently perform and make withdrawals.

Scale-Up Criteria

To qualify for a scale-up in your current tier, you must:

Achieve a cumulative 10% growth in the current tier, measured as (total disbursed withdrawal amount divided by current tier start balance) multiplied by 100.

Complete at least one withdrawal in the current tier.

Once both conditions are met, your account balance and Maximum Loss Limit increase, and your tier is upgraded.
