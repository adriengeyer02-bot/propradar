How often will I receive my Performance Reward?
How often will I receive my Performance Reward?
October 16, 2026

The Performance Reward schedule varies depending on the challenge model you are participating in. Below are the details of the Performance Reward structure for each challenge:

 

Stellar 1-Step Account

Stellar 1-Step Challenge Phase: Traders who successfully pass the Challenge will be eligible to request the 15% reward from Challenge once they meet the criteria for the Scale-Up plan in the FundedNext Account.
​

Stellar 1-Step FundedNext Account: Once you transition to your FundedNext Account, your Reward Share will increase to 80%. Starting from the first trading cycle, you will receive Performance Rewards every 5 business days. Additionally, you may request your Refundable Fee for the subscription fee with your third Performance Reward withdrawal from the FundedNext Account.  Once you fulfill all the scale-up requirements, your Reward Share will increase to 90%.

 

 

Stellar 2-Step Account

Stellar 2-Step Challenge (Phases 1 and 2): Traders who successfully pass both the Challenge Phases will be eligible to request the 15% reward from Challenge from both phases once they meet the criteria for the Scale-Up plan in the FundedNext Account.
​

Stellar 2-Step FundedNext Account: Once you transition to your FundedNext Account, your Reward Share will increase to 80%. You can request your first Performance Reward 21 days after starting trading, with subsequent Performance Rewards occurring every 14 days thereafter. Additionally, you may request your Refundable Fee for the subscription fee with your first Performance Reward from the FundedNext Account. Once you meet all the scale-up criteria, your Reward Share will increase to 90%.

 

Stellar 2-Step traders can now choose from three payout options at checkout:
​

Standard (default)
First Performance Reward is available after 21 days. Subsequent rewards are available every 14 days. Reward share: 80%. No minimum trading day conditions.
​

3 Day Performance Reward
Reward cycle is every 3 days. Requires a minimum of 3 profitable trading days per cycle, with at least 1% profit per day. Reward share: 60%.
​

On-Demand Performance Reward
Withdraw anytime once you have achieved 2% account growth and maintained 40% consistency across trades. Reward share: 90%.
​

Note: If the Lifetime Reward 95% Add-On is purchased, the reward share becomes 95% regardless of which payout option is selected.

 

Stellar Lite Account

Stellar Lite Challenge (Phases 1 and 2): Unlike Stellar 1-Step and 2-Step, there is no Reward Share associated with the Challenge Phase in the Stellar Lite Account.
​

Stellar Lite FundedNext Account: Upon transitioning to your FundedNext Account, your Reward Share will increase to 80%. You can request your first Performance Reward 10 business days after starting trading, with subsequent Performance Rewards made every 10 business days thereafter. You may also request your Refundable Fee for the subscription fee with your third Performance Reward from the FundedNext Account. Users can increase their Reward Share up to 90% once they fulfill all the criteria of the Scale-Up plan

 

Stellar Lite traders can now choose from three payout options at checkout:
​

Standard (default)
First Performance Reward is available after 10 business days. Subsequent rewards are available every 10 business days. Reward share: 80%. No minimum trading day conditions.

 

3 Day Performance Reward
Reward cycle is every 3 days. Requires a minimum of 3 profitable trading days per cycle, with at least 1% profit per day. Reward share: 60%.

 

On-Demand Performance Reward
Withdraw anytime once you have achieved 2% account growth and maintained 40% consistency across trades. Reward share: 90%.

 

Note: If the Lifetime Reward 95% Add-On is purchased, the reward share becomes 95% regardless of which payout option is selected.

 

Important: Accounts purchased or reset on or after October 12, 2026 the trading cycle will be 10 business days.
Accounts purchased or reset before this date will continue to follow the previous rules: 5 minimum trading days per phase, first payout after 21 days, and subsequent payouts every 14 days.

 

Additional Review and Processing Condition

All Performance Reward requests are subject to a standard compliance review conducted by the Risk Management Team prior to processing.

 

During this review, trading activity is assessed to ensure full compliance with FundedNext’s Terms, trading rules, and platform policies. Performance Rewards will only be processed once the review confirms that all trading activity is in accordance with the applicable guidelines.

 

If any discrepancy, irregularity, or potential violation is identified during the review, the Trading Ethics & Standards Team may reach out to the trader for clarification or further assessment before proceeding. Appropriate adjustments may be applied in line with FundedNext’s policies. In cases where profits generated from trades that violated the rules exceed the requested Performance Reward amount, the Performance Reward may be denied in full based on the severity of the violation.

 

Post-Processing Review and Account Status

FundedNext may conduct additional reviews where necessary, including after a Performance Reward has been processed.

 

If the Risk Management Team subsequently identifies any irregularity, policy breach, or material concern related to the trading activity, the account may be temporarily paused pending further assessment.

 

Account reactivation will be subject to a formal review by the Trading Ethics & Standards Team following communication with the trader. Any decision regarding reinstatement, adjustment, or further action will be made in accordance with FundedNext’s Terms and policies.

 

Important Notes:

The 15% reward from Challenge is not available for U.S.-based clients.

For Challenge Accounts Purchased or Reset Before January 12, 2026

Traders with Challenge Accounts purchased or reset before January 12, 2026 will continue to follow the previous structure, where the 15% reward from Challenge is granted upon achieving 10% cumulative growth in the Stellar 1-Step and 2-Step FundedNext Accounts.

For Challenge Accounts Purchased or Reset On or After January 12, 2026

The 15% reward from Challenge is now tied to the eligibility of the updated Scale-Up plan. To learn more about the latest Scale-Up eligibility criteria, click here.
