Stellar Lite Account

Everything a trader needs to know, in sequence

Updated over 2 weeks ago

TL;DR: The Stellar Lite Challenge is a two-phase evaluation offered by FundedNext. Pass Phase 1 with an 8% Profit Target and Phase 2 with a 4% Profit Target, both with a 4% Daily Loss Limit, 8% Maximum Loss Limit, and 2 minimum trading days per phase. Complete KYC and receive a FundedNext Account with an 80% Reward Share. Three payout options are available at checkout. Stellar Lite follows the regular Scale-Up process.

The Stellar Lite Challenge is a two-phase evaluation offered by FundedNext where traders must meet defined Profit Targets across two consecutive Challenge Phases without violating any trading rules to qualify for a FundedNext Account.

The Stellar Lite is designed to offer a more accessible entry point with lower loss limits and a reduced Profit Target in Phase 2, making it a suitable model for traders who prefer a lower-risk evaluation structure.

Available Add-Ons

The following add-ons can be selected at checkout:

Swap-Free Add-On: Removes overnight swap charges from your account.

No Minimum Trading Days Add-On: Removes the minimum trading day requirement from the Challenge Phase. Available on $6,000, $15,000, and $25,000 account sizes only.

Double Up Add-On: Receive a second identical challenge account at a 60% discount, unlocked after passing the first Challenge Phase. Maximum allocation $600,000.

Requirements to Pass the Challenge Phase

To successfully complete both phases of the Stellar Lite Challenge and qualify for a FundedNext Account, you must meet all of the following requirements simultaneously in each phase:

Profit Target — Phase 1: 8% | Phase 2: 4%

You must reach an 8% profit in Phase 1 and a 4% profit in Phase 2 on the starting account balance. There is no time restriction to achieve these targets.

Phase 2 Account Issued

A pop-up notification will appear on your Dashboard. Once the current trading day ends, your Phase 2 Challenge Account will be issued and visible in your Active Accounts.

Daily Loss Limit — 4%

Your account must not lose more than 4% of the initial balance in a single trading day in either phase. If this limit is breached, the account is considered violated.

Learn more: How to Calculate the Daily Loss Limit

Maximum Loss Limit — 8%

Your account balance or equity must not drop below 92% of the initial balance at any point in either phase. If this limit is exceeded, the account is considered violated.

Learn more: What is the Maximum Loss Limit?

Minimum Trading Days — 2 Days

You must place trades on at least 2 separate calendar days per phase, with a minimum of 1 trade per day. Days do not need to be consecutive and there is no maximum number of trades.

 

Even if you reach the Profit Target before completing 2 trading days, you cannot proceed to the next phase or to a FundedNext Account until the minimum trading day requirement is met.

 

Learn more: Minimum Trading Days in FundedNext Challenges

General Rules — Applies to Both Challenge and FundedNext Account

The following rules apply at all times, regardless of which phase you are in:

Copy Trading Policy

Allowed between challenge accounts owned by the same trader.

EAs and Indicators

You are free to use Expert Advisors (EAs) and custom indicators to support your trading strategies on account sizes below $50,000, as long as their settings are aligned with your account's parameters.

You are not allowed to use any methods that involve platform manipulation, latency exploitation, or cheating. It is your responsibility to fully review the Terms of Service to avoid any violation.

Quick Strike Rule

FundedNext defines Quick Strike trades as positions closed within 30 seconds of opening. The Quick Strike percentage represents the proportion of total recorded profit derived from trades closed within 30 seconds, evaluated on a per-cycle basis.

Learn more: Understanding the Quick Strike Parameter on FundedNext

After Passing the Challenge — What Happens Next

Once you have met the Profit Target and minimum trading day requirement in Phase 1, the following process begins automatically at the end of that trading day:

1. Automatic Profit Check  The system verifies that you have met the Profit Target and that all trading rules have been followed correctly.

2. Dashboard Pop-up After Phase 2 Once Phase 2 requirements are met, a pop-up will appear on your Dashboard prompting you to Start KYC.

3. Agreement Signing Before the Verification Center unlocks, you will be presented with an agreement form. Enter your full name and address, check the box to agree to the terms, and click Submit. Only after submitting the agreement will the KYC document upload step become available.

4. KYC Document Upload Once the agreement is submitted, the Verification Center unlocks. Submit your valid photo ID and complete the liveness check to proceed.

5. Post-Challenge Risk Review Before your FundedNext Account is issued, the Risk Management Team conducts a standard compliance review of your trading activity. If a violation of the Terms, trading rules, or platform policies is identified, the account may not be processed and the Trading Ethics and Standards Team may reach out for clarification.

KYC Process

FundedNext uses a single, profile-level KYC system. This means you verify once and it applies to every account you own across all models. Once you pass the Challenge Phase and your account pauses for the server reset, a pop-up will appear on your Dashboard prompting you to Start KYC.

How to Complete KYC — Step by Step

1. Start KYC  Once eligible, a pop-up appears on your Dashboard. Click Start KYC.

 

2. Choose Verification Method You will be given the option to scan a QR code or receive a verification link by SMS.

 

3. Submit Documents Follow the link and submit a valid photo ID along with a liveness check, which is a real-time selfie to confirm your identity.

 

4. Confirmation You will receive a confirmation that your documents have been received.

 

5. Review and Approval Review takes approximately 48 hours. Once complete, you will receive a notification confirming your verified status.

Required Documents

Submit a photo of a physical copy of one of the following:

National ID

Passport

Permanent Residence Permit

Driving Licence — accepted only for traders in the US, Australia, New Zealand, and Canada

FundedNext may also request a Proof of Address such as a utility bill or bank statement issued within the last 3 months.

 

Submission Attempts

Iraq and Nigeria: 5 attempts

All other countries: 3 attempts

 

If you exhaust all your attempts, contact our support team directly, and they will guide you accordingly.

 

Important Notes

Simply submitting your documents does not complete the KYC verification process. Your status must be Approved to receive a FundedNext Account.

If you pass a Challenge but do not complete KYC within 30 days, your account can be deactivated. Complete verification promptly after passing.

Once your KYC is approved, your profile personal details such as name and date of birth are locked to match your verified documents. This is why the Edit Profile button appears greyed out on a verified account.

Note on 90-Day KYC Renewal: The mandatory 90-day KYC renewal process has been temporarily paused. Traders are not required to resubmit their KYC documents at this time, and your approved KYC verification will remain valid until further notice. We will notify all traders in advance before the renewal process is reinstated. Once your KYC is approved, if resubmission is required for your next eligibility, our team will reach out to you directly with further instructions.

Learn more: Understanding the KYC Verification Process at FundedNext

Risk Rule

FundedNext requires traders to limit risk to a maximum of 3% at any given time in the FundedNext Account. Risk refers to the maximum potential loss on a trade at a time, based on stop-loss placement, the maximum realized and unrealized (floating) losses across all trades, calculated against the initial account balance.

Process of Withdrawal
When Can You Withdraw?

A Performance Reward can be requested at the end of each trading cycle, provided the account is in profit for that cycle. All Performance Reward requests are processed within 24 hours of approval. Traders are responsible for covering any transfer gateway charges.

 

How to Request a Withdrawal

The withdrawal process depends on the payout option you selected at checkout:

10-Day Cycle Payout Option

If you selected the Standard payout option, you can request your Performance Reward directly without a wallet transfer step.

 

1. Go to Payout Navigate to the Payout section on your account Dashboard and click Payout Request.

 

2. Confirm via OTP An OTP will be sent to your registered email address. Enter the OTP to confirm and submit your request.

3-Day Performance Reward and On-Demand Performance Reward Options

If you selected the 3 Day Performance Reward or On-Demand Performance Reward payout option, the withdrawal process includes a Transfer to Wallet step first.

 

1. Transfer to Wallet Navigate to your account Dashboard and transfer your reward share to your FundedNext Wallet.

 

2. Go to Payout From the Wallet, go to the Payout section and click Payout Request.

 

3. Confirm via OTP An OTP will be sent to your registered email address. Enter the OTP to confirm and submit your request.

Available Payout Methods

Performance Rewards can be withdrawn via the following methods:

USDT (ERC20 / TRC20)

USDC (ERC20)

Confirmo

RiseWorks

Bank Transfer

Direct Deposit to FNmarkets

Card Payout — available for traders who have at least one saved card on their account. Select from your saved cards, confirm the amount, and submit your request in under 3 steps.

Settlement is completed within 24 hours of approval. Minimum and maximum limits will be communicated separately.

Note: Traders from Iran can only request their Performance Reward using TC Pay.

Refundable Fee

The original challenge purchase/reset price is refunded as a Refundable Fee. You may request your Refundable Fee for the subscription/reset fee with your third Performance Reward from the FundedNext Account.

Reset

Yes, the option to reset is available for the Stellar Lite Challenge. However, please note that once you have acquired a FundedNext account, the option to reset will no longer be available. The reset behavior depends on which phase you are in when you request it.

 

Resetting Phase 1

If you reset your Stellar Lite Phase 1 account, the same account will be reset to its initial balance, and you will need to start from the beginning of Phase 1.

Resetting Phase 2

If you reset your Stellar Lite Phase 2 account, a new account will be generated in your dashboard, and you will receive an email containing the new account credentials. When a reset is requested for Phase 2, you will be provided with a new Phase 1 account. You must complete both Phase 1 and Phase 2 again to become eligible for a FundedNext Account.

 

Learn more: Can I Reset My Account Anytime?

Scale-Up

The FundedNext Scale-Up Program allows eligible traders to grow their account balance by 40% every time they qualify, with a reward share increase and no change to the existing Daily Loss Limit and Maximum Loss Limit rules.

Important: The Stellar Lite FundedNext Account is not eligible for the FundedNext Pro Scale-Up Program. Stellar Lite traders will follow the regular Scale-Up process only.

Learn more: Does FundedNext offer a Scale-Up plan?

Merge

Traders can merge two FundedNext Accounts into one to consolidate their account balance.

 

Merge Rules

Only FundedNext Accounts can be merged. Challenge Accounts cannot be merged.

Both accounts must be from the same challenge model.

Maximum merged balance: $300,000.

Merging is not permitted if there are open trades or if either account is mid-cycle. Complete the cycle and close all trades first.

Accounts with a negative balance cannot be merged.

cTrader accounts cannot be merged with Match-Trader accounts.

The scale-up account cannot be merged.

Note: The Stellar Lite model does not include the 15% reward from Challenge. There is no impact on reward eligibility when merging Stellar Lite FundedNext Accounts.
