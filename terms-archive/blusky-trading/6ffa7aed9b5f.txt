Top questions
Can I really request a payout every day?

Yes. If you meet the criteria and your account balance is eligible, you can request a payout every day.

Does the monthly evaluation fee go away after I pass my evaluation?

Yes. Once you pass the evaluation, the subscription ends. The fee is non-refundable and applies regardless of the outcome of your evaluation. It covers the cost of reviewing your progress.

Are there any fees after I pass my evaluation?

Launch plans have a one-time Launch fee, due when you pass the evaluation. All other plans have no additional fees after passing the evaluation. Transaction or brokerage fees may apply depending on your activity.

Am I risking any of my own funds at the brokerage?

No. You are not risking your personal funds. The brokerage provides the capital for trading, and performance-based fees or splits may apply.

If I'm doing well at the brokerage, can I get more contracts?

Yes. If you perform well consistently, you may qualify for additional contracts and more capital to trade.

Why should I go with BluSky over other prop firms?

BluSky offers competitive terms, flexible evaluations and transparent fees. Free daily coaching and real support set us apart. See how BluSky compares.

Can I use TradingView?

Yes. TradingView works for technical analysis, and many traders prefer it for its charting tools.

What are the rules in funded?

Rules vary by plan. In general, you follow risk management standards, the profit-sharing agreement and the trading guidelines for your account. Full details are in the Help Center.

How does the free coaching work?

Coaching runs daily on our live YouTube streams, where we review strategies, answer questions and help you improve.
