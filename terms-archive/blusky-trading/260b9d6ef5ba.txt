List of Countries we cannot offer services to

Updated this week

Our US FCM (our Clearing firm) and brokers do not let us accept traders who are citizens or residents of the countries listed below. We cannot make any exceptions.
​
During the set up of a funded trading account the broker and clearing firm will run a KYC (Know Your Customer) and AML (Anti Money Laundering) check.
​
You will also be required to provide a bank statement or utility bill as proof of address, and a government-issued ID.
​
If you cannot provide these documents, or fail these background checks (for example, if you are a convicted felon) we will not be able to proceed with opening the funded account, and you will not be eligible for any refunds and BluSky will terminate your account immediately.

 

If our Brokerage rejects your Brokerage application for any reason you will not be eligible for any refunds and BluSky will terminate your account immediately.

 

In some instances if you are allowed to trade in SIM accounts, and are from one of the below countries, your account rules and Payout Caps can be changed by the Risk Team. BluSky creates rules with the goal of getting the trader to a Live Brokerage Account. If you are not eligible for a Live Brokerage Account, your rules and/or Payout Caps or Profit Split will be reduced to half or 50/50.
