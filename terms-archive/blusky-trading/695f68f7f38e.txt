Live Brokerage: Qualifying, Transitioning and Trading a Live Account

Updated this week

 

Live Brokerage is the final stage of your path with us and the only stage where your orders reach the real market.

 

You don’t apply for or purchase a Brokerage account. You earn the opportunity through Sim Funded, and our Risk and Brokerage teams determine when you’re ready to transition.

 

Effective October 1, 2026, we’re updating how new traders transition into Live Brokerage. Existing Brokerage accounts will continue under the structure they were opened with.

How You Qualify

There are two primary paths depending on your plan.

 

Standard Plans

For Launch, Propel, Instant and Stocks, your Brokerage Review is triggered once you reach $10,000 in total profit on the account, including realized profit made and payouts taken.

 

Once you reach the threshold:

Stop trading the account

Open a support ticket requesting a Brokerage Review

Wait for the review to be completed before placing additional trades

Continuing to trade after reaching the threshold without completing your review may result in account action, and profits generated above the applicable threshold may not be payable.

 

Orbit Plans

Your Orbit account is reviewed once you reach the applicable Sim Funded profit ceiling.

Once you reach the ceiling, stop trading and contact Support.

 

We may also review your account at any time based on your trading history, performance, or overall risk profile.

What Happens During a Brokerage Review?

Our Risk Team reviews your overall trading performance, not just your account balance.

 

We may look at:

Trading consistency

Risk management

Position sizing

Payout activity

Previous account history

Overall sustainability

While your Brokerage Review is open, you cannot trade the account and payouts may be held.

 

Most reviews are completed within approximately five trading days, although broader Risk Reviews may take longer.

 

Your review may result in:

Approval for Live Brokerage

A deferred decision while you build additional trading history

Placement into the Live Account Readiness Program

If You’re Called Up to Brokerage

Beginning October 1, 2026, when you’re approved for Live Brokerage, you’ll have two options.

 

Option 1: Go Live

If you choose Live Brokerage:

Your Brokerage account starts at $0

Your live drawdown is based on your qualifying account size

Your live P&L begins at $0

Eligible Sim Funded profits are placed into your Bonus Vault

Your simulated balance is not deposited into your live account as trading capital.

Instead, your Brokerage account operates with real capital and the risk allocation assigned to your account.

Your Live Brokerage account begins with a $0 starting balance. The applicable MLL determines the initial downside threshold assigned to your account.

Your individual Brokerage risk parameters, including contract limits and other account-level controls, may be adjusted by the Risk Team based on your trading performance and overall risk profile.

 

Example: 50K Account

If your qualifying 50K account carries a $2,000 drawdown, your Live Brokerage account would begin with:

 

Starting Balance: $0
​Live Drawdown: $2,000


​Eligible Sim Funded Profit: Bonus Vault

Option 2: Take the Buyout

If you prefer not to move into Live Brokerage, you can choose a one-time cash buyout equal to 50% of your applicable drawdown.

 

Using the same 50K example:

Go Live

	

$0 starting balance + $2,000 live drawdown




Take the Buyout

	

$1,000 cash payout

After taking the buyout, a cooldown applies before you can return to Evaluations.

 

Finality of Your Selection

Once you confirm your selection between Live Brokerage and the buyout, your decision is final.

 

After your selection has been submitted to our Risk Team, the chosen pathway cannot be changed, reversed, or exchanged for the alternative option.

Sim Funded Accounts After Your Brokerage Invitation

Once your Live Brokerage invitation is issued, your active Sim Funded Accounts are considered to have completed the Sim Funded stage and are no longer eligible for further trading or payout activity. If you have multiple active Sim Funded Accounts, this applies to all of them, and your qualifying Sim Funded activity is consolidated into one Live Brokerage account with the applicable live risk parameters and Bonus Vault.

 

You do not continue trading additional Sim Funded Accounts alongside your Live Brokerage account. Once the Live Brokerage invitation is issued, further trading or payout activity on those accounts is no longer eligible.

 

Any trades placed or payout requests submitted on those Sim Funded Accounts after the Live Brokerage invitation has been issued will not be recognized and may be voided. Continued trading, material losses, or a breach of any affected Sim Funded Account after the invitation is issued may result in your Live Brokerage transition being returned to Risk Review and may affect or result in forfeiture of your Live Brokerage eligibility.

 

The transition becomes effective at the time the Live Brokerage invitation is issued.

From that point forward, discontinue all activity on your active Sim Funded Accounts and follow the instructions provided for your Live Brokerage transition.

What Happens to Your Sim Funded Profits?

Under the new structure, your eligible Sim Funded profits are kept separate from your live trading balance through your Bonus Vault.

 

Your Bonus Vault:

Does not become your live starting balance

Is not used as live trading capital

Remains separate from your Brokerage account balance

Is paid according to the applicable Bonus Vault rules

Your Live Brokerage account itself begins at $0.

What If You Already Have a Brokerage Account?

If your Brokerage account was opened before October 1, 2026, nothing changes.

 

Your account continues under the structure it was originally opened with.

 

Under our previous Standard-plan structure, qualifying Sim Funded profits could be used when establishing your Live Brokerage starting balance.

 

Under our previous Orbit structure, your Brokerage account already began at $0, while eligible simulated profits were held in your Profit Vault.

 

The new $0 Brokerage + Bonus Vault structure applies to new Brokerage transitions going forward.

The Five-Day Transition Deadline

Your Live Brokerage invitation is valid for 5 calendar days.

Within that time, you’ll need to begin your required onboarding, which may include:

Selecting your platform

Completing account confirmations

Signing Brokerage documents

Beginning KYC

If you’re unable to complete the required steps within the five-day window, contact Support before your invitation expires.

Ramp-Up Period

When you first move live, we may place your account into a Ramp-Up Period.

This may include:

Reduced contract limits

A daily loss limit

Additional Risk Team monitoring

A required number of profitable trading days before limits are increased

A typical Ramp-Up Period may require 10 profitable trading days before you can request a Performance Review.

 

Once you’ve completed the applicable requirement, email:

Riskteam@blusky.pro

 

We’ll review your performance and determine whether your limits can be adjusted.

Live Brokerage Rules

Once you’re live:

Do not breach your account’s applicable minimum balance or liquidation threshold

News trading is permitted unless your account has a specific restriction

Your maximum contract size is enforced by your Brokerage settings

Positions must be closed within the permitted trading hours

Place at least one trade every 30 days to keep your account active

Your risk limits may be adjusted based on account performance

Your Live Brokerage contract limits may be lower than the limits you had during Evaluation or Sim Funded because they are based on real capital and actual Brokerage risk.

Brokerage Payouts

Brokerage payout requests continue to be processed on business days.

 

Minimum payout request: $500

Effective October 1, 2026, the minimum payout request is $500 across the board.

 

Your account may still have additional payout requirements, including:

Protected minimum balances

Profit thresholds

Profitable-day requirements

Account-specific Risk Team restrictions

Unless your account terms state otherwise, the standard Brokerage payout split remains:

 

90% to you / 10% to us

We strongly recommend maintaining enough of a cushion in your account to handle normal losing periods after a payout.

Ongoing Risk Supervision

Our Risk Team actively monitors Live Brokerage accounts.

Based on your account performance, we may adjust:

Daily loss limits

Contract limits

Temporary trading pauses

Other risk controls

These controls are designed to manage live capital exposure while giving you room to trade sustainably.

 

For questions about your individual risk settings, contact:

Riskteam@blusky.pro

If You Fail a Live Brokerage Account

If you breach your applicable Live Brokerage drawdown or liquidation threshold, your account will be closed.

 

A failed Live Brokerage account is subject to a 30-day cooldown unless otherwise approved by our Risk Team.

 

During your cooldown, you will not be able to open a new profile or additional trading accounts.

 

Once your cooldown has ended, you can return to the applicable trading pathway.

Brokerage Eligibility & KYC

Live Brokerage requires real Brokerage onboarding.

 

You may need to complete:

Identity verification

KYC and AML checks

Brokerage agreements

Proof of address

Tax documentation

Our broker and clearing partners may also request additional documents before your account can be opened.

 

Some countries cannot be supported because of brokerage or clearing restrictions, so make sure you review our current restricted-country information before reaching the Brokerage stage.
