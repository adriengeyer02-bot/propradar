Inactivity Rule
1 min. read
last update: 05.12.2026

Yes, there is an inactivity rule during both, the evaluation and the funded stages. You must place at least one trade every 60 days during the evaluation stage and 30 days during the funded stage to keep your account active. This rule prevents inactive accounts from accumulating on the platform.

If no trade is placed within a 60-day period, the evaluation account will be closed, with no eligibility for refunds.
If no trade is placed within a 30-day period, the funded account will be closed, with no eligibility for refunds or payout bonuses.
