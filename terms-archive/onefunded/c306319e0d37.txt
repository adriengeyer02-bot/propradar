13.15. Consistency Rule (Best Day Simulated Profit)

13.15.1. The Consistency Rule applies to Evaluation phases across the Flash Challenge, Core Challenge, and Value Challenge. It is a simulated metric designed to evaluate consistent trading performance in the fictional trading environment. The rule limits the proportion of your Total Simulated Profit that can come from your single Best Day Simulated Profit to promote steady and consistent simulated results rather than reliance on a single outsized simulated win. 13.15.2. Caps and Application: During Evaluation phases, the Simulated Profit Ratio must be at or below 50% for all Flash Challenges, 50% for Core $100,000 and $200,000 Challenges, and 40% for Value $100,000 Challenge, before you can pass the phase or qualify for a OneFunded TRADER account.

13.15.3. Status Effect: Violation of this rule does not breach or terminate your account. If your Simulated Profit Ratio exceeds the cap, you must continue trading until subsequent simulated profits dilute the ratio to within the limit.

13.15.4. Refresh Cadence: The Simulated Profit Ratio is recalculated automatically every day at 00:00 UTC+0 based on the current phase’s data. You can monitor your current ratio and the applicable cap at any time via your OneFunded Dashboard.

13.15.5. Calculation:

Simulated Profit Ratio = (Best Day Simulated Profit ÷ Total Simulated Profit) × 100%.

The ratio is checked using the most recent daily calculation when you reach the phase’s profit target. If above the cap, you must continue trading to meet the requirement.

13.15.6. Reset: The ratio resets at the start of each new Evaluation phase.
