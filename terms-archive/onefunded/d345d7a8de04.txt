Breaching a funded account
Breaching a funded account
2 min. read
last update: 10.28.2026

A funded account closes if it breaches your daily loss limit, your overall loss limit, Max Exposure, the inactivity rule or other Terms.

When that happens the account is closed permanently. The simulated profit on it is void, and no payout can be made from it — including profit earned before the breach.

Breaching a limit is automatic and cannot be reversed. The limits apply at every moment your positions are open, and floating profit and loss counts towards them, so an account can breach without a single trade being closed.

⚠️ Closures that are not about limits

An account can also be closed because of how it was traded. If we find a prohibited strategy, or any automation of trading or of copying between accounts without authorization, the account may be closed for that reason alone, and your loss limits may never have been touched.

This can come to light at two different moments:

While the account is running. In that case it is closed straight away, without waiting for a payout request
During the review of a payout request. In that case the payout is cancelled and the funded account is closed as well

Which strategies and tools are prohibited is set out in Risk Rules section.

Breaching a trading limit affects only the account it happened on. Your other accounts carry on as normal, and you can buy a new Challenge at any time. A breach of our Terms is different: it can affect every account on your profile, as well as any payout that is pending at the time.

If you have checked the figures in your dashboard and believe a closure is wrong, write to support@onefunded.com from your registered email address.
