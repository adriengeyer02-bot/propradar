Max Exposure Rule
Max Exposure Rule
4 min. read
last update: 10.14.2026

To promote responsible risk management, traders must not open or maintain any trade idea where the combined floating loss exceeds the Max Exposure limit for their account.

The limit is measured against your account Equity at the moment the trade idea was opened.

For challenge sizes 100k and 200k, a 2% Exposure Loss limit is applied (accounts of 100k and 200k purchased before 21 August 2026 remain on a 3% limit)
For smaller account sizes, a 3% Exposure Loss limit is applied.
For Instant accounts, the Max Exposure limit is 1%.

This rule is designed to prevent excessive exposure on a single trade idea, even when that exposure is split across multiple positions or correlated instruments.

What Does This Rule Apply To?

The Max Exposure Rule applies to:
Individual positions
Layered entries
Correlated instruments
Aggregated directional exposure
The rule is evaluated at the level of the trade idea, not only individual trades. It is important that a trader understand that splitting exposure across correlated instruments does not reduce the total measured risk.
Examples of Aggregated Exposure

The following may be considered one combined trade idea:

Multiple XAUUSD buy positions
NAS100 buy + US30 buy + SPX500 buy
EURUSD buy + GBPUSD buy
BTCUSD long + ETHUSD long
Soft and Hard Breach System

To ensure fair compliance with the rule and to avoid misunderstandings, OneFunded applies a Soft and Hard Breach system. How a breach is treated depends on your account type and, on challenge accounts, on the date the account was purchased.

Instant accounts

There is no warning stage on Instant accounts. Reaching the 1% limit is a hard breach: the account is deactivated immediately, from the first occurrence.

Challenge accounts purchased on or after 13 October 2026

This is the structure that applies to challenges bought today, and it covers Flash, Core, Value and Entry. The first violations are treated as warnings before enforcement action is taken.

1st breach on the account: Soft breach — warning only
2nd breach on the account: Soft breach — warning only
3rd breach on the account: Hard breach — the account is deactivated

This structure is intended to give traders a fair opportunity to understand and correct their risk management behaviour before the account is affected.

Previous structures

Challenges purchased between 12 and 13 October 2026

Four breaches rather than three:

1st breach on the account: Soft breach — warning only
2nd breach on the account: Soft breach — warning only
3rd breach on the account: Soft breach — warning only
4th breach on the account: Hard breach — the account is deactivated

Challenges purchased before 12 October 2026

On these accounts, breaches do not deactivate the account automatically:

1st breach on the account: Soft breach — warning only
2nd breach on the account: Soft breach — warning only
3rd breach on the account: Final warning

After repeated breaches, the account may be subject to further Risk & Compliance review and stronger enforcement action. 

How the structure is assigned

The rule set is fixed when the account is purchased and does not change afterwards. If you purchased your challenge before 13 October 2026, it stays on the previous structure for its whole lifetime, including after you progress to the next evaluation phase or to a funded account.

Breaches are counted per account. Each evaluation phase runs on a separate account, so the count starts again when you progress to the next phase.

General

OneFunded reserves the right to review the full trading context when assessing any breach, including position size, floating loss, account equity, correlated exposure, and whether the behaviour appears intentional or repeated.

This rule is enforced to promote disciplined risk management practices among traders and to help ensure prudent control of trading exposure.
