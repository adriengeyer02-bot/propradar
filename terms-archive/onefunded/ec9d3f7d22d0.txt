Expert Advisors (EAs)
2 min. read
last update: 10.23.2026

EAs are allowed but only after pre-approval. You must obtain approval before running any EA on a OneFunded account.

Please note:

EAs that execute trades automatically on behalf of the client are not allowed.
EAs that work as trading assistants — for example, helping calculate lot size, risk management, position sizing, or similar support functions — are allowed but only after pre-approval.
How do I get pre-approval for an EA?

Email our team at risk.team@onefunded.com with:


EA name;
Links to the advisor
Description on a workflow

We’ll review for compatibility with our trading environment and fair-use rules.

To ensure your trading remains compliant with OneFunded’s rules, traders should be aware of the following:

Trade manually unless an EA or tool has been specifically approved. Automated entry systems, trading bots, signal executors and similar software are not permitted.
Avoid excessive order clustering. Repeatedly opening several positions within the same second or in rapid automated-style sequences may be flagged for review.
Use appropriate Stop Loss protection. Frequently trading without a Stop Loss, especially across a large number of trades, may be considered poor or systematic risk management when combined with other unusual activity.
Avoid excessive ultra-short-term trading. Repeatedly opening and closing positions within only a few seconds for very small profits may be considered tick scalping or prohibited systematic trading.
Be mindful of unusually long trading coverage. Trading continuously across exceptionally long periods each day (14 hours+) may trigger additional review, particularly where the execution pattern is highly repetitive.
Use trading tools only for permitted assistance. Tools for lot-size calculation, Stop Loss/Take Profit management, break-even or partial closures may be permitted, but they must not automatically decide when to enter trades unless specifically approved by OneFunded.
Important: OneFunded does not normally assess these characteristics in isolation. Account reviews consider the overall combination, frequency and repetition of trading behaviour. A single short trade, same-second order or trade without a Stop Loss does not automatically constitute a breach; however, a consistent pattern of these behaviours may result in the account being reviewed or breached under OneFunded’s trading rules.
