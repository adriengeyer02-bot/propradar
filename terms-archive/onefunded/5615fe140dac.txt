last update: 11.10.2026

Before you buy a Challenge, make sure you can use our services. 

👤 Who can open an account

- You must be at least 18 years old.
- Accounts are opened for individuals only — we cannot register an account for a company or any other legal entity.

🔒 One profile per person

Creating a second profile is a breach of our Terms. If you end up with two, the duplicate will be blocked — and the profile that stays active is determined by your verification and purchase history, not by your choice. If you have lost access to your email, contact us instead of registering again: your address can be changed.

🔑 Your account is yours alone

You are responsible for everything that happens inside your account. Handing access to a third party, or trading someone else's account, results in closure.

🌍 Restricted jurisdictions

We cannot provide services to residents of the following jurisdictions:

Afghanistan, Belarus, Central African Republic, Crimea and Occupied Parts of Ukraine, Cuba, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Eritrea, Guinea-Bissau, Iran, Iraq, ISIL (Da’esh), Lebanon, Libya, Mali, Myanmar, North Korea (DPRK), Russia, South Sudan, Sudan, Somalia, Syria, Taliban, Yemen, Zimbabwe, United Arab Emirates.

⚠️ This list is not exhaustive and may be extended at any time. The current and binding list is the one published in our Terms and Conditions — please check it there before purchasing. Where the two differ, the Terms and Conditions apply.

🔗 Link for Terms and Conditions

💻 Any device works

The OneFunded website runs in your browser, so a computer, a phone and a tablet all work. Registering, buying a Challenge, following your progress and requesting a payout look and work the same way on each.

Trading itself happens in your trading platform, which has its own desktop and mobile apps.

💳 A completed purchase is not a confirmation

Being able to pay does not by itself confirm that our services are available in your country. If your jurisdiction is restricted, the account cannot be verified or funded.
