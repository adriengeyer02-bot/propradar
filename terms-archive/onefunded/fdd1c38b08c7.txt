Multiple Devices and/or IP Addresses
1 min. read
last update: 05.13.2026

At OneFunded, we recognize the need for flexibility in trading. However, to ensure the security and integrity of our platform, we have specific guidelines for using multiple IP addresses.

Trading from Multiple Devices and IPs

You may trade from various devices and IP addresses. However, if we detect that any of these devices or IPs are associated with another user’s account or observe suspicious activities (such as hedging, etc.), your account may be subject to termination. For security, we recommend trading from a single device and location whenever possible.

Consistency of IP Regions

The geographical region of the IP addresses used to access your account should remain consistent. If we detect a change in region, our team may reach out to verify this and request supporting documentation, such as an airline ticket, passport stamp, or live video confirmation of your location.
