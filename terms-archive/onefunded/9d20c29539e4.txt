Before you request a payout
1 min. read
last update: 10.28.2026

A payout can only be processed if the account is flat. Run through this before you open the request:

Enough time has passed since your previous payout — 14 days, or 7 days with the Weekly Payouts add-on. Before your very first payout the count runs from your first trade on the account instead. Until that time is up, the Request Payout button stays inactive
All open positions are closed
All pending and limit orders are cancelled. An order that triggers while your request is being processed can lead to a compliance review, so they have to be cancelled rather than left in place
Your consistency ratio is within your account's cap. If it is not, the Payouts page shows a notice and the button stays locked until you trade the ratio back inside the cap
The amount you are requesting is at least $100. That figure is what reaches you, after your profit split has been applied

While the request is being processed, the account goes into view-only mode: you can see everything but cannot place trades. Normal trading resumes once the payout is complete.
