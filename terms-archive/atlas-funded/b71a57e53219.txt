1-Step

One 10% target with a 7% static drawdown, then a funded account with the same limits.

1-Step has a single evaluation phase. Reach a 10% profit target over at least 3 trading days without breaking the drawdown or daily limit, and you move to a funded account with the same limits.

#
Evaluation
Target: closed balance at least 10% above your starting balance. Floating profit doesn't count until you close it.
Minimum days: 3 trading days. Any UTC day on which you close at least one trade counts.
No time limit.

When both are met, the phase passes automatically and your funded account is issued. Passing a phase.

#
Funded
Static 7% drawdown and a static 4% daily loss limit: each day you may lose up to 4% of your account size, counted down from your equity at 00:00 UTC.
Payouts every 14 days, starting 14 days after your funded account is created.
5 qualifying days per cycle, each with closed profit of at least 0.5% of your starting balance.
No consistency rule.
EXAMPLE · $25,000 1-STEP
Profit target (balance to reach)	$27,500
Maximum drawdown floor	$23,250
Daily limit if equity at 00:00 UTC was $25,800	$24,800
A qualifying funded day	closed profit ≥ $125
