FAQ
The questions
every forex trader asks
Can I trade news events?

Yes, with one rule once funded. In evaluation there is no restriction. On a funded account, profit from a trade opened or closed within 5 minutes of a red-folder release is deducted. Losing trades stand, and the account is not breached.

Are EAs and algos allowed?

Yes. Expert advisors and trade copiers are allowed, on evaluation and funded accounts.

Can I hold positions over weekends?

Yes. Hold positions through the weekend on every Atlas Forex model, index CFDs included.

What happens if I breach the drawdown?

Your evaluation or funded account ends. You can restart by purchasing a new evaluation. No hidden penalties, no chargebacks against future payouts.

When do I get paid?

Every 14 days. Your first payout opens 14 days after you are funded, then every 14 days. On Access the first opens after 28 days. Some models can add a faster payout cycle at checkout.

Is there a consistency rule?

Instant Zero has none at all, one big day counts the same as ten small ones. For every other model, pick it above and read the Evaluation Rules card for the figures that apply.

Are there minimum trading days?

In evaluation, 3 per phase on 1-Step and 2-Step, 3 on 1-Step Pro, 2 per phase on 2-Step Pro, and none on Access. On Pro, a day counts once it closes at least 0.5% in profit. Once funded, each payout needs 5 trading days on 1-Step, 2-Step, Instant and Instant Zero, and 3 on Access, 1-Step Pro and 2-Step Pro. No Minimum Trading Days is available as an add-on on some models.

Which instruments can I trade?

44 FX pairs at spreads from 0.0 pips, gold and silver, WTI, Brent and NatGas, and 10 global index CFDs.

Can I reset my account if I fail?

If you fail an evaluation, you start again by purchasing a new one, see “What happens if I breach the drawdown?” above.
