Atlas Standard

One evaluation phase with a 6% target and a 50% consistency rule. Funded payouts at any time, with a 40% consistency rule and no minimum days.

Atlas Standard is a one-phase futures evaluation. You pass with a 6% target, at least 2 trading days and a 50% consistency rule. Once funded there are no minimum days: you can request a payout as soon as your profit reaches the goal for your size.

Accounts bought before 22 October 2026

This page describes Atlas Standard as sold from 22 October 2026.

A challenge bought before then keeps its rules until it passes. From 26 October 2026 it is then funded on these funded rules.
A funded account on the original rules moved to these funded rules on 26 October 2026, with its balance and profit carried over. See My funded account moved to the new rules.

Your account's Statistics page always shows the rules that apply to it.

Split: 90%.
Request at any time. There is no waiting period and no minimum number of trading days.
Profit goal: the profit made since your last payout (or since the start, for the first one) must reach the goal for your size before you can request.
Consistency 40%: your best day can be at most 40% of that profit. Your best day is the highest-profit day since the account was opened, so a big day still counts after you have been paid.
How much: any amount from the $500 minimum request up to the cap, as long as your balance stays at or above the balance in the table. That balance is your starting balance plus your max loss plus $100. See Futures payouts.
Contracts: a funded account starts at half the contract limits. See Platforms and contracts.
