#
How it works

Buy and sign

Sign your funded trader agreement in the dashboard and your login activates within minutes.

Trade inside two limits

Your maximum drawdown is 5%, trailing at end of day. Your daily loss limit is 3%, set from your equity at each daily reset.

Build five qualifying days

Each payout cycle needs 5 trading days with closed profit of at least 1% of your starting balance.

Keep your best day under 20%

When you request a payout, your best day can be at most 20% of your profit since the last payout. Your best day is the highest-profit day since the account was opened, so a big day still counts after you have been paid. If it is higher, the payout waits until more profitable days balance it out. Your account is never breached for this. See Consistency rule.

Request your first payout after 14 days

Your first payout window opens 14 days after the account is created, then every 14 days. With the Weekly Payout add-on it is 7 days, then every 7. Accounts purchased before 17 October 2026 keep their original cycle: the first payout after 28 days, then every 14 days (14, then 7 with Weekly Payout).

#
Trailing, end of day

The 5% floor is recalculated once a day, at 00:00 UTC, from the highest closed balance your account has reached. Open profit never raises it, and it never moves down. Your equity must stay above it at all times. More on trailing end of day.
