Funded account rules

Atlas Protector

A safeguard on funded accounts. If the net floating loss of your open positions reaches 2% of your initial balance, every position is closed. The second time, the account is breached.

Atlas Protector is a safeguard on funded accounts. It closes your open positions before an open loss can take the account.

Trigger

Net floating loss ≥ 2% of the account's initial balance

The net floating loss is your equity minus your balance: the combined unrealised result of every position you have open. A winning position offsets a losing one. Only open positions count. Closed trades never do: a loss you have already taken is in your balance.

The threshold is a fixed amount. It is measured against your initial balance, not your current balance or your high. It is not a daily limit and it does not reset at midnight. It looks only at the positions you have open right now.

	WHAT HAPPENS
1st trigger	Every open position is closed automatically. Your account stays active and you can trade again straight away. Your profit split is reduced to 50% for the life of the account.
2nd trigger	Every open position is closed and the account is breached.
#
Examples
ACCOUNT	THRESHOLD	OPEN POSITIONS	NET	RESULT
$5,000	$100	−⁠$60 and −⁠$40	−⁠$100	Triggers
$5,000	$100	−⁠$60, −⁠$50 and +$20	−⁠$90	Does not trigger
$100,000	$2,000	−⁠$500, −⁠$500 and −⁠$1,000	−⁠$2,000	Triggers
$100,000	$2,000	−⁠$500, +$100 and −⁠$1,500	−⁠$1,900	Does not trigger

In the last example the two losses alone total $2,000. It still does not trigger, because the winning position offsets them: the net is −⁠$1,900.

#
Good to know
Funded accounts only. It does not apply during an evaluation, and it does not apply to futures accounts.
The same loss never counts twice. After a trigger, Atlas Protector re-arms only once your open positions show no net loss, for example once they are closed. A second trigger needs a new 2% net floating loss after that, on the same day or a later one.
The split cut is permanent for that account, including with the 100% Profit Split add-on.
Your loss limits still apply. The daily loss limit and the maximum drawdown apply at all times. If one of them is reached first, that limit applies.

Stay well clear

Use a stop loss so that the combined risk of everything you have open is below 2% of your initial balance. On a $50,000 account, that is $1,000 across all open positions.
