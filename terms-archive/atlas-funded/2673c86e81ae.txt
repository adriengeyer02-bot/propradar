Rules

Rules at a glance

Which rules close an account, which only remove profit, and which only delay a payout. With the exact trigger for each.

The risk engine checks your account on every price update and every trade. Each rule does exactly one of three things.

#
Rules that close the account

Breaking one of these breaches the account. It stops trading and cannot be recovered, though you can reset it within 7 days.

RULE	TRIGGER	APPLIES TO
Maximum drawdown	Equity below your drawdown floor	Every account
Daily loss limit	Equity below today's daily floor	Every program except Instant Zero, 1-Step Pro and 2-Step Pro. Off on a Daily account bought with the No Daily Loss add-on
Atlas Protector, 2nd trigger	The net floating loss of your open positions reaches 2% of the initial balance a second time	Funded accounts
No stop loss	80% or more of your trades have no stop loss (excessive risk taking)	Funded accounts
Inactivity	30 days without opening a trade	Every program except Instant Zero
#
Rules that remove profit

The account stays open. The profit on the trades involved is deducted from your balance. Losses on those trades stay yours.

RULE	TRIGGER	APPLIES TO
News trading	A trade opened or closed within 5 minutes of a high-impact news event	Funded accounts
Short trades	A trade held for less than 3 minutes	Funded accounts
Margin usage	Margin in use reaches 80% of available margin. All positions are closed.	Funded accounts
Atlas Protector, 1st trigger	The net floating loss of your open positions reaches 2% of the initial balance. All positions are closed and your split drops to 50%.	Funded accounts
#
Rules that only affect payouts
RULE	EFFECT	APPLIES TO
Minimum trading days	Payout waits until you have enough days in the cycle	Funded accounts
Consistency	Payout waits while your best day is too large a share of the cycle's profit	Instant Funded (20%), Access funded (20%)

Evaluation accounts

News, short-trade, margin-usage and Atlas Protector rules only run on funded accounts. In an evaluation, the drawdown, daily limit and inactivity rules can breach you, and so can a prohibited practice. Building good habits early still helps: they all apply from your first funded trade.

#
The one-minute version
Keep equity above your drawdown floor and your daily floor. Both include open trades.
On funded accounts, avoid trades under 3 minutes, trading 5 minutes either side of red-folder news, and letting the net floating loss of your open positions reach 2% of your initial balance. Don't trade without a stop loss: if 80% or more of your trades have none, the account can be breached. A stop on every trade makes all of that easier.
Trade at least once every 30 days.
Collect your qualifying days, then request your payout.
