4 Evaluations and funded accounts

Each model has its own targets and risk limits. The current parameters for every model and account
size are shown live on the pricing table and in your dashboard, and those figures are the ones that
apply to your account.

4.1 Profitable days

Unless your model states otherwise, passing an evaluation requires a minimum of five profitable
trading days. A day counts as profitable when closed trades produce a net gain of at least the

threshold set for your challenge. Open positions do not count until they are closed.

4.2 Drawdown

Daily and maximum drawdown limits apply to every account and are monitored continuously.

Breaching a limit ends the evaluation or the funded account, depending on the stage you are at.

4.3 Getting funded

When you pass, your funded account is set up within 5 business days at the same balance as the
evaluation account. You are expected to trade it broadly the way you traded the evaluation. We
review funded accounts periodically and at random, and a major, unexplained change of strategy
can put the account at risk.

5.2 Gambling and excessive risk, funded accounts

Funded trading is expected to show structured risk control. All-or-nothing positions, trading
habitually without a stop loss, oversized trades relative to balance, and strategies with a binary
outcome by design may be treated as gambling. These are reviewed account by account and we

may warn, intervene or close the account.

5.3 News trading

News trading is allowed during evaluations. On funded accounts it is restricted: profit from trades
opened or closed within five minutes either side of a high-impact, red-folder economic release, as
classified by Forex Factory or an equivalent calendar, may be deducted from that payout cycle. This

is a payout adjustment, not a breach, and it will not fail your account.

5.4 What happens if you breach

Depending on severity we may disqualify the account, suspend it, or withhold outstanding payouts.
Serious or repeated breaches end the account without notice and void profits earned through
them.
