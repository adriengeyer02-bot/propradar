Breaches and bans

Prohibited practices

Strategies and behaviour that are not allowed on any Atlas account, and how they are handled.

Atlas simulates real market conditions. Strategies that only work because the environment is simulated, or that hide who is trading, are not allowed. These are reviewed by the risk team, usually before a payout, and are governed by your funded trader agreement and our terms.

#
Not allowed
Hedging across accounts or firms. Opening opposite positions across Atlas accounts, or between Atlas and another firm, to pass or be paid on one side.
Copy trading with another prop firm. Copying trades from an account at another prop firm to an Atlas account, or from Atlas to another firm, by any tool or by hand. This applies even when both accounts are yours.
Latency or tick arbitrage. Exploiting price-feed delays, stale quotes or tick-level timing.
Account sharing or passing services. Letting anyone else trade your account, or trading someone else's.
Order flooding. Placing large numbers of orders to stress the platform or game execution.
Logging in from several locations to hide who trades. One trader per account.
Exploiting a bug or pricing error. Report it to support instead.
Knowingly false or misleading posts about Atlas Funded. Honest reviews and criticism are always welcome. See Social media conduct.
#
Futures

These apply to every futures account, in addition to the list above.

Copying another person's trades: a trade on the same market and side as someone else's, opened within 60 seconds of theirs and closed within 60 seconds of theirs. Copying between your own accounts is allowed.
Hedging with another person: opposite sides of the same market, opened within 5 minutes of each other and held at the same time for a minute or more, where each trade made or lost at least half its account's max loss and one won while the other lost.
Hedging across your own accounts: a long and a short on the same market open at the same time on two of your accounts.
All-or-nothing trading: 3 or more trades that each made or lost 75% of the max loss, which are also 20% or more of all your futures trades.
Trading on someone else's behalf, or letting someone trade yours.

Trading the same market at the same time as other traders is normal and is not a breach. These rules are aimed at coordinated group trading.

Every trade is checked. One matching trade is noted and nothing happens. Only a pattern holds a payout for review, and a person on our risk team decides.

#
Allowed
Expert Advisors and algorithmic trading, within the rules.
Holding overnight and over the weekend.
Scalping. On CFD funded accounts, hold trades for at least 3 minutes (see Short trades). On futures funded accounts, see the 3-minute rule.
#
What happens

A confirmed prohibited practice can lead to the profit concerned being removed, a payout being declined, or the account being breached and closed, as set out in your agreement. This applies to evaluation and funded accounts alike.
