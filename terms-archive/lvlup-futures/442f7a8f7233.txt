To pass the Evaluation, traders must:Reach the required Profit TargetComplete at least 5 Trading DaysStay within the Maximum DrawdownMeet the applicable 40% Consistency RuleFollow all Trading RulesThere is no second Evaluation phase.Minimum Trading DaysA minimum of 5 separate Trading Days is required during the Evaluation Stage.A Trading Day is counted when at least one trade is executed during that trading session.The 5-day minimum applies to the Evaluation Stage only.Lvlup Funded AccountsLvlup Funded Accounts do not require 5 profitable days before a payout.Payout eligibility follows the 14-Day Payout Cycle, and those trading days do not need to be profitable.Starter Funded AccountsStarter Funded Accounts use a 5 Profitable Trading Day Reward Cycle.A payout becomes eligible after the trader completes 5 profitable Trading Days and meets all other payout requirements.Account InactivityAn evaluation account must record at least one executed trade within every 30-day period to remain active. The inactivity timer cannot be paused at any account stage.Lvlup Account Profit TargetsThe profit target depends on the selected Lvlup Account size:Account SizeProfit Target25K6%, or $1,50050K6%, or $3,000100K7%, or $7,000150K8%, or $12,000Reaching the Profit Target alone does not complete the Evaluation. The trader must also complete the minimum Trading Days, remain within the Drawdown limit and satisfy the 40% Consistency Rule.Starter Account Profit TargetsThe profit target depends on the selected Lvlup Account size:Account SizeProfit Target25K6%, or $1,50050K6%, or $3,000100K7%, or $7,000150K8%, or $12,000Starter traders must also complete the minimum Trading Days, satisfy the Evaluation Consistency Rule and remain within the applicable Drawdown limit.Starter Account DurationStarter Accounts have a fixed 30-day Evaluation period.The 30-day period begins from the trader's first executed trade.Starter Accounts do not renew or rebill after the 30-day period.Lvlup Account DurationLvlup Accounts do not have a fixed Evaluation expiry date.However, the account must remain active.At least one executed trade must be recorded within every 30-day period.This inactivity requirement applies during both the Evaluation and Funded stages of a Lvlup Account.2Drawdown Rules Lvlup Accounts Maximum DrawdownLvlup Accounts use a 5% Maximum Drawdown.Account SizeMaximum Drawdown25K$1,25050K$2,500100K$5,000150K$7,500Reaching or falling below the active Maximum Drawdown threshold results in a Hard Breach.Starter Accounts Maximum DrawdownStarter Accounts use the following Maximum Drawdown limits:Account SizeMaximum Drawdown25K4%, or $1,00050K4%, or $2,000100K3%, or $3,000150K3%, or $4,500Reaching or falling below the active Drawdown threshold results in a Hard Breach.Starter Accounts EOD Trailing DrawdownStarter Accounts also use an End-of-Day Trailing Drawdown.The applicable Drawdown percentage depends on the selected Starter Account size.Lvlup EOD Trailing DrawdownLvlup Accounts use a 5% End-of-Day Trailing Drawdown.The drawdown is calculated from the account's highest End-of-Day balance and updates after the trading day is completed.Intraday profits, losses and unrealized gains do not move the drawdown threshold during the trading session.The drawdown continues trailing the highest End-of-Day balance until the account reaches 6% profit. It then permanently locks at the account's original starting balance.Lvlup Drawdown LockThe Lvlup EOD Trailing Drawdown continues moving until the account reaches 6% profit.At that point, the Drawdown stops trailing and locks at the account's original starting balance.ExampleA 100K Lvlup Account begins with a $95,000 Drawdown threshold.When the closed balance reaches $106,000, the Drawdown locks at $100,000.It does not continue moving higher after the lock.No Daily Loss LimitLvlup Futures Accounts do not use a separate Daily Loss Limit.Traders are not stopped by an independent daily loss threshold.The applicable Maximum Drawdown remains active at all times.No Daily Loss Limit does not mean unlimited loss.3Consistency RulesFailing Consistency Is Not a BreachExceeding the 40% Consistency Rule does not automatically breach or close the account.The trader can continue trading until the Consistency percentage becomes compliant.Consistency ExampleIf the trader's best day is $2,000 and total accumulated profit is $8,000:$2,000 ÷ $8,000 = 25%The trader meets the Consistency Rule.If the trader's best day is $3,500 and total accumulated profit is $8,000:$3,500 ÷ $8,000 = 43.75%The trader does not yet meet the Consistency Rule.The trader can continue trading until total accumulated profit increases enough for the best day to represent 40% or less.Lvlup Funded ConsistencyLvlup Funded Accounts continue to use the 40% Consistency Rule.The account must satisfy the rule when a payout request is submitted.Lvlup Account 40% Consistency RuleLvlup Accounts use a 40% Consistency Rule during both the Evaluation and Funded stages.The trader's most profitable Trading Day cannot represent more than 40% of total accumulated profit.FormulaBest Trading Day ÷ Total Accumulated Profit × 100The result must be 40% or lower.Starter Funded ConsistencyStarter Funded Accounts have no Consistency Rule.Once the Starter Evaluation has been passed, the 40% Consistency requirement no longer applies.Starter Evaluation ConsistencyStarter Accounts use the 40% Consistency Rule during the Evaluation Stage only.The trader's most profitable Evaluation day cannot exceed 40% of total Evaluation profit.4Trading ConditionsNews Trading Is Fully AllowedNews Trading is allowed on both Lvlup Accounts and Starter Accounts, during both Evaluation and Funded stages.There is no 3-minute news restriction before or after scheduled economic news events.Normal risk and prohibited-trading rules continue to apply.Weekend Holding Is Not AllowedPositions cannot be carried over the weekend.All open positions and pending orders must be closed before the permitted weekend trading cutoff.Hedging Within One AccountHedging within the same account is allowed.Traders may hold opposing exposure inside one account as long as the activity remains compliant with all other trading rules.Cross-account hedging remains prohibited.Trading BotsTraders may use their own Trading Bots or automated trading systems.The bot must belong to and be operated for the verified trader's own trading activity.Automation cannot be used to copy or coordinate trades between different traders.Overnight Holding Is Not AllowedPositions cannot remain open overnight.On standard non-holiday trading days, all open positions and pending orders must be closed or cancelled by 15:55 CST.Traders remain responsible for closing their positions before the permitted trading window ends.Trading Session ReopeningAfter the daily close, trading may resume from the 17:00 CST CME Globex open.Holiday Trading ResponsibilityTrading hours may change during holidays or shortened exchange sessions.Traders are responsible for checking the applicable market schedule and closing positions before the required market close.Minimum Trade DurationThere is no minimum trade duration.Trades are not required to remain open for a minimum number of seconds or minutes.High-Frequency Trading restrictions still apply.High-Frequency TradingHigh-Frequency Trading bots are not allowed.Strategies or automated systems designed to generate extremely high-frequency order activity are prohibited.5Contracts and Account LimitsE-mini and Micro ConversionLvlup uses a 1:10 conversion ratio.1 E-mini Contract = 10 Micro ContractsFor example, a 50K Account may trade:3 E-mini Contracts30
