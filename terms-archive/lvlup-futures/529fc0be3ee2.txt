It provides structured evaluation programs through which traders can demonstrate their performance and qualify for simulated funded accounts.Lvlup offers two separate account structures, Lvlup Accounts and Starter Accounts, across four account sizes.Is Lvlup Futures a registered company?Yes. After passing, the trader completes KYC and pays the required activation fee before the funded account becomes active.Starter AccountsOne-Time Fee evaluations with no monthly subscription and no activation fee after passing.What account sizes are available?Both Lvlup Accounts and Starter Accounts are available in:$25,000$50,000$100,000$150,000The targets, drawdown limits, contract allowances and payout conditions depend on the selected account type and size.What is a Lvlup Account?A Lvlup Account is the main flexible account structure.Traders can choose between Monthly and One-Time evaluation payment options. Funded Lvlup Accounts offer an 80% base profit split and biweekly payout access under the applicable conditions.What is a Starter Account?A Starter Account is a One-Time Fee evaluation designed for traders who prefer a simpler payment structure.Starter Accounts have no monthly evaluation subscription, no activation fee after passing and a 100% funded profit split, subject to their separate payout conditions.What are the current Starter Account prices?Starter Accounts start at the following One-Time Fees (the price depends on the trading platform):Account size One-Time Fee25K - $12950K - $194100K - $259150K - $324Which account type should I choose?Choose a Lvlup Account when you want flexible Monthly or One-Time evaluation payment options and a structure designed for ongoing funded progression.Choose a Starter Account when you prefer a single upfront fee, no activation fee and a 100% profit split.Review the Evaluation comparison before selecting an account because each structure has different funded and payout conditions.What happens after I pass a Lvlup evaluation?After passing, you must complete identity verification and pay the required activation fee before the funded Lvlup Account becomes active.The activation payment must be completed within 30 days of passing.What happens after I pass a Starter evaluation?After passing, complete the required identity-verification and account-review process.Starter Accounts do not require an additional activation fee before funded access.3Billing & PaymentsHow does the Lvlup monthly payment option work?The monthly payment applies only while the evaluation remains active.Billing stops when the evaluation is passed, breached or cancelled. It does not continue as a monthly funded-account subscription.How does the Lvlup One-Time payment option work?The trader pays once for access to the Lvlup evaluation instead of using a recurring monthly subscription.After passing, the required activation fee must still be paid before the funded account becomes active.How does Starter Account billing work?Starter Accounts are available through a One-Time Fee only.There is no monthly Starter subscription and no separate activation fee after passing.Is there an activation fee?Lvlup Accounts require an activation fee after the evaluation is passed.Starter Accounts do not have an activation fee.The applicable Lvlup activation amount is shown in the external dashboard before payment.Are there monthly costs after I become funded?No ongoing monthly evaluation charge applies after a Lvlup Account is passed and activated.Starter Accounts also have no funded monthly subscription.Can I cancel a monthly evaluation?Yes. An active monthly Lvlup evaluation can be cancelled through the dashboard.You will retain access until the end of the current paid billing period, and no further monthly renewal will be charged.Can I cancel a One-Time evaluation?A One-Time evaluation does not have a recurring subscription to cancel.The account remains available according to its evaluation lifecycle unless it expires, becomes inactive or is breached.Can I apply a discount code?Available promotional or affiliate codes can be entered inside the external dashboard.The dashboard will show the adjusted price before payment.Can multiple discount codes be combined?Discount-code stacking depends on the promotion and dashboard rules active at checkout.The dashboard will automatically apply only the discounts that are eligible for the selected account.What payment methods are available?Available payment methods are displayed in the external dashboard during checkout.Payment availability can vary based on location, account type and current payment-provider support.Can I request a refund?Refund eligibility depends on the account status, payment method and applicable purchase terms.Once trading activity has started, the evaluation is generally not eligible for a standard refund. Contact support before placing a trade if you believe a payment was made incorrectly.4DashboardWhat happens when I press Proceed?Pressing Proceed redirects you from the public website to the external Lvlup dashboard.Your selected account type and size should carry into the dashboard, where you can sign in, register and complete the remaining configuration.Do I need a dashboard account before selecting an evaluation?No. You can review account options on the public website before creating a dashboard account.You will be asked to sign in or register when you continue through the Proceed button.What is completed inside the dashboard?The external dashboard handles:Account configurationPlatform selectionMonthly or One-Time payment selectionDiscount codesOptional add-onsPaymentSubscription managementKYCActivation paymentsFunded-account accessPayout requestsAffiliate trackingCan I change my selected configuration in the dashboard?The dashboard may allow you to review or adjust available options before payment, including platform, payment type and eligible add-ons.The final order summary should always be reviewed before completing checkout.Where can I view my account status?Your dashboard displays your active evaluations, funded accounts, payment status and other account information.Account credentials and platform-access details are also delivered through the dashboard or associated account communications.Where do I manage my monthly subscription?Monthly evaluation subscriptions can be reviewed and cancelled from the relevant dashboard billing section.Cancellation prevents the next renewal while maintaining access until the current paid period ends.Where do I pay the Lvlup activation fee?The activation payment is completed inside the external dashboard after the Lvlup evaluation has been passed.The funded account becomes available after the required payment, verification and account review are completed.5PlatformsWhich trading platforms are available?Lvlup Futures currently supports:TickblazeVolumetricaPlatform availability is shown in the external dashboard during account configuration.When do I choose my trading platform?The trading platform is selected inside the external dashboard before the account purchase is completed.Does pricing change by platform?Lvlup Account pricing may vary between Tickblaze and Volumetrica.The final price for the selected account, size, payment option and platform is shown in the dashboard before payment.What is Volumetrica?Volumetrica is a futures trading and market-analysis environment offering professional charting, order-flow tools and execution functionality.Availability and pricing depend on the selected account.What is Tickblaze?Tickblaze is a trading platform used to access supported futures accounts and manage trades through its available desktop or web environment.What is the Lvlup Trading Terminal?The Lvlup Trading Terminal is a proprietary trading environment currently in development.It is planned as a future addition to the Lvlup ecosystem and is not yet available for live account selection.Can I join the Lvlup Trading Terminal waitlist?A waitlist may be made available before launch.When active, the website will provide a dedicated registration form for product updates and early-access announcements.6Verification & PayoutsWhat is KYC?KYC means Know Your Customer. It is an identity-verification process used to confirm that an account belongs to the registered trader.KYC helps protect accounts, payouts and the integrity of the program.Which KYC provider does Lvlup Futures use?Lvlup Futures uses Sumsub for identity verification.When do I complete KYC?KYC is normally completed after passing the evaluation and before funded-account activation or payout access.Additional verification may be requested when required for account security or compliance.What documents may be required?Sumsub may request a government-issued identity document and additional verification information.The exact requirements depend on the trader's country and the verification checks required for the account.What payout methods are available?Approved payouts are currently supported through:RiseCryptoAvailable options may depend on location and compliance eligibility.How long does payout processing take?Approved Lvlup payout requests are generally processed within 24 to 48 hours after review.This processing period is separate from the account's payout-eligibility schedule.Can someone else receive my payout?No. Payouts must be made to the verified account holder through an approved payout method.Account ownership, KYC information and payout details must belong to the same person.Why can a payout require additional review?A request may be reviewed for identity verification, account-rule compliance, trading activity, payout eligibility and payment-provider requirements.Additional review helps ensure that the payout is sent securely to the correct trader.7Add-OnsWhat add-ons are currently available?Payout Protector is the primary optional account enhancement currently offered by Lvlup Futures.Available add-ons are shown during account configuration in the external dashboard.What is Payout Protector?Payout Protector can protect eligible funded gains if a covered funded Lvlup Account experiences a qualifying hard breach.The account still closes after the breach, but eligible gains may remain payable under the applicable conditions.How much does Payout Protector cost?Payout Protector adds 25% to the selected eligible account price.Is Payout Protector required?No. It is an optional enhancement.An account can be purchased and traded without adding Payout Protector.When can I add Payout Protector?Payout Protector must be selected during the eligible account-purchase process.It cannot be added after the account has already experienced a breach.Does Payout Protector prevent my account from breaching?No. Payout Protector does not increase the drawdown limit, remove trading conditions or keep a breached account active.It only affects the treatment of eligible funded gains after a qualifying breach.Is Payout Protector available for every account type?Availability is shown in the external dashboard during account configuration.Only account types and purchases marked as eligible should display the Payout Protector option.8Account BreachWhat happens if I breach my account?A breach closes the affected account and prevents further trading activity through it.The dashboard will display the account status after the breach has been recorded.Can I reset a breached account?No. Breached accounts cannot be reset, restored or reopened.A trader who wants to continue must purchase a new evaluation.Can I reactivate a breached account?No. A breached account cannot be reactivated by paying an additional fee.A new evaluation must be started.What happens to a monthly subscription after a breach?Monthly evaluation billing stops after the account has been breached.The breached account cannot be used for the remaining billing period.Can I purchase another account after a breach?Yes, provided your account remains eligible and you continue to comply with the program's account and allocation requirements.What happens to funded gains after a breach?Eligible gains in a breached funded Lvlup Account may be protected when valid Payout Protector coverage applies.Without applicable protection, remaining gains may become ineligible under the funded-account terms.9Lvlup EliteWhat is Lvlup Elite?Lvlup Elite is a private recognition tier for traders who demonstrate verified performance over time.It is designed to offer recognition, member credentials, events, experiences and rewards beyond the standard funded journey.Can I purchase Lvlup Elite membership?No. Elite membership cannot be purchased through a fee or subscription.It must be earned through verified trading achievements.How do I qualify for Lvlup Elite?A trader qualifies by accumulating 10,000 verified Elite points.The 10,000 entry points are redeemed when membership begins. Any points above the entry requirement remain in the member's balance.How are Elite points earned?Elite points can be earned through:1 point per $1 paid out by Lvlup Futures0.1 points per $1 from an approved external futures payout200 points for a passed Lvlup Account50 points for a verified passed account at another futures firmExternal achievements are subject to verification.How long does Elite membership last?Elite membership remains active for 12 months from the member's activation date.Members must requalify through verified performance to maintain membership for another annual period.What benefits do Elite members receive?Benefits can include:Digital and physical Elite member cardsPrivate member eventsExclusive trips and experiencesProfile recognitionAchievement credentialsAccess to the Elite progression systemThe ability to use points toward eligible experiencesWhat is one Elite point worth?For eligible Elite events and experiences:1 Elite Point = $1Points may be used to cover an experience in full or applied as a partial discount.Do unused points remain after joining?The 10,000 points required for entry are redeemed when membership begins.Any verified balance above 10,000 remains available in the member's account.Do external payouts count toward Elite?Yes. Approved payouts from other futures prop firms can contribute Elite points after valid proof has been submitted and approved.10Affiliate ProgramHow does the Lvlup Affiliate Program work?Approved affiliates receive a unique referral link or discount code.When an eligible customer completes a qualifying purchase using that referral, the commission is recorded in the affiliate dashboard.How do I apply?Complete the Affiliate Application with your personal or business information, audience details and intended promotional methods.Applications are reviewed before affiliate access and codes are activated.How much commission do affiliates earn?Approved affiliates earn 10% commission on eligible:Monthly evaluation paymentsOne-Time evaluation paymentsActivation feesCommission is tracked through the affiliate dashboard.When are affiliate commissions paid?Affiliate payouts are available every 14 days after the applicable verification and approval process.Which affiliate payout methods are available?Affiliate payouts are supported through:CryptoRiseKYC must be completed before the first payout can be processed.Is there a minimum affiliate payout?No minimum affiliate payout amount currently applies.Are there affiliate payout fees?Lvlup Futures does not charge an internal affiliate payout fee.External payment providers may apply their own network or processing costs.Can I request a custom discount code?Yes. Affiliates can request a preferred custom code during the application or approval process.All codes remain subject to availability, review and activation.What can I see in the affiliate dashboard?The affiliate dashboard can display:Referral activityQualifying purchasesCommission earnedPayout historyAvailable commission balanceAffiliate rewards and progressWhat is Path to Retainer?Path to Retainer is a performance-based affiliate progression opportunity.An affiliate who generates at least $3,000 in commission for two consecutive months may qualify for a fixed $5,000 monthly retainer, paid in addition to ongoing commission earnings.Eligibility remains subject to performance review and the applicable agreement.How often is affiliate performance reviewed?Affiliate performance is reviewed every 14 days.Reviews may consider referral quality, sales performance, compliance, promotional conduct and continued program eligibility.Need More Help?Still Have Questions?Get assistance with account access, payments, verification, platforms or dashboard issues through the appropriate support channel.Full NameEmail AddressEnquiry TypeSelect hereAccount AccessVerification & PayoutsBilling & PaymentsPlatforms & TradingAccount TypesLvlup EliteAffiliate ProgramOtherMessageSend Messageinfo@lvlupfutures.comFor General EnquiriesAm Langenwall 1 18439 Stralsund, GermanyRegistered OfficeA refined structure for futures evaluations, built around clarity and discipline.LVLUP One UG (haftungsbeschränkt)Am Langenwall 118439 StralsundGermanyCommercial Register: HRB 23184Register Court: Local Court of StralsundAuthorized Representative: Lennard WenderholmVAT ID: DE461792748Phone: +49 155 661 133 88Email: info@lvlupfutures.comAffiliate and Liability DisclaimerLVLUP One UG is an affiliate of Prop Account, LLC.
