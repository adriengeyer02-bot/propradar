Only the balance is used for this calculation: unrealised gains and losses on open positions do not raise the floor.During the day, your equity (balance + unrealised gains/losses) must never touch this floor: if it does, the rule is breached. Unlike an intraday drawdown, the floor does not adjust in real time, which provides more flexibility in managing positions.The 1 Step Challenge drawdown trails on an End of Day (EOD) basis, both during the challenge and on the WGF account, and it is not capped at the initial capital: it keeps following your balance upwards, with no limit.After a withdrawal: the new starting point of the floor is your balance right after the withdrawal minus 4% of the initial capital, and the floor can never be below the initial capital. It then keeps following your new end-of-day highs. As the Extended Drawdown option ends with the first withdrawal, the gap is always 4% after a withdrawal.Example on a WGF account: Initial capital: $100,000. Your starting floor is $96,000.
