How drawdowns work

- Daily loss: The authorised daily loss is a maximum amount that you can lose in one trading day on your account. This limit is set to help you manage risk and avoid excessive losses over a short period. For example, if the authorised daily loss is 5% on a virtual capital of $100,000, you cannot lose more than $5,000 in a single day. The daily reset occurs at 00:00 GMT: the daily drawdown automatically resets every day at 00:00 GMT. At that precise moment, the account equity value (balance + floating gains/losses) is recorded. The new daily loss limit is calculated from this equity.

- Max Drawdown: The maximum drawdown is the total amount you can lose relative to your account's initial balance. Unlike the daily drawdown which resets every day, the maximum drawdown applies to your entire trading period. For example, if the maximum drawdown is 10% on a virtual capital of $100,000, your account balance must never drop below $90,000. After a withdrawal, this threshold remains fixed at the initial balance and does not recalculate.

How the End of Day (EOD) drawdown works – Challenge Phase
The EOD drawdown corresponds to the maximum allowed loss. The loss threshold (floor) is set at 4% of the initial capital below the highest end-of-day balance recorded on the account. It is recalculated every day at 00:00 UTC (GMT) on the basis of the closing balance of the day just ended, and can only move up, never down. Unrealised gains and losses on open positions are not taken into account for this recalculation. During the day, the account equity must never reach this threshold: any contact between the equity and the threshold constitutes a breach of the rule. Unlike an intraday drawdown, this limit does not adjust in real time. In the 1 Step Challenge, the drawdown trails on an End of Day (EOD) basis with no cap: it is not locked at the initial capital and keeps following the balance upwards, at a constant distance of 4% of the initial capital (increased, where applicable, by the points of the Extended Drawdown option).

Example: Initial capital: $100,000. The starting threshold is $96,000. You close a day with a balance of $103,000: at 00:00 UTC, the threshold moves up to $99,000. If your closing balance then reaches $105,000, it moves up to $101,000; if your balance falls back, the threshold stays at $101,000.

How the drawdown works

It is recalculated every day at 00:00 UTC (GMT) on the basis of the closing balance of the day just ended, and can only move up, never down. After each withdrawal, the threshold is recalculated: its new starting point equals the account balance immediately after the withdrawal, less 4% of the initial capital, and it may never be lower than the initial capital. It then keeps following the end-of-day balance under the same rules. If you withdraw $3,000 while your balance is $106,000, your balance drops to $103,000; the new threshold would be $103,000 − $4,000 = $99,000, so it is set at the initial capital, i.e. $100,000. It will move up again as soon as your end-of-day balance exceeds $104,000.

18.6 – LIVE Programme
18.6.1 – Activation and Discretion
Every WGF trader is subject to the LIVE Programme. The progression of a trader from a simulated environment to the LIVE environment operated via WGF's partner broker is at the sole discretion of the prop firm. Access to the LIVE Programme remains conditional upon continuous compliance with identification and compliance obligations.
Upon activation of the LIVE Programme, any validated and eligible reward amount (hereinafter the 'Eligible Reward') is set at one hundred and ten per cent (110%) of the Eligible Reward. The corresponding amount is referred to as the 'Enhanced Reward'.
Enhanced Reward = Eligible Reward x 110%

18.6.2 – Distribution of the Enhanced Reward
From the activation of the LIVE Programme, the Enhanced Reward is distributed as follows:
a) Immediate payment to the trader: Twenty per cent (20%) of the Enhanced Reward is paid to the trader via the payment method validated by WGF.
b) Initial credit to the LIVE account: Thirty per cent (30%) of the Enhanced Reward is allocated to the trader's LIVE account.
c) Progressive unlocking by performance milestones: The remaining fifty per cent (50%) of the Enhanced Reward is unlocked as additional credits to the LIVE account under the following conditions:
First milestone: Allocation of twenty-five per cent (25%) of the Enhanced Reward when the trader achieves a positive performance equivalent to one per cent (1%) of the nominal initial capital.
Second milestone: Allocation of twenty-five per cent (25%) of the Enhanced Reward when the trader achieves a positive performance equivalent to two per cent (2%) of the nominal initial capital (from the first milestone reached).
The nominal initial capital corresponds to the contractual account size (e.g. 50,000 / 100,000 / 200,000 units), regardless of equity variations. Upon completion of the second milestone, the entirety of the Enhanced Reward is deemed to have been allocated.

18.6.3 – Additional Performance Bonus
Subject to continuous compliance with the LIVE Programme conditions, when a trader achieves a positive performance equivalent to three per cent (3%) of the nominal initial capital from the first 2 milestones reached on their LIVE account, WGF shall pay a bonus. This bonus is repeatable at every +3% milestone as long as the Live account is active. This bonus corresponds to ten per cent (10%) of the 110% credited to the LIVE account. This bonus is deposited into your LIVE account.

18.6.4 – Profit Split Applicable in LIVE
Under the LIVE Programme, the performance achieved on the LIVE account is subject to a profit split of eighty per cent (80%) in favour of the trader and twenty per cent (20%) in favour of WGF, unless otherwise stated in writing. This profit split applies exclusively to net validated profits calculated according to WGF's internal methods.

18.6.5 – General Provisions
The performances used as a basis for calculating the milestones (1%, 2%, 3%) are assessed exclusively on the LIVE account according to WGF's internal calculation methods.
