1
Profit Target and program phases
The program has four Evaluation phases. A 5% Profit Target must be reached in each phase. Profit is counted after all positions are closed and the result is credited to the account balance.
2
Time limit
There is no deadline for activating the account or reaching the Profit Target. The trader may progress at a pace that suits the trader's strategy.
3
Minimum number of real trades
At least 4 real trades must be completed in every applicable Evaluation phase. The requirement refers to independent trading ideas, not merely the number of opened positions.
How one real trade is counted
One real trade means one complete and independent trading idea based on a specific market movement or a new independent market setup.
If a trader opens multiple entries in the same direction during one market move, splits one intended position into several smaller parts, uses a multi-step or three-leg structure, or distributes the same idea across correlated currency pairs or other markets, the complete structure is still counted as one real trade.
Small complementary positions, repeated entries without a new trading idea, artificial volume splitting and orders opened only to increase the trade count are not separate real trades. This type of structuring may also indicate external hedging, hedging between accounts or another prohibited strategy.
Still one real trade
three gradual EURUSD buy entries during the same market move,
one intended position divided into several smaller entries,
EURUSD and GBPUSD positions based on the same USD weakness idea,
adding to the same position without a new independent setup.
A new independent trade
the previous idea is completed and a new independent setup appears,
the trade is based on a different market movement or a different reason for entry,
the order is not merely a continuation or volume split of the previous idea.
Important: The account may be closed when the Profit Target is reached. The required number of real trades must therefore be completed before the target is reached. A manual review may take place after the evaluation. If the requirement is not met, the trader may be required to repeat the relevant phase.
4
Maximum Overall Drawdown
The maximum Overall Drawdown is 10% of the initial account size. It is fixed and includes both closed results and floating profit or loss.
Example: On a 10,000 USD account, equity must not fall below 9,000 USD.
Breach: Open positions are closed automatically, the account is switched to read-only mode and the affected Evaluation phase or RCF account is terminated. Trading cannot continue on the following day and no reward may be requested from a terminated RCF account.
5
Maximum Daily Drawdown
The Daily Drawdown is 5% during Evaluation and 5% on the RCF account.
The daily starting equity is recorded at the liquidity provider's daily rollover at 00:00 chart time. Chart time operates at UTC+3 during daylight saving time and UTC+2 during standard time. The reset therefore occurs at 21:00 UTC in summer and 22:00 UTC in winter. The trading server itself may display UTC.
Calculation: The limit is measured from the daily starting equity snapshot, including open profit and loss. At a starting equity of 10,000 USD, a 5% Daily Drawdown creates an intraday equity floor of 9,500 USD.
Breach: Open positions are closed automatically, the account is switched to read-only mode and the affected Evaluation phase or RCF account is terminated. Trading cannot continue after the next reset, and no reward may be requested from a terminated RCF account.

7
Instruments, holding and trade duration
Traders may use instruments currently available in RF-Trader, including Forex, metals, selected stock indices, energies, stocks and cryptocurrencies.
Every paid Evaluation trade used as part of a trading strategy must remain open for at least 30 seconds. A single accidental close may be reviewed in context, but repeated or deliberate sub-30-second trading is not allowed.
News trading is allowed during paid Evaluation. Traders remain responsible for spread widening, gaps, slippage and compliance with prohibited-practice rules.
Overnight holding and weekend holding are allowed on Evaluation and RCF accounts.
No new trade or pending order may be opened or activated during the final 5 minutes before the relevant market closes.
Existing positions may be closed or held through the market closure.
Cryptocurrency positions may be held over the weekend, but new cryptocurrency trading is unavailable during the weekend.
Swap-free conditions apply to 28 FX pairs and metals. See the current list on the Trading Instruments page.
Execution risk: Spreads may widen and gaps or slippage may occur during news, low liquidity, rollover, weekends and market openings. Protective orders, including Stop Loss, do not guarantee execution at the requested price.
Current instruments, trading hours and holiday changes are available in RF-Trader and on the Trading Hours and Holidays page.
8
Completion, RCF and rewards
Successful completion of the Evaluation makes the trader eligible for a separate RCF assessment. An RCF account is not granted automatically. Access requires account review, KYC/AML and sanctions screening, jurisdiction checks and approval. Before RCF cooperation begins, the trader, RIFM, s.r.o. and FRCSM, s.r.o. must enter into a separate RCF Account Contract.
Evaluation services are provided by RIFM, s.r.o. RCF accounts are fully simulated accounts using real market quotations and are not live brokerage accounts.
First reward request
Not earlier than 14 calendar days after the first trade on the relevant RCF account.
Minimum payout
The minimum eligible RCF reward request is 50 USD.
Before requesting
All open positions must be closed before the reward request is submitted.
Reward split
80% initially, with the possibility of an increase up to 90%.
Program fee refund bonus: A 200% program fee refund bonus is included with the first eligible RCF reward.
After an unsuccessful Evaluation attempt, the Achievements system in the Client Zone may unlock a personalized discount for another RF program. The amount, availability and validity are generated dynamically and are not guaranteed as a fixed percentage.
Under the standard scaling plan, a trader may request a 25% increase of the original account value after generating at least 15% aggregate profit during 3 consecutive months, with at least two profitable months.
The largest available individual Evaluation account across RF programs is 320,000 USD; available account sizes differ by program. A trader may hold multiple RCF accounts, but the maximum aggregate active RCF allocation is 640,000 USD across all RCF accounts and all scaled allocations combined.
9
RCF risk management and news
MANDATORY RULE
Maximum risk of 1.5%
The risk attributable to one trade or one group of related trades must not exceed 1.5% of the RCF account balance recorded when the trade, or the first trade in the related group, was opened.
When no Stop Loss is used, the rule is assessed using the highest floating or realized loss and other available data relating to the complete trading idea.
RECOMMENDATION
Normal risk around 0.5%
For sustainable risk management, normal risk close to 0.5% per trade or related group is strongly recommended. Stop Loss and Take Profit are recommended but are not mandatory.
MANDATORY RULE
Minimum 60 seconds
Every trade opened on an RCF account must remain open for at least 60 seconds. There is no minimum number of trades required before an RCF reward request.
MANDATORY RULE
High-impact news
No new trade or pending order may be opened or activated during the 5 minutes immediately before a red high-impact event shown in the TradingView economic calendar integrated in RF-Trader or the Client Zone.
Existing positions may be held, managed or closed. There is no fixed post-release restriction.
A group of related trades may include split entries, gradual additions to the same idea, positions on the same symbol or strongly correlated instruments, and trades structured to bypass the risk limit. Deliberate exploitation of news gaps, delayed pricing, hedging or technical reactions remains prohibited.
10
Prohibited practices and account use
Personal use of the account
The registered trader must trade the account personally. Account sharing, trading for another person, allowing another person to trade the account and account-management services are prohibited.
Automation
Expert Advisors, automated trading robots and other systems that place or manage trades automatically are not supported on paid Evaluation or RCF accounts.
coordinated, bilateral or multilateral hedging between accounts, persons or other firms,
external hedging or opening opposite positions to bypass the rules,
use of delayed, external or manipulated price feeds,
latency, price, gap, swap or other forms of arbitrage and exploitation of technical errors,
martingale, one-sided position multiplication, excessive exposure or gambling-style trading,
artificial splitting of trades, volumes or related positions to bypass trade-count or risk rules,
any fraudulent, manipulative or prohibited conduct described in the Terms & Conditions.
Reasonable manual trading styles are allowed when they comply with the selected Program Rules, the applicable RCF rules and the Terms & Conditions.
