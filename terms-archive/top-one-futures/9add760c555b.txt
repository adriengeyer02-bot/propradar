Understanding the Profit Split on Sim Funded Accounts

How the 90/10 profit split works on all Sim Funded accounts and where to track your earnings and payouts.

October 2, 2026

All Sim Funded accounts at Top One Futures use the same 90/10 profit split. This article explains how the split works, whether it can be increased, and where you can track your earnings.

 

How does the profit split work?

Our profit split structure is designed to maximize your earnings as a trader:

Share

	

Who receives it




90%

	

You keep 90% of your payout amount




10%

	

The remaining 10% stays with the firm

Can I increase my profit split percentage?

We do not currently offer a way to increase it further, but this structure ensures that traders receive the majority of their earnings.

 

Where can I track my earnings and payouts?

You can view your profit split details and payout history in your Top One Futures dashboard for real-time tracking.
