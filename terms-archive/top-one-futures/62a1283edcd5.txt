Elite ACCESS – Payout Requirements

Everything you need before you can request a payout on a funded Elite ACCESS account, in one place.

October 1, 2026

Elite ACCESS funded accounts offer on-demand payouts, as long as all payout conditions are met. To request a payout you must satisfy all requirements below: the 40% consistency rule, the buffer and profit requirement, the minimum profitable days and the daily progression rule.

Payout buffer and profit requirement

Before requesting a payout, your account must reach the required profit level: the buffer plus $500.

The buffer must remain in the account at all times.

You can only withdraw profits above the required profit level.

Each payout request must meet this threshold.

Minimum profitable days

You need a minimum of 5 profitable trading days before each payout. A day only counts when it reaches the minimum daily profit for your account size (see the table above).

 

Consistency rule (40%)

Your largest winning day must not exceed 40% of your total profits in your current payout cycle. If this rule is not yet met, the payout cannot be requested yet and you may continue trading until compliant.

 

After a payout is taken, your total profits reset and the consistency rule starts again based on new profits moving forward. This ensures that traders demonstrate steady performance.

 

50% profit progression rule

For each payout request, at least 50% of the requested payout amount must come from profits generated since your last payout. There is no restriction on your first payout; from the second payout onward, at least 50% must come from new profits.

 

Profit split

90% of the payout goes to the trader and 10% to the firm.

 

Path to Live

Your payouts also count toward moving from a simulated funded account to a live funded account.

3 successful payouts are required before you become eligible for the transition.

Once you reach this milestone, a risk manager reviews your trading performance.

If approved, your account is transitioned to live, where you trade with real capital.

Only one account transitions to live. If you hold multiple accounts, one account moves over and your remaining accounts are closed at that point. For full details, see the Path to Trading Live article.

Checklist before you request a payout: 5 profitable days, balance above buffer + $500, largest day at most 40% of total profit, at least 50% new profit since your last payout, and a request of at least $500.
