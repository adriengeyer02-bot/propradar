 Getting Funded Process

 The funding process takes 3 business days after your approval.  After completing the Evaluation phase, we submit your results to our funding partners, and upon approval, you will receive and complete the trading contract and other documents required by our funding partners. Within a short period, the account will be created and you will receive your account login credentials and be enabled to trade your Prop Account.

 Pass the Evaluation.
 Complete the Trader Questionnaire.
 Complete the Trader Contract.
 Get funded with our Prop partners.

You are required to submit any valid form of government-issued ID (National Identification Card/Passport/Drivers license/Etc.) Please ensure that it has a valid expiration date and clearly shows your Date of Birth.

NB: You will be required to pay an activation fee once you have passed the evaluation: 

 25k Account: $65 activation fee once approved
 50k Account: $75 activation fee once approved
 Other account sizes follow the same split-payment structure (50% at sign-up and 50% as an activation fee once approved).

 The activation fee is charged automatically when you are approved for funding. If the payment fails, you’ll have 7 days to complete the payment, with three email reminders sent during that time. If the fee is not paid within 7 days, your account will be rolled back to evaluation status.
