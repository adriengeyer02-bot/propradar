Profit Info and Withdrawals
How are my profits taxed?

Funded traders are considered independent contractors, and your share of the trading profits is considered ordinary income. Please consult your country’s laws for matters of taxation.

Profit Split

Receive a generous  90% profit split, with the majority going to the trader. Funded traders keep 100% of their first $10,000 of profits and 90% of the net profits above the withdrawal profit threshold thereafter.

Withdrawal Threshold

In order to allow a true chance for success, limit your risk of drawdowns during withdrawals, and secure the longevity of your trading account, traders must adhere to the withdrawal thresholds to be eligible for withdrawals.

On day 5, he accumulates $6,500 in profits on the account; the trader will be eligible for a withdrawal amount of $3,000 since the threshold for that account size is $3,500.

On day 8, the account balance is now at $109,500; the trader will be eligible to withdraw an amount of $6,000 above the threshold value of $3,500.

Withdrawal Process

To request a profit withdrawal, all you have to do is send an email to your funding provider with the amount you wish to withdraw and the specific method you want to receive your funds. It will be processed on the same day as long as the withdrawal threshold has been met. It's that straightforward!

Withdrawal requests are subject to a minimum transfer amount of $1,000 of the trader's profit share. Withdrawals can be processed via Bank Wire or Cryptocurrency. You may request a withdrawal any time between Monday and Friday. 

Bank wire withdrawals are processed within 24 business hours once approved but may post to your account within 1-3 business days depending on your banking institution (Refer to your banking institution for posting times).

Withdrawals Frequency and Priority Processing Fees

Funded traders are entitled to one withdrawal per calendar week at no additional processing fee, provided all withdrawal eligibility requirements are met.

Traders who wish to request additional withdrawals within the same calendar week may do so through our Priority Withdrawal service, subject to an additional processing fee of 10% of the withdrawal amount.

How it works:

First weekly withdrawal: No additional processing fee.
Second or subsequent weekly withdrawal: A 10% Priority Withdrawal processing fee applies to each additional withdrawal.
Weekly reset: The withdrawal allowance resets every Monday at 12:00 AM Central Time (CT). A calendar week runs from Monday through Sunday.
Multiple funded accounts: The weekly withdrawal allowance applies per trader, across all funded accounts associated with that trader.

The Priority Withdrawal fee is separate from your existing profit-sharing arrangement and any applicable third-party transfer fees. The exact fee and expected payment amount will be displayed before your withdrawal request is confirmed.

There shall be no restriction on the number of eligible bank wire withdrawal requests that TRADER may submit within a calendar week; provided, however, that the second and each subsequent eligible bank wire withdrawal within the same calendar week shall be subject to the Priority Withdrawal processing fee.

Withdrawal Calculation Examples (90% Profit Split)

Example 1: First Weekly Withdrawal (No Additional Processing Fee)

Withdrawal Request: $4,000

Trader's Share: 90% × $4,000 = $3,600

Funding Partner Share: 10% × $4,000 = $400

Priority Processing Fee: $0

Total Trader Receives: $3,600

Example 2: Second Withdrawal in the Same Week (10% Priority Processing Fee)

Withdrawal Request: $4,000

Trader's Share: 90% × $4,000 = $3,600

Funding Partner Share: 10% × $4,000 = $400

Priority Processing Fee: 10% × $3,600 = $360

Total Trader Receives: $3,240

The Priority Withdrawal processing fee applies only to the trader's profit share and is deducted after the standard profit split has been calculated. Other applicable third-party transfer fees are not included in these examples.

Funded Traders may avoid the additional 10% Priority Withdrawal processing fee by combining their withdrawals into a single request per calendar week.

Cryptocurrency Withdrawals

Cryptocurrency withdrawal requests are limited to one (1) per calendar week, with a maximum of Three Thousand United States Dollars ($3,000) per request. Any withdrawal amount exceeding $3,000 is not eligible for cryptocurrency processing and must be submitted as a bank wire transfer request.

Cryptocurrency withdrawals may take up to fourteen (14) business days to complete from the date of approval, depending on network conditions and third-party payment processor timelines. To request a withdrawal, simply send an email to your funding provider with the following information:

Wallet Address
Crypto Name (BTC, ETH, or USDT)
Network Type (Ethereum, Tron, BSC)
Withdrawal Amount

You can request a profit withdrawal Monday through Friday, and it will be processed within 24 business hours. Please note that crypto payments will incur a 5% fee charged by the payment processor, and any other exchange conversion fees will be borne by the TRADER.

IMPORTANT NOTE!

Please make sure to select the correct network type of the crypto you are requesting payment for; otherwise, funds will be lost. Crypto transactions cannot be reversed or canceled once sent due to the irreversible nature of blockchain transfers.

OneUp Trader and its funding partners are not responsible and will not be held liable for any mistakes you make while providing your Wallet Address and specifying the network type to process your withdrawals. You are solely responsible for providing the correct information and managing your crypto account and its access.
