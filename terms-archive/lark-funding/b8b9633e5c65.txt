Can I use a VPN?
Can I use a VPN?
October 3, 2026

As per our Terms and Conditions, all traders participating in our challenge and funded programs must be the sole traders of their accounts. For this reason, we strongly discourage accessing your trading account via VPN or VPS. This is a common practice for services that do not allow multiple users on a single account.

 

It is important to note that you are allowed to use a VPN or VPS, but please be aware that if any suspicious activity is found on your account, the use of these tools will be a significant factor in determining whether or not you have violated our terms and conditions.

 

If you choose to use a VPN or VPS, please make sure that you are not violating any of our terms and conditions, such as having multiple users trading on your account or using any type of prohibited automated trading system.

 

Additionally, please note that you are not allowed to connect to a VPN or VPS during the KYC/AML verification process. If you are found to have completed the KYC/AML process using a VPS or VPN, your account may not be funded or your funded account may be disabled or breached.
