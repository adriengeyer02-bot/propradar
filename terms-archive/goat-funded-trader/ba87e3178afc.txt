Step 1 (Evaluation)Copy link
To pass Step 1, you must:
Achieve a 10% profit target.Complete at least 3 profitable days.Follow all trading rules and loss limits.
After passing Step 1, you automatically proceed to Step 2.
Step 2 (Evaluation)Copy link
To pass Step 2, you must:
Achieve a 5% profit target.Complete at least 3 profitable days.Follow all trading rules and loss limits.
After passing Step 2, your entire evaluation is reviewed. If successful, you become a Funded GOAT Trader.
Step 3 (Funded)Copy link
Complete at least 3 profitable days, each with a minimum of 0.5% to be eligible for a Payout (Resets after each payout)Follow all trading rules and loss limits including goat guard (What Is Goat Guard).
Exception: For accounts purchased on or after July 25th, 2026, the Funded stage requires 4 profitable days, each with a minimum of 0.5% profit to be eligible for a payout, instead of the standard 3-day requirement.
What Is A Minimum Profitable Day?Copy link
A minimum profitable day is a day on which you close at least one position and your profit for that day is at least 0.5% of the initial account balance, net of fees and commissions.

How is it calculated?Copy link
At 5:00 PM EST, we check the higher value between the Account Balance or Account Equity.Subtract 5% from that value to set the Daily Drawdown Threshold for the trading day
Worked exampleAccount Balance$105,000Account Equity$107,000 (Higher)Threshold Calculation($107,000 × 5%) = $5,350$107,000 - $5,350$101,650Your balance/equity must stay above $101,650 for that day.
2. Maximum Loss Limit (Hard Breach) — 10% (Static)Copy link
This is your Absolute Floor. Your account equity or balance must never drop below 90% of your starting capital.
How is it calculated?Copy link
Maximum Loss = Initial Account Size − 10%
Worked exampleAccount Size$100,000$100,000 x 10%$10,000$100,000 - $10,000$90,000. Your Absolute Floor: Your account equity/balance must never drop below $90,000.

Rewards & Profit SplitCopy link
Once you are a Funded GOAT Trader, the following rules apply:Copy link
Profit Split: 80% (Traders keep 80% of their profit).
Payout Cycle: Bi-weekly (Every 14 days).
Daily Profit Cap: Funded accounts have a $3,000 daily profit limit.
*Note: Profits exceeding this are deducted but do NOT result in an account breach.
Initial 2 Payout Caps: For the first two payouts, withdrawals are capped at 6% of the initial account size or $10,000 (whichever is lower). This restriction is removed after the 2nd successful payout.
Exception: For $5k Two-Step accounts, the cap is 4% of the initial account balance instead of 6%. This applies only to accounts purchased from July 25th, 2026 onward.
Optional Add-ons (Available At Checkout)Copy link
100% Profit Split:On-Demand First Reward: Take your first payout early at a 40% split, once you have 3% profit and 4 minimum profitable days on the funded account (3 days for accounts purchased before 25 July 2026).
