1. Max Daily Drawdown – 3% (resets daily)Copy link
What does this mean?
The maximum daily loss limit is 3% of your daily starting balance.
How is it calculated?
The daily drawdown is reset every day at 5 PM EST. At the reset, we take the higher of your balance and equity and set your daily limit 3% below it. The limit stays the same until the next reset.
Worked exampleFor a $100,000 account, the daily drawdown limit on the first day is $3,000, so your balance or equity must stay above $97,000. If your account starts the next day at $104,000, your daily limit for that day is $100,880 (3% below $104,000).
2. Max Total Drawdown – 6% (Trailing)Copy link
What does this mean?
The maximum total drawdown of Instant GOAT accounts is a fixed amount equal to 6% of your initial balance. On a $100,000 account that is $6,000, so your limit starts at $94,000. When your balance or equity reaches a new high, the limit moves up so it stays $6,000 below that high. This happens during the trading day, as soon as the new high is reached. The limit never moves down, and it stops moving once it reaches your initial balance.
Worked exampleFor a $100,000 account:If your account reaches $104,000, your trailing drawdown limit becomes $98,000 ($6,000 below your highest equity).If your account then drops to $103,000, your trailing drawdown limit stays at $98,000.If your account later reaches $107,000, the limit would be $101,000, but it stops at $100,000, your initial balance, and stays there.
N.B - The Maximum Loss Limit resets after each payout.
Once your payout is approved, your balance goes back to your initial balance and your limit is set again, $6,000 below it. From there it trails up the same way as before. Your daily drawdown limit is also set again from the new balance.
Worked exampleFor a $100,000 account:Your account is at $106,000 and your limit has reached $100,000.You request a payout. Once it is approved, your balance is back to $100,000 and your limit resets to $94,000, so you have $6,000 of room again.Your daily limit for the rest of that day is $97,000 (3% below $100,000).If your account then reaches $102,000, your limit becomes $96,000.
Floating Loss Limit (2%)Copy link
If the combined floating (unrealised) loss of all your open trades reaches -2% of your initial account balance at any moment, the account is permanently closed. This is not a per-trade limit. Every open position counts toward the 2%, so the account can close even when no single trade is down 2%.For example, on a $50,000 account, if your combined open trade losses reach -$1,000 (-2%), the account will be immediately closed.

Minimum profitable day requirement for Instant Goat accountsCopy link
Traders on Instant Goat accounts must complete at least 5 profitable days before requesting a reward. These days do not have to be consecutive. To count as a minimum profitable day, the day must close with a profit of at least 0.5% of the initial balance, net of fees and commissions.
Consistency RuleCopy link
What is the consistency rule?
The consistency rule ensures steady and sustainable profits over time. It states that no single trading day can account for more than 15% of your total profits during a payout period.
Worked exampleIf you make $1,500 in one trading day, your total profits must reach at least $10,000 to be eligible to request a payout. You cannot request a payout until the highest profit day is no more than 15% of the total profits for the period.
What Happens if I Violate the 15% Rule?Copy link
Will my account be terminated?
No, violating the 15% rule will not result in account termination. However, you will be unable to request a payout until your highest profit trading day is no more than 15% of your total profits.
What should I do next?
Continue trading and generating profits until your highest profit day is at or below the 15% threshold. Once this happens, you will be eligible to request a payout again.
