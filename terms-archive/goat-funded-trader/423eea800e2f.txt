Is News Trading allowed?

Updated 23 November 2026Copy link

Can I trade during news events with Goat Funded Trader?
Copy link

Yes. Trading during news events is allowed and does not break any Goat Funded Trader rules.

Is there a limit on profit from trades around high-impact news?
Copy link

Yes. From 24 November 2026, the limit applies to each news release, not to each trade. Every trade opened or closed within 5 minutes before or after a high-impact news release (marked with a red folder on ForexFactory.com or Myfxbook.com) counts toward that release. Together, those trades have a maximum profit, and the maximum depends on when you purchased the account:

Purchased on or after 10 November 2026: 3% of the account's initial balance per news release
Purchased before 10 November 2026: 1% of the account's initial balance per news release
Which trades count toward a news release?
Copy link

Every trade opened or closed between 5 minutes before and 5 minutes after the release. That includes:

A trade you opened earlier that closes during the window
A trade you open during the window
A pending order that triggers during the window
A trade closed during the window by Stop Loss, by Take Profit or manually

A trade that opens during one news release and closes during another counts toward both releases.

When is the profit counted?
Copy link

Once every trade linked to the release is closed. If one of them stays open for hours, or until the next day, we wait until it closes. Then we add up the profit from those trades and compare the total with your limit.

Which limit applies to my account?
Copy link

The purchase date decides it, and nothing else. Accounts purchased before 10 November 2026 keep the 1% limit. They do not move to 3%.

A funded account follows the purchase date of the challenge it came from. If you bought the challenge before 10 November 2026 and pass after that date, your funded account stays at 1%. If you reset an account that was purchased before 10 November 2026, it also stays at 1%, even if the reset happens after that date.

Does this policy apply to both the challenge and funded phases?
Copy link

Yes. Both limits apply to the challenge phase and the funded phase.

What happens if the total is above the limit?
Copy link

Only the profit above the limit is removed. You keep the profit up to the limit.

Example on a $100,000 account: a trade you opened earlier closes with $2,000 profit during a red folder release. You also open a second trade inside the window, and it closes three hours later with $2,500 profit. Both trades count toward the same release, so the total is $4,500.

Purchased on or after 10 November 2026: the limit is $3,000. You keep $3,000 and $1,500 is removed.
Purchased before 10 November 2026: the limit is $1,000. You keep $1,000 and $3,500 is removed.

This is not a breach and there is no penalty on the account. The deduction usually happens during account reviews.

What changed on 24 November 2026?
Copy link

Before 24 November 2026, the limit applied to each trade separately. From 24 November 2026, it applies to the total of all trades linked to a news release. The 3% and 1% limits themselves did not change.

The new method applies to every payout request that is still under review on 24 November 2026, including requests submitted before that date. Payout requests already decided before 24 November 2026 stay as they are.
