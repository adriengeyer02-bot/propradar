How can I request a payout
To request a payout, simply visit your payout section in Your dashboard once you are eligible.
Your current payout eligibility date will be visible in your QUANTX Dashboard. Payouts are processed within 2 business day (excluding weekends), and in most cases, you will receive your profits within the same business day.
However, depending on your payment method, it may take up to 1-3 business days for the funds to arrive. The entire process typically takes 1-3 business days.
You can receive your earnings through directly in cryptocurrency. We do not charge any commissions for payouts, but the payout processor may apply fees.
Additional Details:

Minimum payout amount: $600

You must complete the KYC process to receive payouts.

If you are unable to complete KYC, unfortunately, we will not be able to process your payout.
​

Payout Criteria for Accounts Below $50,000

For account sizes below $50,000, the following payout criteria apply:

A minimum of 5 profitable days is required.

1 profitable day is defined as a day with at least 0.5% profit.

The minimum payout threshold is 2.5% of the account size.
​

Payout Cycle

1-Step accounts: payout cycle is every 5 profitable days.

2-Steps and Instant accounts: payout cycle is bi-weekly.
