Withdrawal requests can be initiated directly from your SFX Funded dashboard and will be processed within 2 business days.

We process all payouts via Crypto and Bank Transfer - once approved, your funds are sent directly to your chosen method. For more information on payout methods, you can go here.

If you run into any difficulty getting verified, reach out to our support team. We'll help you resolve the issue or provide an alternative verification method where necessary.

Funded Account Reward Guidelines

At SFX Funded, we prioritise a fair and secure trading environment. To maintain stability, we apply the following rule to funded accounts:

Profit Withdrawal Limits for Initial Rewards
For the first two reward requests, traders can withdraw up to $10,000 per request.
Any profit beyond this limit will be deferred to future payouts.
After the first two rewards, this restriction is removed, and there are no further withdrawal limits.
Why are these limits necessary?

These limits help create a stable and secure trading environment while allowing us to properly assess each trader's performance. The window between purchasing a program and receiving the first rewards is often too short for a full evaluation of trading behaviour. By applying these temporary restrictions, we ensure fairness, sustainability, and long-term opportunity for every trader.

If you have any questions or need further clarification, please contact our support team. We're committed to transparency and efficiency in serving our traders.​​
