Mistakes are inevitable, especially in the first few days of trading. At SFX Funded traders who purchase the Ascend Evaluation Phase Reset add-on can reset their account once during either Phase 1 or Phase 2 of their Challenge Account.​​How Evaluation Phase Reset Works​The Ascend Evaluation Phase Reset applies only to two-phase evaluation accounts.​If the perk wasn't used during Phase 1, it can be activated in Phase 2 (the verification phase). However, the timer for the 10-day period starts from your first trade in Phase 1, and this timer does not reset once you enter Phase 2. This means you have 10 calendar days from your first trade to activate the Evaluation Reset option.

Once activated, the reset starts again from Phase 1 (for free).

The Evaluation Reset option must be activated within 10 calendar days of your first trade of the evaluation, regardless of whether you are in Phase 1 or Phase 2.​To activate the reset, email support@sfxfunded.com
