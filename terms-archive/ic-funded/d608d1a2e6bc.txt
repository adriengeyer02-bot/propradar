2-STEP PROFESSIONAL

Getting started

Detailed guidance aligned with the current 2-Step Professional programme rules for Step 1, Step 2 and the simulated funded stage.

What is the 2-Step Professional programme?

The 2-Step Professional programme is a two-phase evaluation. Step 1 has a 10% profit target, 5% daily drawdown and 10% maximum drawdown. Step 2 has a 5% profit target, 5% daily drawdown and 10% maximum drawdown. After both phases are completed and the applicable review is passed, the trader may progress to the simulated funded stage.
