Getting Started
Do I need to download a trading platform?

Yes.

Once your account credentials are issued, you will receive instructions on how to access your trading account using one of our supported platforms.

IC Funded provides access to MetaTrader 5 (MT5) and cTrader, both available at no additional cost. These platforms can be downloaded directly from their official websites or accessed through their web and mobile versions.

After logging in with your account credentials, you can begin trading immediately.

Is IC Funded a broker?

No. IC Funded is not a broker and does not hold client deposits.

IC Funded provides traders with access to a simulated trading environment through virtual trading accounts designed to evaluate trading performance and risk management.

Traders participate in an evaluation process where they must meet specific trading objectives while respecting risk limits. Upon successfully completing the evaluation, traders may become eligible for a funded trading account based on their demonstrated performance.

Throughout all stages of the program, trading activity takes place in a simulated trading environment that replicates real market conditions.

Who can participate in IC Funded challenges?

To participate in IC Funded challenges, traders must meet the following requirements:

Be at least 18 years of age at the time of registration.
Be legally allowed to enter into a binding agreement under the laws of their country of residence.
Not be located in, or a resident of, any restricted jurisdictions where our services are not available due to regulatory or compliance requirements.
Agree to and comply with all IC Funded Terms & Conditions, Trading Rules, and Risk Management policies.

Traders are responsible for ensuring that participating in a simulated trading evaluation program is permitted in their jurisdiction.

If you are unsure about your eligibility, we recommend contacting our Support team before purchasing a challenge.

What is the daily drawdown based on? (Balance or Equity)

The daily drawdown is calculated based on equity, not only the closed balance.

This means that both open and closed positions are included when calculating the daily loss limit. If the account equity drops below the permitted daily drawdown level at any moment during the day, the rule will be considered violated.

Traders should always monitor their floating losses, as open trades can contribute to reaching the daily drawdown limit even if positions have not yet been closed.

The daily drawdown resets at the end of the trading day at 00:00 server time, which corresponds to:

00:00 GMT+2 or GMT+3 depending on daylight saving time.

Is trading simulated or live?

All trading at IC Funded is conducted in a simulated trading environment. During both the evaluation and funded stages, traders operate on demo accounts that replicate real market conditions.

How long does it take to receive my trading account after purchasing a challenge?

Trading account credentials are typically sent shortly after the challenge purchase is completed.

Once the payment has been successfully processed, traders will receive an email containing:

Trading platform login credentials
Server information
Account details for the selected challenge

In most cases, the account is issued within a few minutes, but in rare situations it may take slightly longer due to payment processing or system checks.

If you do not receive your account credentials after completing your purchase, please check your spam or junk folder, or contact IC Funded Support for assistance.

Can I have multiple IC Funded accounts?

Yes, traders may purchase and participate in multiple IC Funded challenges at the same time.

Multiple funded accounts are permitted, with a maximum total allocation of $500,000 per trader.

All accounts must comply with IC Funded’s trading rules, risk management guidelines, and prohibited trading practices. Any attempt to bypass risk limits or use multiple accounts in a coordinated manner to exploit the evaluation process may result in account suspension or termination.
