Instant Funding models compared

Differences between the Instant funding models

Updated October 15, 2026
📊 Comparison Table
Parameter	Instant Plus	Instant Prime
Profit Target	None	4%
Overall Drawdown	4% (Trailing)	6% (Trailing capped)
Daily Drawdown (EOD)	N/A	3%
Minimum Trading Days	10 per cycle	5 per cycle
Maximum Trading Days	Unlimited	Unlimited
Weekend & Overnight	Yes	Yes
Consistency Rule	20%	N/A
Reward/Payout Split	70% (90% add-on)	70% (90% add-on)
Payout Schedule	Every 14 days	Every 14 days
📝 Summary
Instant Plus suits traders who prefer lower trailing risk and more flexibility (no daily drawdown), but must maintain consistency.
Instant Prime is the most trader-friendly in terms of payout speed (5 days) and flexibility (no consistency rule), though it introduces a profit target and combines trailing + daily drawdown limits.
