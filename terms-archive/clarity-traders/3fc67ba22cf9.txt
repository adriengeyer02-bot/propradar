 How the daily loss limit works
 The limit is measured from your balance at the start of each trading day, and resets when a new day begins.
 On a $50K One Step account, the daily limit is $2,000. If you start the day at $51,000, your equity (including open trades) must stay above $49,000 until the day resets.

 What you can and can't do
 These apply to every account type, in both the evaluation and funded stages.

 Allowed

 Trading the newsNo restrictions around economic releases.
 Holding trades overnight and over the weekend
 Any trading style: scalping, day trading, swing trading
 Expert Advisors (EAs)With the EA add-on at checkout.
 Trading on weekendsWith the Trade on Weekends add-on.
 No minimum days to pass and no time limit

 Not allowed

 High-frequency trading and latency arbitrage
 Trades held for less than 60 seconds
 Hedging or coordinating trades across accountsIncluding accounts held by other people.
 Market manipulation
 Going more than 60 days without placing a tradeTrade at least once every 60 days to keep the account active.

 The consistency rule
 Applies to funded accounts when you request a reward. It makes sure rewards come from steady trading, not one lucky day.
 Say you request a $4,000 reward on a $50K One Step account. With a 20% rule, your best single day can be at most $800. If one day made more than that, keep trading until your total simulated gains are large enough, then request.
