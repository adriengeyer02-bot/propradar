 Reward questionsCan’t find your answer? Our team is on live chat.Live chat →

 How am I paid?In USDT on the Ethereum network (ERC-20), sent to the wallet address you give in your dashboard.
 How often can I request a reward?Every 14 days. With the Express Payouts add-on, every week.
 Is there a minimum number of trading days before a reward?Yes. Every funded account needs at least 7 trading days before you can request a reward. There's no minimum to pass an evaluation.
 Why do you check my identity?KYC is required before any reward. It protects your account and keeps rewards going to the right person.
 What happens if a day breaks the consistency rule?Keep trading. Once your total simulated gains are large enough that your best day falls under the limit, you can request. For example, with a 20% rule and a best day of $1,000, you can request once your reward is $5,000 or more.
 Where can I see the top-ranked accounts?On the leaderboard.
