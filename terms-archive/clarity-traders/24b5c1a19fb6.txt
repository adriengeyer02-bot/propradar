Available payment options may vary by region. All transactions are processed through secure, encrypted payment systems to ensure your information remains protected.How do rewards work?Once you're eligible, submit a reward request from your dashboard. Requests are reviewed to check that all trading rules were followed. After approval, rewards are paid in Tether (USDT, ERC-20) to your wallet address.You can request a reward every 14 days, or weekly with the Express Payouts add-on. Funded accounts have a minimum of 7 trading days.Frequently asked questionsWhat is an Instant account?Instant accounts are built for traders who want quicker access to reward opportunities without passing a challenge. You trade in a simulated environment that mirrors real market conditions, and as long as you follow the rules, the realized gains you make can be used to request rewards.‍No evaluation required – begin trading as soon as your account is created.Simulated trading – positions are not executed in live markets but reflect real time conditions.Performance based rewards – rewards are available from your simulated gains once all rules are met.Do I still need to pass a challenge?No — the Instant Account does not require you to complete a challenge. You can begin trading right away and as long as you follow all rules, the simulated gains you generate can be used to request rewards.Are there any trading rules I must follow?Yes. Instant Accounts still have rules in place to ensure responsible trading and consistent performance. These include:‍Overall Drawdown Limit – Your account cannot fall below the 5% maximum loss threshold.‍Daily Drawdown Limit - Your account cannot fall below the 3% daily loss threshold‍Consistency Rule – No single trading day can account for more than a 10% of your requested reward. ‍General Trading Rules – All standard account policies (such as prohibited strategies) must also be followed.How does the consistency rule work with Instant accounts?On instant accounts, the 10% consistency rule means that when you request a reward, no single trading day can account for more than 10% of the total amount you are requesting. This approach helps confirm that your results are sustainable and repeatable over time, not reliant on one “big win.”‍Example: If you request a $10,000 reward, no single day’s simulated gains should exceed $1,000 of that total.You must demonstrate consistent performance across multiple days in order for your reward to be approved.How soon can I request a reward?Every funded account, including Instant, needs verified ID (KYC) and at least 7 trading days before the first reward request. After that you can request a reward from your dashboard every 14 days, or every 7 days with the Express Payouts add-on.Frequently asked questionsAm I allowed to hold trades over the weekend?Yes, weekend trading and holding positions over the weekend are permitted. You may keep trades open through market close on Friday and hold them until the market reopens.Can I trade during high impact news events?Yes, trading during high-impact news events is permitted. However, traders are responsible for managing increased volatility, slippage, and execution risks.Can I use Expert Advisors (EAs) or automated trading?Automated trading is only allowed if you have purchased the EA’s Allowed add-on. All EAs must comply with our trading policies. Strategies that exploit system delays, pricing errors, or platform manipulation are strictly prohibited.Do you allow high-frequency trading (HFT)?No. Clarity Traders does not allow high-frequency trading (HFT), latency arbitrage, or strategies designed to exploit execution delays or platform inefficiencies. All trades must remain open for a minimum of 60 seconds. Trading activity is monitored for compliance, and accounts that violate this policy may be restricted or denied rewards.How often do I need to trade to keep my account active?Clarity Traders has a 60-day inactivity policy. To keep your account active, you must place at least one trade every 60 days. If no trades are placed for 60 consecutive days, your account may be closed due to inactivity.‍What happens if I violate a rule?If you violate any trading rules, your account may be suspended, restricted, or closed depending on the severity of the violation. In most cases, rule breaches result in account termination and loss of eligibility for rewards. All decisions are made in accordance with our terms and conditions.What is the consistency rule?The consistency rule makes sure your results come from steady trading over several days, not one oversized trade or lucky day. When you request a reward, no single trading day can make up more than a set share of the amount you're requesting.Instant: 10%One Step and Two Step (funded): 20%Example: on a funded One Step account, if you request a $1,000 reward, no single day's simulated gains can be more than $200.What is the daily drawdown rule?The daily loss limit is the most you can lose in one trading day. If your account reaches it, the account is breached and may be closed.Instant: 3%One Step: 4%Two Step: 5%For example, on a $10,000 One Step account the daily loss limit is $400.What is the overall drawdown rule?The max loss limit is the most your account can lose in total. If your equity falls below it at any time, the account is breached and closed.Instant: 5%One Step: 6% in the evaluation, 10% once fundedTwo Step: 8% in the evaluation, 10% once fundedFrequently asked questionsWhat's the Refund Guarantee add-on?The Refund Guarantee add-on allows you to receive a full refund of your challenge fee after your first successful reward, as long as you meet all trading rules and eligibility requirements. This gives you added confidence and reduces your upfront risk.What's the Double Leverage add-on?The Double Leverage add-on increases your available buying power, allowing you to trade larger position sizes with the same account balance. This is ideal for experienced traders looking to maximize opportunities while still following our risk management rules.What's the EA’s Allowed add-on?The EA’s Allowed add-on gives you permission to use Expert Advisors, automated trading systems, and trading bots on your account. This allows you to execute algorithmic strategies without restrictions, provided they comply with our trading policies.What's the Express Payouts add-on?The Express Payouts add-on shortens the wait between reward requests from 14 days to 7 days, so you can request a reward every week instead of every 14 days. All other eligibility rules (KYC, at least 7 trading days on the funded account and the consistency rule) stay the same.What's the Trade on Weekends add-on?The Trade on Weekends add-on allows you to open and manage positions during weekends and extended market sessions. This is ideal for traders who hold swing trades or trade markets that operate outside standard weekday hours.What's the 100% Rewards add-on?The 100% Rewards add-on allows you to receive 100% of your eligible simulated trading gains instead of the standard percentage.
