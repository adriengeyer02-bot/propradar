Choosing an accountCan’t find your answer? Read the full FAQ or chat with our team.What's the difference between Instant, One Step and Two Step?Instant skips the evaluation: you start on a simulated funded account, with a 3% trailing daily loss limit and a 5% max loss limit. One Step has a single evaluation phase with a 10% profit target. Two Step has two phases, 8% then 5%, and gives you the most room during the evaluation, with an 8% max loss limit.Which account is the cheapest?Two Step has the lowest price at every account size. At $100K, for example, Two Step is $599, One Step is $687 and Instant is $789, before any discount code.Is the reward split the same on every account?Yes. You receive 70% of eligible simulated gains on your first reward, 80% on your second and 90% after that, or 100% with the 100% Rewards add-on. Rewards are paid every 14 days, or weekly with Express Payouts, in USDT.Is there a minimum number of trading days or a time limit?There's no time limit and no minimum number of trading days to pass, on any account. Once funded, every account has a minimum of 7 trading days.Can I trade the news or hold trades over the weekend?Yes. News trading and weekend holding are allowed on every account.Which platforms can I use?You can trade on TradeLocker or MT5 with any account.Still deciding?Two Step has the lowest price at every account size.
