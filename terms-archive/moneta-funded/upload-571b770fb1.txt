5.1A. Daily Loss Calculation
Where a Daily Loss Limit applies, the Daily Loss Breach Level is calculated using the higher of the
Account Balance or Account Equity recorded at the Daily Snapshot, less the applicable Daily Loss
Amount.

Daily Loss Breach Level = Maximum (Balance at Daily Snapshot, Equity at Daily Snapshot) - Daily Loss
Amount

The Daily Loss Amount is calculated by applying the Daily Loss parameter applicable to the Account to
the Initial Account Balance. The Daily Snapshot takes place at 22:00 UTC. The Daily Loss Limit resets
following each Daily Snapshot. The applicable Daily Loss parameter is displayed in the Program Rules
applicable to the Account.

5.1B. Static Maximum Loss
Where an Account uses a static Maximum Loss, the Maximum Loss Level is calculated from the Initial
Account Balance using the Maximum Loss parameter applicable to the Account.

Maximum Loss Level = Initial Account Balance - Maximum Loss Amount

The Maximum Loss Level remains fixed and does not increase as the Account generates profit. The
Account is considered breached if its Equity reaches or falls below the applicable Maximum Loss Level.
The applicable Maximum Loss parameter and whether the Account uses a static Maximum Loss are
displayed in the Program Rules applicable to the Account.

5.1C. Trailing Maximum Loss
Where an Account uses a trailing Maximum Loss, the Maximum Loss Level is calculated by reference
to the highest Balance or Equity reached on the Account and the applicable trailing Maximum Loss
Amount.

The Maximum Loss Level may move upward as the Account reaches a new highest Balance or Equity,
does not move downward if Balance or Equity subsequently decreases, cannot move above the Initial
Account Balance, and permanently stops trailing once it reaches the Initial Account Balance.

Where the applicable Program Rules provide for the trailing Maximum Loss to lock in connection with
a Payout, submission of an eligible Payout request will cause the Maximum Loss Level to lock at the
Initial Account Balance. Once locked, the Maximum Loss Level does not reset or move downward as a

5.1D. Profit Targets
Where a Program or phase includes a Profit Target, achievement of the Profit Target is determined by

Balance has reached the applicable Profit Target, there are no open positions on the Account, and all

applicable to the Account.

5.1E. Minimum Profitable Days
Where Minimum Profitable Days apply, the trading result for each day is calculated as follows:

Minimum (Midnight Balance, Midnight Equity) - Previous Day Balance

For this calculation, Midnight Balance and Midnight Equity mean the Account Balance and Account
Equity recorded at the Daily Snapshot. A day will qualify as a Profitable Day where the result of this
calculation meets or exceeds the minimum amount required under the Program Rules applicable to
the Account. Unless otherwise stated in the applicable Program Rules, qualifying Profitable Days do
not need to be consecutive. Where an Account has been scaled, the calculation may be based on the
base account size applicable to the trader's current scaling level. The required number of Profitable
Days and the minimum profit required for a day to qualify are displayed in the Program Rules applicable
to the Account.

5.1F. Consistency Rule
Where a Consistency Rule applies, consistency is calculated using the trader's Best Day Realised P&L
as a proportion of the trader's Total Realised P&L for the current Payout Cycle.

Consistency Percentage = Best Day Realised P&L / Total Realised P&L for the current Payout Cycle x
100

Where the result exceeds the applicable Consistency Limit, the Account will not be eligible for a Payout
until sufficient additional realised profit has been generated to bring the result within the permitted
limit. The Consistency calculation resets when a new Payout Cycle begins. The applicable Consistency
Limit is displayed in the Program Rules applicable to the Account.
