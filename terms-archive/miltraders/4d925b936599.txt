What Are the Progressive Payout Caps?

Zero: fixed caps of $1,000/$2,000/$2,500/$3,000 per request (25K to 150K) with the 50% formula. Pro & Instant: progressive caps that grow with every payout. No limit on the number of payouts on any program.

M
Written by MEDHI
Updated this week

Payout caps at MILTRADERS depend on your program. Zero uses fixed caps with a simple withdrawal formula. Pro and Instant use progressive caps that grow with every successful payout.

Zero funded accounts — fixed caps

Each payout is calculated as: 50% of your profit above the locked MLL, capped per request.

Example (Zero 50K): with a balance of $53,000 and the MLL locked at $50,100, your profit above the lock is $2,900. Your payout is 50% × $2,900 = $1,450. From your second payout onwards, your balance must first climb back above the level it was at when you requested your previous payout — each new payout requires fresh gains, so you can't withdraw twice from the same profits. There is no limit to the number of payouts on a Zero account. At any time, on any account type, MILTRADERS may invite a consistent trader to transition to a live account — an opportunity offered at the firm's discretion, never an automatic step after a set number of payouts.

Pro & Instant funded accounts — progressive caps

Your maximum withdrawal amount increases with each successful payout. Progressive caps reward consistency and protect the sustainability of the firm for all traders.

Pro 25K: $750 for the 1st payout, $1,000 for the 2nd and 3rd, $1,250 for the 4th and 5th, then $1,500 from the 6th payout onwards (minimum $500). Instant is not available in 25K.

The minimum payout amount for any withdrawal is $500 on all programs.
