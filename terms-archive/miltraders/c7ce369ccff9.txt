Zero vs Pro vs Instant: Which Account Should I Choose?

Side-by-side comparison of the three MILTRADERS programs to help you pick the right one. Pro has the lowest entry price; Zero has the simplest funded phase; Instant skips the evaluation entirely.

M
Written by MEDHI
Updated this week

Three programs, one question: how fast do you want to be funded, and how much do you want to pay upfront?

• Zero — the one-step evaluation with the simplest funded phase: one single rule during the evaluation, then no consistency rule and no buffer once funded.

• Pro — the classic one-step evaluation. Lowest entry price, same targets as Zero, more contracts (up to 12 minis), progressive payout caps once funded.

• Instant — no evaluation at all. Pay once, get funded immediately, request payouts as soon as you hit each profit goal.
