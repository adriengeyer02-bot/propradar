 Consistency rule during the evaluation?
During your evaluation, your best single trading day must not exceed 50% of your total profit at the moment you reach the profit target (a 1% tolerance is applied). This naturally implies a minimum of 2 trading days. Example: on a 50K account ($3,000 target), no single day may exceed $1,530. If your best day is $2,000, you must reach $4,000 in total profit before the evaluation validates. Breaking this rule does not close your account — simply keep trading and dilute your profits until you can validate.

 What happens if the rule is triggered?
Rule violations fall into two categories:

Soft Breach (flag for review): Your account stays active. The flag triggers a review by our risk team. If your trading reflects a legitimate strategy, your account standing is typically not affected. Examples: micro-scalping (the profits from trades held under 10 seconds may be deducted), news gambling patterns, suspicious login activity.

Hard Breach (immediate closure): Account is closed and all profits forfeited, with no warnings. Examples: account sharing / copying another trader's trades (verified), use of bots or automated systems, drawdown breach.

Hedging is handled on a graduated scale rather than as an immediate closure: it is detected automatically and you are notified by email, and all profits made on that trading day are cancelled on every account involved. Repeated violations — as with the high-impact news rule on Zero funded accounts — can lead to the closure of the accounts involved and a ban.

 What is considered gambling behavior?
At MILTRADERS, we fund traders, not gamblers. Gambling behavior is any position whose potential loss equals all, or nearly all, of the account's drawdown, with no identifiable risk management. This includes, but is not limited to, entering at maximum size from the first day with no stop or with a stop equal to losing the account, as well as abusive DCA, meaning stacking losing positions until the entire drawdown is at risk.

Reasonable DCA, built progressively within the account's drawdown, remains fully allowed.

In case of gambling behavior, MILTRADERS honors the payout currently requested, closes the funded accounts involved, and refunds any evaluations not yet started. The trader is also permanently banned and may no longer purchase new accounts. The final assessment rests with MILTRADERS.

Payout

 How do payout caps work?
Caps work differently depending on your program. Zero funded accounts use fixed caps: $2,000 (50K) / $2,500 (100K) / $3,000 (150K) per payout, applied to the 50% formula. Pro and Instant use progressive caps that grow with every payout (50K / 100K / 150K):

1st payout: $1,500 / $2,000 / $2,500

2nd & 3rd: $2,000 / $2,500 / $3,000

4th & 5th: $2,500 / $3,000 / $3,500

6th and beyond: $3,000 / $3,500 / $4,000

Minimum payout: $500

Caps protect the sustainability of the firm, keep processing fast, and give every trader fair and predictable payouts. The minimum request is $500 on every program, and you always keep 90% of what you withdraw.

 Payout Frequency?
There is no fixed payout window — you can request any day, as soon as your program's conditions are met. On Zero and Pro, a payout cycle is 5 winning days at 0.2% of capital minimum ($100 / $200 / $300). On Instant there is no minimum day count at all: your profit goal is the trigger.

 First Payout?
It depends on your program. On Zero, your first payout unlocks once your drawdown is locked — balance reaches $52,100 (50K) / $103,100 (100K) / $154,600 (150K) — and you have completed 5 winning days; you then withdraw 50% of your gains above the locked level. On Pro, same 5-day cycle: your balance must reach the buffer at those same thresholds, and 100% of the profit above it is withdrawable, so roughly $52,600 / $103,600 / $155,100 to reach the $500 minimum payout. On Instant there is no minimum day count: your first payout unlocks when you earn $3,000 / $6,000 / $9,000 in profit.

 Processing Time?
Payouts are processed within 48 hours, on every program and from your very first request.

 Payment Method & KYC?
Payouts are processed through Rise, in crypto (USDC) or by bank transfer. KYC is done through Rise: on Zero and Pro when you pass your evaluation, before your funded account goes live; on Instant, before your first payout request. No hidden fees from MILTRADERS; only processor and network fees apply.
