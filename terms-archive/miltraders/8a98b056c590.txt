What Is MILTRADERS Instant?

Skip the evaluation and start earning immediately: no minimum trading days — profit goals per payout are the clock.

• Consistency rule: your best single trading day must not exceed 20% of the profit in your current cycle

• Payout caps: progressive, from $1,500 / $2,000 / $2,500 on your 1st payout up to $3,000 / $3,500 / $4,000 from the 6th — minimum $500 per request, 90/10 split

Additional Rules

Overnight positions are not allowed. No reset option available. No multi-account discount — Instant accounts are purchased individually. Inactivity: 30 days without a trade closes the account. Maximum 6 funded accounts per trader (all types combined).

Reaching the daily loss limit is a soft breach: your open positions are closed and you can trade again from the next session. Your account is not closed.
