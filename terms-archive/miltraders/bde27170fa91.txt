ARTICLE 11: PROHIBITED PRACTICES

11.1 The following practices are prohibited, as further defined in the Program Rules:

Automated trading: the use of bots, expert advisors, algorithms or any automated system to place orders. Copying your own trades between your own MILTRADERS Accounts with the copy tools provided on the platform is permitted, provided the original trade is placed manually.
Account sharing and third-party trading: letting another person access or trade your Account, trading another person's Account, or copying another trader's trades or any signal service.
Hedging: holding opposite positions in the same product group, within one Account, across your Accounts or with another trader.
High-impact news on Zero Funded Accounts: holding a position on a Zero Funded Account during the restricted window around the high-impact announcements defined in the Program Rules. News trading is otherwise permitted on all programs as part of a coherent trading strategy.
Gambling Behavior, on Funded Accounts: any position whose potential loss equals all, or nearly all, of the account's drawdown, with no identifiable risk management. This includes, but is not limited to, entering at maximum size from the first day with no stop or with a stop equal to losing the account, as well as abusive DCA, meaning stacking losing positions until the entire drawdown is at risk. Reasonable DCA, built progressively within the account's drawdown, remains fully allowed.
Exploitation of errors: taking advantage of a technical error, latency, a price feed error or any feature of the simulated environment that does not exist in a real market.
Multiple identities and circumvention: any breach of Article 4, including operating several user profiles or circumventing a restriction or a ban.
Fraud: providing false information or documents, payment fraud, or any other fraudulent conduct.

11.2 The final assessment of whether a practice falls within this Article rests with MILTRADERS.

12.4 Hard breaches, including a drawdown breach, automated trading, account sharing, copying another trader, the exploitation of errors and fraud, lead to the immediate closure of the Account concerned and the forfeiture of all profits not yet paid.
