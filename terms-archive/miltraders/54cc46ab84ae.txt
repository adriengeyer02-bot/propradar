What Is Considered Gambling Behavior?

Positions that put the entire drawdown at risk with no risk management are not allowed on funded accounts. Reasonable DCA remains allowed.

M
Written by MEDHI
Updated over 2 weeks ago

At MILTRADERS, we fund traders, not gamblers. Gambling behavior is any position whose potential loss equals all, or nearly all, of the account's drawdown, with no identifiable risk management. This includes, but is not limited to, entering at maximum size from the first day with no stop or with a stop equal to losing the account, as well as abusive DCA, meaning stacking losing positions until the entire drawdown is at risk.

 

A stop loss alone does not make a position acceptable: if hitting it would wipe out all or nearly all of the account's drawdown, the position is still considered gambling.

 

Reasonable DCA, built progressively within the account's drawdown, remains fully allowed.

 

In case of gambling behavior, MILTRADERS closes the funded accounts involved and refunds any evaluations not yet started. Any payout currently requested is reviewed and may be approved or declined at MILTRADERS' discretion; the decision and its reasons are communicated to the trader. The trader is also permanently banned and may no longer purchase new accounts. The final assessment rests with MILTRADERS.
