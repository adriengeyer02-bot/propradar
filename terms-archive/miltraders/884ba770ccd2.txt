Can I Trade During High-Impact News Events?

Yes on Pro, Instant and all evaluation accounts, as part of a trading strategy. On Zero funded accounts, USD red folder announcements and speeches (ForexFactory calendar) are restricted: flat 60 seconds before and after.

News trading is allowed on MILTRADERS — with one exception on Zero funded accounts.

Pro, Instant, and all evaluation accounts

Yes, you are allowed to trade during high-impact news releases (NFP, FOMC, CPI, etc.) on Pro and Instant funded accounts and on all evaluation accounts, as long as it is part of a coherent trading strategy. The only account where trading an announcement is not allowed is the Zero funded account (see below).

Zero funded accounts — high-impact news restriction

On Zero funded accounts only, trading during high-impact USD announcements is not allowed. High-impact means USD announcements and speeches marked as a red folder on the ForexFactory calendar — for example CPI, NFP, or FOMC speeches. Red folder events in other currencies (EUR, GBP, etc.) are not restricted. ForexFactory is the sole reference calendar.

You must be flat from 60 seconds before to 60 seconds after the release. Our system detects any position held inside this window automatically and notifies you by email.

What happens if you break the rule

Profits generated by a trade held inside the restricted window are removed from your account. Repeated violations of this rule — as with the hedging rule — can lead to the closure of the accounts involved and a ban from MILTRADERS.
