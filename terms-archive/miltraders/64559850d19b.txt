INTRODUCTION

This Refund Policy applies to all purchases made from MILTRADERS L.L.C-FZ (“MILTRADERS”, “we”, “our”, or the “Company”) via the website www.miltraders.com or any other authorized distribution channel, including: MILTRADERS Zero and MILTRADERS Pro Evaluation Fees, account bundles, Resets, and Instant Funded Account purchases (together, the “Services”). All MILTRADERS purchases are one-time payments: no subscription, no automatic renewal, and no recurring charge of any kind applies to any MILTRADERS product. This Policy should be read in conjunction with the MILTRADERS Terms & Conditions. In case of conflict on any question relating to refunds, this Refund Policy prevails. Card, Apple Pay and Google Pay payments are settled by Odeonpay ALE S.R.L. (“Paysagi”), acting solely as Merchant of Record for transaction settlement purposes; cryptocurrency payments are processed through CryptoChill / Uniwire. Refunds, where granted under this Policy, are made in accordance with Article 3.

ARTICLE 1: GENERAL RULE — ALL SALES ARE FINAL

The Services are digital services performed in a simulated trading environment, whose performance begins immediately upon purchase: Evaluation credentials and dashboard access are delivered without delay after payment confirmation. FOR THIS REASON, AND SUBJECT ONLY TO THE EXCEPTIONS SET OUT IN ARTICLES 2 AND 3 BELOW, ALL SALES ARE FINAL AND NO REFUND, WHETHER TOTAL, PARTIAL, OR IN ANY OTHER FORM, IS GRANTED ONCE PERFORMANCE OF THE SERVICES HAS BEGUN. This applies in particular, and without limitation, to the following situations:

Failure of an Evaluation for any reason whatsoever (drawdown violation, prohibited practice, refusal of validation under the Terms & Conditions);
Account closure or permanent ban for violation of the Terms & Conditions or of the Program Rules, except as provided in Article 3;
Dissatisfaction with the Services, the platform, technical performance, or customer support;
Change of mind or personal circumstances of the customer;
Technical problems, service interruptions, or unavailability attributable to third parties or beyond MILTRADERS' reasonable control;
Modifications to the Program Rules, pricing, or the Terms & Conditions made in accordance with their terms;
Partial use of the Services, regardless of the time elapsed since purchase or the extent of use.

Differences between an indicative price displayed in a currency other than US dollars and the final amount debited, as well as currency conversion fees or bank fees applied by your bank or card issuer, are not refundable.

ARTICLE 2: EU/UK CONSUMERS — RIGHT OF WITHDRAWAL

If you are a consumer residing in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you benefit in principle from a fourteen (14) day right of withdrawal for distance purchases (Directive 2011/83/EU; Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013). The Services are digital services whose performance begins immediately upon purchase.

(a) Where, before payment, you have expressly requested the immediate performance of the Services and acknowledged that you thereby lose your right of withdrawal once performance has begun, your right of withdrawal is lost as from the delivery of your Evaluation or Instant credentials, and Article 1 applies.

(b) Where you have not given this express request and acknowledgment, or where performance of the Services has not begun, you may exercise your right of withdrawal within fourteen (14) days of purchase by writing to the live chat available on miltraders.com, and the amount paid will be refunded in full.

ARTICLE 3: CASES IN WHICH A REFUND IS GRANTED

Notwithstanding Article 1, MILTRADERS will refund the amounts concerned in the following situations:

Duplicate charge: the same purchase was charged more than once due to a technical or processing error — the duplicate amount is refunded in full;
Payment without delivery: you were charged but the corresponding Evaluation account or product was not delivered, and the situation is not resolved within a reasonable time after you contact support;
Incorrect amount: the amount charged differs from the total price displayed on the payment page at the time of your purchase — the difference is refunded;
Withdrawal before performance: the case described in Article 2(b) above;
Closure for gambling behavior: When MILTRADERS closes a trader's accounts for Gambling Behavior as defined in Article 11 of the Terms & Conditions, any purchased evaluations not yet started are refunded on a pro-rata basis of the price actually paid, including bundle purchases. Evaluations already started and funded accounts are not eligible for a refund, as the service has been consumed. Any payout requested before closure is reviewed and may be approved or declined at MILTRADERS' discretion, with the reasons communicated to the trader. The trader is permanently banned and may no longer purchase any account.
Selection for a Live Account: when a trader is selected for a Live Account under Article 10 of the Terms & Conditions, evaluations purchased but not yet started are refunded in full, and the evaluation fee paid for any funded account closed because it has not received a payout is refunded.
Mandatory law: any other situation in which a refund is required by mandatory provisions of applicable law from which the parties cannot derogate.

Approved refunds are made in United States dollars. Card, Apple Pay and Google Pay payments are refunded to the original payment method through Paysagi as Merchant of Record, generally within five (5) to ten (10) Business Days of approval. Cryptocurrency payments, and any payment that cannot be refunded to its original method, are refunded through Rise or by bank transfer. The time for the funds to appear depends on your bank, card issuer or payment provider.

ARTICLE 4: HOW TO SUBMIT A REFUND REQUEST

Any refund request or billing claim must be submitted to the live chat available on miltraders.com, indicating your account email, the transaction date and amount, and the reason for the request. MILTRADERS acknowledges and responds within seven (7) Business Days. If you disagree with the outcome, you may request one internal review in accordance with Article 21 of the Terms & Conditions.

ARTICLE 5: CHARGEBACKS

If you believe a charge is incorrect or unjustified, CONTACT US FIRST at the live chat available on miltraders.com before initiating any dispute with your bank or card issuer — most billing issues are resolved quickly through support. Initiating a chargeback, payment dispute, or reversal request in respect of fees actually due, without first submitting a claim to MILTRADERS and allowing a reasonable opportunity to resolve it, constitutes a material breach of the Terms & Conditions. In such case, MILTRADERS closes all of your accounts (Evaluation and Funded), cancels all profits not yet paid, and permanently bans you from the Services. MILTRADERS reserves the right to contest any chargeback with all available evidence (acceptance logs, usage data, delivery records) and to recover chargeback fees and reasonable costs incurred.
