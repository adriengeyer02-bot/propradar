UNDERSTAND YOUR OBJECTIVES

KNOW THE RULES BEFORE YOU TRADE.

Clear definitions of every rule metric to ensure complete transparency before you place your first trade.

Profit Target

The simulated profit objective that must be reached during an evaluation phase before the account can progress to review.

Maximum Daily Loss

The maximum permitted loss during a trading day. Your dashboard should clearly display your current daily-loss usage and remaining allowance.

Maximum Loss

The maximum total permitted loss on the account. Reaching or exceeding the defined maximum-loss level may result in an account breach.

Minimum Trading Days

The minimum number of qualifying trading days required before completing an evaluation stage.

Consistency

Certain CK Propfirm models may include a consistency requirement designed to encourage sustainable and disciplined simulated trading behaviour. Always check the requirements associated with your selected evaluation.
