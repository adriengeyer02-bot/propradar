Are there any payout caps?

Updated November 17, 2026

What are the payout caps?

The amounts below are maximum payout caps, not minimum profit targets. Your first payout may be requested when you have sufficient withdrawable profit, subject to your account buffer (where applicable) and all other payout conditions. From the second payout onward, you must earn at least 1% of your initial account size in profit since your previous payout. This requirement continues after payout five.

Payout caps apply to the withdrawal amount before the 80/20 profit split. Meeting the applicable conditions does not guarantee approval.

Is there a minimum payout amount?

No. There is no minimum payout amount. Partial withdrawals are permitted, provided you meet the applicable profit requirement and maintain the protected buffer where required. Every withdrawal, including a partial withdrawal, counts as one of the first five capped payouts.

When do unrestricted withdrawals begin?

The fixed payout cap is removed after your fifth payout. This does not remove other payout conditions: the 80/20 split, the 1% profit requirement between payouts, and the protected buffer where applicable continue to apply. Every withdrawal counts toward the first five, even if you withdraw less than the cap.
