News trading on the funded account. No trades in the 10-minute window around major news: 5 minutes before and 5 after. The profit of that trade is deducted. This is a soft breach and does not breach your account. Take profits on trades held at least 48 hours are exempt.

 

After the evaluation

Complete your KYC from the Verification section of your dashboard. Once it is approved, your account goes through a Security Check by our Risk Team.

 

Important Notes

BrightFunded does not offer services to individuals under 18 years of age, those listed on sanction lists, persons with criminal records related to financial crime or terrorism, or individuals previously excluded due to a breach of the BrightFunded Account Agreement.

Detection of any of these conditions may result in immediate account termination.

The full rules of each model are in its folder: 1-Step Daily, 2-Step Bright and Instant.
