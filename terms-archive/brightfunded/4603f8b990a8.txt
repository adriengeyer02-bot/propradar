Instant: account rules

Account rules, payout requirements and general trading rules.

Updated yesterday

Instant has no evaluation. You are funded when you buy, so these are the rules of your funded account.

Account summary

Rule

	

Value

None




Max loss

	

5% trailing, on the highest balance or equity you reach. Stops at your starting balance




Daily loss

	

3%




Max floating loss

	

1% of starting size, all open positions combined, in real time




Weekend holding

	

Not allowed. Trades still open on Friday are closed automatically at market close

Max loss: 5% trailing

It trails the high-water mark of your balance or equity and stops moving at your starting balance.

Highest balance or equity reached

	

Max loss limit




$100,000 (start)

	

$95,000




$102,000

	

$97,000




$104,000

	

$99,000




$105,000

	

$100,000 (locks here)

Daily loss: 3%

It is calculated on the higher value between your balance and equity at the start of the day. Example: you start the day with $100,000, so your equity cannot drop below $97,000.

The daily limit resets every day between 11:30 PM and 11:59 PM CET (rollover). We do not recommend trading in that window.

Max floating loss: 1%

The total open loss of all your positions together cannot reach 1% of your starting size. On $100,000 that is $1,000. It is checked in real time and it breaches the account even if you have not closed a trade.

Weekend holding

Positions cannot be held over the weekend. Any trade still open on Friday is closed automatically when the market closes. Holding overnight during the week is allowed.

Payout requirements

You need all of these at the same time. There is no payout cycle.

Requirement

	

What it means




Profit buffer

	

3% of account size, built before your first payout and kept in the account




Minimum payout

	

1% of account size ($1,000 on $100K)




Consistency

	

No single day above 15% of your total profit in the cycle. It resets when you request a payout




Profitable days

	

5 days with a profit of at least 0.5%




Win and loss

	

Biggest losing trade not larger than your biggest winning trade

The 15% Evaluation Reward does not apply to Instant, because there is no evaluation phase.

KYC

If you have not passed KYC before buying, your Instant account stays paused until you complete it. Once KYC is passed, your account is activated automatically.

Inactivity

	

Limited

	

A new trade is needed every 30 calendar days. Inactivity is a hard breach. Reactivation can be requested until 30 Nov 2026 only.

News trading on the funded account. No trades in the 10-minute window around major news: 5 minutes before and 5 after. That covers opening and closing trades, stop loss, take profit, and buy stop and sell stop orders. The profit of that trade is deducted. This is a soft breach and does not breach your account. Take profits on trades held at least 48 hours are exempt.

Inactivity. Place a new trade within 30 calendar days of your last closed trade. The trade must stay open at least 1 minute. While a trade is open the account counts as active, but pending orders do not. Inactivity is a hard breach. If the account is deactivated, you can request reactivation until November 30th, 2026. From December 1st, 2026 reactivation is no longer possible, even if the breach happened before that date.

Positions held overnight can incur swap charges unless you have the Swap-Free add-on.

Breaches

Reaching a loss limit is a hard breach and closes the account. News trading on a funded account and the first hedging detection across accounts are soft breaches: you get a warning or a profit deduction and the account stays open. A second hedging detection is a hard breach. Inactivity is also a hard breach: reactivation can be requested until November 30th, 2026 only.
