Rule Details
1. Minimum Trading Days

You must trade for at least 5 days per stage. These do not need to be consecutive.

A day counts as a trading day when at least one trade remains open for a minimum of 1 minute (60 seconds) — regardless of whether the trade closes in profit or loss.

2. Daily Drawdown

This is the maximum loss allowed in a single trading day. If your balance or equity hits this level at any point during the day, your account will be considered breached and terminated.

The daily limit resets every day between 11:30 PM – 11:59 PM CET (rollover). It is not recommended to trade during this window.

For a detailed explanation with examples, check our How Does My Daily Permitted Loss Work? article.

3. Maximum Drawdown

This is the absolute maximum loss allowed on your account at any point. If your balance or equity reaches this level, the account will be terminated immediately.

Static — fixed from the original account balance, does not move.

Trailing — follows the high-water mark (highest equity ever reached). Once the equity grows by 6% above the initial balance, the limit locks at the original balance and stops moving.

After the Evaluation

Once you've passed all stages and our Risk Team has reviewed your account, you'll need to provide a valid ID document (passport, national ID card, or driving license) to complete the KYC process and finalize your funded account agreement.

Important Notes

BrightFunded does not offer services to individuals under 18 years of age, those listed on sanction lists, persons with criminal records related to financial crime or terrorism, or individuals previously excluded due to a breach of the BrightFunded Account Agreement.

Detection of any of these conditions may result in immediate account termination.
