What is Instant?

No evaluation. Your account is funded from the purchase. Rules, drawdown and payout requirements at a glance.

Updated yesterday

Instant is the BrightFunded model with no evaluation. Your account is funded from the purchase, so you can start trading right away and request payouts as soon as you meet the payout requirements. The profit split starts at 90%.

How it works

Buy your account. Choose your account size and add-ons. There is no profit target and no evaluation phase.

KYC. If you have not passed KYC before buying, your account stays paused until you complete it from the Verification section of your dashboard. Once it is approved, your account is activated automatically.

Trade. Follow the loss limits and trading rules from day one.

Payouts. Request a payout when you meet all the payout requirements at the same time.

How your loss limits work

Daily loss (3%). It is calculated on the higher value between your balance and equity at the start of the day. On a $100,000 account your equity cannot drop below $97,000 that day. It resets every day at the rollover, between 11:30 PM and 11:59 PM CET.

Max loss (5% trailing). The limit follows the highest balance or equity you reach. It starts at $95,000 on a $100,000 account. When you reach $105,000, the limit locks at $100,000 and stops moving.

Max floating loss (1%). The total open loss of all your positions together cannot reach 1% of your starting size. On a $100,000 account that is $1,000. It is checked in real time and it breaches the account even if you have not closed a trade.

Payout requirements

You need to meet all of these at the same time. There is no payout cycle.

Requirement

	

What it means




Profit buffer

	

3% of account size, built before your first payout and kept in the account




Minimum payout

	

1% of account size ($1,000 on $100K)




Consistency

	

No single day above 15% of your total profit in the cycle. It resets when you request a payout




Profitable days

	

5 days with a profit of at least 0.5%




Win and loss

	

Biggest losing trade not larger than your biggest winning trade

Add-ons

The 90% profit split is already included. You can add the 100% Payout Split, the 100% Account Fee Refund and Swap-Free when you buy the account.
