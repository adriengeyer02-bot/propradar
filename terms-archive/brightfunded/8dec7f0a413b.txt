Challenge rules

Reach the 10% profit target without breaching a loss limit. There is no minimum number of trading days and no time limit.

 

Daily loss: 3%

It is calculated on the higher value between your balance and equity at the start of the day. If your balance or equity reaches the limit at any point in the day, the account is breached. On $100,000 the daily loss is $3,000.

The daily limit resets every day between 11:30 PM and 11:59 PM CET (rollover). We do not recommend trading in that window.

Max loss: 6% trailing

The limit follows your highest equity in real time, not only at the end of the day. It stops moving once it reaches your starting balance.

Floating profit counts. With a $4,000 floating profit your equity is $104,000 and the limit moves to $98,000. If that trade reverses and closes at a $3,500 loss, your balance is $96,500, below the limit, and the account is breached.

 

Funded account rules

When you pass the challenge, complete your KYC from the Verification section of your dashboard. Once it is approved, your account goes through a Security Check by our Risk Team.

Rule

After a payout

	

A new account is issued. On cTrader the payout is deducted from your balance, but it still counts as a new account

The funded account uses the same parameters as the challenge: 3% daily loss and 6% trailing max loss.

Inactivity

	

Limited

	

A new trade is needed every 30 calendar days. Inactivity is a hard breach. Reactivation can be requested until 30 Nov 2026 only.

News trading on the funded account. No trades in the 10-minute window around major news: 5 minutes before and 5 after. That covers opening and closing trades, stop loss, take profit, and buy stop and sell stop orders. The profit of that trade is deducted. This is a soft breach and does not breach your account. Take profits on trades held at least 48 hours are exempt.

 

Inactivity. Place a new trade within 30 calendar days of your last closed trade. The trade must stay open at least 1 minute. While a trade is open the account counts as active, but pending orders do not. Inactivity is a hard breach. If the account is deactivated, you can request reactivation until November 30th, 2026. From December 1st, 2026 reactivation is no longer possible, even if the breach happened before that date.

 

Positions held overnight can incur swap charges unless you have the Swap-Free add-on.

 

Breaches

Reaching a loss limit is a hard breach and closes the account. News trading on a funded account and the first hedging detection across accounts are soft breaches: you get a warning or a profit deduction and the account stays open. A second hedging detection is a hard breach. Inactivity is also a hard breach: reactivation can be requested until November 30th, 2026 only.
