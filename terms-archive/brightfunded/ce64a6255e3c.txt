Which model is right for me?

Compare 1-Step Daily, 2-Step Bright and Instant side by side and choose the one that fits how you trade.

Updated yesterday

BrightFunded offers three account models. They differ in how you get funded, how much room you have to lose, and how and when you can request payouts. Use this guide to choose the one that fits your trading style.

Accounts bought before 16 Nov 2026 keep the rules they were bought with. You can find them in the OLD plans folder.

Which one fits you?
Choose 1-Step Daily if…

You want the fastest path to a funded account and the freedom to request a payout any day. There is only one phase, no minimum trading days and no consistency rule. The trade-off is a tighter trailing max loss of 6%, which follows your highest equity in real time. After each payout you receive a new account.

Choose 2-Step Bright if…

You prefer more room to trade and a loss limit that never moves. The max loss is a static 8% from your starting balance and the daily loss is 4%. You have two phases with achievable targets (8% and 5%), no time limit and no consistency rule. Payouts come every 14 days, or every 7 days with the Weekly Payouts add-on.

Choose Instant if…

You want to trade a funded account from day one without an evaluation, with a 90% profit split included. The rules are stricter: 5% trailing max loss, 3% daily loss, a 1% max floating loss, a 15% consistency rule and no positions over the weekend. To request a payout you need to meet all the payout requirements at the same time. If you have not passed KYC when you buy, the account stays paused until you complete it.

Add-ons

Add-ons are optional and depend on the model. See "Add-ons by model" for the full list: 90% and 100% Payout Split, 100% Account Fee Refund, Swap-Free, Weekly Payouts and No Min. Trading Days.
