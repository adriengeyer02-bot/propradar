Instead of risking your own money, you prove your skills through an evaluation, then trade the firm's capital and keep up to 80–100% of the profits. Your only cost is the evaluation fee, which is refunded after your first successful payout.How does a prop firm challenge work?You choose an account size, pay a one-time evaluation fee, and trade a simulated account under the firm's rules — hitting a profit target without breaching drawdown limits. Once you pass, you complete KYC verification and receive your funded account. At FundingTraders, you can choose a 1-Step, 2-Step PRO6, or 2-Step PRO10 evaluation, or skip the challenge entirely with Instant Funded.How do prop firm payouts work?Once funded, you trade and earn a Reward Share — 80–90% by default at FundingTraders, with upgrades to 100% available. On 1-Step PRO, 2-Step PRO6, and 2-Step PRO10 accounts, the default payout schedule is every 21 days, with a 14-day payout schedule available as an add-on. On Instant Accounts, payouts can be requested every 14 days. Withdraw via Crypto (USDT, min $50) or bank transfer via Rise (min $200). Your evaluation fee is refunded with your first payout.What is the difference between Instant Account and Evaluation Accounts?Evaluation accounts (1-Step PRO, 2-Step PRO6, 2-Step PRO10) require you to pass a challenge first but come with more flexible funded rules — including news trading with restrictions, no consistency score on PRO6/PRO10 (50% on 1-Step PRO), and a default payout schedule of every 21 days (14-day available as add-on). Instant Account gives you immediate access to capital with no challenge and a higher default Reward Share (90%), but tighter rules — including no news trading, a 15% consistency score, a 3% non-withdrawable safety cushion, and payouts every 14 days.What are the news trading rules?News trading rules depend on your account type. On Instant Funded accounts, news trading is not allowed — opening, closing, or holding positions within the restricted window around high-impact news events is a Hard Breach. On 1-Step, 2-Step PRO6, and 2-Step PRO10 accounts, news trading is unrestricted during the challenge phase. On funded 1-Step, 2-Step PRO6, and 2-Step PRO10 accounts, you may not open or close trades within 2 minutes of high-impact events, and no more than 30% of total profits can come from news-influenced trades.How do crypto payments and payouts work?You can pay for any account using crypto at checkout. For payouts, go to the Payouts tab in your dashboard, select Crypto, and enter your USDT (ERC-20) wallet address. Minimum crypto payout is $50, and processing takes 2–5 business days. If you originally paid with crypto, your evaluation fee refund is also sent in USDT-ERC20 with your first payout.What are the copy trading rules?You are allowed to manually copy your own trades across your own FundingTraders accounts. Using copy trading software between FT Instant Accounts is not allowed. Copying trades to or from another trader's account — manually or through signals — is strictly prohibited and will result in termination of all your accounts.What is the Consistency Score?The Consistency Score ensures your profits are spread across multiple trading days, not concentrated in one big win. Your highest single-day profit cannot exceed a set percentage of your total profits — 15% on Instant Accounts and 50% on 1-Step PRO funded accounts. 2-Step PRO6 and 2-Step PRO10 accounts do not have a consistency score requirement.
