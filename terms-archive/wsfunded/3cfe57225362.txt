(1 PHASE) Wall Street POWER
October 15, 2026
Rules Summary


​Structure: 1 Phase

Balances: $5K, $10K, $25K, $50K and $100K

Maximum Drawdown: 6%

Daily Drawdown: 3%

Profit Target Phase 1: 6%

Minimum Days: 4 days (Phase 1 only)
​Withdrawal Requirement: 3 profitable days with at least 0.50% profit (Funded only)

Consistency: 20%Only at Funded

Payouts: First payout after 14 days; recurring payouts every 14 days

Platform: MT5 only

Purchase Price: Very low initial price of $9.99; the remaining amount is billed when moving to Funded

Add-ons: NO ADD-ONS
​
​Maximum Trailing Drawdown (FUNDED Phase Only) will be 6%

Profit Target

In Phase 1, the trader must achieve a 6% profit target.

Withdrawal Requirement

The trader must achieve a 0.50% profit on at least 3 trading days.
This requirement applies only to the Funded stage.

 

Once you have met the phase objectives, you can proceed with KYC verification via your dashboard. The process may take up to 48 hours to complete.

 

If everything is in order, the button to proceed with payment for the funded account will be enabled. 

You have a maximum of 30 days to redeem the account once the payment button becomes available.

Along with your login credentials, you will receive a contract that must be signed to activate your account. Funded simulated accounts are activated on Wednesdays and Sundays.

Drawdown Rules
Daily Drawdown – 3%

The account may not lose more than 3% during a trading day.

If there is an open position, the limit is calculated based on the floating equity.

If there are no open positions, the limit is calculated based on the closed balance at the start of each trading day.

This limit resets daily.

Maximum Drawdown – 6%

The account may not fall more than 6% below the initial account balance.

Maximum Risk per Trading Idea

The maximum risk allowed for a single trading idea is 1% of the account balance.

Consistency Rule

The consistency rule is set at 20%. No single trading day's profit may exceed 20% of the total profits made.

Payouts

The first payout is available 14 days after becoming Funded.

Subsequent payouts are available every 14 days.

Platform

The Power challenge is available on MT5 only.
