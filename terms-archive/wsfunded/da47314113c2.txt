(2 PHASES) Wall Street CLASSIC

Features

September 15, 2026

Phase 1 - Objective 8%

Phase 2 - Objective 5% (

No maximum time

Minimum of 4 trading days

Daily Drawdown: 5% (The account cannot lose more than 5% of the floating equity if there is an open position; otherwise, 5% of the closed balance with which it starts each day. This limit resets daily.)

Maximum Drawdown 8% (Static) (The account (balance/equity) cannot fall more than 8% from the initial balance with which the account was purchased; this limit is static and does not move.)

Payment Split: 80% - 20%

Maximum risk per trading idea: 50% of the daily drawdown

Inactivity time: 30 days

EAs (Expert Advisors): Allowed

15 days for payment, recurring payments 10 days

Stop Loss (SL): All trades must have the Stop-Loss (SL) added within a maximum of two minutes from the time the position is opened.

 A balanced path to funding: two phases with progressive targets and a static maximum drawdown of 8%. Designed for those who prefer to split the target into two stages rather than concentrating it into a single one.

 

 

For what type of trader?

 

 

 

Traders who prefer a progressive path to funding, clearing two distinct targets (8% and 5%) while maintaining a static 8% drawdown limit throughout the process.

 

 

 

Key point

 

 

 

Classic is the middle-ground option in the lineup: moderate targets, a generous drawdown allowance, and a two-phase structure that allows for the progressive validation of a trading strategy.
