(2 PHASES) Wall Street ULTRA
(2 PHASES) Wall Street ULTRA

Features

September 15, 2026

Phase 1 - Objective 10%

Phase 2 - Objective 5% 

No maximum time 

Minimo di 4 giorni di trading

Daily Drawdown: 5% (The account cannot lose more than 5% of the floating equity if there is an open position; otherwise, 5% of the closed balance with which it starts each day. This limit resets daily.)

Drawdown massimo 10% (statico) (Il conto (saldo/equity) non può scendere di oltre l’10% rispetto al saldo iniziale con cui è stato acquistato il conto; questo limite è statico e non si muove.)

Payment Split: 80% - 20% 

Rischio massimo per idea di trading: 50% del drawdown giornaliero

Inactivity time: 30 days 

EAs (Expert Advisors):

Allowed 15 days for payment, recurring payments 10 days

Stop Loss (SL): All trades must have the Stop-Loss (SL) added within a maximum of two minutes from the time the position is opened.

 

Designed for traders who prioritize having more operating room on their path to funding. It retains the two-phase structure but increases the total maximum drawdown to 10%.

 

 

 

 

 

Who is this for?

 

 

 

Traders who want the largest drawdown buffer among WSF evaluation challenges and prefer that extra margin, even if it means hitting a 10% target in the first phase.

 

 

 

 

 

Key feature

 

 

 

Its distinguishing factor is the 10% static maximum drawdown—the largest total margin among WSF’s standard evaluation challenges.
