What is the max daily loss?

If there are no open trades at 5 PM, the drawdown is calculated based on the closed balance.

This rule applies exclusively to funded accounts. Every trade must remain open for at least 2 minutes. It follows the same disciplined approach used by professional traders in major trading institutions worldwide and ensures that each position has enough time to capture genuine market movement.

This policy helps maintain a fair, transparent, and professional trading environment for everyone.

This rule forms part of the foundation that separates disciplined traders from impulsive ones, creating a trusted ecosystem where strategy, skill, and patience lead to real, lasting success.

Consistency rules

Good news first: there is no consistency rule during your evaluation. On every model, across every phase, your best day is never held against you while you work toward your target. The rule applies only once you are funded, and only as a payout condition.

WHERE IT APPLIES

MODEL	CONSISTENCY THRESHOLD
1 Step, 2 Step, 3 Step	25% (funded accounts only)
Instant Standard	15%
Elite range	None, ever

HOW IT WORKS

No single trading day should account for more than the threshold of your total profit in a payout period. If one day sits above it, you simply keep trading. As your total profit grows, that day's share falls, and the condition clears itself.

WHAT IF ONE DAY GOES OVER?

Your account stays active and safe. This is a payout condition, not a breach: nothing is closed, nothing is removed, and no profit is lost. You keep trading as normal and request your payout once your best day sits below the threshold.

PREFER NO CONSISTENCY RULE AT ALL?

The Elite range has none, in the evaluation and on the funded account. One trading day can be your entire profit and your payout still goes through in full. See the *Elite range* entry.

Trade Risk Limit

Your combined loss on a single trade idea is capped at 2% of your starting balance (1.5% on Elite accounts).

A trade idea groups together:

• A single position

• Several positions on the same instrument in the same direction

• A position reopened on the same instrument and direction within 10 minutes of closing the previous one

Realised and floating losses both count toward the limit.

ACCOUNT SIZE	MAXIMUM LOSS PER TRADE IDEA	ON ZERO ACCOUNTS
$10,000	$200	$150
$25,000	$500	$375
$50,000	$1,000	$750
$100,000	$2,000	$1,500
$200,000	$4,000	$3,000

Example — $100,000 account

You close a long EUR/USD at −$1,400, then reopen long EUR/USD five minutes later and lose another $800. Combined, that trade idea lost $2,200 — over the $2,000 limit, even though neither position exceeded it on its own.

Waiting more than 10 minutes, or switching instrument or direction, starts a fresh trade idea.
