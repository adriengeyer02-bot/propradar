Using VPN / VPS
Using VPN / VPS
October 16, 2026

To ensure the security and integrity of all trading accounts, every trader must be the sole authorized user of their account.

You can use a VPN or a VPS to access your account – for example to run your EA around the clock.

If your account is accessed from different locations – for example your own computer and a VPS abroad – your payout may get a manual check by our team. This is a normal check, not a violation.

Any violation of the single-user policy is considered a breach of the Terms & Conditions (T&C).

The only condition is that you are the only person using your account:

Only you log in – from your own devices or your own VPS.

You do not share your account or allow anyone else to log in.

VPN / VPS During KYC Verification

To maintain data security and ensure accurate identification during the KYC / AML verification process, the use of VPN or VPS connections is strictly prohibited.

Verification must be completed from your actual location using your own device.

If a VPN or VPS is detected during the verification:

The KYC process will not be completed,

Your account may not be funded, or it may be disabled or breached afterward.
