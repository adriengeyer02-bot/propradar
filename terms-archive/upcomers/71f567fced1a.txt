Profit split

Keep up to 100% of everything you earn, depending on the profit split selected at checkout. Here’s how it works.

Updated over a week ago
The Short Answer

Your profit split is the percentage of eligible payout profit you receive. At checkout you choose 80%, 90% or 100%, with 90% usually preselected. The selected option affects the purchase price and stays linked to that account.

 

This applies to every program on CFD, Futures and Perpetuals, including Horizon, Odyssey and Apex. These three programs had a fixed 80% split until November 17, 2026; accounts bought before that date keep it.

 

Existing accounts: Accounts bought under earlier terms retain the split assigned at purchase. The account terms shown in your Dashboard apply.

15% Challenge Phase Bonus

On top of your funded profits, you can also earn a bonus from your Challenge phase performance.

 

Once you achieve 20% cumulative growth on your funded account, you become eligible for a 15% Profit Share Bonus calculated from your Challenge phase profits.

 

How it works:

Trade your funded account and reach 20% total growth

Contact our support team to claim your bonus

The bonus will be added to your next payout

This structure rewards traders who demonstrate long-term consistency. You benefit not only from your live trading performance but also from your success during the evaluation stage.

 

Important: The bonus is not applied automatically. You must contact support to claim it after reaching the 20% growth milestone.

 

Eligibility condition: Your entire funded trading history must have a clean payout record. If at any point during the funded phase you receive a Partially Approved or Declined payout, you permanently lose eligibility for the 15% Challenge Phase Bonus

Why a Variable Profit Split?

A profit split and a payout cap are different. Your split determines your share of an eligible withdrawal. The payout cap limits the amount that may be requested in a payout cycle. A higher split does not remove caps, minimum profit, qualifying days, consistency requirements, or a payout schedule.

 

For example, an eligible $1,000 gross withdrawal at an 80% split produces an $800 share before processing fees. At 90%, the share is $900; at 100%, it is $1,000. The requested withdrawal must still fit the rules and cap for your account.

 

See CFD and Perpetuals payout structure or Futures payout conditions for the separate withdrawal requirements.
