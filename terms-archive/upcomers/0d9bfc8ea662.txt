What Are the Differences Between Upcomers Programs?

11 programs. One philosophy: give traders better conditions than anyone else on the market. Here's every rule, every number, every difference, side by side.

Updated this week

Upcomers offers 11 programs. Five are step-based Challenges that lead to a Funded account, three are Instant Funding programs, and three are time-limited instant funding formats available in your Upcomers dashboard only. Unless stated otherwise, all rules and parameters described here refer to the Classic versions of each program. Turbo and Legacy variants operate under different conditions - see their respective program pages for full details.

This article compares all of them. No marketing. Just facts.

All numbers in this article are for CFD accounts. Horizon and Odyssey follow exactly the same rules on Perpetuals. Futures programs on Upcomers X have their own rules – see Upcomers Futures programs overview.

Individual Program Highlights

Ash. Half-Step Challenge

The gentlest way in. 2% profit target. That's it. The lowest target of any Upcomers program. No minimum trading days. $5K to $1M accounts. If you've never traded with a prop firm before, start here. Prove you can hit 2%, and the door opens.

News Trading: Allowed. Weekend Holding: Allowed. Funded up to: $1,000,000.

Thunderbolt. 1-Phase Challenge

One phase, one target, the fastest path to funded trading we offer. 5% profit target, 6% Dynamic Risk Shield, 1:125 leverage on forex. No minimum trading days. For traders who trade with conviction.

News Trading: Allowed. Weekend Holding: Allowed. Funded up to: $2,000,000.

Phoenix. 2-Phase Challenge

6% Static drawdown in Challenge. The only program that stays Static when you're funded. Your stop-out is fixed. It never chases your equity. It never moves against you. No minimum trading days. If you want a line in the sand that never shifts, Phoenix is it.

News Trading: Allowed. Weekend Holding: Allowed. Funded up to: $2,000,000.

Horizon (CFD & Perpetuals). 1-Phase Challenge, no consistency rule

One phase, 9% profit target, and no consistency rule – not in the challenge, not on the funded account. 3% daily drawdown, 6% Dynamic Risk Shield, 1.5% max single trade loss, at least 2 trading days to pass. Once funded: 3% minimum profit before each payout, payouts every 14 days, profit split up to 100%. For traders who don't want any rule about how their profit is spread across days. Horizon on Futures has different rules: payouts are on demand, with no 14-day cycle. Once you have 3% profit since your last payout and 5 profitable trading days, you can request a payout at any time – see Futures: 1-Step Horizon Complete Rules & Overview.

Funded up to: $2,000,000 on CFD. Full Horizon rules

Odyssey (CFD & Perpetuals). 2-Phase Challenge, no consistency rule

Two phases, 6% + 6%, and no consistency rule anywhere. 4% daily drawdown, 6% Dynamic Risk Shield, 2% max single trade loss, at least 2 trading days in each phase. Once funded: 3% minimum profit before each payout, payouts every 14 days, profit split up to 100%.

Funded up to: $2,000,000 on CFD. Full Odyssey rules

Vanguard. Instant Funding

No challenge. No evaluation. No profit target. No waiting. You sign up, you trade, you keep up to 100%. From day one. 8% Dynamic Risk Shield, 2% max single trade loss, $2M maximum account size. For experienced traders who don't need an evaluation to prove they can trade.

News Trading: Allowed. Weekend Holding: Allowed. Funded up to: $2,000,000.

Oracle. Instant Funding

One of the most accessible programs we offer. No challenge, no evaluation, no profit target. The tradeoff: tighter limits. 2% max single trade loss. 20% best day rule. 5% Dynamic Risk Shield. Built for traders who are disciplined by nature, not by requirement.

News Trading: Allowed. Weekend Holding: Allowed. Funded up to: $2,000,000.

Apex (CFD). Instant Funding, no Best Day Rule

No challenge, no evaluation and no Best Day Rule: one strong day never holds back your payout. 2% daily drawdown, 3% Dynamic Risk Shield™ (locks at your initial balance) and 1.5% max single trade loss. Once you have 3% profit and 4 profitable trading days (7 on the 4% Max Loss option), you can request a payout every 14 days. Profit split up to 100%. Accounts from $5,000 to $200,000.

Apex on Futures is different: payouts are on demand, with no 14-day cycle. Once you have 6% profit and 5 profitable trading days (each at least 0.5% of your account size) since your last payout, you can request a payout at any time. See Futures: Apex (Instant Funding) Complete Rules & Overview.

News Trading: Allowed. Weekend Holding: Allowed. Funded up to: $2,000,000 on CFD, $500,000 on Perpetuals. Full Apex rules

SUPERNOVA. 24-Hour Instant Funding

One session. 24 hours. 3% target. Fixed payout caps based on account size. The clock starts when you open your first trade. Hit the target, meet the rules, and walk away with up to 100% of your eligible net profit within the payout cap. That simple.

Uses the Nonstop Dynamic Risk Shield™ (never locks, always trails). News Trading: Allowed. Weekend Holding: Allowed. Funded up to: $200,000.

HYPERNOVA. 4-Hour Instant Funding

Four hours. One account. One shot. HYPERNOVA exists for traders who don't need a full day to read the market. Same rules as SUPERNOVA, including fixed payout caps based on account size, compressed into a 4-hour window with a 2% profit target. If you can find the setup and execute with precision, four hours is more than enough.

Uses the Nonstop Dynamic Risk Shield™ (never locks, always trails). News Trading: Allowed. Weekend Holding: Allowed. Funded up to: $200,000.

Ultranova. 1-Hour Instant Funding

One hour. One account. One shot. Ultranova is the most compressed instant funding program at Upcomers, built for high-conviction traders who can identify a setup, execute with discipline, and manage risk in a single hour.

Same core structure as Supernova and Hypernova, including fixed payout caps based on account size, compressed to 60 minutes with a 2% profit target. Uses the Nonstop Dynamic Risk Shield™ (never locks, always trails).

News Trading: Allowed. Weekend Holding: Allowed. Funded up to: $200,000.

Profit split note: The standard Upcomers profit split is 90%. Depending on the option selected at checkout, it can be adjusted to 80% or 100%. Changing the profit split also changes the Challenge price. The selected profit split remains linked to that account. Accounts purchased under previous conditions retain the profit split assigned at the time of purchase. This applies to every program, including Horizon, Odyssey and Apex.
