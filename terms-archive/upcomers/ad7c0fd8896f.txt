1-step Horizon Challenge (CFD) Complete Rules & Overview

Our 1-Step evaluation with no consistency rule. One target, then funded.

Updated over a week ago

This article is about CFD accounts (MT5, TradeLocker, Match-Trader, cTrader). Trading futures on Upcomers X? See Futures: 1-Step Horizon Complete Rules & Overview. Perpetuals: see 1-step Horizon Challenge (Perpetuals).

Horizon is our 1-phase challenge with no consistency rule, in the challenge or on the funded account. One evaluation, one target. Hit 9% and you are funded. Account sizes from $5,000 to $2,000,000.

Available Account Sizes

$5,000 · $10,000 · $25,000 · $50,000 · $100,000 · $200,000 · $300,000 · $500,000 · $1,000,000 · $1,500,000 · $2,000,000

Challenge Phase Rules

Profit Target: 9%

Reach 9% profit on your account balance. All positions must be closed before the challenge is considered complete.
​

Daily Drawdown: 3%

Maximum loss allowed within a single trading day (00:00 to 23:59 UTC). On a $100,000 account, your maximum daily loss is $3,000.
​

Dynamic Risk Shield™: 6%

Your account is protected by our Dynamic Risk Shield™, a drawdown mechanism that moves up with your profits but never goes down.
​

It tracks your account's highest equity point (High Water Mark). The drawdown limit is always 6% below this peak. As your equity grows, the shield rises with it. Once the shield rises, it stays at that level permanently and never decreases. The maximum shield level equals your initial balance.
​

Minimum Trading Days: 2

A day counts as a trading day only when it reaches at least 0.5% profit, calculated from the initial balance. On a $100,000 account, that is $500.
​

Consistency: none

There is no consistency rule in the challenge.

How to Pass the Challenge

Reach at least 9% net profit over at least 2 trading days. Stay above the 3% Daily Drawdown and the 6% Dynamic Risk Shield™ at all times.

Close all positions before completion.

Once you meet all conditions, you are eligible for a funded account.

Funded Phase. This Is Where It Counts

You passed.

Now the rules tighten, because now the capital is real. And there is still no consistency rule.
​

Profit Split: up to 100%

You keep up to 100% of your profit. Your profit split is the option selected at checkout (80%, 90% or 100%). Accounts bought before November 17, 2026 keep the 80% split assigned at purchase.
​

Daily Drawdown: 3%

On a $100,000 funded account, your maximum daily loss is $3,000.
​

Dynamic Risk Shield™: 6%

The same 6% Dynamic Risk Shield™ carries over from the challenge. It tracks your highest equity point, with the drawdown limit fixed 6% below the peak. It only moves upward, never down. Maximum shield level equals your initial balance.
​

Max Single Trade Loss: 1.5%

Your realized loss on any single trade cannot exceed 1.5% of the initial account balance. On a $100,000 account, that is $1,500 maximum loss per trade. On a $500,000 account, it is $7,500.
​

No Consistency Rule

There is no Best Day Rule and no consistency requirement on this account. How your profit is spread across days does not affect your payout.
​

Payout Requirement: 3%

Before requesting a payout, you must generate at least 3% profit calculated from your initial balance. On a $100,000 account, that is $3,000 minimum profit before you can withdraw. The 3% requirement applies between payouts as well.
​

Payout Frequency: every 14 days (bi-weekly)

You can request your first payout 14 days after your funded account starts, and every 14 days after that.

Common Questions

What's the profit target?

9% in the challenge. On the funded account, at least 3% profit from the initial balance is required before each payout.
​

Are there minimum trading days?

Yes. The challenge requires 2 qualifying days at every available size. Funded accounts require 3 days on $5K, $10K, $25K and $50K; 4 days on $100K; and 5 days on $200K and larger accounts. Each qualifying day must reach at least 0.5% realized profit of the initial balance.
​

Is there a consistency rule?

No. This Horizon program has no consistency rule and no Best Day Rule, in the challenge or on the funded account. Horizon Futures also has no consistency rule, in the challenge or on the funded account – see its own rules article.
​

When can I withdraw?

Every 14 days, once you have generated at least 3% profit from your initial balance and met the minimum trading days. (Horizon Futures is different: there is no 14-day cycle, and payouts are on demand once its payout conditions are met.)
​

What breaches my account?

Exceeding the Daily Drawdown limit, falling below the Dynamic Risk Shield™, or exceeding the 1.5% Max Single Trade Loss.
​

What's my profit split?

Up to 100%. Your profit split is the option selected at checkout (80%, 90% or 100%).
