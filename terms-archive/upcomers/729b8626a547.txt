Payout Structure (CFD + Perpetuals)
Updated this week

This guide explains the standard tiered payout structure for CFD and Perpetuals accounts. Your exact cap can depend on the program, variant, account size, and payout number. Product-specific terms take precedence over the general table below.

Trading futures on Upcomers X? See Futures payout structure (payout caps by account size) instead.

Every payout request is reviewed against the account’s eligibility rules and trading history. Submission does not guarantee approval or same-day delivery.

 

Processing and receipt times depend on the review and payout method. See Processing Time and Confirmation for the current timeline.

Why This Matters

Use the cap shown for your account. The table is a general schedule, not a promise that every program and variant uses identical limits.

 

Caps are stated in dollars for each account-size band and payout tier. Do not apply one account’s cap percentage to a different size or program.

 

The standard schedule becomes uncapped from the eighth payout. A product-specific schedule may retain a cap, so check the terms and withdrawal preview for your account.

 

What the cap limits

A payout cap limits the gross amount that can be requested in that cycle. Your profit split and any applicable fees determine the amount you receive. It does not guarantee same-day processing.

How does it work

You do not lose your account or profits when you request a payout.

 

After a payout is completed, remaining profit stays in your account balance and a new payout segment begins. Your next request depends on new eligible profit in that segment: usually at least 1% of its starting equity, or 3% on Horizon and Odyssey. See Payout Segments and Minimum Profit Requirements.

 

Where a consistency rule applies, the new payout segment must meet that rule again. Horizon and Odyssey have no Best Day Rule; session-based programs use their own conditions.

 

Under the standard table, the eighth and later payouts have no tier cap. This does not remove the other eligibility rules, and account-specific caps can still take precedence. Follow the balance and segment shown in your Dashboard.

Who the cap applies to

Payout caps apply even when every trade complies with the rules. They limit the amount available in a cycle; they are not a penalty for poor trading. The account’s schedule determines whether and how the cap progresses.

Payout Cap Progression Policy

An increase in the payout cap is applied only when a withdrawal request is fully approved and the payout cycle is completed without any rule violations.
​

If a withdrawal request is partially approved or declined, the payout cap does not progress. In such cases, the trader remains eligible only for the previous payout cap level on their next withdrawal request.
​

How payout cap progression works

Payout caps are structured to increase step by step, and progression depends on the outcome of the previous payout:

Fully approved withdrawal with no rule violations → payout cap increases to the next level

Partially approved withdrawal → payout cap remains unchanged

Declined withdrawal → payout cap remains unchanged

The payout cap will increase only after a full approval at the current level with no rule violations during the payout cycle.

 

Example: $100,000 funded account

On a $100,000 funded account:

First payout cap: 1% ($1,000)

Second payout cap: 2% ($2,000)

If the first withdrawal request is:

Fully approved with no rule violations → the trader becomes eligible for a 2% ($2,000) payout cap on the next withdrawal request.

Partially approved or declined → the trader remains eligible for 1% ($1,000) on the next withdrawal request

The trader becomes eligible for the 2% payout cap only after a fully approved 1% withdrawal request with no rule violations.

 

Cap percentages are not the same for all account sizes. For example, the standard first cap is $100 on $5,000 (2%) and $100 on $10,000 (1%). Larger accounts use fixed dollar bands. Refer to the dollar table and any program-specific terms, not a universal 1%, 2%, 3% sequence.

 

Important note

Payout cap progression is designed to reward consistent, compliant trading performance. Partial approvals, declined payouts, or payouts involving a rule violation do not reset progress, but they also do not unlock the next payout tier.

Payout processing fees

Each payout is subject to a processing fee, which depends on the selected payout method. These fees cover the operational costs of handling and delivering your payout.

 

Bank Transfer (IBAN): fee of 19.9 USD + 2.49% processing fee

Cryptocurrency: fee of 19.9 USD + 10% processing fee

If more than 50% of your orders were paid in cryptocurrency, you can withdraw your payout in crypto for the $19.90 base fee plus 2.49%, the same rate as a bank transfer. The 10% crypto fee does not apply to you.

The fee is deducted from the payout amount before funds are transferred.

Use the cap shown for your account. The table is a general schedule, not a promise that every program and variant uses identical limits.

 

Supernova, Hypernova, and Ultranova use fixed single-session payout caps based on account size, so the tiered payout cap progression above does not apply to them.

 

For other CFD and Perpetuals programs, use the applicable standard or program-specific tier schedule. Available programs and variants are shown at checkout; the account’s own terms and withdrawal preview determine its cap.

Summary

Before requesting a payout, check the segment’s eligible profit, current cap, profit split, any qualifying-day or consistency requirement, and the processing fee.
