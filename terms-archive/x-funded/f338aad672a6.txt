Payout Split Policy — Affiliate-Referred Accounts
August 3, 2026

Accounts registered through an affiliate partner (including Expert Advisor providers, introducing brokers, and referral partners) are subject to a modified payout structure:

First Payout

The first approved payout on any Funded Account is paid at the profit split stated in the User’s agreement, up to 100% where applicable.

Subsequent Payouts

All payouts following the first approved payout — regardless of the Funded Account from which they originate — are capped at a base profit split of fifty percent (50%) of the payout amount. The remaining balance is retained by XFunded as an affiliate referral cost.

Scope

This policy applies to the User across all current and future accounts held under the same registration, and survives account resets, upgrades, breaches, and the issuance of new Challenge or Funded Accounts.

Add-ons

The “100% Profit Split” checkout add-on does not apply to affiliate-referred accounts beyond the first payout, unless explicitly stated otherwise at the time of purchase.

Combination with Other Adjustments.

The 50% cap is applied first, before any other payout adjustment permitted under these Terms (including the Free Account Reset Adjustment), which are then deducted from the capped amount.

XFunded reserves the right to verify a User’s referral source at any time and to apply this policy retroactively to any account found to have been referred by an affiliate partner, including by deducting any overpaid amounts from future payouts.
