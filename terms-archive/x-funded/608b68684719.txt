Is There a Maximum Withdrawal?
April 14, 2026

At X-Funded, there is no maximum withdrawal limit for standard accounts. However, if your total account allocation exceeds $1,000,000, a withdrawal cap applies.

In this case, each withdrawal request is limited to 2% of your total account allocation.

Example:

You manage accounts totaling $1,000,000:

$300,000 × 3 accounts
$100,000 × 1 account

Total allocation = $1,000,000

2% of $1,000,000 = $20,000

The maximum withdrawal you can request per payout is $20,000 in total.

If you submit a withdrawal request exceeding this amount, X-Funded will process only the maximum allowed ($20,000), and any excess amount will not be processed and cannot be recovered.
