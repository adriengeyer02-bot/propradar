How do Rewards work?
September 7, 2026

Traders become eligible to request their first payout 14 days after placing their first trade on the funded account.
​

After a payout is approved, the next payout becomes available 14 days after the first trade placed after that withdrawal.
​

To request a payout, the account balance must be above the initial account balance, there must be no rule violations, and all trades and pending orders must be closed.
​

You can request a payout for any amount you choose, as long as it meets the minimum payout requirement. You do not need to withdraw all available profits.
​

AquaFunded offers a 24 business hour payout guarantee. If your approved payout is not processed within 24 business hours, we will add an extra $1,000.
​
The minimum funded account payout is $100.​
​

Reward via Crypto
​

Crypto payouts are available for amounts under $5,000.
​
​Reward via Rise
​

Payouts of $500 or more can be processed through Rise.

 

Rise allows traders to withdraw by bank transfer or crypto.

 

All AquaFunded payouts are subject to a 3% processing fee. This fee is deducted from the requested payout amount during processing.

 

 
Profit Withdrawal Limits for Initial Rewards (100K Accounts and Above)


For traders with $100K accounts or larger, the first two reward requests are capped at a maximum withdrawal of $10,000 each.

 

After the first two rewards, this restriction is removed, and there are no further withdrawal limits.
​

Why Are These Limits Necessary?

These limits are designed to support a fair and consistent trading environment while allowing our team to properly evaluate each trader’s approach. The time between purchasing a program and receiving the first rewards is often too short for a complete review of trading patterns.

 

By applying these short-term limits, we promote stability, transparency, and long-term success for every trader in the Aqua community.

 

For any questions or further clarification, our support team is always available to help.
