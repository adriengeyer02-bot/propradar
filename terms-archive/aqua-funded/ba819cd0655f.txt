Instant Funding Pro Model

The Instant Funding Standard Model offers immediate access to a funded account with specific rules for managing your trading risks and profits.

Account Violations (Hard Breach)

All Instant funding Models have trailing drawdown.

Hard beach are serious violations to our established rules. A hard breach happens when a trader exceeds the maximum daily loss or total drawdown limit, resulting in the immediate closure of the account.

Max Daily Drawdown – 3%

What is it?
The maximum daily loss limit is 3%.
​

How is it calculated?
The daily drawdown is calculated daily at 00:00 UTC based on your account balance or equity. Your equity must not drop by more than 3% in a single trading day.

Example:
For a $100,000 account, the daily drawdown limit is $3,000. If your account balance or equity drops below $97,000, you will exceed your daily loss limit.

Max Total Drawdown – 6% (Trailing)
​

What is it?
The maximum total drawdown is 6% below the highest equity reached on your account.
​

This is a trailing drawdown, meaning the drawdown limit moves upward whenever your account reaches a new equity high. It will not move back down if your equity later decreases.
​

How is it calculated?

The drawdown limit is calculated using your highest recorded equity, including both closed and floating PnL.
​

If your equity reaches a new high, the drawdown limit will move up and remain 6% below that highest equity level.
​

Example:
​

For a $100,000 account:

If your equity reaches $105,000, your trailing drawdown limit becomes $99,000.

If your equity later drops to $103,000, the drawdown limit remains at $99,000.

The drawdown limit will only move again if your equity reaches a new high above $105,000.

Your trailing drawdown is therefore based on the highest equity reached, not your closed account balance.

Consistency Rule

Limited Time Offer
​
Instant Funding Pro accounts purchased on or after 8 September 2026 will receive a 20% Consistency Rule instead of the standard 15% Consistency Rule for a limited time.
​

Instant Funding Pro accounts purchased before 8 September 2026 will remain subject to the 15% Consistency Rule.
​
​What does this mean?
​

For eligible Instant Funding Pro accounts purchased on or after 8 September 2026, your highest profitable trading day must account for less than 20% of your total profits when requesting a reward.
​

Example:
​

If your highest profitable trading day is $1,500, your total profits must exceed $7,500 before your highest profitable day represents less than 20% of your total profits.
​

What happens if I exceed the 20% rule?
​

Your account will not be breached.
​

You can continue trading and generating profits until your highest profitable trading day represents less than 20% of your total profits.
​

Once the consistency requirement has been met, you will become eligible to request a reward, provided all other reward requirements have also been satisfied.

Maximum Loss Per Trade Policy

If your floating PnL (profit and loss) drops below -2% of the account starting balance, the account will be permanently closed. This ensures strict adherence to risk management protocols and safeguards the integrity of our funding process.

Example:

 

For a $50,000 account balance, if your combined open PnL across all active trades reaches a loss of $1,000 (-2%), the account will be closed immediately.

 

This rule emphasizes the importance of disciplined trading and risk management, ensuring that traders operate within acceptable risk parameters to maintain the longevity of their trading careers.

Consequences for Exceeding the -2% Risk Limit:


- Immediate and permanent closure of the account.

- No further trading activity will be permitted on the breached account
​
​Important Note for $300k & $400k Accounts

 

For $300,000 and $400,000 accounts, a stricter floating risk limit applies.

If your floating PnL drops below -1% of the account balance, the account will be permanently closed.
