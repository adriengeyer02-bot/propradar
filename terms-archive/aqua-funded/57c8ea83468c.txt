Instant Funding Standard
October 22, 2026

The Instant Funding Standard Model gives traders immediate access to a funded account without completing an evaluation.
​

The model is designed as our most affordable Instant Funding option while still requiring traders to demonstrate consistent trading and responsible risk management

Account Violations (Hard Breach)

A hard breach occurs when a trader exceeds either the 3% Maximum Daily Loss or the 5% Maximum Loss, resulting in the immediate closure of the account.

Daily Drawdown – 3%

What does this mean?

The maximum daily loss allowed is 3%.
​

How is it calculated?
​

At 00:00 UTC each day, we use the higher of your account balance or equity at the daily reset.
​

Your Daily Drawdown Limit is set at 3% of your previous days ending account balance/equity below this value and remains fixed until the next daily reset.
​

Example:
​

If your balance is $102,000 and your equity is $103,000 at 00:00 UTC, your Daily Drawdown Limit for the new trading day is:
​

$103,000 - 3% ($3,090) = $99,910

Maximum Loss Limit: 5% Trailing

What does this mean?
​

The Maximum Loss Limit is a 5% trailing loss limit that can move upwards as your account grows.
​

How is it calculated?
​

At 00:00 UTC each day, we use the higher of your account balance or equity at the daily reset.
​

Your Maximum Loss Limit is set 5% of your initial account balance below this value.
​

If this creates a higher Maximum Loss Limit than the previous day, the limit will move upwards. Once the Maximum Loss Limit has moved upwards, it cannot move back down.
​

Example:
​

For a $100,000 account, 5% is $5,000 and your initial Maximum Loss Limit is $95,000.
​

If your account finishes a trading day with a balance of $102,000 and equity of $103,000, the higher value at the 00:00 UTC reset is $103,000.
​

Your new Maximum Loss Limit would therefore be:

$103,000 - $5,000 = $98,000
​

If your account later decreases, the Maximum Loss Limit remains at $98,000. It will only move again when a future daily reset produces a higher limit.

 

Consistency Rule

What does this mean?
​

The 15% Consistency Rule is designed to encourage consistent trading.

Your highest profitable trading day must account for less than 15% of your total profits when requesting a reward.
​

Example:

If your highest profitable trading day is $1,500, your total profits must exceed $10,000 before that trading day represents less than 15% of your total profits.
​

You will need to continue trading until your highest profitable day falls below the required 15% threshold before requesting a reward.
​

What happens if I violate the 15% rule?
​

Your account will not be breached.
​

You can continue trading and generating profits until your highest profitable trading day represents less than 15% of your total profits.
​

Once the consistency requirement has been met, you will become eligible to request a reward, provided all other requirements have also been satisfied.

Maximum Loss Per Trade Policy

The Maximum Loss Per Trade Policy applies to funded accounts only and is designed to encourage disciplined risk management.
​

If your combined floating PnL reaches -1% of your account starting balance, the following progression will apply.
​

First Violation

Your reward split will be reduced to 50%.

Your account will remain active and you may continue trading.
​

Second Violation

Your reward split will be reduced to 25%.

Your account will remain active and you may continue trading.
​

Third Violation

Your funded account will be permanently breached.
​

Example

For a $100,000 account, if your combined floating PnL across all open positions reaches -$1,000 (-1%), this will count as a violation of the Maximum Loss Per Trade Policy.
​

The progression is:

First violation: Reward split reduced to 50%

Second violation: Reward split reduced to 25%

Third violation: Account permanently breached

This policy is separate from your Daily Drawdown Limit and Maximum Loss Limit. Traders must remain within all account risk parameters at all times.​

Minimum Trading Days 

There is a minimum requirement of 5 qualifying trading days before you can request a reward.
​

Each qualifying trading day must achieve at least 0.5% profit based on the initial account balance.
​

These days do not need to be consecutive.
​

Example:

For a $100,000 account, a trading day must generate at least $500 in profit to count as one of your 5 qualifying trading days.
