Instant Funding Standard MODEL
Discover our consistency rule and how it works

The consistency rule applies to AquaFunded instant funded challenges. Please make sure to read the below carefully before you start trading.

 

On instant funded challenges a consistency rule of 15% applies, that means that one trading day can not equal or be greater than 15% of the total profits made.

 

When the profits of one trading day equals or is greater than 15% of total profits made that payout period, you CANNOT request a payout until the highest profit trading day falls below the 20% of total profits made on that account within that particular period.

 

What if I make more than 15% of my total PnL in one day? Will that breach my account?

Your account will not be terminated if you violate the 15% rule, you must continue trading and make more profits until the highest profit trading day falls below the 15% of total profits.

 

For example if you make $1000 in one trading day, you will at least need to reach $5000 in total profits within that period to be able to withdraw profits from your account.

On instant funded challenges, a consistency rule of 20% applies. This means that the profit made in a single trading day cannot equal or exceed 20% of the total profits made during the payout period.

If the profits from one trading day equal or exceed 20% of the total profits, you will not be able to request a payout until the highest profit trading day falls below 20% of the total profits on that account within the payout period.

What if I make more than 20% of my total PnL in one day? Will that breach my account?

 

Your account will not be terminated if you violate the 20% rule. However, you must continue trading and generate more profits until the highest profit trading day falls below 20% of the total profits made during that period.
