The helpdesk built
for AI and humans

Intercom is the AI-powered helpdesk for your support team. It brings every conversation, ticket and channel into one shared Inbox. Copilot works alongside your team on each one, so every teammate is more efficient and every customer gets a better experience.

Start free trial
View demo
What that means
for your team
01
Productivity

Copilot, AI tools and no-code automation in a fast, modern Inbox, so your team ramp quickly and work faster.

02
Collaboration

Fin, the Agent for your customers, and your team work in the same Inbox. Fin asks for a review or an approval when it needs one, and handoffs carry the full context.

03
Integration

Connect 450+ apps like Jira, Slack and Salesforce. Details from those tools - order history, billing, account info - show up next to the conversation, and every channel lands in one Inbox, so your team acts without switching tools.

COLLABORATION
Fin and your team
work together seamlessly

Fin and your team work from the same Inbox, with the same customer history and the same knowledge base. Whether Fin answers or a person does, the context is there and the customer gets one consistent experience.

One Inbox for Fin and your team

When Fin hands a conversation to a teammate, the whole history goes with it: what the customer said, what Fin did and why. Your team picks up where Fin left off, and the customer never starts over.

Learn more
Fin asks your team when it isn't sure

With Human in the Loop, Fin asks a teammate in the Inbox when it needs a review, some information or an approval - with the context and a draft reply ready. Once they weigh in, Fin finishes the job. A teammate can take over at any time.

Learn more
See AI and human performance in one place

Reports, quality checks and monitoring cover Fin and your team together, so you see the whole operation in one place - what Fin resolved, what your team handled, and how customers rated both.

Learn more
INTEGRATION
Integrated with
every tool your team uses

Intercom connects to 450+ apps - Jira, Slack, Salesforce, Shopify, Stripe - and brings every channel into the same Inbox, so your team has the data they need in the conversation and takes action without switching tools.

Learn more
Connect every tool your team uses

Intercom connects to 450+ first- and third-party apps, so your team takes action in Jira, Slack or Salesforce without leaving the Inbox.

Full customer context before the first reply

Order history, billing and account details from tools like Shopify and Stripe sit in the conversation sidebar, so your team knows who they're talking to before they type.

Every channel in one Inbox

Email, live chat, phone, SMS, WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook all route to the same Inbox, so your team moves between channels without changing tools.
