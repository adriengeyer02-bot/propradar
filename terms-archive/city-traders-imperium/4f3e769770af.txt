WE TAKE CONCERNS SERIOUSLY
Complaints Process
We are committed to upholding the highest standards of integrity, compliance, and ethical conduct. If you have a concern, here is exactly what happens after you contact us.
If your complaint concerns a payment or refund, please first review our Refund Policy to confirm your eligibility. Many payment concerns can be resolved directly through our support team without the need for a formal complaint.
1
Submit Your Complaint

Send a detailed email to support@citytradersimperium.com with the subject line Formal Complaint, including your email address, a clear description of the issue, relevant dates, transaction references where applicable, and any supporting documents or screenshots.

2
Acknowledgement

We will acknowledge receipt of your complaint within 1 business day at the latest.

3
Investigation

Our compliance team investigates thoroughly. Most complaints are resolved within 2 business days. Complex matters may take up to 30 business days, and you will be kept informed throughout the process.

4
Resolution

You will receive a written response explaining the outcome, any action taken, and next steps if you remain dissatisfied with the resolution.

5
Escalation

If you are not satisfied with the resolution, you may request escalation to senior management by replying to the response email with Escalation Request in the subject line. Escalated complaints are reviewed within 5 business days.

How To Report Suspicious Activity, Fraud, or Impersonation

If you need to report an issue related to suspicious activity, impersonation, fraud, or any other matter requiring urgent attention, please send a detailed email to support@citytradersimperium.com with all relevant information to assist in a thorough review.

Please include in your report:

✓
A description of the issue.
✓
Screenshots or evidence where possible.
✓
URLs of any suspicious websites or accounts.
✓
The date and time the issue occurred.
