 Static Drawdown

For all programs except Instant Funding Pro and Instant Funding Lite, Max Loss is static — it is based on your initial account balance and does not increase with profits.

 Smart Drawdown

Instant Funding Pro and Instant Funding Lite use a Smart Drawdown system, you can read the full explanation here.

It starts at -10% and automatically adjusts to -5% once you achieve a 5% gain on your account balance. After that point, the drawdown remains fixed at -5% of your starting balance, even if your profits grow.

Example: starting balance: $10,000, initial drawdown limit: $9,000 (-10%). After reaching a 5% gain ($10,500), the new drawdown limit becomes $9,500 (-5%) — and stays fixed going forward.

 IF Micro Lite runs its own version of this model: it starts at 6% of starting balance and locks to 3% once your Balance (not Equity) gains +3%. See the IF Micro Lite FAQ for the full breakdown.

 EOD Drawdown

Evolve: End-of-Day Trailing Drawdown is a loss limit that updates once per day at 5:00 PM EST.

 It is calculated using the account’s highest balance recorded at the end-of-day snapshot. When the account reaches a new highest end-of-day balance, the drawdown limit moves up accordingly.
 The End-of-Day Trailing Drawdown:

Updates once per day at 5:00 PM EST.

Is based on the account’s highest recorded end-of-day balance.

Can move upwards but can never move back down.

Stops trailing once it reaches the account’s original starting balance.

 For example, on a $50,000 account, once the maximum loss limit reaches $50,000, it locks at that level permanently.
