Understanding Reward Frequency and Eligibility for FunderPro Challenges

FunderPro offers various challenges that allow users to earn rewards. This guide explains key components of rewards processes, including frequency and eligibility requirements, for different challenges such as One-Phase, Classic, and Pro.

 

Overview of Rewards Frequencies

FunderPro organizes its rewards structures based on the challenge type. Here's an overview of the rewards frequencies:

One-Phase Challenge: Rewards are available on a bi-weekly basis. This means you can request rewards every 14 days once funded. Daily rewards are not available for One-Phase challenges, and the bi-weekly structure cannot be upgraded.

Classic Challenge: Similar to the One-Phase Challenge, rewards for the Classic Challenge are bi-weekly (every 14 days). Daily rewards are not offered for this challenge type.

Pro Challenge: For accounts in the Pro Challenge, users can request rewards daily with 60% profit split of weekly with 90% profit split (you can select Pro - Daily rewards or Pro - Weekly rewards on the checkout page). To be eligible, you must achieve 1% of your initial account balance before making a reward request. Additionally, Pro – Weekly Rewards accounts are subject to specific trading rules, including a daily drawdown limit of 5% of the starting balance, a maximum overall drawdown of 10%, and a margin usage cap of 20%. Violations of these rules may result in penalties, such as profit removal or account closure.

Minimum Profit Requirements for Rewards

To ensure accessibility and fairness across challenges, FunderPro imposes minimum profit requirements for reward requests:

One-Phase and Classic Challenges: You must earn at least $100 in profit on your funded account before requesting a reward. This applies to both the first reward and any subsequent rewards requests.

Pro Accounts: Rewards are contingent on achieving at least 1% of your initial account balance. This condition enables flexibility for more frequent rewards requests.

Special Notes:

The bi-weekly schedule of the One-Phase and Classic Challenges is fixed and cannot be upgraded to a daily or weekly rewards structure.

The $100 minimum profit applies universally across all rewards requests for One-Phase and Classic challenges, including the very first reward.

For One-Phase and Classic Challenges you can purchase the "First Reward in 7 Days" add-on at checkout, which allows you to claim your first reward after 7 days from getting funded, instead of 14.
