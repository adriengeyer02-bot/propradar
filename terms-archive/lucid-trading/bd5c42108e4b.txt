RISK DISCLOSURE STATEMENT

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Risk Disclosure Statement is incorporated into our Terms of Use and applies to all of our simulated and live trading programs.

Most programs offered by Lucid Trading Group LLC (“Lucid”) operate in a simulated futures trading environment, in which the financial risk of live markets is largely removed but the fees you pay are at risk. For eligible traders we also offer live trading programs, in which real money is traded through third-party brokerages and futures commission merchants and real losses can occur. In either environment, you should be aware of the following risks.

CFTC Rule 4.41

Hypothetical or simulated performance results have certain limitations. Unlike an actual performance record, simulated results do not represent actual trading. Also, because the trades have not actually been executed, the results may have under-or-over compensated for the impact, if any, of certain market factors, such as lack of liquidity. Simulated trading programs, in general, are also subject to the fact that they are designed with the benefit of hindsight. No representation is being made that any account will or is likely to achieve profit or losses similar to those shown.

No Sponsorship

All content published and distributed by Lucid and its affiliates (collectively, the “Company”) is to be treated as general information only. None of the information provided by the Company or contained herein is intended as (a) investment advice, (b) an offer or solicitation of an offer to buy or sell, or (c) a recommendation, endorsement, or sponsorship of any security, company, or fund.

Testimonials appearing on the Company’s website may not be representative of other clients or customers and are not a guarantee of future performance or success. Use of the information contained on the Company’s websites is at your own risk, and the Company and its partners, representatives, agents, employees, and contractors assume no responsibility or liability for any use or misuse of such information.

Program Fees

The fees you pay are the price of access to our programs. They are not deposits, investments, or trading capital, they do not earn a return, and they are not refundable except as stated in our Disclaimer of Liability, Refunds and Chargeback Policy. You should assume that fees may be lost.

Program Rules and Automated Risk Controls

Every account is subject to the rules of its program, which may change. Rules may be enforced automatically and without notice, including by liquidating positions, closing accounts, and withholding payouts.

Emotional and Psychological Factors

Simulated trading does not fully capture the emotional and psychological aspects of live trading. The absence of real financial risk can lead to different decision-making behaviors. You may experience overconfidence, excessive risk-taking, or lack of discipline in a simulated environment, which can significantly differ from behavior in live trading where real money is at stake.

Market Access and Conditions

Lucid provides access to a variety of simulated markets and instruments. However, the availability, conditions, and behavior of these markets may differ from live markets. Factors such as trading hours, liquidity, and market dynamics can vary, affecting the comparability of simulated trading results to live trading outcomes.

No Guarantee of Future Performance

Past performance in the simulated trading environment is not indicative of future results in live trading. Success in simulated trading does not guarantee success in live trading due to the differences outlined in this disclosure.

Engaging in simulated trading with Lucid involves significant risks that you must carefully consider. Use the simulated trading environment as a learning tool, not a predictor of future success. Conduct thorough research, develop sound strategies, and understand your risk tolerance before transitioning to live trading.

Software and Technology

Our programs rely on technology, including trading platforms and market data provided by third parties. Technical issues such as system failures, connectivity problems, and delayed or inaccurate data can affect both simulated and live trading results. Our responsibility for such issues is limited as described in our Disclaimer of Liability, Refunds and Chargeback Policy.

No Guarantee of Funding or Payouts

Passing an evaluation does not guarantee a funded account, and holding a funded or live account does not guarantee any payout. Payouts are subject to the rules of your program and to identity verification, and may be delayed, reduced, or denied while your account is under review. Accounts may be closed, and profits forfeited, for violations of our rules.

Live Trading Accounts

Live accounts trade real money in real markets through third-party firms, and you can lose the entire balance allocated to the account. Live accounts are subject to your live trading agreement and to the third-party firm’s own terms, risk controls, and disclosures.

Day Trading

Day trading is risky and is generally not appropriate for someone with limited resources or experience. You should be prepared to lose all funds you use for day trading. Be skeptical of claims of large profits, as day trading can also lead to substantial and immediate losses. It requires an in-depth understanding of futures markets and strategies, and you will be competing with professional traders. Under certain market or technical conditions, it may be hard or impossible to liquidate positions quickly. Day trading involves frequent transactions, and the resulting commissions can significantly reduce profitability.

Leverage

Futures are leveraged instruments, and a small price movement can produce large gains or losses. In a live account, losses can exceed the funds allocated to the account.

Lucid’s Own Trading Activity

Lucid and its partners may trade for their own accounts in the same markets you trade, and may use aggregated or de-identified information about trading activity in our programs to inform that trading, including by taking positions that mirror or offset simulated activity. Such trading is conducted for our own or our partners’ benefit and may be contrary to positions held in your account. You will not receive any compensation for the use of such information.

Potential Registration Requirements

If you provide trading advice to others or trade on behalf of others, you may be required to register with the Commodity Futures Trading Commission or under federal or state securities laws. Trading on behalf of others through our programs is prohibited by our Terms of Use.
