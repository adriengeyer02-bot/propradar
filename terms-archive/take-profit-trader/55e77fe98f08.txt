Got questions?

A prop firm provides traders with capital (both simulated and live-market) to trade financial markets like futures, in exchange for a share of the profits in their live-market accounts.




Traders can use a prop firm to sharpen their skills and discipline, while having the opportunity to access account sizes which may not be possible if they had to fund it themselves. By paying and passing a test, a trader gets the opportunity to access accounts ranging from $25K-$150K in both simulated and live-market environments.

So, How Exactly Does Take Profit Trader Work?

The Trader's Journey at Take Profit Trader

1. Evaluation Accounts

Traders first prove they can manage risk and hit profit targets by trading a test account in a simulated environment.

2. PRO Accounts

Once their test is passed traders are moved into a PRO account in a simulated environment where they can withdraw profit from day-one and daily.

3. PRO+ Live-Market Accounts

The best PRO traders are eligible for a live review and given the opportunity to be transitioned into PRO+ where they’re able to trade the firm’s capital on the live market.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
How do I pass the test?

You pass your test by hitting the specific profit target required on that account and by trading for a minimum of 3 days. You can scalp or use other strategies.

What are the test rules & how many can I trade?

Inside your test account you have an end of day drawdown, a maximum contract allotment per account size, and a consistency rule. You’re allowed to trade the news, have no daily loss limits, and you can trade as many accounts simultaneously as you’d like.

Can I copy trade my test accounts?

Yes you can copy trade your test accounts.

Is there a consistency rule in the test?

Yes, but it will not fail you. It simply means you cannot make 50% or more of your test account’s profit target in a single day. It does not fail you. You can keep trading until your single biggest day no longer exceeds 50%.

How do test resets work?

You can reset any test account for as low as $79, depending on the account size. It’s totally optional and there are no limits to test resets.

Test Account Questions:
How soon can I withdraw in a PRO account?

In a PRO account you can withdraw from day-one and daily.

How many PRO accounts can I trade?

You can trade up to 5 PRO accounts at the same time. When you move to live, your full 5-account limit moves with you, so you can trade up to 5 live-market PRO+ accounts with a 90% profit split.

Is there a consistency rule in the PRO account?

No, there is no consistency rule in the PRO account.

Can I copy trade my PRO accounts?

Yes, you can copy trade up to 5 PRO accounts.

What are the PRO rules?

Inside your PRO account you have intraday drawdown, maximum contract allotment per account size, and no news trading on specific major events.

Can I trade the news?

No, in a PRO account we do not allow news trading on specific major events. You can find these here.

PRO Account Questions:
What’s The Path To Live-Market Trading?

Here at TPT, advancement into PRO+ is based on a comprehensive review of trading performance and behavior across multiple objective factors. These include consistency, risk management, execution patterns, and overall trading discipline over time.

How Many PRO+ Accounts Can I Have?

You can copy trade up to 5 PRO+ live-market accounts at the same time while continuing to trade as many Test accounts as you’d like alongside them.

Is The Profit Split The Same As PRO?

No. In PRO the split is 80% and in PRO+ the profit split is 90%.

✅No delayed payout rules.

How Does The Buffer Work In PRO+ Accounts?

In PRO+ accounts, there’s no buffer zone requirements. Any profit you generate in that account, you can make it and take it daily without any restrictions at all.

Are there any ongoing fees in PRO+ accounts?

When you move from PRO to PRO+ there’s no PRO+ activation fees to pay. 

There are also no ongoing monthly fees with a PRO or PRO+ Account.

What are the PRO+ rules?

As of June 30th, new PRO+ live-market accounts now have EOD drawdown. Then you have the same rules as in PRO with the same maximum contract allotment per account size, and no trading during specific major news events. You also need to place at least one trade per week to keep your account active.

Payout Policy Questions:
What is Take Profit Trader's payout policy?

As mentioned above, you can withdraw from day-one and then daily in your PRO account. We have traders who withdraw multiple times a week and even multiple times in a day. There are no minimum profitable days required, no payout windows, and no payout delays. There’s no withdrawal restrictions on your profits above the buffer. You can get in, get out, and get paid on day one, day two, day three, or whatever day or days best fit your strategy.

Is there a maximum withdrawal amount?

No, there is no maximum withdrawal amount.

Why do you do daily payouts?

We allow everyday payouts because everyone’s trading story is different. Some do this full-time, some trade before work, some trade in their spare time or when the kids are asleep. Which is why we believe everyone should be able to withdraw profit when it’s convenient for them, not just when it’s convenient for the firm.

What is the buffer zone?

The buffer zone is equal to your accounts drawdown. You can withdraw anything above it at 80% profit split in PRO. There is no buffer in PRO+ and the profit split is 90%. You can still withdraw from inside the buffer, but typically it’s only recommended for people who need it as it’ll close that account and those profits will be at a 50% profit split.

What payout methods can I use?

If you’re in the US, you can securely use Plaid to get real-time transfers directly to your bank or through ACH transfer. If you’re international, you can use either Paypal or Wise.
