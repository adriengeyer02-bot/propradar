1​      Introduction
        Eightcap International Limited (‘Eightcap’, ‘Company’, ‘we’, ‘us’ or ‘our’) is incorporated in
        Seychelles with registration number 8427413-1 and registered address at Office 12, 3rd Floor,
        IMAD Complex, Ile Du Port, Mahé, Seychelles. Eightcap administers the Eightcap Challenges
        business, which is a developer and provider of simulated trading experiences. These
        simulations are provided for educational purposes and for the assessment of trading
        strategies and capabilities. Eightcap Challenges does not form part of Eightcap’s regulated
        product offering.
        Eightcap is committed to complying with all applicable anti-money laundering and
        counter-terrorism financing (‘AML/CTF’) obligations, as well as international sanctions laws
        and regulatory regimes. In relation to our participants (‘Participant’ or ‘you’), we conduct
        due diligence in accordance with the requirements of relevant authorities, and we may
        suspend, restrict or refuse access to our products or services where a Participant,
        transaction, jurisdiction or activity presents a sanction or AML/CTF risk. This Participant
        Verification Policy (‘Policy’) sets out the verification requirements that Participants must
        satisfy in order to receive a Payout under the General Terms and Conditions.
        If you fail to satisfactorily pass the verification requirements of this Policy, Eightcap
        reserves the right to withhold any funds, delay, suspend or deny any Payouts, or return
        funds to the original source (subject to any applicable laws). You acknowledge that
        Eightcap is under no obligation to provide any Payout where doing so would contravene
        this Policy, the General Terms and Conditions, any other Company terms or policy, or any
        applicable laws.

2​      Participant Verification Requirements
2.1​    Initial Payment Requirements
        Before receiving your first Payout (as that term is defined in the General Terms and
        Conditions) from Eightcap (‘Initial Payment’), you must satisfy the requirements of this
        Policy. Eightcap reserves the right, at any time and at its sole discretion, to request
        documents and information to verify your identity, source of funds, and source of wealth.
        This may include, without limitation:
        ●​   government-issued photo identification;
        ●​   proof of residential address (e.g. utility bill, bank correspondence);
        ●​   bank statements or account confirmations;
        ●​   evidence of source of funds or source of wealth; and
        ●​   any further documentation reasonably required to discharge Eightcap’s legal or
             regulatory obligations.
        Eightcap may refuse, delay, suspend or condition the processing of any Payout until the
        requested documents are provided and verified to its satisfaction.
2.2​    Payments after the Initial Payment
        Any subsequent Payout to you may also be subject to renewed verification (on the same
        conditions as in section 2.1), as determined by Eightcap in its sole discretion.

3​      Unacceptable Jurisdictions
        If you are located in, a resident of, or transacting from, a Restricted Territory (as that term
        is defined in the General Terms and Conditions) or such other jurisdiction deemed
        unacceptable by Eightcap for legal, sanctions, AML/CTF or risk reasons, you will not
        satisfactorily pass the verification requirements of this Policy.

4​      Unacceptable Risk
        If Eightcap determines, in its sole discretion, that there is an unacceptable legal, regulatory,
        financial, operational or reputational risk associated with a proposed Payout
        (‘Unacceptable Risk’), you will not satisfactorily pass the verification requirements of this
        Policy. Without limiting the generality of the below, an Unacceptable Risk may arise where:
        ●​ your identity, source of funds or source of wealth cannot be verified to Eightcap’s
           satisfaction;
        ●​ the documents or information you provide are incomplete, inconsistent, inaccurate, or
           appear false or misleading;
        ●​ you are located in, a resident of, or transacting from a Restricted Territory or other
           jurisdiction that Eightcap considers high-risk, sanctioned or otherwise unacceptable;
        ●​ the destination of the payout is a new, altered or unverified account or payment
           method;
        ●​ there is reason to suspect fraud, money laundering, terrorist financing, sanctions
           evasion, tax offences, or any other unlawful or improper activity;
        ●​ you are in breach of this Policy, the General Terms and Conditions, the Rules and
           Conditions, any other Company policy, or any applicable law or regulation; or
        ●​ any other circumstance exists in which Eightcap reasonably considers that processing a
           Payout would be improper or expose Eightcap to liability, investigation or enforcement
           action.
        Eightcap may, but is under no obligation to, provide reasons for determining that there is
        an Unacceptable Risk.

5​      Revisions of this Policy
        This Policy will be reviewed at least once per calendar year. The Company may amend or
        modify the terms of this Policy from time to time. Any such amendments will be effective
        immediately upon being published on the Company’s website.
