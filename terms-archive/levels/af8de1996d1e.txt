Frequently Asked Questions
How many trades does it take to level up?

There’s no fixed number of trades. You level up by earning pips—how many trades it takes depends on your strategy. The more pips you earn, the faster you move up!

What are the targets for each Level?

Targets vary depending on your starting level. Generally, you’ll need to earn enough pips to double the amount you started with at each level.

What instruments can I trade?

You can trade a wide range of instruments, including forex pairs, commodities, and more. We offer the flexibility to choose what suits your trading style best.

When do I get paid?

Depending on the level structure of the challenge you achieve payouts on hitting various levels, as indicated when you select a challenge. You'll be eligible for payout as soon as you reach first level containing a payout.

Do I need to deposit funds to start?

No. You can start a challenge with as little as $50, and there are no additional deposits required to progress through the levels.

Is there a time limit to complete each level?

No! You can take as long as you need to complete each level. Your focus should be on performance, not rushing through.

Can I pause my challenge if I need time off?

Yes, you can pause your challenge. Take the time you need, and pick up where you left off when you’re ready.

What happens if I lose all my pips at Level 1?

If you lose all your pips at Level 1, you’re out of the challenge. However, you can always start a new challenge from Level 1 if you’d like.

How does the AI help me in the challenge?

The AI analyzes your trading patterns and supports your decisions, helping to predict market movements and enhance your performance. It’s like having an advanced trading assistant.

Can I trade during market news or events?

Yes! The platform is designed to let you trade anytime, including during news or high-volatility events. Just focus on getting pips!

Why do I need levels if I can already trade?

Levels adds a unique challenge to your trading. While you may already be skilled, our system helps refine your strategy, optimizes performance with AI, and rewards consistency. The levels offer a structured path with tangible goals and bigger payouts as you progress, ensuring that your trading keeps evolving.

Is there spread?

Yes, just like any other platform, there will be a spread. It’s a natural part of trading and can vary based on the instrument and market conditions.

Are there any commissions?

No, there are no commissions on your trades. You focus on your performance, and we take care of the rest, keeping the process as simple as possible.

How long can I hold my trades for?

You have the freedom to hold your trades as long as you wish. There are no time restrictions on trade duration, allowing you to manage your trades in line with your strategy.

Can I use custom indicators or external tools?

Yes! While our platform comes with advanced tools and AI to support your trades, you're free to use custom indicators and other tools that align with your strategy. You have full control over your trading setup.

What happens when I reach a level with a payout, but then lose pips?

Once you pass a level with a payout - if you lose pips afterwards, you won't lose your payout. However, you may be demoted to a lower level, and you’ll need to earn back the pips to progress onward again.

Are there any restrictions on the number of trades I can make per day?

No, there are no restrictions on the number of trades you can make. You can trade as much or as little as you want, but your goal is to focus on earning pips with precision.

Does the AI intervene in my trades or just offer insights?

The AI doesn't take control of your trades but helps guide your decisions by offering insights and predictive analytics. You maintain full control, but the AI works in the background to enhance your strategy and suggest improvements.

Can I withdraw my earnings before reaching a level with payout?

No, payouts are only available once you’ve passed the level with the first assigned payout in a challenge. This ensures you’ve demonstrated consistent performance and earned enough pips to start benefiting from the platform.

Can I trade on multiple devices?

Yes, you can trade on multiple devices. Whether you prefer trading on your computer, phone, or tablet, you can seamlessly switch between them, keeping your progress synchronized.

How does Levels compare to other platforms that offer funded accounts?

Unlike traditional funded accounts, which may have strict rules, high fees, or complex risk management, Levels allows you to trade freely with no commissions, no account balance visibility, and no risk limits, empowering you to trade with your skill and strategy, not rules set by others.

What happens if I pass Level 6?

Once you pass level 6, you’ll start earning payouts! It’s the point where your hard work truly starts to pay off.

How much do I need to start trading with Levels?

You can start with as little as $50! Everyone is welcome, and our platform is designed to be accessible for traders of all levels.

Do I need to be an experienced trader to join?

Not at all! Levels is designed to be user-friendly and accessible to all traders. Whether you're just starting out or have years of experience, you’ll find everything you need to succeed.

How do I choose my targets?

At the start of each level, you decide your target. You can go for a smaller target with multiple trades or aim for a larger target in one go. It’s all about what fits your style and strategy!

What happens if I fail Level 1?

If you fail Level 1 by losing all your pips, unfortunately, you're out. However, you can always come back and try again with a fresh challenge whenever you're ready.

What if I lose all my pips?

No worries! If you lose all your pips at Level 1, you’ll be out. However, if you're at a higher level, you can still drop down a level and keep going. You always have the opportunity to try again and improve.

What happens if I reach Level 6?

Once you pass Level 6, you’ll start earning payouts! It’s the point where your hard work truly starts to pay off.

Can I trade anytime?

Absolutely! You have the freedom to trade whenever it suits you, Whether it's during market hours, news events, or late at night. It’s all in your hands.

How soon will I be paid?

Our verified traders have received payouts within one business day (24hours). We ensure fast and hassle-free payments to keep you focused on trading.

Are there any hidden fees or charges?

No, there are no hidden fees. Our platform is transparent after the challenge cost, so you can focus on trading without worrying about unexpected costs. What you see is what you get!

What if I need help during the challenge?

Our support team is available to assist you whenever you need help. Whether it’s a technical question or you need guidance on how the challenge works, we’re here to provide the support you need.

Ready to take your trading to the next level?

Don’t wait! Unlock your full potential with Levels. Join today, start your challenge, and turn your skills into success!
