TRADER CONTRACT AGREEMENT

This is an Independent Contractor Agreement (the ‘Agreement’)
Between

LEVELS
(The ‘Company’)

And
(The ‘Trader’)

LOCATION:

Fully remote

MANDATORY WORKING HOURS:

The Trader has the freedom to set their own working hours and schedule, with no mandatory requirements from the Company.

DUTIES:

There are no requirements expected from the Trader that go beyond the use of our Platform. The Trader has zero obligations toward the Company.

DEADLINES:

There is no time limit or deadline for an Level to be completed by the Trader. You can take as much time as vou like to go up a Level.

PERFORMANCE BENEFITS:

Levels pays the Trader benefits once they reach certain levels, including a starting benefit at Level 10 and additional benefits at Levels 15, 20, and 25.
