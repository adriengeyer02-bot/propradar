Account sharing, passing services and IP sharing
Account sharing, passing services and IP sharing
October 8, 2026

Your account is personal. Only you may log in and trade it, from evaluation to funded.

 

Sharing credentials, letting someone trade for you, or using a passing service is not allowed.

IP sharing means your account is accessed from the same IP addresses as other traders. We monitor IPs on the trading platform, dashboard, payments and KYC.

How we handle it:

Deliberate cases (a group trading from shared IPs, account management, passing services, fraud) mean immediate closure of all accounts and a ban.

Non-malicious flags (your ISP, a shared office network, a VPN you forgot to tell us about, a copy-trading tool) are a soft breach: the account stays open. See Hard breach vs soft breach for what this means.
