Example (Atom 1-Step 100K): target $8,000, daily loss $4,000, max loss floor starts at $94,000 and trails your highest equity. Once funded, a day counts as profitable when you close it at least $500 up.

 

How does the daily loss work?

Your daily limit is measured from the higher of your balance and equity at the previous daily close. If your equity drops by the daily loss amount below that level, the account is breached. Open trades count.

 

Static vs trailing max loss

Static · Atom 2-Step: the floor is your starting balance minus 10% and never moves. On 100K it is always $90,000.

Trailing · Atom 1-Step: the floor follows the highest equity you reach (open profit included) and never moves down. On 100K it starts at $94,000; reach $103,000 equity and the floor moves to $97,000.

 

How does the 50% consistency rule work?

Your best single day must be less than 50% of your total profit (once funded, the profit since your last payout).

It is not a breach. If your best day is too big, just keep trading until your total profit is at least twice your best day. Best day $3,000 → you need $6,000 total before you can pass (1-Step) or request a payout.

 

Is there a minimum number of days or a time limit?

No. Neither Atom challenge has minimum trading days, minimum profitable days or a deadline, and funded accounts do not expire. Only on the funded Atom 1-Step you need 5 profitable days of at least 0.5% of the starting balance before each payout. Normal inactivity rules still apply.

 

Can I trade the news?

Yes during the challenge. On funded Atom accounts, trading high impact news is not allowed.

 

What leverage do I get?

1:30 by default. On the Atom 2-Step you can pick higher leverage at checkout.

Example (100K funded): each payout request is capped at $3,000; any profit above the cap stays in the account for the next cycle. Your risk on any single instrument in one day must stay at or below $1,500.

 

Is there a bonus for buying several accounts?

Yes. Each extra Atom account in the same order gets a bigger discount: 25% off the 2nd, 30% off the 3rd and 35% off the 4th. Buy 3 in one order and you also get a free Market Maker Pack + 6,000 credits. Credits can only be used to open Elite Packs.

 

EAs, copiers and house rules

Expert Advisors are allowed as support tools, and trade copiers only between your own accounts. All FundedElite house rules apply, including the 3 minute minimum hold, no hedging across accounts and no account sharing. See Allowed and Prohibited Practices.

 

All Elite Atom accounts, including funded accounts, are simulated trading accounts. Terms apply.
