What is the consistency rule?
What is the consistency rule?
October 8, 2026

Your best single day may not be more than a set share of your total profit. It is not a breach: if your best day is too large, you simply need more total profit before you pass or get paid.

 

Program

	

Consistency




Instant

	

25%




Flash Activation (funded)

	

30%, removable at checkout




Catalyst

	

30%, removable at checkout




1-Step, 2-Step and LITE at $300K

	

60%




Instant Elite, and 1-Step, 2-Step, LITE below $300K

	

None

 

How the required profit is recalculated: required profit = best day ÷ consistency %.

 

Example: Instant $50,000 (25%). You have $2,000 total profit, and your best day was $800. $800 ÷ 25% = $3,200, so you need $1,200 more profit (assuming no bigger day) before you can request a payout.

 

 

The calculation restarts after every approved payout.
