How Payouts Work at RZR Funded. Everything You Need to Know
How Payouts Work at RZR Funded. Everything You Need to Know
Written by
Ishwa junaid
Published on
19 Oct 2026
Funded Trading

Getting paid is the reason you trade a funded account. This guide explains how prop firm payouts work at RZR Funded, from your first eligible request to the money reaching you. 

Quick answer: At RZR Funded, you keep 80% of your simulated profits, or 95% with an add-on. On most Challenges, your first reward request opens 14 days after your first trade. Eligible requests are approved or rejected within 24 business hours, or your reward is doubled, up to $1,000. 

Key takeaways 
The standard reward split is 80%, or 95% with the 95% Reward Share add-on. 
Your payout cycle starts from your first trade, not your purchase date. 
The 24-Hour Reward Guarantee covers approval, not the arrival of funds. 
Close all trades and cancel pending orders before you request a reward. 
Leave a buffer after withdrawing so one bad day does not breach your account. 
How prop firm payouts work: the four parts 

Every payout question fits into one of these four areas: 

Reward split – how much of your profit you keep 
Payout cycle – when you can request a reward 
Approval process – what happens after you request 
Post-withdrawal rules – how your account changes after you are paid 
How much do you keep? 

The standard reward split at RZR Funded is 80%. With the 95% Reward Share add-on, it rises to 95%. 

Eligible profit 	At 80% 	At 95% 
$1,000 	$800 	$950 
$5,000 	$4,000 	$4,750 
$10,000 	$8,000 	$9,500 

The formula is simple: eligible profit × reward split = your payout. 

“Eligible” matters. Your account must follow the rules, your KYC must be complete and your payment details must be correct. 

What is the minimum payout? 

Your reward must clear a minimum before you can request it: 

Express, Enhanced, Endurance, Instant Lite and Instant Funding: $20 
EnhancedX: 2% of your starting balance 
Instant24: 3% of your starting balance 
When can you request a reward? 

Your payout cycle starts from your first trade, not the day you bought your Challenge. 

Standard cycle: every 14 days after your first trade 
With the Weekly Payout add-on: every 7 days 
Instant24: once your 24-hour trading window ends 

Some Challenges also need a minimum number of profitable days in each cycle. Instant Lite needs 5 days at 0.5% or more. Enhanced needs 3 days at 0.5% or more. 

On every Challenge, you must close all open positions and cancel pending orders before you submit a request. Your dashboard shows your next eligible date, so check it rather than guessing. 

What is the 24-Hour Reward Guarantee? 

The 24-Hour Reward Guarantee means RZR Funded approves or rejects an eligible reward request within 24 business hours. If that window is missed, your reward is doubled, up to a maximum of $1,000. 

How the compensation works: 

A $600 reward approved late becomes $1,200 in total. 
A $2,000 reward approved late becomes $3,000, because the bonus is capped at $1,000. 

Compensation is confirmed after a review of the request and is paid separately from your reward. 

What the guarantee does not cover: the arrival of funds. Settlement time after approval depends on your payment method and provider. 

When the clock runs: business hours are Monday 10:00 to Friday 17:00 (GMT+4). A request made outside those hours starts the clock on the next business day. 

When the guarantee does not apply: 

A rule breach is found during review 
The request needs a prohibited-trading investigation 
Further verification is needed 
Your payment details are wrong or incomplete 

In each case, the request is rejected. Once you fix the issue, you submit a new request and a fresh 24-hour window starts. 

What happens after you request a reward? 
Eligibility: your account reaches its eligible date and your profit clears the minimum. 
Submit: go to the Withdrawals page in your dashboard, choose a payment method and enter your details carefully. 
Read-only mode: your account switches to read-only while it is reviewed. This is normal. 
Risk review: the team checks rule compliance, trading activity, KYC and payment details. 
Decision: you are told whether the request is approved or rejected, and why. 
Payment: processing begins on approval. Settlement time depends on your payment method. 
Trading resumes: your account leaves read-only mode and you can trade again. 
Why your buffer matters after a payout 

After a payout, your breathing room can shrink fast. Plan for it before you withdraw. 

On Instant Lite: the first 3% of profit always stays in your account as a buffer. On a $50,000 account that grows to $53,000, you can withdraw $1,500, not $3,000. 

On Express, Enhanced and Instant Funding: after your first payout, your maximum total loss locks at your starting balance. For example, a $50,000 account grows to $53,000 and you withdraw $2,000. Your balance is now $51,000 and your floor is $50,000. That leaves only $1,000 of room. 

On EnhancedX and Endurance: your floor stays at your starting balance minus 8%. 

The practical rule: before you withdraw, make sure what remains can absorb at least one normal losing day. 

KYC and the contractor agreement 

Complete KYC as early as you can. Leaving it late is one of the easiest ways to delay your first payout. 

When KYC is needed: 

Evaluation Challenges (Express, Enhanced, EnhancedX, Endurance): after you pass, before funded trading begins 
Instant options (Instant24, Instant Lite, Instant Funding): when your first reward request becomes eligible 

You will need one proof of identity and one proof of address. Your dashboard lists the exact requirements. Once KYC is approved, you sign a contractor agreement by email. Standard turnaround for both is 1–3 business days. 

Which payment methods are available? 

RZR Funded pays rewards by: 

Bank transfer 
Crypto 
PayPal 
Local methods (these vary by country) 

Your dashboard shows the methods available to you. Check them before your first payout date. 

Six payout mistakes to avoid 
Requesting too early. The cycle starts from your first trade, not your purchase date. 
Entering the wrong payment details. This delays payment and takes your request outside the guarantee. 
Leaving KYC too late. Complete it as soon as your dashboard asks. 
Breaching a rule while in profit. A breached account loses its eligible profit. 
Withdrawing too much. Always leave a buffer for a normal losing day. 
Requesting before a planned trade. Your account is read-only during review, so time your request carefully.

Frequently asked questions 

What is the reward split at RZR Funded? 

The standard reward split is 80% to the trader. You can raise it to 95% with the 95% Reward Share add-on, which you buy at the same time as your Challenge. 

Does the 24-Hour Reward Guarantee mean I get paid in 24 hours? 

No. The guarantee covers the approval decision only. Once your request is approved, settlement time depends on your payment method and provider. 

How soon can I request my first payout? 

On most Challenges, you can request your first payout 14 days after your first trade, or 7 days with the Weekly Payout add-on. On Instant24, you request it once your 24-hour window ends. 

Why can’t I trade after requesting a payout? 

Your account goes into read-only mode during the review. This makes sure the profit being reviewed is still there when the decision is made. You can trade again once the withdrawal clears. 

Can I get a refund on my Challenge fee? 

Challenge fees are not refunded after you pass or withdraw. A fee is refunded in three cases: within 14 days of purchase if you have not traded, if your funded account request is rejected at the risk review, or if a payout is rejected on a funded account that is also breached. 

Final thought 

Prop firm payouts at RZR Funded follow a clear process. Know your eligible date, complete KYC early, enter your payment details carefully and leave a buffer when you withdraw. Do that, and getting paid is simple and predictable
