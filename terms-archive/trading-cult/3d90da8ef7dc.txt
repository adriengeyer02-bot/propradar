Funded Account Consistency Rule

This rule applies only to funded accounts, including both new and existing accounts.

At a payout request, the largest profitable individual trade and the largest profitable trading day are each measured against total profits for the period. Neither may exceed 25%.

After any earlier payout, only trading activity since the previous payout is counted for the next payout request.

Standard: Failure to meet this objective will result in the payout being rejected and the new payout date being extended by 15 days.

Pay After Pass: Failure to meet this objective will result in the payout being rejected and the account being breached.
