How often can I request Reward Payments after the first one?
Explains cooldown after first payout.
Copy for LLM

After the first approved Reward Payment, cooldowns apply between subsequent Reward Payment Requests unless the Dashboard specifies otherwise.

Challenge-Funded, Pro, and Instant Funding Standard accounts generally have a 7-calendar-day cooldown after the first approved Reward Payment. Instant Funding Lite has a 14-calendar-day cooldown after the first approved Reward Payment.

Related Terms: Sections 8.3, 9.3, and 10.3
