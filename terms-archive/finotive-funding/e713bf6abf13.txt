 Floating drawdown
 The combined floating profit on each symbol may drop to −2% of the initial account balance on Challenge accounts, or −1.5% on Instant accounts. On Instant accounts, the first time is only a warning.

 2%
 $2,000 per symbol

 For example

 On a $100K account with an initial balance of $100,000, you have 2 open positions on XAUUSD with a combined floating P&L of −$3,000. This equates to a floating drawdown of 3% on XAUUSD positions combined. That is over the limit on every account (2% on Challenge, 1.5% on Instant), so it would be a breach.

 What happens

 During Funded Status, if the account experiences more than 2% floating drawdown on any symbol, a Strike will be issued, and the next payout reduced to a 10% profit split.

 Notional volume
 The total open volume across all open positions should not exceed 1,250% of the account balance.

 For example

 On a $100K account your open trades can add up to $1,250,000. A new trade that takes the total to $1,300,000 is a breach, even if margin allows it.

 What happens

 During the challenge, exceeding the Notional Volume limit will result in a Strike, plus an account reset if in profit. If the account is not in profit, then no reset will occur until the account reaches the initial balance. During Funded Status, if you exceed the Notional Volume limit, a Strike will be issued and the next payout reduced to a 10% profit split.
