Sign Up with Rise for TFF Payouts
Sign Up with Rise for TFF Payouts
T
Written by The Forex Funder
September 4, 2024

Rise is a platform that facilitates payouts to either your crypto wallet or bank account. Once your payout is approved, you will receive an invitation from Rise. To proceed, you need to sign up via this invitation, complete the KYC process, and sign the agreement with Rise. After verification by the Rise team, your payout will be reflected in your Rise account.

Steps to Get Started:

1.Invitation from Rise:

•After your payout is approved by our team, you will receive an invitation from Rise. Ensure that the email address you use for Rise matches the one associated with your TFF account.

2.Register with Rise:

•Sign up using your accurate first name, last name, and email address. Click “Sign Up” to proceed.

3.Confirmation Email:

•After registering, you will receive an email with a login button to start the onboarding process. Note that this button expires within 20 minutes for added security. This method will be used whenever you log in to your account, ensuring that your account remains secure even if someone discovers your password.

4.Complete KYC and Sign Agreement:

•Follow the instructions to complete the KYC process and sign the agreement with Rise.

5.Verification and Payout:

•Once the Rise team verifies the process, your payout will be reflected in your Rise account.

For more details on the signing-in steps and to ensure a smooth payout process, please refer to the instructions provided in the invitation email.
