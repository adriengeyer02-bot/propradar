How do I request a payout?
T
Written by The Forex Funder
September 4, 2024

To request a payout, follow these steps:

1.Log in to Your Dashboard: On the TFF website, navigate to your dashboard.

2.Select “Payouts”: In the menu, click on “Payouts” to access the payout request form.

3.Fill Out the Form: Complete the payout request form. Note that once this form is submitted, your connected trading account will switch to view-only mode until the payout is processed, so ensure all trades are closed.

4.Processing Time: Payouts are processed within 1-3 working days. The minimum payout amount is 1% of the initial balance, including our split.

Payment Methods:

•Payouts below $400: Processed through crypto USDT TRC20.

•Payouts above $400: Processed via Rise, which supports both bank transfers and crypto.

If Rise does not support your country, the payout will be processed via crypto. For a list of restricted countries, visit: Rise Restricted Countries

Sign Up Instructions:

First, sign up with your accurate first name, last name, and email address on the Sign Up page. Then, click “Sign up” to proceed.
