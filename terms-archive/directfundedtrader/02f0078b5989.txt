 What determines whether a trader receives a 100% reward share or an

 they fail to reach this milestone, the reward share defaults to

 incentivizes consistent performance and rewards traders who

 How Do You Calculate Overall Drawdown?

 You can have an overall maximum
 loss limit of 10% of your initial account balance. Thus, your
 account balance or equity can't go below 100% of the initial balance
 at any time during your whole trading journey. It implies that your
 account balance or equity should always be over 100% of the initial
 balance to avoid rule violations.

 For example, for a 100k account, your maximum loss limit is $10k.
 So, as long as your balance/equity (whichever is lower) is over 90k,
 you will not violate the overall drawdown

 What is the Consistency Rule and when does it apply?

 The Consistency Rule requires traders to maintain a consistent trading pattern throughout the funded trading period.
 Note: This rule applies only to Instant Funded plans once a trader has been approved and is actively trading.

 What is the Single Trade Loss rule and when does it apply?

 The Single Trade Loss rule limits any individual trade to a maximum loss of 2% of your current day’s balance. This helps manage risk and promotes disciplined trading.
 Note:It applies only to certain plans, so make sure to check your plan details to see if this rule is enforced.
