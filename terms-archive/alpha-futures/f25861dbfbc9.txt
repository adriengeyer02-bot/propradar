 Alpha Futures Payout Rules by Plan: Zero, Standard, Advanced and Direct (2026)

By the Alpha Futures Team · Updated October 26, 2026
Alpha Futures payout rules depend on your plan. On Zero, Standard and Advanced Qualified Accounts, you can request a payout after every 5 winning days of $200 or more, for up to 50% of your profit. Direct accounts use a Withdrawal Profit Target instead. Every plan allows up to 4 payouts a month, and you receive 90% of each request.
In your account, a payout is a "withdrawal request", and the money you receive is a performance fee.

All plans: up to 4 payouts per month.
Alpha Futures Evaluation and Qualified Accounts are simulated accounts. Trading involves risk, and payouts depend on meeting every rule for your plan.
Zero plan payout rules
Zero plan payout rules follow the shared Evaluation-to-Qualified model:
Winning days: request a payout every 5 winning trading days of $200 or more. The days do not need to be consecutive, but you need 5 between each withdrawal request.How much: up to 50% of the profit in your account per request, up to your account's maximum. The rest stays in the account for drawdown or future payouts.Minimum payout: $200.Maximum payout: $1,000 on 25K, $1,500 on 50K and $2,500 on 100K.Consistency: a 40% Consistency Rule applies on Zero Qualified Accounts. There is no Consistency Rule on Zero Evaluations.Profit split: 90%.Payout dates: no assigned payout dates. You can pay yourself up to 4 times per month.Maximum Loss Limit: the Maximum Loss Limit does not slide to $0 at your first withdrawal. If it was at $49,200 before a payout, it stays there after.
See the plan details on the Zero plan page.
Standard plan payout rules
Standard plan payout rules use the same winning-day model as Zero, with higher caps:
Winning days: every 5 winning days of $200 or more, not necessarily consecutive.How much: up to 50% of your profit per request, up to the maximum.Minimum payout: $500.Maximum payout: $3,000 on 50K, $4,000 on 100K and $5,000 on 150K.Consistency: 50% on the Standard Evaluation and 40% on Standard Qualified Accounts.Profit split: 90%, with no tiered system.Payout dates: no assigned payout dates, up to 4 times per month.Maximum Loss Limit: it does not slide to $0 at your first withdrawal.
See the Standard plan page.
Advanced plan payout rules
Advanced plan payout rules have the highest cap per request and no Consistency Rule once you are Qualified:
Winning days: every 5 winning days of $200 or more.How much: up to 50% of your profit per request, up to the maximum.Minimum payout: $1,000.Maximum payout: $15,000 per request, per account, on every Advanced account size.Consistency: 40% on the Advanced Evaluation only. There is no Consistency Rule on Advanced Qualified Accounts.Profit split: 90%, with no tiered system.Payout dates: no assigned payout dates, up to 4 times per month.
See the Advanced plan page.
Direct plan payout rules
Direct accounts skip the Evaluation and start as Qualified Accounts. They have no winning-day requirement. To request a payout, you must reach your Withdrawal Profit Target and satisfy a 20% Consistency Rule.
The Withdrawal Profit Target is the profit you need to earn between withdrawal requests. After each payout, the target resets and you must earn fresh profit from new trades. Leftover profit from the previous cycle does not carry over.

Maximum payout per request
Yes, there is a maximum payout on every Qualified plan. It applies per request:
Zero: $1,000 (25K), $1,500 (50K), $2,500 (100K)Standard: $3,000 (50K), $4,000 (100K), $5,000 (150K)Advanced: $15,000 on all sizes, per request, per accountDirect: $1,000 (25K), $2,000 (50K), $2,500 (100K) and $3,000 (150K)
On Zero, Standard and Advanced, the 50% limit also applies, so a request can be up to 50% of your profit or the plan maximum, whichever you reach first.
Minimum payout and winning days
The minimum payout is $200 on Zero, $500 on Standard, $1,000 on Advanced and $500 on Direct.
On Zero, Standard and Advanced, a winning day is a trading day with $200 or more in profit. You need 5 of them between each withdrawal request, and they do not need to be in a row. Direct has no winning-day requirement; it uses the Withdrawal Profit Target instead.
Consistency rule for payouts
On Qualified Accounts, the consistency rule decides whether the payout button is available:
Zero and Standard (40%): profit from any single trading day cannot be greater than or equal to 40% of net profit made since your last withdrawal request.Direct (20%): the same rule at 20%.Advanced: no Consistency Rule on Qualified Accounts.
The formula is: biggest day ÷ total net profit since your last request. Your current value is always visible in your dashboard, and the withdrawal request button stays unavailable until the rule is met. Going over the limit does not breach your account; you keep trading and making profit until the rule is satisfied. The rule resets after each withdrawal request.
For worked examples, read our consistency rule guide.
How to request a payout
Complete KYC. Go to Profile, then KYC, in your dashboard and follow the Veriff prompts. Identity verification is required before a Qualified Account is issued or performance fees are processed.Meet your plan's condition. That means 5 winning days of $200 or more (Zero, Standard, Advanced) or your Withdrawal Profit Target (Direct), plus any Consistency Rule on your plan.Submit a withdrawal request. Choose an amount between your plan's minimum and maximum. The amount you request is removed from your trading account, and you receive 90% of it.
Before you request, keep these rules in mind:
News trading: on Direct, Standard and Zero Qualified Accounts, no orders may be executed within 2 minutes before or after high-impact news events. Breaking this voids the profit on that trade and the payout is denied. A first violation is a warning only.Prohibited practices: profits from prohibited trading practices are void.Inactivity: place a trade at least once every 10 trading days to keep your account active. See our inactivity rule guide.
For more on how the fee works, read Alpha Futures performance fee rules explained and what is a performance fee.
Alpha Futures payout methods
All payments are made in USD. The available payout methods are:

If you choose Rise, you will be emailed an agreement to sign before your first payout.

Alpha Futures payout FAQ
What are the Alpha Futures payout rules?On Zero, Standard and Advanced, you can request up to 50% of your profit after every 5 winning days of $200 or more. Direct requires a Withdrawal Profit Target and a 20% Consistency Rule. All plans allow up to 4 payouts a month at a 90% profit split.
Is there a maximum payout for Alpha Futures?Yes, per request. It is $1,000 to $2,500 on Zero, $3,000 to $5,000 on Standard, $15,000 on all Advanced sizes and $1,000 to $3,000 on Direct, depending on account size.
What is the minimum payout at Alpha Futures?$200 on Zero, $500 on Standard, $1,000 on Advanced and $500 on Direct.
How many times a month can I request a payout?Up to 4 times per month on every plan. There are no assigned payout dates on Zero, Standard and Advanced.
What are the Alpha Futures performance fee rules?You receive 90% of each withdrawal request. The full requested amount is removed from your trading account, and identity verification (KYC) is required before performance fees are processed.
Is there a consistency rule for Zero plan payouts?Yes. Zero Qualified Accounts have a 40% Consistency Rule: no single trading day's profit can be 40% or more of net profit since your last request. Zero Evaluations have no Consistency Rule.
What payout methods does Alpha Futures offer?ACH (US bank accounts), wire transfer, SWIFT, Wise and Rise. All payments are made in USD.
