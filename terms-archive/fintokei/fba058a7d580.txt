How does the ProTrader challenge work? What are the rules?

Challenge phases, profit targets, daily and overall loss limits, and minimum trading days. Discover everything you need to know to earn a ProTrader account.

Updated over 3 weeks ago

To complete our challenge and receive the virtually funded ProTrader or ProTrader Swing account and Performance reward from it, you must successfully pass two phases.

 

In each phase, your goal is to make 8% (1st Phase) and 6% (2nd Phase) profit while avoiding suffering a daily loss limit of  5% and a Maximum loss limit of 10%. Please note both of these rules apply and are monitored in parallel, and breaching any of them results in failing the particular Challenge Account or Virtually Funded ProTrader or ProTrader Swing account.

 

We recommend you to check this FAQ where we explain in detail how the Daily loss limit is calculated on both ProTrader and ProTrader Swing accounts.

 

There are a few other rules that make sure that only diligent traders pass the challenge phase. Before you start, make sure to check the verification rules in detail.

It is also important to follow the rule of maximum risk on open trades. Breaking this rule does not lead to losing account, but it triggers warning or eventually Consistency rules application.

 

Once you complete your profit target, check that you also have the required amount of 3 trading days. A definition of trading days is available HERE.

If your profit and 3 trading days requirements are complete, make sure to close all open trades. Only once the trades are closed and you are in the required % profit, can the phase be considered as passed.

 

As soon as you pass the second phase, we will contact you through email and set up the ProTrader account. We will ask you to verify your identity using an automated system and send you a contract for becoming a professional trader. 

 

In order for the account to remain active, you must fulfill the condition of at least 1 trade per 30 days on the account.
