What is the reward split on Instant Funding?

First reward: 70% of profits. From the second reward onward: 80% of profits. Rewards are on demand and can be requested at any time once all rules and requirements are met.

Traders can request a reward through the dashboard once all requirements are met and all rules are complied with.

IF A RULE IS VIOLATED

If any rules are violated, the trader will not be able to withdraw or request a reward on profits. Example 1: you have a $10,000 account and $300 in profit — if rules are violated you will not be able to withdraw the $300. Example 2: you have a $10,000 account and start the day at $10,400. You then lose $250 in one day, more than your $200 daily allowance (2% of $10,000). That is a violation, so the remaining $150 profit cannot be withdrawn.

How does the on-demand reward work on Instant Funding?

We provide an on-demand reward system, which means traders can request a reward as soon as they meet the withdrawal requirements. There is no need to wait for fixed cycles.

REQUIREMENTS
At least 7 profitable trading days, with each day achieving a minimum of 0.5% profit by the end of the day.
A consistency score of 20% or lower: your best day is no more than 20% of your total profit.
At least 3% profit on the account.
No rule breaches since the account started.

You also need to complete identity verification (KYC) before your first reward.

What are the minimum trading days on Instant Funding?

Traders are required to have at least 7 profitable trading days, with each day achieving a minimum of 0.5% profit by the end of the day. Only days that end in profit count toward your minimum trading days.

This rule ensures consistent performance and disciplined trading over a period of time, rather than relying on a single big win.

How is the consistency score calculated on Instant Funding?

Consistency score = (biggest winning day ÷ current total account profit) × 100%. The maximum profit you can earn in one day is 20% of your total profit.

EXAMPLE: A $10,000 ACCOUNT THAT COMPLIES
Day 1: $518 profit
Day 2: $497 profit
Day 3: $508 profit
Day 4: $459 profit
Day 5: $492 profit
Day 6: $520 profit — biggest winning day
Day 7: $506 profit

Add up all the days, including losses, to get your current total account profit: $3,500. The maximum allowable profit in one day is $3,500 × 0.20 = $700. The consistency score is ($520 ÷ $3,500) × 100% = 14.86%. A 14.86% score means you successfully adhered to the 20% consistency rule.

EXAMPLE: A $10,000 ACCOUNT THAT DOES NOT COMPLY
Day 1: +$518
Day 2: +$497
Day 3: +$508
Day 4: +$459
Day 5: +$492
Day 6: +$520
Day 7: +$506
Day 8: +$1,000 — new biggest winning day

Current total account profit: $4,500. The maximum allowable profit in one day is $4,500 × 0.20 = $900. The consistency score is ($1,000 ÷ $4,500) × 100% = 22.22%. A 22.22% score means you have not adhered to the 20% consistency rule.

WHAT TO DO NEXT

The trader is required to continue trading while keeping the biggest profitable day under $900 in order to comply with the 20% consistency requirement — or to reach at least $5,000 in total profit, which is the biggest winning day ($1,000) × 5.

What is the maximum loss per trade on Instant Funding?

Traders are not allowed to risk or lose more than 1% of their account initial balance on any single trade. For every position you open, the maximum potential loss based on your stop loss must not exceed 1% of your account size.

EXAMPLE

On a $10,000 account, no trade can ever be more than $100 in loss. If an open trade goes past that limit at any moment, the account is breached, even if the trade later recovers.

This rule is designed to enforce disciplined risk management and prevent over-leveraging or gambling behaviour.

What is the maximum drawdown on Instant Funding?

Your maximum drawdown is 3% of your initial account balance. It is static: the level is fixed when your account starts and does not move as your profit grows. If your balance or equity falls below it at any time, it is a rule violation.

EXAMPLE

You start trading with a $10,000 account and your maximum drawdown is capped at 3% ($300). If your account balance or equity falls below $9,700 at any time, it is a rule violation.

What is the daily drawdown on Instant Funding?

Your daily drawdown is 2% of your initial account balance, and it is static: the amount you can lose each day is fixed by your original account size and never grows with your balance. If your balance or equity falls below the day’s floor at any point, it is a rule violation. The floor resets at midnight (broker time).

EXAMPLE

On a $10,000 account, the daily allowance is always $200. You start the day at $10,000, so your floor is $9,800. If you make $100 profit during the day, your floor stays at $9,800, so you now have $300 of room for the rest of the day.

How many trades can I have open at the same time on Instant Funding?

You can only take one trade at any one time.

Can I trade during news on Instant Funding?

Trading during high-impact economic news is prohibited on Instant Funding accounts. Opening or closing trades 5 minutes before and 5 minutes after high-impact economic news is not allowed.

High-impact news events are identified as red folder news on ForexFactory. Failure to follow this rule will be considered a violation.

What leverage applies on Instant Funding?

Leverage 1:50.

Is the fee refundable on Instant Funding?

There is no refund on Instant Funding. What you pay is the program fee, which grants you access to the account. The fee is non-refundable under any circumstances.
