Understanding prop trading
⁨6⁩ QUESTIONS
What is a prop trading firm?

A proprietary trading firm is a financial institution that allocates its own capital to prop traders, allowing them to engage in market activities with the goal of earning returns for both the trader and the firm.

What does a prop trading firm do?

A prop trading firm is a company that provides its traders with access to capital. In return, the traders share a percentage of the profits they generate with the company.

What happens if you lose money in a prop firm?

Traders may face risk limits, suspended trading, or even termination if they consistently exceed the agreed-upon loss limits.

Why do you charge a fee?

The fee covers what FOREXIVE provides to you as a FOREXIVE trader, including the trading platform and brokerage services. Importantly, this fee represents the maximum financial risk to you, as any losses on the FOREXIVE account are absorbed by us.

On 1-Step and 2-Step, the fee is refunded with your first reward, plus an extra 20%. On 1-Step Access it is paid back with your third reward, and the Instant Funding program fee is not refundable. The FOREXIVE program is a premium service we offer, and its pricing reflects its value.

Why should I join FOREXIVE?

Joining FOREXIVE provides traders with exceptional benefits, making it an ideal choice for aspiring professionals. We offer innovative services such as no time limit challenges, allowing traders to achieve their goals without the pressure of deadlines.

Our competitive offerings include balance-based drawdowns, the lowest commissions and spreads, and extremely good customer support, ensuring a supportive and cost-effective trading environment. At FOREXIVE we prioritise our traders' success and well-being, providing unparalleled care and resources to help you thrive in the financial markets.

How do I join a prop trading firm?

Choose between the FOREXIVE models, sign up, select the one you prefer, and start trading. Account setup is instant and payment methods are flexible.

1
1-Step model: pass a single evaluation by meeting the profit target and following the risk management rules, and you are funded.
2
2-Step model: complete two evaluation phases, both with specific profit targets and risk limits, before earning your funded account.

Trading rules and conduct
⁨8⁩ QUESTIONS
Which trading strategies are restricted or prohibited?

FOREXIVE operates a zero-tolerance policy against any form of abuse, manipulation or unethical behaviour on the platform. Any activity that attempts to exploit systems, manipulate pricing or gain an unfair advantage is strictly prohibited.

PROHIBITED TRADING PRACTICES
High-frequency trading (HFT)
Latency trading
Reverse trading
Gap trading
Any form of arbitrage, including long/short and reverse arbitrage
Tick scalping — no more than 20% of trades may be closed within 1 minute; profits from such trades will be removed at the funded stage
One-sided betting strategies
Hyperactive trading patterns designed to exploit system limitations
Use of limit orders to guarantee profits during low-liquidity conditions
Hedging across different accounts, whether between your own accounts or as group hedging with other traders
ACCOUNT MISUSE AND COLLABORATION
Copying trades to or from another trader
Using third-party services, such as “pass your challenge” providers
Account management by external individuals or companies
Account sharing
PLATFORM EXPLOITATION
Using EAs, bots or scripts to manipulate trading activity
Taking advantage of server errors, price freezes or data delays
Any behaviour intended to bypass risk controls or system safeguards
ENFORCEMENT

We actively monitor all accounts using advanced risk and behaviour analysis systems. Any violation of these rules may result in immediate action, including profit removal, account suspension or permanent account termination. On a funded account, once any rule is broken you cannot withdraw or request a reward on your profits.

ALLOWED

Hedging on the same account is allowed: you can open opposing positions on one account, for example to hedge risk or offset a loss. Instant Funding allows one open trade at a time, so it cannot be done there. Copy trading between accounts you own yourself is also allowed.

Are there any lot size restrictions when taking a trade?

No. There are no restrictions on lot sizes, minimum or maximum, allowing traders the freedom to trade any style provided trades stay within the risk management principles.

Using a stop loss or take profit is not necessary, but it is suggested for better risk management. Bear in mind that the above does not extend to gambling.

Does FOREXIVE allow Expert Advisors (EAs)?

Yes, using a third-party Expert Advisor is allowed, but only if it serves as a trade or risk manager. The EA must focus solely on managing account risk, such as adjusting stop losses, take profits or position sizes.

To activate an EA, email support@forexive.com.

ANY OTHER TYPE IS NOT ALLOWED

Any other type of Expert Advisor, especially one that autonomously executes trades, is not allowed. Violating this rule will result in the denial of your evaluation or reward and the closure of your account.

Is copy trading allowed at FOREXIVE?

Yes, but only between accounts you own yourself. Copying trades to or from another trader is prohibited.

If it happens, all accounts involved are closed and no money is returned. Using “pass your challenge” services or any third party to trade for you, having your account managed by another person or company, and sharing your account with anyone are also not allowed.

Can I trade during the news?

On 1-Step, 2-Step and 1-Step Access, news trading is allowed. In the evaluation phases there are no restrictions. On a funded account, any profit made on a trade closed between 5 minutes before and 5 minutes after a high-impact news event (broker time) is deducted and does not count towards your rewards.

The deduction applies even if the trade was opened before the news window, and whether the trade is closed manually or by a stop loss or take profit.

EXAMPLE

High-impact news is released at 14:30, so the window runs from 14:25 to 14:35. You opened a trade at 10:00 and it closes at 14:32 with $400 profit. The $400 is deducted and is not included in your reward.

Trading during the news is not a rule violation and does not fail your account. Losses made in the window count against your balance and drawdown limits as normal. High-impact news means red-folder events on the ForexFactory economic calendar.

INSTANT FUNDING

News trading is restricted on Instant Funding. You may not open or close trades — market orders, stop loss and take profit executions, or pending orders — 5 minutes before or 5 minutes after high-impact news. Doing so is a rule violation.

Are there any floating risk limits?

Yes. On 1-Step, 2-Step and 1-Step Access, the floating risk limit is 3%: open positions must never show an unrealised loss of 3% or more of the account balance or equity. Reaching it is a rule violation, even if the trades later recover. On Instant Funding, no trade may lose more than 1% of the initial balance.

Is weekend trading allowed?

You cannot open or close trades between Saturday 00:00 and Sunday 23:59 (broker time). Any trade opened or closed in that window is a rule violation, including a trade opened earlier in the week that is closed during the weekend, manually or by a stop loss or take profit.

All crypto trading is prohibited between Saturday 00:00 and Sunday 23:59 (broker time), including buying, selling, pending orders, stop losses and take profits, and closing trades opened before the weekend. This applies to all accounts.

Can I hold my trades overnight or over the weekend?

Traders are permitted to hold their trades overnight and over the weekend.

Account security and use
⁨5⁩ QUESTIONS
Am I allowed to change my trading account password?

Changing the investor password is prohibited and will be considered a violation of the rules.

Account information is available on your dashboard and is sent to your email. Its security, and keeping the credentials safe, is the trader’s responsibility.

Does FOREXIVE have an IP address policy?

We strongly advise traders to adhere to a single IP address to prevent any potential breaches of our policies concerning multiple IP addresses. Hedging across multiple accounts from the same IP address, or similar discrepancies in trading strategies, is regarded as a violation.

We acknowledge that there may be exceptional circumstances requiring the use of multiple devices and IP addresses, for instance when travelling. We encourage traders to communicate any such circumstances with us in advance, allowing us to make appropriate accommodations while ensuring compliance with our regulations.

IF THE RISK TEAM IDENTIFIES A BREACH
A warning will be issued, accompanied by a deduction of profits, and the account will undergo a reset. If the account is not in a profitable state, a reset will not be implemented.
Depending on the severity of the violation, the account may face termination without any profit share.
What is the inactivity period?

If you fail to log into your trading account and execute a trade for 15 consecutive days, your account is designated as inactive and the system classifies it as failed. There is no minimum or maximum trade size required when placing a trade.

In such cases, contact our support team through a ticket on your dashboard to request a new account. If you have placed a trade but do not execute another for 15 consecutive days, your account will still be considered failed.

REQUESTING A REPLACEMENT

If your account becomes inactive and you have not placed any trades, we will issue you a new account on request. This request must be made within 6 months of your purchase date; beyond this period we will not be able to provide a new account or offer a refund.

How many accounts can I have with FOREXIVE?

There is no limit on how many accounts you can have with FOREXIVE, but the maximum capital allocation is $400,000.

Traders are able to merge their accounts into one account if they wish — funded accounts only. For example: 4 × $50,000 accounts and 2 × $100,000 accounts.

How does the Scale-Up plan work?

Account growth takes place over a 3-month cycle, with a 40% increase to your balance every time, up to a maximum of $2 million.

ACCOUNT GROWTH REQUIREMENTS
Achieving at least 8% profit within the 3-month cycle
Account must be in profit for the last trading cycle

Once your account has scaled up, your share of each reward rises to 95%.

Verification and rewards
⁨3⁩ QUESTIONS
When do I need to complete identity verification (KYC)?

Once traders successfully pass their challenges, the verification section in the dashboard becomes available, allowing them to proceed with the KYC process. Verification can take up to 72 hours.

Alternatively, email kyc@forexive.com and request verification once you have successfully passed your challenges.

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
1
Proof of identity — a valid government-issued document such as a passport, national ID card or driver’s licence.
2
Proof of address — a utility bill for electricity, water or internet no older than 3 months; a bank statement issued within the last 3 months; or a tax bill valid for the current year.
3
Liveness verification — required to ensure that the user is present during verification.
What are the available withdrawal methods?

At present the available withdrawal methods include bank transfer, cryptocurrency (USDT TRC20, USDC ETH, BTC, ETH, LTC) and Rise, with plans to introduce a wider range of convenient methods in the near future.

How can I open a Rise account?

Sign up on the Rise homepage, complete onboarding and identity verification, then accept the invitation from FOREXIVE to be added to the team and receive rewards into your Rise account.

1. SIGN UP
Visit the Rise homepage and navigate to the sign-up section.
Fill in your personal details — first name, last name and email address — then sign up.
Verify your email by entering the code sent to you, or sign in using the Google option.
2. ONBOARDING
Select “Receiving Payments”, which is free to sign up for.
Enter your date of birth, recovery email, phone number and address. Make sure the address is accurate, for identity verification later.
Register as an individual — we do not provide rewards to business accounts.
Answer the questions regarding U.S. income tax and services performed in the U.S.
Provide your tax or national ID.
3. AGREEMENT AND SIGNATURE

Agree to the terms and conditions and use an electronic signature to sign the necessary documents.

4. IDENTITY VERIFICATION

Confirm your identity by uploading an identification document such as a driving licence, ID card, residence permit or passport. You can continue this process on your phone or proceed on the website.

5. DASHBOARD AND RECEIVING REWARDS
Once your identity is verified, you will access the Rise dashboard.
The next step is to receive an invitation from FOREXIVE.
After accepting the invitation and signing any agreements with FOREXIVE, you will be added to the team and eligible to receive rewards into your Rise account.
6. WITHDRAWING YOUR SHARE
Navigate to the Payout section of the FOREXIVE dashboard.
Choose your preferred withdrawal method, such as Rise, and enter your Rise registered email address.
Once set up, you can start withdrawing rewards into your Rise account.
