Phase 1 Requirements

8.3.1 To pass the Peak Challenge Phase 1, the Customer must:

   ●​ Execute at least one demo trade on five (5) separate calendar days.
   ●​ Avoid daily drawdowns exceeding 4% of balance or peak equity.
   ●​ Avoid total losses greater than 8%.
   ●​ Achieve a total profit target of 8% of initial capital.
   ●​ Close all trades before final evaluation.​


Verification Phase Requirements

8.3.2 To pass Verification:

   ●​ Maintain the same drawdown limits as in Phase 1.
   ●​ Achieve at least 5% total profit.
   ●​ Trade on five (5) different days.
   ●​ Close all trades for evaluation.
   ●​ Meet all conditions outlined in Clauses 5, 6, and 7.​


8.3.3 If successful, the Customer will be recommended as a candidate for the Alpine Funded
Trader Program.
