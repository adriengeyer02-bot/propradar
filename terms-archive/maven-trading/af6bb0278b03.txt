Simulated Trading Environment

Maven-EDU offers a simulated trading environment aimed at education and skill development. It is important for clients to understand that this simulated setting differs significantly from actual financial markets as it does not involve real money trading and may give a different impression of a trader's real-world performance.

Our simulated environment strives to replicate real market conditions, including volatility. However, clients should be aware of its limitations, such as potential differences in market liquidity, execution timing, and the absence of financial risk, which could lead to misconceptions about live trading realities.
