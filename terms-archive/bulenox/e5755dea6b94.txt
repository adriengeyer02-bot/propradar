What is the consistency rule?
COPY LINK

The consistency rule keeps your results balanced — your best single trading day should not make up too large a share of your total profit for the payout period. If a request does not meet it, nothing is violated and your account stays open: just keep trading with your usual position size until the balance evens out. The exact figure for your account type is in the Help Center — Fast Track, Momentum or Master.
