Can I use Expert Advisors(EAs)/Indicators?

The utilisation of Expert Advisors (EAs) to facilitate trading is certainly allowed. Nevertheless, it's crucial to be aware that there are specific types of EAs that we consider inappropriate due to their potential negative impact on the trading ecosystem.

To provide more clarity, the following types of EAs are prohibited:
1. High-Frequency Trading (HFT): EAs that execute an unusually large number of trades within extremely short timeframes are not permitted. This precaution is in place to prevent excessive market volatility and the potential for unfair advantages.

2. Grid / Martingale Strategies: EAs that implement grid or martingale trading strategies, which involve progressively increasing trade sizes after losses, are sanctioned. These strategies carry a heightened risk of substantial losses.

3. Arbitrage: EAs that seek to exploit price discrepancies between different markets or brokers are not allowed. Arbitrage strategies could disrupt market integrity and create an uneven trading landscape.

4. Tick Scalping: EAs that focus on profiting from minuscule price movements within a single tick are prohibited. Such strategies can strain the trading platform and might not contribute positively to overall market stability.

By establishing these restrictions, we intend to cultivate a secure and equitable trading environment that fosters success for all traders. Familiarizing yourself with and adhering to these guidelines is essential to ensure a positive trading experience for all participants.

What is Stimulus Add-on?

The "Stimulus Add-On" is an account enhancement feature crafted to provide traders with the opportunity to expand their drawdown limits, allowing for extended and more flexible trading capabilities. This enhancement comes at a nominal fee and is equivalent to 50% of the actual drawdown per cent.

The Stimulus Add-on costs 45% of the enrolment fee and is activated upon a 70% realised drawdown.

What are the criteria to activate a Stimulus Add-on?

A trader must have had a realised loss of 70% of the program's overall drawdown.

How is drawdown calculated at WFunded? Is it equity- or balance-based?
Is the drawdown balance-based or equity-based?

Drawdown is balance-based, not equity-based.

Daily Drawdown

The daily drawdown is calculated as 5% of your starting balance for the day.

If your balance increases during the day, your daily drawdown resets and is recalculated based on the new balance.

Example:

Start with $10,000 → Daily limit = $500
Balance grows to $10,500 → New daily limit = $525
Overall Drawdown

The overall drawdown is also calculated using your account balance.

The concept is the same as daily drawdown, but it is not limited to a single day.
