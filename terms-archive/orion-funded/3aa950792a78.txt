📋 PROGRAMS & RULES
How do the Orion programs compare?

Every evaluation structure across all five Orion programs.

Programs at a glance
PROGRAM	STEPS	PROFIT TARGET(S)	DAILY LOSS	MAX DRAWDOWN	DRAWDOWN TYPE
Orion Zero	Instant	None	3%	6%	Trailing Lock
Orion Nova	1	2%	Eval 4% · Funded 3%	Eval 8% · Funded 5%	Trailing Lock
Orion Standard	2	6% | 6%	3%	6%	Static
Orion Swing	2	8% | 5%	5%	8%	Static
Orion Select	3	5% | 5% | 5%	5%	5%	Static
Minimum trading days

Orion Standard, Orion Swing, and Orion Select each require a minimum of 4 trading days during the evaluation. Orion Zero and Orion Nova have no minimum. No program requires a minimum number of trading days once you're funded.

Advancing between phases

Each phase activates automatically once you meet the trading objectives for the previous phase. No action needed from your side.

After passing all phases

Orion recommends you as a candidate for the Orion Trader Program. A third-party entity reviews your application and, if accepted, offers an Orion Trader Account contract for electronic signature.

ℹ️
Orion Nova: Pay After You Pass
Orion Nova works on a Pay After You Pass basis. You pay a small registration fee (EUR 7 / USD 7) to start the evaluation, and the activation fee for the funded account is only due once you've passed. There's no time limit to complete the Nova evaluation.
Once you complete your evaluation, you have 30 days to pay the activation fee.
✅
Hold positions whenever you like
Every Orion program allows holding positions overnight and over weekends, in both evaluation and funded phases.
