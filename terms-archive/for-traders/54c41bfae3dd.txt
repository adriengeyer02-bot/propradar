How does the Consistency Score work on Instant and Pay After Pass accounts?
Updated over a week ago

The Consistency Score means your best day's profit must not exceed a set share of your total profit. It applies to the Instant Master Account (15%) and the Pay After Pass Master Account (20%).

​

​

Consistency Score by account type

Account type

	

Best day must not exceed




Forex Instant – Master Account

	

15% of total profit




Forex Pay After Pass – Master Account

	

20% of total profit




Forex Fast, Fast Static, Classic, Instant Pro, 24/7 Trading Fast

	

No Consistency Score

Accounts no longer sold have their own rules: Crypto Fast PRO (35%) and Futures Fast (40%).

If your best day's profit is above the limit, keep trading until it falls within it.

​

​

How to calculate it

Best day's profit ÷ limit = minimum total profit you need

Account

	

Best day's profit

	

Calculation

	

Minimum total profit




Instant (15%)

	

$500

	

$500 ÷ 0.15

	

$3,333.33




Instant (15%)

	

$600

	

$600 ÷ 0.15

	

$4,000




Pay After Pass (20%)

	

$500

	

$500 ÷ 0.20

	

$2,500

Example (Instant): your best day is $500, so you need at least $3,333.33 total profit without making more than $500 on any single day. If you later make $600 in one day, recalculate: you now need $4,000.
