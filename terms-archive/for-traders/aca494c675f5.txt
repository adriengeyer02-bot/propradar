What Is Allowed With Us
What Is Allowed With Us
Updated over a week ago

At For Traders you can trade almost any legitimate strategy. You may hold trades over the weekend, trade the news (on Master Accounts with a 5-minute window around high-impact news), use Expert Advisors that support your decisions and use copy-trading software between your own accounts (with your For Traders account as the master). Every trade must stay open for at least 5 seconds.

Holding trades over the weekend

You can hold open positions over the weekend in every phase, from the Challenge to the Master Account. On 24/7 Trading, markets stay open over the weekend.

Exception: existing Futures accounts close open positions automatically at the end of market hours – see Market Hours – Futures Accounts.

​

​

News trading

Challenge phase (all accounts): you can trade the news freely.

Master Account (Forex and 24/7 Trading, including Instant and Instant Pro): you cannot open new trades 5 minutes before or 5 minutes after a high-impact news release. You can keep positions open through the news.

Profit from a trade opened inside that 10-minute window is not counted. This is not a breach of the account.

A trade opened at least 5 minutes before the news and closed inside the window keeps its profit.

Existing Crypto and Futures accounts have no news restriction.

High-impact news to watch: FED interest rate decisions, FOMC minutes, CPI, Non-Farm Payrolls, unemployment rate, GDP growth rate and crude oil inventories. Check the economic calendar in your dashboard every morning, or filter for red-folder events on Forex Factory.

​

​

Expert Advisors (EAs)

You can use EAs that support your trading: alerts for setups, position-size calculation, managing partial closes or applying your own pre-defined rules under your supervision. You must remain the decision-maker.

An EA that opens, manages or closes trades on its own is an automated bot and is forbidden – see What Is Forbidden With Us.

​

​

Copy trading between your own accounts

You may use copy-trading software, but only between your own accounts:

Between your own For Traders accounts – allowed.

With an external account (your personal account or a funded account at another firm) – allowed only if your For Traders account is the master and the external account is the slave.

Copying trades into a For Traders account from an external account, from someone else's account or from signals is forbidden.

​

​

Minimum trade duration: 5 seconds

Every trade must stay open for at least 5 seconds before you close it. This applies to all accounts and prevents high-frequency trading. Plan your strategy with trades comfortably longer than 5 seconds.
