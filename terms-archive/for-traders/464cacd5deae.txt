Instant PRO is a Master Account from day one with only three rules: a 6% trailing Max Drawdown, a 7-day inactivity rule and a news rule. There is no Daily Drawdown, no Drawdown Protection, no consistency rule and no minimum profitable days. Your profit split starts at 60% and grows by 10% with every payout up to 100%.

How the rules work

Profit Target – only closed trades count; no target on the Master Account.

Max Drawdown: trailing or balance-based (static).

Daily Drawdown – resets every day at 00:00 CE(S)T; open trades count.

Drawdown Protection – on the Master Account, open trades are closed at −2% floating loss.

Minimum Profitable Days – a profitable day is at least 0.5% of the starting balance.

News trading, weekend holding and Expert Advisors: What Is Allowed With Us.

Inactivity rule – the days count from your last closed trade.
