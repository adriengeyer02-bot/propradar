Reward Policy – Instant Account (Forex)
Updated over a week ago

On the Instant Account you can request a reward every 14 days. Your profit split starts at 70% and rises by 5% with every reward up to 90%. A 3% reward buffer must always stay on the account, and one reward can be at most $15,000.

​

Reward schedule

You can request a reward every 14 calendar days.

The cycle starts on the account activation date or on the date of your last approved reward.

Requests sent before the cycle ends are not processed.

​

​

3% reward buffer

The first 3% of profit above your starting balance must stay on the account. Only profit above this buffer can be withdrawn.

Maximum reward per cycle

One reward can be at most $15,000 per 14-day cycle. Profit above this amount stays on the account and can be requested in a later cycle.

​

​

Eligibility

The 14-day cycle is complete.

The 3% reward buffer is in place.

You meet the Instant Account rules, including 7 profitable days and the 15% Consistency Score.

KYC is verified and the contract is signed.

You submit the request in your dashboard.

If any condition is not met, the request is declined or postponed until it is.
