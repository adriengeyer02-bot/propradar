How your maximum drawdown works

Your maximum drawdown is 10%, and it trails your equity highs upward. It stops trailing once it reaches your starting balance, and then locks there permanently.

On a $10,000 account, your limit starts at $9,000. As your equity makes new highs the limit follows, always 10% below your highest equity, until it reaches $10,000. Once locked at $10,000 it never moves again, no matter how far the account grows.

 

There is no daily drawdown limit on Instant Elite. Your maximum drawdown is the only loss limit that applies.

 

A worked example on a $10,000 account

Your maximum drawdown is $1,000, so your account breaches if equity drops below $9,000.

You make $200. Your balance is $10,200 and your limit recalculates to $9,200, which is higher than $9,000, so it updates.

You lose $100. Your balance is $10,100 and the calculation gives $9,100, which is lower than $9,200, so the limit stays at $9,200. It only ever moves up.

You open a trade and equity spikes to $11,200 without closing. The calculation gives $10,200, but the limit cannot exceed your starting balance, so it locks at $10,000. This holds even if the trade later closes at breakeven.

From that point your limit is $10,000 permanently. Growing to $11,100 does not move it.

 

Floating equity counts. The limit responds to equity highs whether or not the trade is closed, which is what causes the lock in the example above.

Reaching this limit is a hard breach and results in immediate account closure.

 

The limit applies three ways:

A single trade. Your largest loss on any one trade must not reach the limit.

A trade split into several positions. The combined loss across all positions on the same idea must not reach the limit.

Re-entering the same idea. Opening a new position in the same direction, on the same instrument or a correlated instrument, within 10 minutes of closing a losing trade counts as the same idea. The combined realized loss must not reach the limit.

For more information, see 1.5% Risk Per Trade Idea Rule.

 

Getting paid

You keep 80% of profits. There are no tiers, no scaling milestones and no delays.

To request a payout you need:

At least $100 in realised profit

At least 5 active trading days in the payout cycle. An active day is one where you close trades generating at least 0.5% profit on balance.

Payouts are available every 14 days by default.

Three add-ons are available: the 3 Minimum Trading Days add-on reduces the requirement from 5 days to 3, the 7-Day Payout add-on shortens the cycle, and the OnDemand Payout add-on lets you request as soon as your trading days are met.

 

Consistency

Instant Elite accounts have no consistency rules. You manage your activity and position sizing at your own discretion.

 

Inactivity

Open and close at least one trade within any 30-day window. An account with no completed trade for 30 consecutive days is closed automatically.

 

Breaches

Instant Access accounts do not receive warnings for violations. You are expected to operate within the rules at all times, and any breach will result in account closure whether or not it was intentional.
