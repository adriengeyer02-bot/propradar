Is there an inactivity period?

Yes. Close at least one trade every 30 days. An account with no closed trades for 30 days is automatically breached, and this cannot be paused.

September 17, 2026
Copy for LLM

You need to close at least one trade every 30 days.

 

If no closed trade is recorded in a 30-day period, the account is automatically breached. This applies to every account, including funded Earnings Accounts.

 

 

When the clock starts

The clock starts when your account is purchased and activated, and resets each time you close a trade. So the 30 days run from whichever is later: the day your account was activated, or the date of your most recent closed trade.

 

A new account that is never traded is breached 30 days after activation. Buying an account and leaving it untouched does not pause anything.

 

 

What counts as activity

Only a closed trade counts.

 

These do not count:

A pending order that has not been filled. If it never triggers, no trade happened.

An open position. A position you are still holding has not closed yet, so it does not reset the clock no matter how long you hold it.

Logging in, watching charts, or changing settings.

If you are holding a long-term position and approaching 30 days, close something. A position held open for six weeks counts for nothing until the day you close it.

 

 

The account is breached automatically

The breach is applied by the system, not by a review. You will receive a warning about a week before.

 

 

You cannot pause your account

There is no mechanism to pause an account for a holiday, illness, travel or any other reason. If you know you will be away for more than 30 days, close a trade before you go.

 

 

There is no time limit, only this rule

There is no deadline for completing an evaluation. You can take as long as you need, provided you close a trade at least once every 30 days.

 

 

How to check

Your dashboard shows your recent trading activity. If you are not sure where you stand, contact us with your account number and we will tell you.

 

If you have any questions, contact us at support@blueberryfunded.com.
