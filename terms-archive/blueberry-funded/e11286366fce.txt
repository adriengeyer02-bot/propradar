Does Blueberry Funded enforce a consistency rule in trading?

Three plans carry a consistency rule: Instant Lite accounts purchased from 17 August 2026 at 15%, synthetic accounts at 25% once funded, and Synthetic Instant Core at 15% from the start. Every other Blueberry Funded account has none.

Updated this week
Copy for LLM

Most Blueberry Funded accounts have no consistency rule. Three carry one: Instant Lite accounts purchased from 17 August 2026, synthetic accounts, and Synthetic Instant Core.

 

Your account

	

Consistency rule




Instant Lite, purchased from 17 August 2026

	

Yes, 15%. Your best single trading day must sit within 15% of the total profit behind a payout.




Instant Lite (Legacy), purchased before 17 August 2026

	

None




Instant Elite

	

None




1-Step

	

None




Flex 1-Step

	

None




2-Step (Legacy)

	

None




3-Step (Legacy/Promotional)

	

None




Synthetic Starter, 1-Step, 2-Step and Instant Funding, purchased from 8 October 2026

	

Yes, 25%, once your account is funded. Instant Funding accounts carry it from the start. Evaluation phases have none.




Synthetic Instant Core

	

Yes, 15%, from the start.




Synthetic (Legacy), purchased before 8 October 2026

	

30% on Funded




Prime

	

None

The rules are separate and the numbers are not interchangeable. Instant Lite and Synthetic Instant Core both use 15%, but they are different plans with different payout rules, and no other synthetic account is ever assessed at 15%.

 

 

Not a consistency rule: minimum trading days on Flex 1-Step

If a single trade idea made more than 60% of your profit target during your evaluation, your Flex 1-Step funded account carries a condition of 4 minimum trading days before your first payout request. That is a separate rule, checked once when you pass, and it sets a trading days requirement rather than testing how your profit is spread. See How do payouts work on a Flex 1-Step account?

 

 

Not a consistency rule: the minimum hold time on synthetic plans

On synthetic accounts purchased from 8 October 2026, profit from trades held under 5 minutes does not count toward a payout once you are funded. That is a separate rule and it tests how long you held a trade, not how your profit is spread across days. See Is there a minimum hold time on synthetic trades?

 

 

If a consistency rule applies to your account

For Instant Lite, see [New] What is the 15% Consistency Rule on Instant Lite?

On Instant Lite, the rule affects when a payout is released, not whether you keep it. If your request does not meet the check it is paused rather than declined. Nothing is removed from your account and no profit is forfeited. The payout releases once further trading brings the balance into range.

 

For Synthetic Instant Core, see What is the 15% consistency rule on Synthetic Instant Core?

 

For any other synthetic accounts, see What is the 25% consistency rule on synthetic plans?

 

 

Your account keeps the rules it was sold under

Accounts purchased before 17 August 2026 continue on their existing terms, and no consistency requirement has been applied retrospectively to any account.

 

Synthetic accounts purchased before 8 October 2026 keep the 30% rule. If you are in an evaluation, your account moves to the current rules when you pass a phase. If you are already funded, it moves at your next payout. That next payout is still assessed at 30%. See Which rules apply to my synthetic account?

 

 

If no consistency rule applies

You are free to manage your trading activity and position sizing at your own discretion. We encourage consistency as a matter of good risk management, but it is not a requirement on your account.

 

 

What applies to every account

All accounts remain subject to Prohibited Strategies at all times:

Prohibited Strategies (Pre-March 12, 2026)

Prohibited Strategies (After March 12, 2026)

Non-compliance results in a breach regardless of profitability, and regardless of whether a consistency rule applies to your account.

 

 

Stock accounts

Stock based accounts are no longer available for new purchases. Traders with active stock accounts remain subject to the consistency rules specific to those accounts.

 

Not sure which account you hold, or when you bought it?

Check your dashboard, or contact support@blueberryfunded.com and we will confirm your account type and purchase date.
