Stop loss and take profit placement

Synthetic instruments have a minimum distance for stop loss and take profit orders. An order placed closer than that distance to the current price will be rejected. No other Blueberry Funded product works this way, so check this before you place your first trade. See Why was my stop loss or take profit rejected?

 

 

Consistency rule

Once your account is funded, no single trading day may account for more than 25% of the profit behind a payout. Synthetic Instant Core uses 15% instead.

 

Instant plans carry the rule from the start, because they are funded from the start.

Evaluation phases carry no consistency requirement. You can pass any phase on a single day's profit.

 

Going over the threshold does not breach your account and nothing is deducted. Your payout becomes available once your best day falls within the threshold. See What is the 25% consistency rule on synthetic plans? and What is the 15% consistency rule on Synthetic Instant Core?

 

 

Minimum hold time

Once your account is funded, a trade needs to be held for at least 5 minutes for its profit to count toward a payout. Profit from trades closed inside 5 minutes is removed when your payout is calculated.

 

This is a soft breach. Your account stays open and nothing happens to it at the time. Partial closes count, so if you close part of a position inside 5 minutes and hold the rest for longer, the profit on the part closed early is removed. See Is there a minimum hold time on synthetic trades?

 

 

Maximum risk per trade idea

Synthetic Instant Core is the only synthetic plan with a maximum risk per trade. No single trade idea may lose more than 1% of your initial account size, and going over it is a hard breach.

 

The other four plans have no risk per trade limit. See Does the 1% risk per trade idea rule apply to my synthetic account?

 

 

Payout limit on Synthetic Instant Core

Each payout on a Synthetic Instant Core account of $10,000 or more releases up to 2% of your initial account balance, and you receive your 60% share of that amount. Profit above the limit stays in your account for your next payout. See How much can I withdraw from a Synthetic Instant Core account?

 

The $2,500 and $5,000 accounts have no payout limit. The other four plans have no payout limit at any size. 

 

 

Automated trading

Expert Advisors are permitted on synthetic plans, on the same terms as our standard plans. See Do you allow bots and EA (Expert Advisor) trading?

 

Third-party EA usage is checked closely. If you did not build the software yourself, read the conditions in that article before you run it.

 

Automated trading is not permitted on synthetic accounts opened before 8 October 2026. Those accounts continue on the rules they were sold under.

 

 

Grid and Martingale

Grid trading and martingale are not permitted on synthetic plans. This is in addition to the strategies prohibited across all Blueberry Funded products.

 

Grid trading means opening multiple positions on the same instrument at set price intervals, without a stop loss, so the position size grows as the price moves against you. Martingale means increasing your position size after a loss in order to recover it.

Both are prohibited because they depend on the account absorbing an open loss that keeps growing, which is the opposite of the risk control every plan is built around.

 

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a time limit to complete the challenge?
No. Every phase is unlimited in duration. However, an account with no trading activity for more than 30 days is breached.

 

Is the max drawdown trailing or static?
Static on every plan except Synthetic Instant Core, which is trailing. A static max loss is calculated from your initial balance and does not move as your account grows. On Synthetic Instant Core the level moves up with your profit and locks once it reaches your starting balance. Your daily limit is calculated from the higher of the previous day's closing equity or balance on all plans.

 

Which platform do I use?
MT5 or TradeLocker.

 

I cannot find the synthetic instruments in MT5.
Ensure that you have purchased a synthetic account, not a regular CFD account. Open the Market Watch panel, right-click anywhere in it, and select Show All. This reveals the full list of tradable instruments.

 

What is the smallest and largest trade I can place?
Minimum trade size for FX Volatility is 0.01 lots and 0.2 lots for the rest. The maximum is 15 lots. On smaller accounts your available margin will stop you well before 15 lots. An order outside those limits is rejected by the platform, and a rejected order is not a rule breach. See What are the contract specifications for synthetic instruments?

 

Can I trade forex, gold or crypto on a synthetic account?
No. Synthetic plans trade synthetic instruments only. If you want to trade forex, metals or crypto, you need one of our standard plans.

 

Do I have to trade every day?
No. There is no time limit and no daily requirement. You need a minimum number of active trading days to qualify, and an active day means one where you made at least 0.5%.

 

Is there a consistency rule?
Yes. No single trading day may account for more than 25% of the profit behind a payout, or 15% on Synthetic Instant Core. Evaluation phases carry no consistency requirement. Instant plans carry the rule from the start.

 

Which plan has the lowest entry price?
Synthetic Starter. It has three phases instead of one or two, which is how the price is kept low.

 

Why does Synthetic Instant Core pay 60% instead of 80%?
It is funded from day one, with no evaluation to pass and no evaluation fee. The lower share is what pays for that.

 

Is there a limit on how much I can withdraw?
Only on Synthetic Instant Core accounts of $10,000 and above, where each payout releases up to 2% of your initial balance. Every other synthetic plan and size has no limit.

 

Can I use a grid or martingale strategy?
No. Grid trading and martingale are not permitted on synthetic plans purchased from 8 October 2026. Accounts opened before that date continue on the rules they were sold under.

 

Can I use an Expert Advisor?
Yes, on synthetic plans purchased from 8 October 2026, on the same terms as our standard plans. Accounts opened before that date cannot.

 

Can I reset my account if I fail?
Yes, on evaluation phases, at 20% off the base price of your plan. Resets are not available on funded synthetic accounts, or on Instant Funding and Synthetic Instant Core.

 

What is the most I can be funded?
$300,000 in synthetic account size across your profile.

 

Can I use a discount code?
Synthetic promotions run from time to time. When one is active, the code applies at checkout on the plans it covers. Codes issued for our standard plans do not work on synthetic plans.
