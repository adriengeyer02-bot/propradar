What is your IP rule regarding multiple accounts?

IP Rule

June 16, 2026
Copy for LLM

Our IP rule restricts multiple accounts from different individuals from being accessed from the same IP address to ensure the integrity and fairness of our trading environment. We also do not allow IP or trade reason masking in the platform.

 

You can log into your Blueberry Funded account using up to two devices simultaneously (such as a desktop and a mobile device) to accommodate modern trading needs while maintaining security. 

 

What This Means:

Only one account per household can be accessed from the same IP address.

If someone living in the same household wishes to purchase an account, it will not be allowed due to this restriction.

If we see any trades that we deem to be masking an IP or trade reason we consider this a hard breach of an account due to not acting in good faith, with the exception of static IP VPN/VPS usage which is permitted. E.g. Using technology that places a VPS desktop trade as a mobile trade.

If we notice rapid changing between country IP addresses in a way that is not geographically possible, we would consider this a hard breach due to not acting in good faith. e.g. In the same trading day we see IP Addresses in two distant countries being used interchangeably without a VPS or travel notification. International Travel Notification Requirement

Implications of Non-Compliance

Failing to adhere to Blueberry Funded's IP regulations may result in actions such as account review, suspension, or termination. This includes behaviors like fraudulent IP changes, sudden and unjustified location changes, or any attempts to bypass established platform rules. If you plan to travel from one country to another, please notify us in advance by sending an email with your travel itinerary to support@blueberryfunded.com 

 

 

Acceptable Practices

Consistent Device Usage: Variations in IP based on standard network transitions, such as moving from mobile data to home Wi-Fi, are acceptable as long as the device remains consistent.

Travel Notifications: International IP variations due to travel are allowed provided prior notification is made to Blueberry Funded for validation.

Prohibited Actions

IP Masking and Trade Manipulation: Using technologies that obscure true trading behaviors or hide real IP addresses violates platform regulations.

Logistically Improbable IP Changes: Rapidly switching between country IP addresses in ways that are infeasible without proper notification or verification may be flagged for investigation.

Travel dates: Notify us about your travel dates in advance.

Destination countries: Inform us about the countries you will be traveling to.

Flight itinerary: Share your departure and arrival times to help us validate IP changes.

When changing IP addresses due to international travel, retaining proof (such as tickets) can help resolve any issues efficiently.

We appreciate your understanding and cooperation in adhering to this policy. If you have any questions or need clarification, please feel free to reach out to us via Live Chat or email at support@blueberryfunded.com .
