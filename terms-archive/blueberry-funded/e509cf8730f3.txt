Do you allow News Trading?

News trading is restricted on standard plans for 2 minutes before and 2 minutes after high-impact news releases, on both evaluation and funded accounts. It does not apply to synthetic plans.

Updated this week
Copy for LLM

News trading is not allowed on our standard plans, on both evaluation and earnings accounts.

 

It does not apply to synthetic plans. Synthetic indices are generated algorithmically and do not track any real market, so economic news releases do not affect them. There is no news trading restriction on any synthetic plan.

 

If you hold both a synthetic account and a standard account, everything below applies to your standard account only.

 

What the restriction is

You must not open or close a trade in the 2 minutes before or the 2 minutes after a high-impact news release. High-impact releases are the ones marked red on the economic calendar.

 

The window covers more than manual entries:

Opening a trade

Closing a trade

A pending order triggering, whether a buy or sell stop or a limit

Modifying a trade

A take profit or stop loss being hit, even on a position you opened well before the window

 


​The six hour exception

If you opened a position more than 6 hours before the release and its stop loss is hit inside the window, that is allowed. It is not treated as a violation.

 

The exception covers the stop loss only, and only on positions held for more than six hours.

 

 

Which news events

The restriction applies to high-impact releases, which typically include:

Central bank interest rate decisions

Employment reports, such as Non-Farm Payrolls

Inflation reports

GDP releases

Other significant economic data releases

 

 

Which instruments are restricted

The restriction applies to the instruments affected by that currency’s news, not to your whole account.

News currency

	

Restricted instruments




USD

	

All pairs involving USD. Gold (XAU/USD) and Silver (XAG/USD). UK Oil (XBR/USD) and US Oil (XTI/USD). US30, US100 and SP500. Bitcoin (BTC/USD) and Ethereum (ETH/USD)




EUR

	

All EUR pairs, and DAX (GER30)




GBP

	

All GBP pairs, and FTSE (UK100)




JPY

	

All JPY pairs, and NIKKEI (JPN225)




AUD

	

All AUD pairs, and ASX 200 (AUS200)




CAD

	

All CAD pairs

For news affecting any other asset, the same restrictions apply to every forex pair involving it, in either position. In a pair such as EUR/USD, the base is the first three letters and the quote is the last three.

 

 

What happens if you trade during the window

On an earnings account:

Profit removal. Profit from trades executed during a restricted window is removed when you request a payout.

Voided payout. If three or more trades in a single payout request are high-impact news trades, the whole payout is voided and the earnings account is reset to its starting balance.

Account breach. Continued news trading across multiple payout periods, or during an evaluation, despite warnings, may lead to the account being breached.

 

How to prepare

Keep track of the economic calendar and know when high-impact releases are due.

Plan around them. Adjust your approach in advance rather than during the window.

Set stop loss and take profit levels with the window in mind, since a level being hit inside it still counts.
