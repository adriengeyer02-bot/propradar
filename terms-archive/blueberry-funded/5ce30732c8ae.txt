Prohibited Strategies (After March 12, 2026)

The strategies prohibited on all Blueberry Funded accounts, including arbitrage, hedging across platforms, high-frequency trading, tick scalping, copy trading and non-proprietary automated systems. Synthetic plans also prohibit grid trading and martingale

October 8, 2026
Copy for LLM

 

📌 Important Notice: Accounts Purchased On or After March 12th, 2026

With the launch of our updated ruleset, the following strategies have been removed from our prohibited list and are no longer restricted for new accounts:

Reverse Hand Post Taking Losses

Martingale / Grid Trading

Lot Size Restriction

Position Stacking

All-In Trading

Excessive Scalping

One-Sided Betting

One exception. Martingale and grid trading remain prohibited on synthetic plans purchased from 8 October 2026. See below.

 

The strategies below are prohibited across all account stages, Challenge and Funded, on every account. Engaging in any of them is a Hard Breach and will result in immediate account closure or denial of payout.

 

List of Prohibited Strategies

The following remain prohibited under all circumstances, regardless of when your account was purchased:

Arbitrage

Hedging across accounts or platforms

High-Frequency Trading

Tick Scalping

Exploiting our pricing or execution environment

Bad Faith Trading

Copy Trading with Third Parties

Third-Party Account Management

Non-Proprietary Automated Trading

 

Additional rules on synthetic plans

Synthetic plans purchased from 8 October 2026 carry two prohibitions that do not apply to our other products:

Grid trading - Opening multiple positions on the same instrument at set price intervals, without a stop loss, so that the total position size grows as the price moves against you.

Martingale - Increasing your position size after a loss in order to recover it. Both are prohibited because they depend on the account absorbing an open loss that keeps growing, which is the opposite of the risk control every plan is built around.

Neither applies to synthetic accounts purchased before 8 October 2026, or to any non-synthetic account purchased on or after 12 March 2026.

 

 

Arbitrage

Any form of arbitrage is prohibited. This includes latency arbitrage, long-short arbitrage and reverse arbitrage. Exploiting price inefficiencies between platforms, brokers or instruments undermines fair trading conditions and will not be tolerated.

Examples:

Using a faster data feed from an external broker to anticipate price movements on your Blueberry Funded account before our pricing updates.

Holding a long position on EURUSD with one broker and a short with another to lock in a risk-free profit from price discrepancies.

Trading against stale quotes during high-impact news events.

 

Hedging across accounts or platforms

Hedging is permitted within a single Blueberry Funded account only. Opening opposing positions across two Blueberry Funded accounts, or between your Blueberry Funded account and any external account, broker or platform, is prohibited.

Allowed:

Opening a long and a short position on the same or correlated instrument within your single Blueberry Funded account as part of a legitimate trading strategy.

Prohibited:

Holding a long position on XAUUSD on your Blueberry Funded account while holding a short on XAUUSD elsewhere to neutralise the risk.

Using a personal live account on another platform to offset positions held on your Blueberry Funded account.

 

High-frequency trading

Strategies that rely on executing very large numbers of trades in very short windows, using automated systems or algorithms built to capture micro price movements, are not permitted.

Examples:

Running an algorithm that opens and closes large volumes of trades in rapid succession to capture fractional price movements.

Using automated tools that react to order book data faster than a trader could act manually, to get ahead of price changes on our platform.

 

Tick scalping

Entering and exiting positions in extremely short windows to capture minimal price movements is prohibited. This includes any strategy that consistently targets the spread rather than market direction.

Examples:

Consistently entering and exiting trades within a very short window across multiple instruments throughout a session.

Repeatedly targeting minimal price movements over a very short window, whether by EA or manually.

 

Exploiting our pricing or execution environment

Any strategy built to take advantage of our pricing model, platform behaviour or execution conditions rather than to trade a genuine market view is prohibited.

Examples:

Placing large orders during low-liquidity windows specifically to move prices before closing at a favourable rate.

Exploiting spread widening during rollover periods to enter at artificially favourable prices.

Tick scalping is not the same as the minimum hold time on synthetic plans. They are separate rules with separate consequences.

 

Tick scalping is a prohibited strategy. It is a hard breach and ends the account.

The minimum hold time applies to funded synthetic accounts and removes the profit from trades held under 5 minutes when your payout is calculated. Your account is not closed and no breach is recorded. See Is there a minimum hold time on synthetic trades?

 

Bad faith trading

Trading in a way that is deliberately deceptive or manipulative, or designed to game the evaluation rather than demonstrate genuine trading skill, is prohibited. This includes any behaviour that misrepresents your trading activity or works around the intent of our rules.

Examples:

Deliberately inflating profit near the end of a challenge phase using risk that would not be sustainable on a funded account.

Coordinating trades with another trader to pass evaluation thresholds.

 

Copy trading and third-party account management

Copy trading with external traders, or having your account managed by a third party, is prohibited at all stages. These activities will result in immediate account termination.

This includes:

Subscribing to any signal service that executes trades on your behalf.

Granting login access to a third-party individual or service to trade your account.

Using copy trading software that mirrors trades from an external source into your Blueberry Funded account.

Note: Copy trading between your own Blueberry Funded accounts remains permitted.

 

 

Non-proprietary automated trading

Automated trading software is permitted only when it is proprietary. That means three things at once: you built it, it runs your own strategy and risk decisions, and you disclosed it to us and received our consent before running it.

Prohibited:

Running a commercial or commercial-license EA, whether paid or free.

Running an EA built for you by a developer, freelancer or third party on commission.

Running an EA obtained from a friend, a Discord group, a signal group or a marketplace.

Running any automated system, including one you built yourself, without disclosing it and receiving our consent first.

Allowed:

An EA you coded yourself, running your own strategy, disclosed and approved before use.

Utility tools that only assist with trades you decide on, such as position sizers, risk calculators and partial-close managers.

Being able to adjust an EA's settings does not make it proprietary. If you are unsure whether your setup qualifies, see Do you allow bots and EA (Expert Advisor) trading? and contact us before you run it.

 

⚠️ If you are found using any of the above strategies at any stage, your account is subject to immediate closure and any pending payouts will be denied. If you have a question about whether a specific strategy or tool is compliant, contact us via Live Chat or at support@blueberryfunded.com before you use it.

 

 

How a trade is counted

A trade is a position held on a specific pair, and can include multiple entries with similar timings and lot sizes. Multiple entries on the same pair at the same time may count as one trade. Opening a new position in the same direction within 10 minutes of closing a losing trade is treated as part of the same trade idea.

 

Instant Access sim accounts

Instant Access sim accounts do not receive warnings for violations. You are expected to trade within the rules at all times. We understand unintentional breaches happen, but staying compliant is your responsibility, and any deviation, intentional or not, leads to immediate account termination.

 

Legacy account holders

If your account was purchased before March 12th, 2026, your original trading rules remain in effect. 

 

The automated trading requirements above apply to all accounts regardless of purchase date.

 

Synthetic accounts run on a separate timeline. The changes on this page apply to them, and the synthetic-only rules above apply from 8 October 2026. See Which rules apply to my synthetic account?

 

For anything else, contact us at support@blueberryfunded.com.
