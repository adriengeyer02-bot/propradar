What is the Prime Challenge?

Prime is a two-phase evaluation with an 8% then 6% profit target, a 4% daily and 10% maximum drawdown, and an 80% profit split. It carries no consistency rule, no lot size restriction and no risk per trade limit.

Updated over 3 weeks ago
Copy for LLM

 

Prime is a two-phase evaluation for traders who want a clear path to funding with as few restrictions on their trading as possible.

 

Pass Phase 1 by reaching an 8% profit target, then Phase 2 by reaching 6%. Once funded, you keep 80% of your profits.

What Prime does not have

Prime runs a deliberately lean rule set, and three restrictions that apply elsewhere do not apply here.

No consistency rule. No requirement on how your profit is spread across days or trades.

No risk per trade idea limit. The 1.5% Risk Per Trade Idea rule introduced on 12 March 2026 does not apply to Prime.

No lot size restriction. Trade any size that fits your risk management.

 

Your drawdown limits

Both limits are calculated from your initial account balance and are fixed dollar amounts. They do not grow as your balance grows.

 

The 4% daily drawdown resets each day. Your starting point for the day is your balance or your equity, whichever is higher. On a $100,000 account the daily limit is $4,000 every day, so a day starting at $105,000 has a floor of $101,000.

 

The 10% maximum drawdown is a hard floor that never moves. On a $100,000 account it sits at $90,000, and it stays at $90,000 even if the account grows to $110,000.

 

Reaching either limit breaches the account. See What are the drawdown limits on a Prime account?

 

 

Active trading days

An active trading day is any day you close trades generating at least 0.5% profit. Floating profit on open trades does not count. See How many active trading days do I need?

 

 

Getting paid

Rewards are available every 14 days once you are funded, at an 80% split. See Reward Structure.

 

 

What does apply

Prime has fewer rules, not none.

News trading is restricted. Do not open new positions within 2 minutes either side of a scheduled high-impact release. Closing and managing existing trades is always permitted. See News Trading Policy for Prime Challenge.

Prohibited strategies apply. See Trading Style & Risk Rules for the full list.

 

Prime's two phases are not the 2-Step Challenge

Prime is a separate product with its own targets, drawdown limits and rule set. The 2-Step Challenge is no longer available to purchase effective 17 August 2026, and Prime has not replaced it or inherited its rules.
