Active Trade Days: built to make you consistent

Every payout cycle requires a set number of Active Trade Days (ATD). On standard Elite plans that is 8 for your first cycle and 10 for each one after; Direct to Funded varies by account size (5, 10, 15, or 20 per cycle). An Active Trade Day is a day you actually trade and meet your plan's criteria. It is not busywork. It is the mechanism, on purpose.

The market will test you, sim or live

Here is the lesson underneath all of it: the market will test you no matter what. Sim or live, each has its own challenges, and neither lets you off the hook. Trading involves your entire mind and body, and, for some, their soul. It is not a spreadsheet exercise; it is a mirror.

The sooner you learn these lessons, the sooner you start making better decisions and taking control of your own outcomes. The goal is not to predict the market, because no one can do that every time. The goal is to become the kind of trader who survives being wrong and compounds being right.

The lessons that actually pay

These are the things every funded trader eventually learns. Learn them early and they cost you less.

Feel both sides

You have to feel the sting of a real loss and the rush of a real win, and learn to do your job through both. The trader who never feels it never learns to manage it.

Do not celebrate too soon

A good morning is not a good month. Banking one big trade and then giving it all back is the most common way funded accounts die. Stay level when you are winning.

Patience is a position

Being patient and strategic, waiting for your setup instead of forcing one, is itself a skill. So is controlling the impulse to revenge-trade after a loss.

You are accountable

Trading shows you who you really are. It is easy to blame the news, the broker, the algo, but the one person responsible for that loss is you. Owning it is where improvement starts.

Respect the hive mind

The market is a hive mind no one predicts every time. You might win today, but you are guaranteed to lose tomorrow at some point. That is why you manage risk on every trade, not just the ones you feel unsure about.

Zoom out

It is not smart to YOLO, load up max NQ, or wait for a tweet and get wiped. The traders who last zoom out, size sensibly, and let an edge play out over hundreds of trades.

Practice anywhere, even your own cash account

We are serious about this: you should learn these lessons wherever you can, and that includes your own cash account. We actually recommend it, because there is no faster teacher than your own money.

But learn in a safe, well-supported environment like the one we provide. Trade any of the many platforms we support (TradingView, NinjaTrader, Tradovate, Quantower, Sierra Chart, MotiveWave, and many more) and find the setup that fits how you think. See every supported platform.

None of this is theater. Our mission is to find talented traders and put them under our umbrella in live markets, with our capital behind them. Sim is how we identify that talent, and payouts and Active Trade Days are how we measure it.

Prove you can stay consistent and protect what you make, and you do not stay in sim. You move up. LIVE ELITE is where that pathway leads: real capital, real markets, and payouts with no cap.
