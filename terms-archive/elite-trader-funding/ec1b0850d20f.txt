Guide to Our Commissions and Exchanges We Support

Elite Trader Funding (ETF) is committed to providing clear information about the commissions associated with trading and the exchanges we currently support. This guide outlines the key details to help you navigate our platform.

Supported Instruments

ETF exclusively supports futures products for trading. We do not offer trading in other types of financial instruments, such as stocks or options. Our focus is solely on futures markets, providing traders access to top-tier exchanges.

Supported Exchanges

The exchanges ETF supports for futures trading are:

CBOT (Chicago Board of Trade)
CME (Chicago Mercantile Exchange)
COMEX (Commodity Exchange, Inc.)
NYMEX (New York Mercantile Exchange)

These exchanges offer a wide range of futures instruments, allowing traders to engage with various commodities, financial instruments, and energy products.

Commissions

Commissions vary based on the instruments traded. For a full breakdown of commissions associated with each futures instrument, refer to the Supported Instruments table on our platform. This table provides detailed information on the specific fees tied to trading each instrument on the supported exchanges.

Commission per contract: the commissions in the Supported Instruments table are per side. A round turn (entering and exiting one contract) is double the per-side commission. Round-turn commissions for the most-traded contracts:
