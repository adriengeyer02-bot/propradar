2 Step Pro V2 - When Can I Request a Payout?

The 2-Step Pro V2 model applies to all 2-Step Pro accounts purchased after May 18th.

Written by Lars
October 5, 2026

Funded 2-Step Pro V2 traders may request a payout once all payout eligibility requirements have been met. Payouts are available on a bi-weekly basis by default. Weekly payouts may be available if you purchased the weekly payout add-on.

 

Payout essentials: minimum 3% profit, 3 profitable days of at least 0.5%, maximum 6% of the account size per request (capped at $15,000), 85% base profit split.

To request a payout, your account must have generated at least 3% profit. The account must have at least 3% profit available before a payout request can be submitted.

Requirements Before Requesting a Payout

Before submitting a payout request, the account must meet all requirements below:

Complete at least 3 profitable trading days. A profitable trading day is any trading day that closes with a net profit of at least 0.5% of your initial account balance

Minimum payout requirement of 3% must be reached

Payout request must follow the correct payout frequency

Account must not have breached the Daily Loss Limit

Account must not have breached the Maximum Loss Limit

Account must comply with the News Trading Rule

Trades must comply with the 5-minute minimum trade duration rule

All profits must be closed and reflected in the account balance

 

No Funded Consistency Rule

There is no consistency rule on funded 2-Step Pro V2 accounts. This means payout eligibility is not based on a largest winning day percentage. However, all other payout and risk requirements must still be satisfied.

 

Evaluation Fee Refund

100% of the evaluation fee is refunded on the 4th approved payout. This refund is not issued on the first, second, or third payout.
