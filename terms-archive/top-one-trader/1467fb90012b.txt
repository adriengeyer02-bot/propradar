2-Step Pro V2 Overview

The 2-Step Pro V2 model applies to all 2-Step Pro accounts purchased after May 18th.

Written by Lars
October 5, 2026

The 2-Step Pro V2 plan is designed for traders who want a structured two phase evaluation with static drawdown, high evaluation leverage, clear risk limits, and access to funded payouts once both phases are completed.

 

Applies to: all 2-Step Pro accounts purchased after May 18th.

$5,000, $10,000, $25,000, $50,000, $100,000, $150,000 and $300,000.

Key Rule Highlights

Rule

	

Details




Two Phase Evaluation

	

Complete Phase 1 (8% profit target) and Phase 2 (5% profit target). Once both phases are completed and all rules are respected, you become eligible for a funded account.




Static Drawdown

	

The maximum loss limit is static: it does not trail upward as the account grows. Daily Loss Limit 4%, Maximum Loss 9%.




Minimum Trading Days

	

To pass each phase, complete 3 profitable trading days. Each qualifying day must be at least 0.5%.




Equity Shield

	

The regular Equity Shield applies: Single Symbol Risk 2%, Total Open Risk 2.5%. Stay within these limits at all times.




Soft Breaches

	

Maximum of 8 soft breaches per account. Once the limit is reached, the account may be breached or restricted.




Weekend Holding

	

Allowed on 2-Step Pro V2 accounts.




News Trading

	

Allowed during Phase 1 and Phase 2. Not allowed on funded accounts: you may not execute trades around restricted news events. See the News Trading Rule article for full details.




5-Minute Rule

	

The 5-minute minimum trade duration rule applies. Trades must remain open for at least 5 minutes.




Expert Advisors (EAs) & External Tools

	

Evaluation phases: you may use your own unique Expert Advisor (EA), provided it complies with our trading rules.
​Funded accounts: EAs, trading bots, and external tools that connect to your trading account are not permitted. More information here.
