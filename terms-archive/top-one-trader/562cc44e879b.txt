What are the Rules for the 1 Step "Flash" Challenge Account?

An overview of the Rules for the Challenge account.

Written by Todd Hodges
October 5, 2026

This article gives an overview of all the rules for the 1-Step FLASH challenge account, from the profit target and risk limits to trading rules, payouts and platforms. Each rule links to a more detailed article.

 

Key figures: 10% profit target, 3 profitable trading days, 4% max daily loss, 7% max trailing drawdown, unlimited trading period.

Trading rules

Rule

	

Details




Trading Period

	

Unlimited, no time limit to complete




Inactivity Rule

	

You must place a trade at least once every 30 days (hard breach) (more information)




Stop-Loss

	

Required for each trade unless an add-on is purchased (soft breach) (more information)




Expert Advisors (EAs)

	

Allowed during the challenge, but must be your own unique EA. You cannot use the same EA as other traders placing identical trades (more information)




News Rule

	

Evaluation stage: you are free to trade during news events without any restrictions.
Simulated Funded Account: it is prohibited to execute or close any trade within the window of 5 minutes before and 5 minutes after a high-impact news event (more information)




Max Lot Size Rule

	

No lot size limits during the Challenge Phase.
Simulated Funded Account: max 20% of account size, capped at 20 lots total (more information)




Prohibited Strategies

	

Ensure you do not violate any prohibited trading strategies (more information)

Payouts

	

Payouts are available after a set period, with ongoing withdrawals every 14 days. Profit share ranges from 80% to 90%, with minimum payout and trading day requirements.

	

When Can I Request a Payout?




Lock Upon Payout

	

When you request a payout, your Max Drawdown locks at your starting balance and no longer trails. This affects how much buffer you have remaining.

	

How Payouts Impact Drawdown on 1-Step FLASH Accounts




Daily Profit Limit

	

Funded 1-Step FLASH accounts have a 3% daily profit cap. Once reached, trading is paused for the day and excess profits are voided. This is not a breach.
