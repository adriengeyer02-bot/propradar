Not allowed. Any system placing dozens or hundreds of orders within seconds is prohibited.

Latency / Reverse Arbitrage

Not allowed. Exploiting price feed delays or using one broker's faster feed to profit from another's slower feed is forbidden.

Absolutely not allowed. Any attempt to manipulate spreads, swaps, commissions, execution delays, or server glitches is considered fraudulent.

Not allowed in any form — within the same account, between multiple accounts under your name, or across accounts with other prop firms. Creates risk-free arbitrage.

Limited hedging within a single account is not allowed. Using hedge loops — opening buy & sell simultaneously just to bypass rules — is forbidden.

Lot Size Manipulation / Stacking

Not allowed. Opening multiple oversized positions within seconds to simulate stacking a trade is prohibited.

Exploiting Promotional Accounts

Not allowed. Using promotional accounts, trial challenges, or discounts for unfair arbitrage between firms is strictly banned.

Only if approved by our team. EAs for news trading, HFT, latency arbitrage, grid, martingale, or tick scalping are not allowed.

Gambling / Random Lot Spikes

Not acceptable. Traders must show consistent risk management. Random oversized trades, all-in positions, or lottery-style trading is not acceptable.

Not allowed. You must maintain a balanced approach. 85% or more of your trades cannot be in one direction. Placing small 0.01 lot trades to artificially balance your history is also not permitted.
