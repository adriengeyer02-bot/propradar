Earn2Trade Removes 10-Day Minimum Trading Rule; Updates Progression Ladder and Profit Split Structure

Update: Some of these rules changed on November 2, 2026. For subscriptions purchased on or after that date, the Progression Ladder is replaced by fixed contract limits, and funded traders need five profitable trading days before each withdrawal request. Read the November update.

Earn2Trade is updating its evaluation rules and profit split structure for purchases made on or after July 6, 2026.

No Minimum Trading Days to Pass

This update gives traders the opportunity to complete the evaluation sooner once they have demonstrated the trading skill, discipline, and consistency expected of funded traders.

To pass an evaluation, traders must still:

Follow risk management limits
Reach the profit target
Maintain consistency*

*Under the Maintain Consistency rule, which applies during the evaluation phase only, no single trading day can account for more than 30% of the total PnL. In practice, this means traders will need at least four profitable trading days to complete an evaluation. If one trading day accounts for more than 30% of the total profit, the trader must continue trading until that day represents 30% or less of the total.

Progression Ladder Rule Update

Under the rules introduced on July 6, 2026, exceeding the Progression Ladder limit during an evaluation resulted in a failed evaluation and required a reset before the trader could continue. In the funded phase, exceeding the limit resulted in the loss of the funded account. As explained in the November 2, 2026 update above, these rules have since been replaced by fixed contract limits for new subscriptions, with over-limit orders automatically blocked instead of causing account failure. Earlier subscriptions retain their original rules.

Profit Split Structure Update

On $25,000–$200,000 accounts, the applicable split is based on profit withdrawal amount (see the table below). If the profit withdrawal amount reaches or exceeds the threshold set for the account size, the standard 80/20 profit split applies to the full amount. If the amount is below the threshold, the profit split is 50/50.

For Trader Career Path® accounts, the threshold updates with every progression to a larger account size.

On $400,000 Trader Career Path® accounts, a fixed profit split of 60/40 applies.

Important: The updated evaluation rules and profit split structure apply to evaluations purchased on or after July 6, 2026, and to any funded accounts granted after completing those evaluations.

Profit Split Thresholds by Starting Funded Account Balance
STARTING FUNDED ACCOUNT BALANCE	PROFIT SPLIT
$25,000	50/50 under $1,500 | 80/20 from $1,500+
$50,000	50/50 under $2,250 | 80/20 from $2,250+
$100,000	50/50 under $3,000 | 80/20 from $3,000+
$150,000	50/50 under $4,000 | 80/20 from $4,000+
$200,000	50/50 under $5,000 | 80/20 from $5,000+
$400,000	Fixed 60/40 profit split
