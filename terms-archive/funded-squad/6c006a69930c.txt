Your Payout Guide: Eligibility, Requests & Processing

Learn how payouts work, including eligibility requirements, how to submit a request, processing times, and when to expect your payment.

Updated yesterday
Copy for LLM

Payouts can only be requested on weekdays. Payout requests cannot be submitted on weekends.

Reward requests can be submitted directly from your dashboard and are processed within 24-72 business hours.

A minimum withdrawal amount of $100 is required to submit a reward request. For Instant Rapid accounts, the minimum withdrawal amount is $12.50.

We process rewards through Rise and Crypto. The name on your FundedSquad account must match the name on your payout account, and your payout address must not be used on any other Fundedsquad account.

If you're found sharing the same crypto wallet address with another user, your payout will be rejected and your account permanently banned.

Your payout eligibility date and time displayed on the dashboard are shown in UTC.

🔹 Rise Payout Details

Rise is the exclusive payout method for all withdrawals above $300

A $40 processing fee applies to every Rise payout

This fee is deducted on each payout you receive

🔹 Crypto Payout Details

Crypto is used for all payouts below $300

A processing fee between $5 and $12 applies to every crypto payout

This fee depends on the amount charged by our payment provider at the time your payout is processed

When Will Your Payout Arrive?

You will receive your payout within 24–72 hours of submitting your request. Weekends and public holidays are not counted as business days.

Submit on Monday → Expect your payout by Thursday.

Submit on Friday → Expect your payout by Wednesday.

To avoid delays, please complete the required steps before you request your payout:

Complete your KYC from your dashboard → Once your KYC has been completed and approved, you will receive a confirmation email → Request your payout → If you selected Rise as your payout method, you will also receive a Rise invitation → Accept the Rise invitation to ensure your payout can be processed without delays.

Important: KYC must be completed before you can request a payout. For Instant Accounts, you can complete it at any time from the day you purchase the account.

Important: If you do not accept the Rise invitation, your payout may be delayed.

After Risk Review and Payout approval → Your payment will be processed and sent to your Rise account or crypto wallet, depending on your selected payout method, within 48 hours.

If your approved payout is not processed within 48 hours, you will receive an additional $1,000 bonus.
