Instant Account Trading Rules

A clear overview of trading strategies and practices that are not allowed on FundedSquad accounts.

October 21, 2026
Copy for LLM

A clear overview of the key trading rules and requirements you need to follow while completing your Instant account.

How Is Account Inactivity Calculated?

If your account has no trading activity for 21 consecutive calendar days, it will be breached. The inactivity period starts from the day the account is purchased, not from the day you place your first trade. 

 

This rule applies to both Evaluation and simulated Funded Accounts, so make sure to keep your account active while following all trading rules.

Stop Loss & Take Profit Rule

FundedSquad does not require traders to use Stop Loss (SL) or Take Profit (TP) levels. However, using them is strongly recommended as part of responsible risk management and to help control potential losses.

Expert Advisors (EAs)

 

Expert Advisors are allowed on FundedSquad accounts as long as they follow all trading rules and do not use any prohibited trading practices. High-Frequency Trading (HFT) and Gold Arbitrage EAs are not permitted.

VPN and VPS Policy

 

VPN and VPS usage is not allowed on FundedSquad accounts. All trading must be done without a VPN or Virtual Private Server (VPS) to maintain account security and comply with our policies.

 

Using a VPN or VPS may result in an account violation. Please ensure you trade directly from your regular device and connection.

Copy Trading
Which Accounts Can I Copy Trades Between?

You can copy trades between your own FundedSquad evaluation and funded accounts in any direction:

Evaluation to Evaluation

Evaluation to Funded

Funded to Evaluation

Funded to Funded

The following are not allowed:

Copying trades from any external account, even if you own it.

Copying trades from other traders, signal providers, or trading groups.

Using third-party trade copiers, copy-trading tools, or copier EAs.

Important: Trade copying is permitted only between your own eligible FundedSquad accounts.

Weekend Holding Rule

For Instant Account models, weekend holding is not included by default. If you want to keep your trades open over the weekend, you must purchase the Weekend Holding Add-On. Without this add-on, all positions must be closed before the weekend.

Weekend Trading Policy

 

Weekend trading is disabled across all models. Traders must not open any new positions during the weekend.

 

With the Weekend Add-On, traders are only allowed to hold existing positions over the weekend.

 

For Instant funded accounts, the Weekend Add-On permits weekend holding only. It does not allow traders to open new positions during the weekend.

What is the minimum trade duration rule, and how does the add-on work?

This rule applies only to accounts purchased after 2:00 AM UK time on 21 October 2026.

 

What is the minimum trade duration rule?

For Instant accounts, all trades must be held for a minimum of 60 seconds. Any trade closed before 60 seconds will be considered a violation of FundedSquad’s trading rules.

 

When did the 60-second rule take effect?

The 60-second minimum trade duration rule applies to funded accounts purchased after 2:00 AM UK time on 21 October 2026.

 

What if I purchased my account before 2:00 AM UK time on 21 October 2026?

If you purchased your account before this date and time, the existing 2-minute minimum trade duration rule continues to apply. You must hold each trade for at least 2 minutes unless you have the applicable No 2-Minute Rule add-on.

 

Can I bypass the minimum trade duration rule?

Yes. You can purchase the applicable minimum trade duration add-on to remove the holding-time restriction. With the add-on active ( depends on model ) , closing a trade before the required holding time will not be considered a violation of this rule.

 

What happens if I close a trade before the required holding time?

Funded accounts purchased after 2:00 AM UK time on 21 October 2026: Closing a trade before 60 seconds will be considered a rule violation unless the applicable add-on is active.

Accounts purchased before 2:00 AM UK time on 21 October 2026: Closing a trade before 2 minutes will be considered a rule violation unless the applicable No 2-Minute Rule add-on is active.

What is the minimum trade duration rule, and how does the add-on work?

This rule applies only to accounts purchased before 2:00 AM UK time on 21 October 2026.

 

What is the minimum trade duration rule?

 

For all Instant Models, all trades must be held for a minimum of 2 minutes, across all Models. Any trade closed before 2 minutes will be considered a violation of FundedSquad's rules.

 

Is there a way to bypass the minimum trade duration rule?

 

Yes. FundedSquad offers a 2-Minute Add-On. If you purchase this add-on along with your account, trades closed before 2 minutes will not be counted as a violation.

 

Which accounts is the 2-Minute Add-On available for?

 

The add-on is available for account sizes up to $10,000, across all models, including Instant accounts.
​
​Note : The Add-on is not available for Instant Flex Model.

 

Does the add-on remove the minimum trade duration rule entirely?

 

Yes. If you have the No 2-Minute Rule add-on, a trade closed before 2 minutes will not be considered a violation.

 

The add-on removes the violation consequence for early trade closures. Without the add-on, the standard minimum trade duration rule applies.

Max Risk Rule

This rule applies only to floating losses from open trades.

 

If your floating loss reaches 1.5% of your initial account balance at any time, it is considered a violation.

 

Breach Handling

 

For Instant Accounts, the first violation of the Max Risk Rule will result in an immediate account breach and termination.

 

When Does the Rule Apply?

 

The 1.5% Max Risk limit can be reached through:

One open trade reaching a floating loss of 1.5%.

Multiple open trades together reaching a total floating loss of 1.5%.

Example

For a $50,000 account:

1.5% Max Risk = $750

If one open trade reaches -$750 floating loss, the Max Risk Rule is violated.

If multiple open trades together reach -$750 floating loss, the rule is also violated.

The rule applies even if the trade later recovers or is closed in profit.

 

Important Note

 

The 1.5% Max Risk limit does not decrease because of previous closed losses.

 

Daily Drawdown and Maximum Drawdown work differently. Previous losses reduce the remaining room available under these drawdown limits.

 

Risk Management

 

Please manage your risk carefully and keep your floating loss within the 1.5% Max Risk limit. Exceeding the 1.5% limit on an Instant Account will result in an immediate account breach and termination.

Max Risk Rule (Pre 31 August, 12:00 PM UK time)

First Breach: If the account breaches the 1.5% limit, the trader will receive a 40% profit split.

 

Second Breach: If the account breaches the rules for the second time, the trader must purchase a new account. This applies to accounts purchased before 31 August, 12:00 PM UK time.

 

The 1.5% Max Risk Rule is an additional risk management rule and applies independently of the 3% Daily Drawdown and 6% Static Maximum Drawdown. All three rules must be respected at all times.

What are the news trading rules for Instant Funded accounts?

The following News Trading Rule applies only to Instant Lite and Instant Pro account models.

General Rules

Is news trading allowed on Instant Funded accounts? 

 

No. Opening new trades is not allowed during the restricted window around news on Instant Funded accounts.

 

 Am I allowed to hold trades during news? 

 

Yes, holding trades during news is allowed at all times. Only opening new trades during the restricted window is not allowed. 

 

Are All Day News events and tentative news trading allowed? 

 

Yes. All Day News events and tentative news trading are allowed, and you're free to trade during these periods. However, manage your positions carefully  FundedSquad is not responsible for losses caused by market volatility during news events.

Restricted Window

What is the restricted window? 

 

You may not open new trades 5 minutes before and 5 minutes after a news event (10 minutes total). 

 

What happens to trades opened before the restricted window?

 

 - Trades opened 2+ hours before news are fully safe both profits and account status are unaffected. 

 

- Trades opened before the restricted window are safe to hold. 

 

- If closed during news in profit, the profit is removed but the account remains safe.

 

 - If closed at a loss, it's treated as a normal loss with no issue. 

 

What happens if I open a trade inside the restricted window? 

 

This is a violation and your payout may be rejected. 

 

What if a pending order was placed before the window but executes during news?

 

 This is also a violation, and your payout will be rejected, even though the order itself was placed before the restricted window began.

 

 Can you give an example of the restricted window?

 

 If news is scheduled for 2:00 PM, the restricted window is 1:55 PM – 2:05 PM. New trades cannot be opened in this period  holding existing trades is allowed.

Scenarios

- A trade opened 2+ hours before news and closed by SL/TP or manually: no violation, account safe, P/L stands as normal. 

 

- A trade opened before the restricted window that closes in profit during news: no violation, account safe, but the profit is removed during review. Losses remain your responsibility. 

 

- A trade opened inside the restricted window that closes during or after news: violation, payout rejected. 

 

- A pending (limit) order placed before the restricted window but triggered during it: violation. 

 

- Holding trades during news under any condition: not a violation.

Risk Review and Rule Violations

 

If FundedSquad detects a rule breach or prohibited trading activity on your account, the Risk Team will conduct a full review. This review may take place when you request a payout or after you successfully complete the challenge phases.

 

If a violation is confirmed, your account will be terminated, and your payout will be rejected.

 

FundedSquad reserves the right to take appropriate action to maintain fair and consistent trading conditions across the platform.
